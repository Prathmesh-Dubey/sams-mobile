Field notes · Germany

More about Germany that matters for portable equipment

Germany: projects and investment

Germany plans to phase out coal-fired electricity generation by 2038 at the latest and aims for renewables to supply at least 80% of gross electricity consumption by 2030.

Germany: logistics and access

Germany imported 96% of its petroleum and 89% of its natural gas in 2025, and imports covered 65.6% of its total energy needs, so ports, pipelines and storage terminals are central.

Germany: logistics and access

Domestic hard coal production ended in 2018, so all hard coal is imported, adding port coal-handling and rail-transfer sites to Germany's dust-hazard areas.

Germany: geography and layout

Lignite is mined in the extreme west and east, mainly in North Rhine-Westphalia, Saxony and Brandenburg, and burned in plants near the mines, so dust and fuel-handling hazards cluster in those regions.

The term · IECEx

What “IECEx” means for a smartphone

IECEx is the international scheme for certifying equipment used in explosive atmospheres. A certificate of conformity is issued by an accredited certification body against a specific edition of the IEC 60079 standards and carries a schedule of conditions of safe use. It is not a legal requirement in most countries on its own, but many national schemes accept it as the technical basis.

The IECEx certificate for the IS-04Z1 is in progress and has not been issued. The marking target is Ex ib IIC T4, the intrinsic safety concept for Zone 1 gas with the hardest gas group (IIC) and a 135 °C temperature class.

In Germany. Large German chemical parks host international groups and contractors that are used to the IECEx scheme in other markets. A certificate of conformity names the equipment, and its schedule of conditions sets the conditions of use. IECEx certification for the IS-04Z1 is in progress, so no document can be cited yet.

Frequently asked questions

Can we buy an IECEx cell phone in Germany today?

Not yet. The IS-04Z1 is pre-launch and its certification is still in progress ahead of a Q4 2026 launch. No unit goes into a classified area before the certificate covering it exists. The datasheet, the certification schedule and safe-area evaluation units are available now.

What does an IECEx cell phone in Germany cost?

Does the IS-04Z1 hold an IECEx certificate?

Which sites in Germany does this cover?

The market guide for Germany covers Ludwigshafen, Leverkusen, Marl, Frankfurt Höchst, among others. Site-level pages are linked below.

How do I become a SAMS distributor for Germany?

Apply through the distributor page with your company details and the territory you cover. SAMS reviews applications company by company and has not yet granted a territory for Germany.

Where are German LNG and fuel import sites?

German LNG terminals supplied about 10.3% of natural gas imports in 2025, and crude, products and coal arrive through ports and pipelines. Individual terminal locations and rules change, so confirm the current list and each operator's contractor requirements before planning work.

What hazardous areas exist in Germany's lignite regions?

Lignite is mined in North Rhine-Westphalia, Saxony and Brandenburg and burned at nearby power plants, so coal dust, conveyor and bunker areas are typical dust zones. Choose devices with a dust group marking suited to the area classification, and check with the operator.

Does a German site accept an international certificate?

Acceptance is the decision of the operator and its approved inspection body. ATEX is the usual reference for equipment in the EU, and IECEx papers may support a review. Ask the site's explosion protection officer which evidence they require before ordering.
