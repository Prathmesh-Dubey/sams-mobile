More about Lagos that matters for portable equipment

Lagos: geography and layout

Lagos is built on lands separated by creeks around the south-west mouth of the Lagos Lagoon and protected from the Atlantic by barrier islands and sand spits, a humid coastal setting that is hard on equipment.

Lagos: logistics and access

Lagos has three ports, at Apapa, Tin Can Island and the Lekki deep-sea port, plus four expressways and a standard-gauge rail link to Abeokuta and Ibadan, and handles 88% of Nigeria's exports.

Lagos: infrastructure

The Lagos Free Zone covers 830 hectares about 60 km east of Lagos city, is integrated with the Lekki Deep Sea Port, and counts Raffles Oil among its first companies.

Lagos: people and language

The term · Ex-proof

What “Ex-proof” means for a smartphone

“Ex-proof” is shorthand for equipment carrying an explosion-protection marking (the Ex mark) that matches the area it enters. The marking states the protection concept, gas or dust group and temperature class, and it must be backed by a certificate issued by a recognised body, so a phone described as Ex-proof should come with a certificate number you can check.

The IS-04Z1 is designed to carry the marking II 2 G Ex ib IIC T4. That is a target: certification is in progress, no certificate has been issued, and SAMS will publish the certificate numbers when they exist.

In Lagos. At the Dangote refinery, a fuel or LPG storage site or a tanker jetty, a marking check reads zone, gas group and temperature class on the label and compares each with the operator's classification. LPG and fuel vapours fall within common gas groups, and the IS-04Z1 target marking II 2 G Ex ib IIC T4 would be compared with them.

Frequently asked questions

Can we buy an Ex-proof mobile phone in Lagos today?

Not yet. The IS-04Z1 is pre-launch and its certification is still in progress ahead of a Q4 2026 launch. No unit goes into a classified area before the certificate covering it exists. The datasheet, the certification schedule and safe-area evaluation units are available now.

What does an Ex-proof mobile phone in Lagos cost?

There is no list price yet. SAMS gives indicative pre-launch pricing in writing, against a stated quantity and site, and does not commit to a lead time before certification completes.

What Ex marking will the IS-04Z1 carry?

The marking target is II 2 G Ex ib IIC T4. It becomes a marking only when the certificate is issued; until then it is a design target.

Which areas at Lagos would use it?

Likely candidates are refinery process units, fuel and lpg storage, port tanker jetties and loading. Which areas are classified, and to what zone, is set by the site operator's own area classification drawings.

Is intrinsically safe the same as explosion proof?

Not exactly. Explosion proof is a broad everyday term; intrinsic safety is one protection concept, which limits energy so ignition cannot occur. Flameproof (Ex d) is a different concept that contains an explosion. The IS-04Z1 is intrinsically safe by design.

Where are the main hazardous-area sites in Lagos?

The Dangote refinery at Lekki is the largest, and the port areas of Apapa, Tin Can Island and Lekki handle fuel and bulk cargo. Shipyards, breweries and pharmaceutical plants also have classified areas. Confirm each site's zone classification and device rules with its operator.

How should I plan equipment logistics to Lagos?

Most imported equipment arrives through Apapa, Tin Can Island, Lekki or the international airport, and port congestion and customs clearance can add time. Agree a clearing agent, check radio-device import rules and allow buffer time before any shutdown or commissioning date.

Which Nigerian portal do oil and gas suppliers use?

Oil and gas suppliers often register on the NCDMB-managed NOGIC-JQS portal, and large operators also run their own formal vendor registration. Check which applies to your route to market. Confirm current requirements with the site operator or its procurement team.
