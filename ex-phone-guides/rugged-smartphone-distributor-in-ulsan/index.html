Ulsan: climate and equipment wear

Ulsan has a monsoon-influenced humid subtropical climate, with monthly means from 2.0 degrees Celsius in January to 25.9 degrees in August and high rainfall from April to September.

Ulsan: infrastructure

The Ulsan Green Hydrogen Town project had laid 188 km of underground pipelines by October 2024 to carry hydrogen, a by-product of petrochemical complexes, to the city centre.

Ulsan: background

Ulsan was designated a Special Industrial Zone in 1962 under South Korea's first five-year plan, and Hyundai Heavy Industries founded its shipyard there in 1973, turning it into a company town.

The term · rugged

What “rugged” means for a smartphone

A rugged phone is built to survive water, dust, drops and rough handling, usually rated by an IP code and a drop specification. Ruggedness is an environmental property: it says nothing about whether a phone may be used where flammable gas or dust is present. That is decided by explosion-protection certification and marking.

The IS-04Z1 combines both: an IP68 sealed rugged build and a design for Zone 1 and Zone 21 (marking target Ex ib IIC T4). The explosion-protection certificates are in progress and none has been issued.

In Ulsan. Ulsan is a south-east coastal industrial city, so sea air, summer rain and winter cold are routine for outdoor crews. IP68 sealing is designed in for the IS-04Z1 and helps with those conditions. It is a dust and water rating, not explosion protection, which depends on certification in progress.

Questions · Ulsan

Frequently asked questions

Can we buy a rugged smartphone in Ulsan today?

Not yet. The IS-04Z1 is pre-launch and its certification is still in progress ahead of a Q4 2026 launch. No unit goes into a classified area before the certificate covering it exists. The datasheet, the certification schedule and safe-area evaluation units are available now.

What does a rugged smartphone in Ulsan cost?

No price is published. While certification is in progress there is no list price and no committed lead time, and a supplier quoting a firm figure for an uncertified Ex handset is promising something it cannot stand behind. Indicative pre-launch pricing is issued in writing against a stated quantity and site.

How rugged is the IS-04Z1?

It is designed to IP68 sealing, with glove-friendly controls. Ruggedness is not explosion protection: that depends on the certificates, which are in progress.

Which areas at Ulsan would use it?

Likely candidates are refinery process units, petrochemical plants, tank farms, shipyard tank and cargo-space work. Which areas are classified, and to what zone, is set by the site operator's own area classification drawings.

How do I become a SAMS distributor for Ulsan?

Apply through the distributor page with your company details and the territory you cover. SAMS reviews applications company by company and has not yet granted a territory for Ulsan.

Which hazardous-area industries are concentrated in Ulsan?

Two very large refineries, petrochemical complexes, a major shipyard, an automobile plant and a growing hydrogen network. Gas, vapour and hydrogen areas are the typical concerns, and each operator classifies its own plant, so request zone drawings before using handheld devices.

Can shipyard work in Ulsan involve classified areas as well as refinery work?

Yes. Fuel and cargo tanks, paint stores, coating bays and hot-work areas can all be treated as hazardous locations. Shipyards usually issue gas-free and hot-work permits, so ask how portable devices are controlled under those permits.

Do shipyard and refinery requirements differ in Ulsan?

They can. Shipyards work in vessel tank and cargo spaces under permits for each vessel, while refineries classify fixed process areas, so approvals and markings may differ. The IS-04Z1 has no certificate yet. Ask each facility for its own device rules.
