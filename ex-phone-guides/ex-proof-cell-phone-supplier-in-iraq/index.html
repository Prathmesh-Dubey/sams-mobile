More about Iraq that matters for portable equipment

Iraq: logistics and access

Fields managed by Basra Oil Company exported about 3.23 million barrels a day from southern terminals in December 2024, nearly all of Iraq's export volume that month.

Iraq: climate and equipment wear

Summers from May to October are very hot and dry, with temperatures above 46 degrees Celsius, and dry dusty winds, while winters are wetter and cooler.

Iraq: logistics and access

Umm Qasr is Iraq's main deep-water port, opened in July 1967, with a railway link to Basra and Baghdad.

Iraq: rules and approvals

Iraq's Communications and Media Commission issues type approval certificates valid for one year, and the application normally goes through a registered local partner.

The term · Ex-proof

What “Ex-proof” means for a smartphone

“Ex-proof” is shorthand for equipment carrying an explosion-protection marking (the Ex mark) that matches the area it enters. The marking states the protection concept, gas or dust group and temperature class, and it must be backed by a certificate issued by a recognised body, so a phone described as Ex-proof should come with a certificate number you can check.

The IS-04Z1 is designed to carry the marking II 2 G Ex ib IIC T4. That is a target: certification is in progress, no certificate has been issued, and SAMS will publish the certificate numbers when they exist.

In Iraq. An Iraqi marking check would compare the device's gas group, category and temperature class with the zone plan at the degassing station, gas plant or terminal. Sour crude and associated gas may affect classification, which the operator decides. The IS-04Z1 marking target is II 2 G Ex ib IIC T4, and it is not yet granted.

Frequently asked questions

Can we buy an Ex-proof cell phone in Iraq today?

Not yet. The IS-04Z1 is pre-launch and its certification is still in progress ahead of a Q4 2026 launch. No unit goes into a classified area before the certificate covering it exists. The datasheet, the certification schedule and safe-area evaluation units are available now.

What does an Ex-proof cell phone in Iraq cost?

There is no list price yet. SAMS gives indicative pre-launch pricing in writing, against a stated quantity and site, and does not commit to a lead time before certification completes.

What Ex marking will the IS-04Z1 carry?

The marking target is II 2 G Ex ib IIC T4. It becomes a marking only when the certificate is issued; until then it is a design target.

Which sites in Iraq does this cover?

The market guide for Iraq covers Rumaila, West Qurna, Zubair, Majnoon, among others. Site-level pages are linked below.

How will SAMS supply Iraq?

Through authorised distributors once certificates exist. Until then SAMS can send the datasheet and certification schedule and arrange safe-area evaluation units.

Where is Iraq's main oil-field activity?

The south around Basra is the heart of production and export, with Rumaila, Majnoon and other fields, while the north has Kirkuk and the Kurdistan Region has its own fields. Each area has separate operators and access rules.

How hot does it get for field equipment?

Summer temperatures can exceed 46 degrees Celsius, and southern Iraq regularly sees higher peaks. Dust-laden winds are also common. Choose devices with a wide operating temperature range and dust protection.

Does the Kurdistan Region have separate requirements?

The market guide notes that the Kurdistan Region runs on its own registrations and logistics. Requirements for a project there may differ from those in the south. Check with the operator and local authorities which documents and registrations are needed before ordering.
