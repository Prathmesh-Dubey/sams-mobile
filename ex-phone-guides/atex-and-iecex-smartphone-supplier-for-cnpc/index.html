CNPC: projects and investment

CNPC reports around 30 international exploration and production projects, with operations in countries such as Azerbaijan, Canada, Indonesia, Oman, Peru and Thailand, so some staff work under foreign site rules.

CNPC: background

CNPC originated as a government department after 1949, when a Fuel Industry Ministry was formed, and the group was restructured in 1998 along upstream and downstream lines.

CNPC: who works there

CNPC is the government-owned parent of listed PetroChina, created in 1999, which holds most of the group's exploration, refining, marketing, chemicals and natural gas businesses, so site buyers are often PetroChina units.

CNPC: projects and investment

In July 2013 CNPC and Eni signed a 4.2 billion dollar deal for a 20% stake in an offshore natural gas block in Mozambique, an example of its overseas gas investments.

The term · ATEX and IECEx

What “ATEX and IECEx” means for a smartphone

ATEX is the European legal framework for equipment used in explosive atmospheres (Directive 2014/34/EU). IECEx is the international certification scheme built on the IEC 60079 standards. Buyers outside Europe often ask for both: an IECEx certificate is the technical evidence most national regimes build on, while ATEX is the legal route into the European market.

For the IS-04Z1 both routes are in progress with TÜV in Germany and neither certificate has been issued. The marking target is II 2 G Ex ib IIC T4: equipment Group II (surface industry), Category 2 (Zone 1 gas), protection concept ib (intrinsic safety), gas group IIC and temperature class T4.

In CNPC. A CNPC reviewer assessing a handset for the Changqing oilfield or a refinery or petrochemical plant would look for a certificate of conformity, schedule of conditions and marking. ATEX and IECEx evidence helps international contractors and EPCs working with CNPC in China and overseas. The IS-04Z1 is in certification and nothing has been issued.

Frequently asked questions

Can CNPC buy the IS-04Z1 today?

Not yet. The IS-04Z1 is pre-launch and its certification is still in progress ahead of a Q4 2026 launch. No unit goes into a classified area before the certificate covering it exists. The datasheet, the certification schedule and safe-area evaluation units are available now.

What does an ATEX and IECEx smartphone for CNPC cost?

SAMS has not published a price. Any figure you are offered for an uncertified Ex phone should be treated with caution; written indicative pricing is available for a defined quantity and site.

Is the IS-04Z1 ATEX and IECEx certified?

Not yet. Both routes are in progress with TÜV in Germany and no certificate has been issued. Any listing that says otherwise is wrong.

How does CNPC approve suppliers?

Supplier registration and equipment approval are run by the organisation itself; confirm current requirements with its procurement and HSE teams.

How will SAMS supply CNPC?

Through authorised distributors once certificates exist. Until then SAMS can send the datasheet and certification schedule and arrange safe-area evaluation units.

What language is expected on documentation for Chinese sites?

Chinese is the working language, so labelling, safety instructions and training for hazardous-area devices are normally expected in Chinese, with English accepted for some engineering discussion. Confirm the documentation language with the site's procurement and safety departments.

Is CNPC or PetroChina the right contact for supplying Ex phones?

PetroChina holds most of the operating assets, including refining, chemicals and gas, while CNPC is the parent group with other businesses such as drilling services. The right contact depends on the site, so ask the local oilfield or refinery safety and procurement teams which entity places the order.

Does CNPC accept IECEx documents for equipment approval?

CNPC runs its own supplier registration and equipment approval, so the answer depends on its current requirements and any national rules. IECEx documents help comparison. The IS-04Z1 has no certificate yet. Confirm requirements with CNPC's procurement and HSE teams before planning an evaluation.
