The term · ATEX and IECEx

What “ATEX and IECEx” means for a smartphone

ATEX is the European legal framework for equipment used in explosive atmospheres (Directive 2014/34/EU). IECEx is the international certification scheme built on the IEC 60079 standards. Buyers outside Europe often ask for both: an IECEx certificate is the technical evidence most national regimes build on, while ATEX is the legal route into the European market.

For the IS-04Z1 both routes are in progress with TÜV in Germany and neither certificate has been issued. The marking target is II 2 G Ex ib IIC T4: equipment Group II (surface industry), Category 2 (Zone 1 gas), protection concept ib (intrinsic safety), gas group IIC and temperature class T4.

Producers and service contractors with international operations may ask for dual ATEX and IECEx evidence so the same device record can be reused abroad. The IS-04Z1 certificates are in progress and not issued.

Which areas at Midland and Odessa, Texas would use it?

Likely candidates are wellsites and tank batteries, drilling rigs, compressor stations, gas processing plants. Which areas are classified, and to what zone, is set by the site operator's own area classification drawings.

Is intrinsically safe the same as explosion proof?

Not exactly. Explosion proof is a broad everyday term; intrinsic safety is one protection concept, which limits energy so ignition cannot occur. Flameproof (Ex d) is a different concept that contains an explosion. The IS-04Z1 is intrinsically safe by design.

What is the best way to reach Midland Odessa sites from overseas?

Midland International Air and Space Port sits between the two cities and is the closest airport; most visitors connect through a larger Texas hub. Allow for long road transfers because the Permian Basin covers about 86,000 square miles.

Do oilfield sites around Midland Odessa have different needs from refineries?

Oilfield sites are spread over a large area with wellpads, tank batteries and gas processing plants, so lone-worker and coverage requirements often matter as much as area classification. Confirm classified zones and approval rules with each operator.

Who buys handhelds for oilfield crews in Midland and Odessa?

Producers and service contractors usually buy through their own procurement or field contractors, so the buyer may be a service company rather than the operator. The IS-04Z1 is pre-launch with no certificate. Ask the relevant site operator which approvals apply to contractor devices.
