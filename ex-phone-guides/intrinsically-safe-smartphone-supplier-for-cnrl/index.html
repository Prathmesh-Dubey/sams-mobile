The term · intrinsically safe

What “intrinsically safe” means for a smartphone

Intrinsic safety limits the electrical and thermal energy in a circuit so that neither a spark nor a hot surface can ignite a gas or dust atmosphere, even when a fault occurs. The ib level, used for Zone 1, tolerates one fault. It is the usual way to make a handheld device that a worker carries continuously through a hazardous area.

The IS-04Z1 is designed on this principle for Zone 1 and Zone 21 (marking target II 2 G Ex ib IIC T4). Intrinsic safety is a design basis, not a certificate: certification is in progress and none has been issued yet.

In CNRL. Upgrader rounds, shutdown inspections and field checks at Alberta production sites involve photographing equipment, reading tags and recording faults in classified areas. A handheld designed as intrinsically safe could carry that on one device. The IS-04Z1 is designed for this use, though certification is still in progress.

Questions · CNRL

Frequently asked questions

Can CNRL buy the IS-04Z1 today?

No. Nothing can be bought or taken into a classified area today: certification is in progress and launch is planned for Q4 2026. What is available now is the datasheet, the written certification schedule and evaluation units for safe-area use.

What does an intrinsically safe smartphone for CNRL cost?

SAMS has not published a price. Any figure you are offered for an uncertified Ex phone should be treated with caution; written indicative pricing is available for a defined quantity and site.

What does ‘intrinsically safe’ mean for the IS-04Z1?

The circuits are designed to limit energy so a spark or hot surface cannot ignite a gas or dust atmosphere, even with one fault (level ib, Zone 1). Certification of that design is in progress.

How does CNRL approve suppliers?

CNRL uses ISNetworld (ISN) for health, safety and environment qualification; businesses register through the prospective suppliers page, and all contractors must ultimately qualify with ISN to work for CNRL.

How will SAMS supply CNRL?

Through authorised distributors once certificates exist. Until then SAMS can send the datasheet and certification schedule and arrange safe-area evaluation units.

How cold do oil sands sites get and does it affect phone use?

Winters in northern Alberta can be severe, with long periods well below freezing, which reduces battery capacity and can affect screens and gloves-on use. Check the device's rated operating temperature and ask the site about its cold-weather work rules.

Are Horizon and the Athabasca project different sites for equipment approval?

They are separate large mining and upgrading operations with their own contractors, inductions and permit systems. A device approved at one site is not automatically accepted at another, so confirm each site's requirements with its safety and electrical teams.

Does Alberta winter cold matter for a handset at Horizon?

Yes. Low temperatures reduce battery capacity and make touchscreens harder to use with gloves. Check the device's stated operating temperature range against site conditions, and keep spares warm in a safe area. IP68 helps against snow melt and dust but is not explosion protection. Confirm with the operator.
