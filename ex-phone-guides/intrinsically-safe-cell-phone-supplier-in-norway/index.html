More about Norway that matters for portable equipment

Norway: industry on the ground

Norway was the eighth largest crude oil exporter and the third largest natural gas exporter in 2011, with gas exports of 99 billion cubic metres, so gas processing and export terminals are major classified-area sites.

Norway: who works there

Equinor, formed by the 2007 merger of Statoil with Norsk Hydro's oil and gas division, is headquartered in Stavanger, with the Norwegian state as its largest shareholder at 67% as of 2017.

Norway: industry on the ground

Norway: industry on the ground

The term · intrinsically safe

What “intrinsically safe” means for a smartphone

Intrinsic safety limits the electrical and thermal energy in a circuit so that neither a spark nor a hot surface can ignite a gas or dust atmosphere, even when a fault occurs. The ib level, used for Zone 1, tolerates one fault. It is the usual way to make a handheld device that a worker carries continuously through a hazardous area.

The IS-04Z1 is designed on this principle for Zone 1 and Zone 21 (marking target II 2 G Ex ib IIC T4). Intrinsic safety is a design basis, not a certificate: certification is in progress and none has been issued yet.

In Norway. Rounds on a wet deck in the northern North Sea, at Hammerfest and Melkøya inside the Arctic Circle, or on a January winter round at Mongstad are long and outdoors. An intrinsically safe handheld lets an operator log findings and photograph equipment without waiting for permits. Cold hands and gloves are part of the specification conversation.

Questions · Norway

Frequently asked questions

Can we buy an intrinsically safe cell phone in Norway today?

No. Nothing can be bought or taken into a classified area today: certification is in progress and launch is planned for Q4 2026. What is available now is the datasheet, the written certification schedule and evaluation units for safe-area use.

What does an intrinsically safe cell phone in Norway cost?

SAMS has not published a price. Any figure you are offered for an uncertified Ex phone should be treated with caution; written indicative pricing is available for a defined quantity and site.

What does ‘intrinsically safe’ mean for the IS-04Z1?

The circuits are designed to limit energy so a spark or hot surface cannot ignite a gas or dust atmosphere, even with one fault (level ib, Zone 1). Certification of that design is in progress.

Which sites in Norway does this cover?

The market guide for Norway covers Stavanger and Sola, Bergen and Flesland, Kårstø, Kollsnes, among others. Site-level pages are linked below.

How will SAMS supply Norway?

Through authorised distributors once certificates exist. Until then SAMS can send the datasheet and certification schedule and arrange safe-area evaluation units.

Do Norwegian offshore installations require ATEX or IECEx devices?

Norway applies ATEX through the European Economic Area agreement, and operators commonly accept IECEx as well. Offshore, each installation adds its own rules for equipment approval and for use of wireless devices. Ask the operator for the approved equipment procedure before shipping devices.

What should a supplier ask the operator about device approvals offshore?

Ask which equipment marking is required, whether wireless devices need a radio permit onboard, how devices are registered and tagged, and whether English documents are accepted. Offshore installations often require pre-approval before devices are shipped by helicopter or supply vessel.

Will the IS-04Z1 manage a Norwegian winter on an exposed deck?

IP68 sealing and a rugged build are designed for wet and cold work, but the ambient range is fixed by the certificate, which is not yet issued and will be published with the datasheet. Compare that range with your coldest site conditions and confirm with the operator before use.
