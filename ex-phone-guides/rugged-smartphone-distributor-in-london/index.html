Local conditions · London and the Thames

What London and the Thames means for portable equipment

Thames Oilport

The former Coryton refinery, which stopped refining in 2012 after Petroplus collapsed, became a fuel import terminal. Greenergy holds two thirds and Shell one third after Greenergy bought Vopak's share.

West Thurrock

The terminal at West Thurrock, Essex, with about 86 tanks for petroleum products, chemicals and liquefied gas, was acquired in 2016 by Navigator Terminals, a Greenergy and Macquarie joint venture.

Field notes · London and the Thames

More about London and the Thames that matters for portable equipment

London and the Thames: climate and equipment wear

London has a temperate oceanic climate with about 600 mm of rain a year but 109.6 rainy days, so showers are frequent although annual totals are low.

London and the Thames: rules and approvals

The Port of London Authority has jurisdiction on the tidal Thames, and the London Fire Brigade is the statutory fire and rescue service for Greater London, so riverside and fire-safety rules involve separate bodies.

London and the Thames: infrastructure

The Thames is tidal and London is vulnerable to flooding, a risk that has grown with rising high-water levels, which affects riverside and low-lying industrial sites.

London and the Thames: logistics and access

The Port of London covers the tidal Thames from Teddington Lock to a boundary line out in the North Sea, is governed by the Port of London Authority (a public trust since 1908), and was the UK's largest port in 2020.

The term · rugged

What “rugged” means for a smartphone

A rugged phone is built to survive water, dust, drops and rough handling, usually rated by an IP code and a drop specification. Ruggedness is an environmental property: it says nothing about whether a phone may be used where flammable gas or dust is present. That is decided by explosion-protection certification and marking.

The IS-04Z1 combines both: an IP68 sealed rugged build and a design for Zone 1 and Zone 21 (marking target Ex ib IIC T4). The explosion-protection certificates are in progress and none has been issued.

In London and the Thames. The Thames estuary brings wind, rain, damp and some salt air to open tank farms and jetties. Conditions are generally temperate rather than extreme. An IP68 rating helps with wet work and accidental drops, but it is separate from explosion protection, which depends on certification that remains in progress for SAMS.

Questions · London

Frequently asked questions

Can we buy a rugged smartphone in London today?

Not yet. The IS-04Z1 is pre-launch and its certification is still in progress ahead of a Q4 2026 launch. No unit goes into a classified area before the certificate covering it exists. The datasheet, the certification schedule and safe-area evaluation units are available now.

What does a rugged smartphone in London cost?

No price is published. While certification is in progress there is no list price and no committed lead time, and a supplier quoting a firm figure for an uncertified Ex handset is promising something it cannot stand behind. Indicative pre-launch pricing is issued in writing against a stated quantity and site.

How rugged is the IS-04Z1?

It is designed to IP68 sealing, with glove-friendly controls. Ruggedness is not explosion protection: that depends on the certificates, which are in progress.

Which areas at London and the Thames would use it?

Likely candidates are fuel storage terminals, marine jetties, road tanker loading, gas network sites. Which areas are classified, and to what zone, is set by the site operator's own area classification drawings.

How do I become a SAMS distributor for London and the Thames?

Apply through the distributor page with your company details and the territory you cover. SAMS reviews applications company by company and has not yet granted a territory for London and the Thames.

Who should I contact about explosive atmospheres in London workplaces?

Employers are responsible under the Dangerous Substances and Explosive Atmospheres Regulations, enforced by the Health and Safety Executive or local authorities, and the London Fire Brigade handles fire safety. Ask the premises for its hazardous-area register and risk assessment.

Is London a hazardous-area site or mainly a place for meetings and approvals?

Mainly the latter. Head offices, regulators, consultants and insurers are concentrated in London, while major process plants and terminals are in estuary and regional locations elsewhere. Confirm the actual site and its classification before assuming any hazardous-area requirement.

Does London have refineries or only terminals?

No refinery. The former Coryton refinery stopped refining in 2012 and became the Thames Oilport fuel import terminal, and a large terminal operates at West Thurrock in Essex. Both are on the Thames estuary outside Greater London. Confirm access and equipment requirements with the site operator.
