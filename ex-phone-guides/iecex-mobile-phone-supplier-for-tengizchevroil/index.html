The term · IECEx

What “IECEx” means for a smartphone

IECEx is the international scheme for certifying equipment used in explosive atmospheres. A certificate of conformity is issued by an accredited certification body against a specific edition of the IEC 60079 standards and carries a schedule of conditions of safe use. It is not a legal requirement in most countries on its own, but many national schemes accept it as the technical basis.

The IECEx certificate for the IS-04Z1 is in progress and has not been issued. The marking target is Ex ib IIC T4, the intrinsic safety concept for Zone 1 gas with the hardest gas group (IIC) and a 135 °C temperature class.

In Tengizchevroil. The Tengiz joint venture has international shareholders and contractor teams, so its reviewers are used to IECEx documents. They look for a certificate of conformity and its schedule of conditions. The IS-04Z1 is targeting IECEx, which is in progress, so Tengizchevroil staff should treat it as a planned route and not a held approval.

How does the climate near the north-eastern Caspian affect phones in the field?

Continental climates around the north Caspian typically bring very cold winters and hot, dusty summers. For handheld devices this means checking operating temperature range, battery behaviour in cold, and ingress protection against dust. Confirm the site's temperature limits with the operator.

Is Tengiz a sour-gas environment that affects mobile device selection?

Tengiz is a large Caspian oil field, and sour hydrocarbon handling is common in such operations, so gas-detection and hazardous-area discipline matter. Whether specific areas are classified Zone 1 or Zone 2 is decided per installation, so confirm each work area's classification with the operator.
