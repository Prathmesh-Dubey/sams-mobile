Bahrain: geography and layout

Bahrain covers about 694 square kilometres, with Bahrain Island making up roughly 78 percent. It lies about 24 km off the Saudi coast and 28 km from Qatar.

Bahrain: logistics and access

Mina Salman is Bahrain's primary cargo port and customs point, covering 80 hectares since 1962, and more than 75 percent of its cargo is containerised.

Bahrain: background

The term · explosion proof

What “explosion proof” means for a smartphone

“Explosion proof” is the phrase most buyers use for equipment that may be taken into a classified hazardous area. Strictly, it covers several protection concepts: a flameproof enclosure (Ex d) contains an internal explosion, while intrinsic safety (Ex i) limits electrical energy so that an ignition cannot start. Phones are normally intrinsically safe, because a flameproof handset would be too heavy to carry all shift.

The IS-04Z1 uses intrinsic safety, with a marking target of Ex ib IIC T4, and is designed for Zone 1 and Zone 21. It is pre-launch, certification is in progress and no certificate has been issued.

In Bahrain. At Sitra, Askar and Hidd the phrase explosion proof often means flameproof Ex d equipment. A handheld in a refinery tank farm or a potline area is a different case, and the IS-04Z1 uses intrinsic safety to limit energy. The refinery gas areas and the smelter dust areas are classified by the operators, not SAMS.

Frequently asked questions

Can we buy an explosion proof cell phone in Bahrain today?

Not yet. The IS-04Z1 is pre-launch and its certification is still in progress ahead of a Q4 2026 launch. No unit goes into a classified area before the certificate covering it exists. The datasheet, the certification schedule and safe-area evaluation units are available now.

What does an explosion proof cell phone in Bahrain cost?

No price is published. While certification is in progress there is no list price and no committed lead time, and a supplier quoting a firm figure for an uncertified Ex handset is promising something it cannot stand behind. Indicative pre-launch pricing is issued in writing against a stated quantity and site.

Is the IS-04Z1 explosion proof or intrinsically safe?

It is intrinsically safe (marking target Ex ib IIC T4), which is the usual way to make a phone for hazardous areas. It is not a flameproof (Ex d) design, and certification is still in progress.

Which sites in Bahrain does this cover?

The market guide for Bahrain covers Sitra, Askar, Hidd, Awali, among others. Site-level pages are linked below.

How will SAMS supply Bahrain?

Through authorised distributors once certificates exist. Until then SAMS can send the datasheet and certification schedule and arrange safe-area evaluation units.

Do phones sold in Bahrain need TRA approval?

Yes. Telecommunications equipment imported into Bahrain is subject to TRA type approval, and a certificate normally lasts three years. The application is made through a Bahraini supplier, and the TRA has just released version 1.1 of its technical specifications, so check current requirements.

Is Bahrain small enough to cover all sites from one base?

The country is about 694 square kilometres and Bahrain Island is only 48 km long, so Manama is within short road distance of Sitra and the main industrial areas. Site access, not distance, is usually the planning constraint.

Can the camera be used inside Bahrain plants?

Photography often runs on permit at Bahraini sites. Android 16 with EMM and MDM lets the camera policy and corporate profile be set centrally, so the camera can be restricted or allowed per site. The 50 MP camera is a feature, not a right of use. Check the permit rules with the site operator.
