Field notes · Poland

More about Poland that matters for portable equipment

Poland: industry on the ground

Poland's energy sector is the fifth largest in Europe, with an installed electricity capacity of about 61 GW at the end of 2024, so power plants and mines are large sources of classified areas.

Poland: industry on the ground

In 2020 coal made up 69.5% of national energy production and 68.5% of electricity generation, so coal handling, bunkers and mills create combustible dust zones at many power sites.

Poland: background

PKN Orlen was created in 1999 by merging the state fuel retailer CPN with Petrochemia Płock, the firm running the Płock refineries, and has since grown into a multinational refiner and gas trader.

Poland: infrastructure

Bełchatów Power Station in the Łódź region supplies almost 20% of Poland's energy and is the largest brown coal power plant in the EU, a major example of a large dust-hazard site.

The term · rugged

What “rugged” means for a smartphone

A rugged phone is built to survive water, dust, drops and rough handling, usually rated by an IP code and a drop specification. Ruggedness is an environmental property: it says nothing about whether a phone may be used where flammable gas or dust is present. That is decided by explosion-protection certification and marking.

The IS-04Z1 combines both: an IP68 sealed rugged build and a design for Zone 1 and Zone 21 (marking target Ex ib IIC T4). The explosion-protection certificates are in progress and none has been issued.

In Poland. Poland asks a device to hold up in a cold, wet outdoor round, and to be honest about where it may not go. IP68 sealing and a rugged build suit weather and wash-down at refinery and fertiliser sites. IP68 is not explosion protection, and the IS-04Z1 is Group II surface equipment.

Frequently asked questions

Can we buy a rugged smartphone in Poland today?

Not today. Until the certificates are issued the IS-04Z1 stays out of classified areas, and until launch there is nothing to purchase. You can ask for the datasheet and the certification schedule, or book a safe-area evaluation unit.

What does a rugged smartphone in Poland cost?

How rugged is the IS-04Z1?

It is designed to IP68 sealing, with glove-friendly controls. Ruggedness is not explosion protection: that depends on the certificates, which are in progress.

Which sites in Poland does this cover?

The market guide for Poland covers Płock, Gdańsk, Włocławek, Puławy, among others. Site-level pages are linked below.

How do I become a SAMS distributor for Poland?

Apply through the distributor page with your company details and the territory you cover. SAMS reviews applications company by company and has not yet granted a territory for Poland.

What language should Polish sites receive documentation in?

Polish is the working language and ATEX instructions are typically expected in the language of the destination country. A Polish-language instruction sheet with certificate pack usually speeds up site acceptance. Ask the operator whether English documents are also accepted for contractors.

Does Poland follow ATEX for handheld devices in hazardous areas?

Poland is an EU member, so ATEX Directive 2014/34/EU applies to equipment and Directive 1999/92/EC to workplace zoning. Zone 1 and Zone 2 devices should carry CE and Ex markings with instructions in Polish. Operators and mines usually add their own approval rules.

Does one device cover both gas and dust on a fertiliser site?

The gas marking target is II 2 G Ex ib IIC T4, and Zone 21 dust protection belongs to the same programme, so one device is designed to cover both. Certification is in progress, so confirm the final marking and the plant's zone drawing with the operator.
