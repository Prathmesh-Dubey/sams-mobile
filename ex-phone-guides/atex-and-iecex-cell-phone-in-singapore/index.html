Field notes · Singapore

More about Singapore that matters for portable equipment

Singapore: geography and layout

Land is scarce, so Singapore stores hydrocarbons underground and builds industry on reclaimed ground: Jurong Island was formed by joining seven islands, and storage caverns free up about 150 acres of surface land.

Singapore: infrastructure

Natural gas now supplies over 90 percent of Singapore's electricity generation, up from under 20 percent in 2000, and the LNG terminal sends out about 9 million tonnes a year as its average capacity.

Singapore: rules and approvals

The Ministry of Manpower enforces explosive-atmosphere duties under the Workplace Safety and Health Act, while the Singapore Civil Defence Force regulates flammable-materials storage under the Fire Safety Act.

Singapore: logistics and access

Singapore sold 56.775 million tonnes of marine fuel in 2025, making it the largest bunkering port in the world by a wide margin and keeping fuel handling and barge work active around the clock.

The term · ATEX and IECEx

What “ATEX and IECEx” means for a smartphone

ATEX is the European legal framework for equipment used in explosive atmospheres (Directive 2014/34/EU). IECEx is the international certification scheme built on the IEC 60079 standards. Buyers outside Europe often ask for both: an IECEx certificate is the technical evidence most national regimes build on, while ATEX is the legal route into the European market.

For the IS-04Z1 both routes are in progress with TÜV in Germany and neither certificate has been issued. The marking target is II 2 G Ex ib IIC T4: equipment Group II (surface industry), Category 2 (Zone 1 gas), protection concept ib (intrinsic safety), gas group IIC and temperature class T4.

In Singapore. A Singapore HSE or procurement reviewer on Jurong Island would expect IECEx evidence, since the sheet names IECEx and IMDA registration as the route in progress. ATEX alongside it helps the multinational operators and EPCs whose global standards already reference both. No certificate has been issued for the IS-04Z1, so any review today concerns the schedule and the datasheet.

Frequently asked questions

Can we buy an ATEX and IECEx cell phone in Singapore today?

No. Nothing can be bought or taken into a classified area today: certification is in progress and launch is planned for Q4 2026. What is available now is the datasheet, the written certification schedule and evaluation units for safe-area use.

Is the IS-04Z1 ATEX and IECEx certified?

Which sites in Singapore does this cover?

The market guide for Singapore covers Jurong Island, Pulau Bukom, Seraya and Merbau, Pulau Sebarok and Pulau Busing, among others. Site-level pages are linked below.

Is intrinsically safe the same as explosion proof?

Not exactly. Explosion proof is a broad everyday term; intrinsic safety is one protection concept, which limits energy so ignition cannot occur. Flameproof (Ex d) is a different concept that contains an explosion. The IS-04Z1 is intrinsically safe by design.

Are ATEX and IECEx markings both understood in Singapore?

Singapore adopts IEC 60079 as SS EN 60079, and both IECEx and ATEX certificates are built on that technical base. Many operators accept either, but an individual refinery or yard may name one scheme in its specification, so confirm with the operator's HSE or instrumentation team.

Do phones and tablets sold in Singapore need IMDA registration?

Cellular mobile devices are generally registered with IMDA under the Simplified Equipment Registration route, which relies on a supplier's declaration of conformity. The registration is valid for five years, and the registering supplier is typically a local entity. Confirm the exact route for a specific model with IMDA or a local compliance adviser.

How does the IS-04Z1 cope with island and boat-access sites such as Bukom?

The design pairs a 4,000 mAh battery with IP68 sealing so the unit lasts through a shift where a spare may be a boat away. Charging stays in a safe area. Test battery life on a typical round and confirm access rules with the island operator.
