The term · intrinsically safe

What “intrinsically safe” means for a smartphone

Intrinsic safety limits the electrical and thermal energy in a circuit so that neither a spark nor a hot surface can ignite a gas or dust atmosphere, even when a fault occurs. The ib level, used for Zone 1, tolerates one fault. It is the usual way to make a handheld device that a worker carries continuously through a hazardous area.

The IS-04Z1 is designed on this principle for Zone 1 and Zone 21 (marking target II 2 G Ex ib IIC T4). Intrinsic safety is a design basis, not a certificate: certification is in progress and none has been issued yet.

In Panipat, Haryana. With expansion under way, more units are being built, tested and commissioned. Inspectors and commissioning teams make frequent short visits, and an intrinsically safe handheld supports logging at the asset. The IS-04Z1 is designed for such rounds, though certification is still in progress.

Frequently asked questions

Can we buy an intrinsically safe mobile phone in Panipat India today?

No. Nothing can be bought or taken into a classified area today: certification is in progress and launch is planned for Q4 2026. What is available now is the datasheet, the written certification schedule and evaluation units for safe-area use.

What does an intrinsically safe mobile phone in Panipat India cost?

There is no list price yet. SAMS gives indicative pre-launch pricing in writing, against a stated quantity and site, and does not commit to a lead time before certification completes.

What does ‘intrinsically safe’ mean for the IS-04Z1?

The circuits are designed to limit energy so a spark or hot surface cannot ignite a gas or dust atmosphere, even with one fault (level ib, Zone 1). Certification of that design is in progress.

Which areas at Panipat, Haryana would use it?

Likely candidates are refinery process units, naphtha cracker and petrochemical units, product tank farms, loading gantries. Which areas are classified, and to what zone, is set by the site operator's own area classification drawings.

Is intrinsically safe the same as explosion proof?

Not exactly. Explosion proof is a broad everyday term; intrinsic safety is one protection concept, which limits energy so ignition cannot occur. Flameproof (Ex d) is a different concept that contains an explosion. The IS-04Z1 is intrinsically safe by design.

What hazardous areas are at Panipat?

The refinery has process units, hydrogen and cracker areas, tank farms and loading facilities, and the naphtha cracker complex adds ethylene and propylene handling. The operator defines classified areas, so request the drawings before visiting.

Is Panipat part of a larger petrochemical complex?

Yes, the refinery has paraxylene and PTA units and a naphtha cracker complex alongside it. The units may have different operators and rules, so ask each site.

How does Panipat's winter fog and cold affect handhelds?

Cold can reduce battery capacity and fog adds moisture, so sealing and warm storage between uses help. An IP68 housing handles moisture ingress. Ask the operator about its rules for portable equipment outdoors in winter and where devices may be kept warm.
