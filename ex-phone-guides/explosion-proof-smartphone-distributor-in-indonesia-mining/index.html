The term · explosion proof

What “explosion proof” means for a smartphone

“Explosion proof” is the phrase most buyers use for equipment that may be taken into a classified hazardous area. Strictly, it covers several protection concepts: a flameproof enclosure (Ex d) contains an internal explosion, while intrinsic safety (Ex i) limits electrical energy so that an ignition cannot start. Phones are normally intrinsically safe, because a flameproof handset would be too heavy to carry all shift.

The IS-04Z1 uses intrinsic safety, with a marking target of Ex ib IIC T4, and is designed for Zone 1 and Zone 21. It is pre-launch, certification is in progress and no certificate has been issued.

In Indonesian mining regions. Mining blends hazard types. Coal preparation plants have combustible dust, fuel stores have flammable vapour, and underground work at Grasberg has its own rules. Explosion-proof is a loose term across these. Intrinsic safety suits a handheld carried between areas, while dust zones need a dust-suitable marking.

Frequently asked questions

Can we buy an explosion proof smartphone in Indonesia Mining today?

No. Nothing can be bought or taken into a classified area today: certification is in progress and launch is planned for Q4 2026. What is available now is the datasheet, the written certification schedule and evaluation units for safe-area use.

What does an explosion proof smartphone in Indonesia Mining cost?

No price is published. While certification is in progress there is no list price and no committed lead time, and a supplier quoting a firm figure for an uncertified Ex handset is promising something it cannot stand behind. Indicative pre-launch pricing is issued in writing against a stated quantity and site.

Is the IS-04Z1 explosion proof or intrinsically safe?

It is intrinsically safe (marking target Ex ib IIC T4), which is the usual way to make a phone for hazardous areas. It is not a flameproof (Ex d) design, and certification is still in progress.

Which areas at Indonesian mining regions would use it?

Likely candidates are coal handling and preparation plants (dust, zone 21), fuel and lubricant storage, underground mine areas, smelter and process plant areas. Which areas are classified, and to what zone, is set by the site operator's own area classification drawings.

How do I become a SAMS distributor for Indonesian mining regions?

Apply through the distributor page with your company details and the territory you cover. SAMS reviews applications company by company and has not yet granted a territory for Indonesian mining regions.

Who regulates mining safety and equipment in Indonesia?

Mining activity falls under the Energy and Mineral Resources Ministry, with the Directorate General of Mineral and Coal setting policy under the mining law. Equipment and radio requirements may involve other agencies, so confirm approvals with the operator and your adviser.

How remote are mining sites in Indonesia for supporting equipment?

Many sites are in Kalimantan, Sulawesi or Papua and need sea or air freight plus long road legs. Typically contractors stock spare batteries and chargers on site, and allow longer lead times for repairs and import clearance.

Does the IS-04Z1 target marking cover coal dust areas?

The target marking, II 2 G Ex ib IIC T4, is for gas atmospheres. Coal preparation plants are dust areas, which need a dust marking and separate assessment. Do not assume coverage. Confirm requirements for each area with the mine operator's safety department.
