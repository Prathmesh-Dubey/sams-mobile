The term · hazardous area

What “hazardous area” means for a smartphone

A hazardous area is a place where flammable gas, vapour or combustible dust may be present. Standards divide these into zones: Zones 0, 1 and 2 for gas and vapour, and Zones 20, 21 and 22 for dust. Zone 1 and Zone 21 are the tier where an explosive atmosphere is likely to occur in normal operation, which is where the IS-04Z1 is designed to work. Anything carried in must be marked for the zone, gas group and temperature class of the area.

No certificate has been issued for the IS-04Z1 yet, so it must not be taken into a classified area until the certificate covering it exists and the site operator has accepted it.

In Baddi, Himachal Pradesh. The hazardous areas at Baddi are mainly rooms inside plants: solvent storage and dispensing, granulation and coating, reactor areas and warehouse solvent stores. Each plant's operator classifies its own rooms, not SAMS and not a device supplier. A buyer should obtain the classification record for the room before deciding where any handheld may go.

Which rules apply to storing solvents in Indian plants?

India's Petroleum and Explosives Safety Organisation administers rules covering petroleum and flammable materials storage. Plants usually work with licensed storage and their own safety teams, so ask the operator which licences and equipment standards they require.
