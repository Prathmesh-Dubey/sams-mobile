The term · explosion proof

What “explosion proof” means for a smartphone

“Explosion proof” is the phrase most buyers use for equipment that may be taken into a classified hazardous area. Strictly, it covers several protection concepts: a flameproof enclosure (Ex d) contains an internal explosion, while intrinsic safety (Ex i) limits electrical energy so that an ignition cannot start. Phones are normally intrinsically safe, because a flameproof handset would be too heavy to carry all shift.

The IS-04Z1 uses intrinsic safety, with a marking target of Ex ib IIC T4, and is designed for Zone 1 and Zone 21. It is pre-launch, certification is in progress and no certificate has been issued.

In Riyadh. Riyadh's factories produce machinery, chemicals, construction materials and food, so hazard profiles differ by plant. Explosion-proof may mean flameproof Ex d for a chemical plant's gas area, while dust areas need other concepts. The IS-04Z1 is designed for intrinsic safety in gas atmospheres.

Questions · Riyadh

Frequently asked questions

Can we buy an explosion proof mobile phone in Riyadh today?

No. Nothing can be bought or taken into a classified area today: certification is in progress and launch is planned for Q4 2026. What is available now is the datasheet, the written certification schedule and evaluation units for safe-area use.

What does an explosion proof mobile phone in Riyadh cost?

No price is published. While certification is in progress there is no list price and no committed lead time, and a supplier quoting a firm figure for an uncertified Ex handset is promising something it cannot stand behind. Indicative pre-launch pricing is issued in writing against a stated quantity and site.

Is the IS-04Z1 explosion proof or intrinsically safe?

It is intrinsically safe (marking target Ex ib IIC T4), which is the usual way to make a phone for hazardous areas. It is not a flameproof (Ex d) design, and certification is still in progress.

Which areas at Riyadh would use it?

Likely candidates are refinery process units, chemical and manufacturing plants, industrial plant dust areas (zone 21). Which areas are classified, and to what zone, is set by the site operator's own area classification drawings.

Is intrinsically safe the same as explosion proof?

Not exactly. Explosion proof is a broad everyday term; intrinsic safety is one protection concept, which limits energy so ignition cannot occur. Flameproof (Ex d) is a different concept that contains an explosion. The IS-04Z1 is intrinsically safe by design.

Does the Riyadh climate stress phones at low temperature?

Rarely, but winters are cooler than many expect, with a January average low around 7 C. The bigger issue is summer heat. Check the datasheet operating range and avoid leaving devices in vehicles; the plant operator may set its own limits.

Does dust in Riyadh matter for phone selection?

Yes. Dust storms can cut visibility to under 10 m, so a sealed device with a stated ingress protection rating and good port covers is sensible. Dust ingress and combustible dust in a plant are different issues; follow the operator's dust-zone classification for hazardous areas.

Are Riyadh buyers mostly purchasing for Riyadh sites?

Not necessarily. Many buyers and contractors are based in Riyadh even when the classified areas are elsewhere, because most hydrocarbon sites are in the Eastern Province. Ask where the device will be used, and check the classification for that site, not for the city of purchase.
