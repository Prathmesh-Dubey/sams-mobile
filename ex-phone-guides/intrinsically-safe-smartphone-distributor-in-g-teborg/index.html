The term · intrinsically safe

What “intrinsically safe” means for a smartphone

Intrinsic safety limits the electrical and thermal energy in a circuit so that neither a spark nor a hot surface can ignite a gas or dust atmosphere, even when a fault occurs. The ib level, used for Zone 1, tolerates one fault. It is the usual way to make a handheld device that a worker carries continuously through a hazardous area.

The IS-04Z1 is designed on this principle for Zone 1 and Zone 21 (marking target II 2 G Ex ib IIC T4). Intrinsic safety is a design basis, not a certificate: certification is in progress and none has been issued yet.

In Gothenburg (Göteborg). Rounds across pumping stations, valve areas and tanker berths involve many short stops in Zone 1 areas. A handheld intrinsically safe device would let an operator record readings and photograph equipment at each stop, without carrying a separate terminal. The IS-04Z1 is designed for that style of short, frequent data capture.

No. Nothing can be bought or taken into a classified area today: certification is in progress and launch is planned for Q4 2026. What is available now is the datasheet, the written certification schedule and evaluation units for safe-area use.

What does an intrinsically safe smartphone in Göteborg cost?

What does ‘intrinsically safe’ mean for the IS-04Z1?

The circuits are designed to limit energy so a spark or hot surface cannot ignite a gas or dust atmosphere, even with one fault (level ib, Zone 1). Certification of that design is in progress.

Which areas at Gothenburg (Göteborg) would use it?

Likely candidates are refinery process units, tanker berths and loading arms, pumping stations and valve areas, bulk fuel tank storage. Which areas are classified, and to what zone, is set by the site operator's own area classification drawings.

Why is Gothenburg useful for shipping equipment to Swedish sites?

The Port of Gothenburg is Scandinavia's largest harbour, with frequent container and ro-ro services, so imports to Sweden and the region often arrive here. Allow time for customs and conformity checks on electrical and radio equipment, and confirm requirements with your forwarder.

Which industries around Gothenburg involve hazardous areas?

Marine and port activities, fuel and chemical storage on the waterfront, and manufacturing plants for vehicles and components can all include flammable gas, vapour or dust areas. Each operator sets its own zones, so request classification drawings before using handheld devices.

Does rain and cool weather matter for a handheld used at the tanker berths?

The west coast has a maritime climate with cool, damp winters, so outdoor equipment should tolerate rain and low temperatures. The IS-04Z1 is designed to IP68 for water and dust, and has a 4,000 mAh battery. Cold-weather runtime has not been published, so ask for the datasheet.
