The term · intrinsically safe

What “intrinsically safe” means for a smartphone

Intrinsic safety limits the electrical and thermal energy in a circuit so that neither a spark nor a hot surface can ignite a gas or dust atmosphere, even when a fault occurs. The ib level, used for Zone 1, tolerates one fault. It is the usual way to make a handheld device that a worker carries continuously through a hazardous area.

The IS-04Z1 is designed on this principle for Zone 1 and Zone 21 (marking target II 2 G Ex ib IIC T4). Intrinsic safety is a design basis, not a certificate: certification is in progress and none has been issued yet.

In Nigeria. Flow stations in the Niger Delta, FPSOs on Bonga, Egina, Akpo and Agbami, and export terminals at Escravos and Qua Iboe involve rounds on foot, by boat and by helicopter. An intrinsically safe handheld lets an operator photograph and log findings without gathering permits at each gate. It is a simple tool for a hard footprint.

Questions · Nigeria

Frequently asked questions

Can we buy an intrinsically safe cell phone in Nigeria today?

Not today. Until the certificates are issued the IS-04Z1 stays out of classified areas, and until launch there is nothing to purchase. You can ask for the datasheet and the certification schedule, or book a safe-area evaluation unit.

What does an intrinsically safe cell phone in Nigeria cost?

No price is published. While certification is in progress there is no list price and no committed lead time, and a supplier quoting a firm figure for an uncertified Ex handset is promising something it cannot stand behind. Indicative pre-launch pricing is issued in writing against a stated quantity and site.

What does ‘intrinsically safe’ mean for the IS-04Z1?

The circuits are designed to limit energy so a spark or hot surface cannot ignite a gas or dust atmosphere, even with one fault (level ib, Zone 1). Certification of that design is in progress.

Which sites in Nigeria does this cover?

The market guide for Nigeria covers Port Harcourt, Rivers State, Warri and Escravos, Bonny Island, Lekki Free Zone, Lagos, among others. Site-level pages are linked below.

How do I become a SAMS distributor for Nigeria?

Apply through the distributor page with your company details and the territory you cover. SAMS reviews applications company by company and has not yet granted a territory for Nigeria.

What approval does a mobile phone need in Nigeria?

The Nigerian Communications Commission requires type approval for telecom equipment, including customer devices such as mobile phones, and publishes approved handset and equipment lists. Approval is based on international standards. Confirm the current application route and fees with the NCC or an authorised agent before importing.

Where does hazardous-area work concentrate in Nigeria?

Typically in the Niger Delta's onshore and shallow-water oil and gas facilities, flow stations and terminals, and in refineries and LNG and fertiliser plants in the south, including the Lekki refinery near Lagos. Each operator issues its own area classification, so request it before choosing equipment.

Does the device work where coverage varies by kilometre?

Coverage depends on carrier and site, not on the handset. The IS-04Z1 is designed to connect over cellular and plant Wi-Fi, but remote Delta locations and offshore units may have weak signal. Confirm with the operator what network will be available at each site before relying on it.
