The term · explosion proof

What “explosion proof” means for a smartphone

“Explosion proof” is the phrase most buyers use for equipment that may be taken into a classified hazardous area. Strictly, it covers several protection concepts: a flameproof enclosure (Ex d) contains an internal explosion, while intrinsic safety (Ex i) limits electrical energy so that an ignition cannot start. Phones are normally intrinsically safe, because a flameproof handset would be too heavy to carry all shift.

The IS-04Z1 uses intrinsic safety, with a marking target of Ex ib IIC T4, and is designed for Zone 1 and Zone 21. It is pre-launch, certification is in progress and no certificate has been issued.

In Nigeria. Delta crews say explosion proof for anything used near hydrocarbons, but the concepts differ. Flameproof Ex d encloses an explosion and suits fixed pumps and motors. The IS-04Z1 uses intrinsic safety, Ex ib, which suits a handset carried on a flow station walkround or a jetty inspection at Bonny Island.

Questions · Nigeria

Frequently asked questions

Can we buy an explosion proof cell phone in Nigeria today?

Not yet. The IS-04Z1 is pre-launch and its certification is still in progress ahead of a Q4 2026 launch. No unit goes into a classified area before the certificate covering it exists. The datasheet, the certification schedule and safe-area evaluation units are available now.

What does an explosion proof cell phone in Nigeria cost?

SAMS has not published a price. Any figure you are offered for an uncertified Ex phone should be treated with caution; written indicative pricing is available for a defined quantity and site.

Is the IS-04Z1 explosion proof or intrinsically safe?

It is intrinsically safe (marking target Ex ib IIC T4), which is the usual way to make a phone for hazardous areas. It is not a flameproof (Ex d) design, and certification is still in progress.

Which sites in Nigeria does this cover?

The market guide for Nigeria covers Port Harcourt, Rivers State, Warri and Escravos, Bonny Island, Lekki Free Zone, Lagos, among others. Site-level pages are linked below.

How will SAMS supply Nigeria?

Through authorised distributors once certificates exist. Until then SAMS can send the datasheet and certification schedule and arrange safe-area evaluation units.

What approval does a mobile phone need in Nigeria?

The Nigerian Communications Commission requires type approval for telecom equipment, including customer devices such as mobile phones, and publishes approved handset and equipment lists. Approval is based on international standards. Confirm the current application route and fees with the NCC or an authorised agent before importing.

Where does hazardous-area work concentrate in Nigeria?

Typically in the Niger Delta's onshore and shallow-water oil and gas facilities, flow stations and terminals, and in refineries and LNG and fertiliser plants in the south, including the Lekki refinery near Lagos. Each operator issues its own area classification, so request it before choosing equipment.

Does the device work where coverage varies by kilometre?

Coverage depends on carrier and site, not on the handset. The IS-04Z1 is designed to connect over cellular and plant Wi-Fi, but remote Delta locations and offshore units may have weak signal. Confirm with the operator what network will be available at each site before relying on it.
