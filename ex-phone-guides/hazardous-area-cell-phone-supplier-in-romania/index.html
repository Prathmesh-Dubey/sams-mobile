Field notes · Romania

More about Romania that matters for portable equipment

Romania: industry on the ground

Romania had five refineries left by 2018 with about 13.7 million tonnes a year of capacity, far above domestic demand, and exports lubricants, bitumen, fertilisers and petrochemicals across the region.

Romania: projects and investment

Neptun Deep, about 160 km off the Romanian coast in 100 to 1,000 m of water, is owned 50/50 by OMV Petrom and Romgaz, is budgeted at about 4 billion euros and targets 8 billion cubic metres of gas a year.

Romania: infrastructure

The national gas transmission system is owned by the state company Transgaz and interconnector pipelines link Romania with Hungary, Bulgaria and Ukraine, so border compressor and metering stations are part of the network.

Romania: industry on the ground

Petrobrazi near Ploiești, founded in 1934, runs one module with a capacity of 4.5 million tonnes a year and has a pipeline link to the Port of Constanța rated at 10,000 tonnes a day.

The term · hazardous area

What “hazardous area” means for a smartphone

A hazardous area is a place where flammable gas, vapour or combustible dust may be present. Standards divide these into zones: Zones 0, 1 and 2 for gas and vapour, and Zones 20, 21 and 22 for dust. Zone 1 and Zone 21 are the tier where an explosive atmosphere is likely to occur in normal operation, which is where the IS-04Z1 is designed to work. Anything carried in must be marked for the zone, gas group and temperature class of the area.

No certificate has been issued for the IS-04Z1 yet, so it must not be taken into a classified area until the certificate covering it exists and the site operator has accepted it.

In Romania. Hazardous-area classification in Romania runs from the Prahova valley refineries to the Black Sea terminals at Constanța and the inland chemical plants at Borzești and Râmnicu Vâlcea. The operator or site owner defines the zones and gas groups on its drawings. SAMS does not classify any area; it designs a device for a stated classification.

Questions · Romania

Frequently asked questions

Can we buy a hazardous area cell phone in Romania today?

No. Nothing can be bought or taken into a classified area today: certification is in progress and launch is planned for Q4 2026. What is available now is the datasheet, the written certification schedule and evaluation units for safe-area use.

What does a hazardous area cell phone in Romania cost?

No price is published. While certification is in progress there is no list price and no committed lead time, and a supplier quoting a firm figure for an uncertified Ex handset is promising something it cannot stand behind. Indicative pre-launch pricing is issued in writing against a stated quantity and site.

Which hazardous areas is the IS-04Z1 designed for?

Zone 1 and Zone 21 (gas and dust) with gas group IIC and temperature class T4, subject to certification. It must not be used in a classified area before its certificate is issued and the site has accepted it.

Which sites in Romania does this cover?

The market guide for Romania covers Ploiești and Brazi, Năvodari, Constanța, Vadu and Corbu, among others. Site-level pages are linked below.

How will SAMS supply Romania?

Through authorised distributors once certificates exist. Until then SAMS can send the datasheet and certification schedule and arrange safe-area evaluation units.

Does Romania require ATEX certified phones for hazardous areas?

Romania is an EU member, so ATEX Directive 2014/34/EU applies to equipment and Directive 1999/92/EC to workplace zoning. Zone 1 and Zone 2 devices should carry CE and Ex markings with instructions in Romanian where required. Operators usually add their own approval and permit procedures.

Who runs the main oil and gas sites in Romania?

OMV Petrom, owned by OMV since 2004, is the best-known upstream and downstream operator, and Transgaz owns the gas transmission system. Each company runs its own supplier registration and contractor induction, so contact their procurement and HSE teams for current requirements.

Does one handset cover both gas and dust at a Romanian fertiliser or chemical plant?

The target is II 2 G Ex ib IIC T4 for gas, with Zone 21 dust protection in the same programme. Certification is still in progress, so no certificate exists. Check the final certificate and schedule of conditions against the operator's zone drawings before any use.
