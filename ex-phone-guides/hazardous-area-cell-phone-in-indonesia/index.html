Field notes · Indonesia

More about Indonesia that matters for portable equipment

Indonesia: background

Pertamina was created in August 1968 by merging Pertamin, founded in 1961, and Permina, founded in 1957 from nationalised Shell assets, and it is headquartered in Jakarta.

Indonesia: infrastructure

Pertamina operates six oil refineries with a combined capacity of around 1 million barrels a day, as of 2013, and has not built a new refinery since Balongan opened in West Java in the mid-1990s.

Indonesia: rules and approvals

Radio and telecom equipment certification by SDPPI is compulsory in Indonesia, with a three-year validity, local testing and a local representative required, and a marking requirement.

Indonesia: rules and approvals

Technical requirements for 5G devices follow Perdirgen No. 5/2021, so 5G-capable phones are tested against that rule as part of SDPPI certification.

The term · hazardous area

What “hazardous area” means for a smartphone

A hazardous area is a place where flammable gas, vapour or combustible dust may be present. Standards divide these into zones: Zones 0, 1 and 2 for gas and vapour, and Zones 20, 21 and 22 for dust. Zone 1 and Zone 21 are the tier where an explosive atmosphere is likely to occur in normal operation, which is where the IS-04Z1 is designed to work. Anything carried in must be marked for the zone, gas group and temperature class of the area.

No certificate has been issued for the IS-04Z1 yet, so it must not be taken into a classified area until the certificate covering it exists and the site operator has accepted it.

In Indonesia. Indonesian classified areas include Pertamina refineries, the Rokan block gathering stations, LNG trains at Bontang and Tangguh, the Cepu field, crackers at Cilegon and fertiliser plants. The operator classifies each area, not SAMS. The IS-04Z1 is designed for Zone 1 and Zone 21.

Questions · Indonesia

Frequently asked questions

Can we buy a hazardous area cell phone in Indonesia today?

No. Nothing can be bought or taken into a classified area today: certification is in progress and launch is planned for Q4 2026. What is available now is the datasheet, the written certification schedule and evaluation units for safe-area use.

What does a hazardous area cell phone in Indonesia cost?

No price is published. While certification is in progress there is no list price and no committed lead time, and a supplier quoting a firm figure for an uncertified Ex handset is promising something it cannot stand behind. Indicative pre-launch pricing is issued in writing against a stated quantity and site.

Which hazardous areas is the IS-04Z1 designed for?

Zone 1 and Zone 21 (gas and dust) with gas group IIC and temperature class T4, subject to certification. It must not be used in a classified area before its certificate is issued and the site has accepted it.

Which sites in Indonesia does this cover?

The market guide for Indonesia covers Cilacap, Central Java, Balikpapan, East Kalimantan, Dumai, Riau, Balongan, West Java, among others. Site-level pages are linked below.

Is intrinsically safe the same as explosion proof?

Not exactly. Explosion proof is a broad everyday term; intrinsic safety is one protection concept, which limits energy so ignition cannot occur. Flameproof (Ex d) is a different concept that contains an explosion. The IS-04Z1 is intrinsically safe by design.

What conditions should devices handle at Indonesian industrial sites?

Tropical sites typically combine heat, high humidity, heavy rain and, near the coast, salt air, so check ingress protection, corrosion resistance and operating temperature range. Confirm site-specific requirements with the operator, including any area classification and approved-device lists.

How does Indonesia's island geography affect equipment logistics?

With thousands of islands, many sites are reached by sea or air, and delays are common. Plan extra lead time and spares for devices, chargers and batteries, and ask the operator or contractor about shipping, customs and site approval rules for each island location.

How would a remote island site get support?

Supply after certification would be through authorised distributors, and none has been appointed yet. For remote sites, ask about spares, replacement time and training before purchase, and agree the logistics route with the operator. Trial units can be tested to check connectivity and battery use first.
