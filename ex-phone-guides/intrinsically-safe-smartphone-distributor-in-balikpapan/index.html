The term · intrinsically safe

What “intrinsically safe” means for a smartphone

Intrinsic safety limits the electrical and thermal energy in a circuit so that neither a spark nor a hot surface can ignite a gas or dust atmosphere, even when a fault occurs. The ib level, used for Zone 1, tolerates one fault. It is the usual way to make a handheld device that a worker carries continuously through a hazardous area.

The IS-04Z1 is designed on this principle for Zone 1 and Zone 21 (marking target II 2 G Ex ib IIC T4). Intrinsic safety is a design basis, not a certificate: certification is in progress and none has been issued yet.

In Balikpapan, East Kalimantan. With a new residual cracking unit and a naphtha unit being added, there will be commissioning walk-downs and startups across the refinery. A handheld intended for intrinsic safety could carry checklists and photos through those rounds. That use depends on certification and on Pertamina's acceptance.

What does ‘intrinsically safe’ mean for the IS-04Z1?

The circuits are designed to limit energy so a spark or hot surface cannot ignite a gas or dust atmosphere, even with one fault (level ib, Zone 1). Certification of that design is in progress.

Which areas at Balikpapan, East Kalimantan would use it?

Likely candidates are refinery process units, residual fluid catalytic cracking unit, crude and product storage, marine crude mooring and tanks. Which areas are classified, and to what zone, is set by the site operator's own area classification drawings.

Which operator should I contact for equipment approval at the Balikpapan refinery?

Refinery Unit V is run by PT Kilang Pertamina Balikpapan, part of the Pertamina group. Contractors and suppliers normally register through Pertamina's vendor processes and follow the site's own permit and induction rules, so confirm the current route with the plant.

Are there new and old process areas at Balikpapan to consider for classification?

Yes. The upgrade added a residue catalytic cracking unit and raised capacity by 100 thousand barrels a day beside the existing units, so area classification drawings can differ between revamped and legacy sections. Ask for the current hazardous-area drawings for the unit you will work in.

Will the RDMP upgrade change what handsets are accepted at Balikpapan?

Possibly, because new units bring new classified areas and contractor equipment rules. The operator sets the requirements for each phase. Ask Pertamina or its contractors for current device approval procedures and do not assume earlier approvals cover new areas. Confirm with the site operator.
