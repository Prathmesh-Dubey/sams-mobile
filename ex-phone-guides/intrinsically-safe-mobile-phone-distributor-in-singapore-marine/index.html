The term · intrinsically safe

What “intrinsically safe” means for a smartphone

Intrinsic safety limits the electrical and thermal energy in a circuit so that neither a spark nor a hot surface can ignite a gas or dust atmosphere, even when a fault occurs. The ib level, used for Zone 1, tolerates one fault. It is the usual way to make a handheld device that a worker carries continuously through a hazardous area.

The IS-04Z1 is designed on this principle for Zone 1 and Zone 21 (marking target II 2 G Ex ib IIC T4). Intrinsic safety is a design basis, not a certificate: certification is in progress and none has been issued yet.

In Singapore marine and shipyard sector. Tank entry, repair and conversion work means climbing through confined spaces with little room for a second device. A handheld intrinsically safe phone is intended to let a surveyor or supervisor record photographs and readings in those spaces. This depends on certification being completed and on the yard's permit rules.

Frequently asked questions

Can we buy an intrinsically safe mobile phone in Singapore Marine today?

Not yet. The IS-04Z1 is pre-launch and its certification is still in progress ahead of a Q4 2026 launch. No unit goes into a classified area before the certificate covering it exists. The datasheet, the certification schedule and safe-area evaluation units are available now.

What does an intrinsically safe mobile phone in Singapore Marine cost?

SAMS has not published a price. Any figure you are offered for an uncertified Ex phone should be treated with caution; written indicative pricing is available for a defined quantity and site.

What does ‘intrinsically safe’ mean for the IS-04Z1?

The circuits are designed to limit energy so a spark or hot surface cannot ignite a gas or dust atmosphere, even with one fault (level ib, Zone 1). Certification of that design is in progress.

Which areas at Singapore marine and shipyard sector would use it?

Likely candidates are vessel tank and cargo spaces, repair and conversion work, paint and coating areas, offshore unit conversion. Which areas are classified, and to what zone, is set by the site operator's own area classification drawings.

How do I become a SAMS distributor for Singapore marine and shipyard sector?

Apply through the distributor page with your company details and the territory you cover. SAMS reviews applications company by company and has not yet granted a territory for Singapore marine and shipyard sector.

Does bunkering create hazardous areas around the port?

Fuel transfer between barges and ships can create temporary vapour zones around connections and vents, depending on the fuel. Port and operator procedures control ignition sources during transfer. Ask the barge operator or ship's officer what equipment may be used on deck.

Which Singapore yards handle gas carriers and floating production units?

Seatrium lists LNG and LPG carriers and floating production vessels among the vessels it repairs and converts at its Singapore yards. These ship types generally bring the strictest hazardous-area controls, so confirm yard and vessel rules with the project team.

Does one approval cover every shipyard in Singapore?

No. Yards and vessel owners set their own device rules, and the yard list here is not exhaustive. A device accepted at one yard may still need separate agreement at another. Confirm requirements with the yard safety team where the work will happen.
