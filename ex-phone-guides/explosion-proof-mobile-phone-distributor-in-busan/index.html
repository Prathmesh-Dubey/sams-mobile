The term · explosion proof

What “explosion proof” means for a smartphone

“Explosion proof” is the phrase most buyers use for equipment that may be taken into a classified hazardous area. Strictly, it covers several protection concepts: a flameproof enclosure (Ex d) contains an internal explosion, while intrinsic safety (Ex i) limits electrical energy so that an ignition cannot start. Phones are normally intrinsically safe, because a flameproof handset would be too heavy to carry all shift.

The IS-04Z1 uses intrinsic safety, with a marking target of Ex ib IIC T4, and is designed for Zone 1 and Zone 21. It is pre-launch, certification is in progress and no certificate has been issued.

In Busan. In a repair yard or fuel store, explosion-proof is a loose phrase. Flameproof Ex d housings protect fixed equipment, whereas a handheld phone taken into a vessel tank space relies on intrinsic safety. The IS-04Z1 is designed on that principle, with certification in progress and each yard's permit rules applying.

Questions · Busan

Frequently asked questions

Can we buy an explosion proof mobile phone in Busan today?

Not yet. The IS-04Z1 is pre-launch and its certification is still in progress ahead of a Q4 2026 launch. No unit goes into a classified area before the certificate covering it exists. The datasheet, the certification schedule and safe-area evaluation units are available now.

What does an explosion proof mobile phone in Busan cost?

No price is published. While certification is in progress there is no list price and no committed lead time, and a supplier quoting a firm figure for an uncertified Ex handset is promising something it cannot stand behind. Indicative pre-launch pricing is issued in writing against a stated quantity and site.

Is the IS-04Z1 explosion proof or intrinsically safe?

It is intrinsically safe (marking target Ex ib IIC T4), which is the usual way to make a phone for hazardous areas. It is not a flameproof (Ex d) design, and certification is still in progress.

Which areas at Busan would use it?

Likely candidates are vessel tank spaces, ship repair yards, fuel and chemical storage, port cargo handling. Which areas are classified, and to what zone, is set by the site operator's own area classification drawings.

How do I become a SAMS distributor for Busan?

Apply through the distributor page with your company details and the territory you cover. SAMS reviews applications company by company and has not yet granted a territory for Busan.

Are there language or workforce factors for foreign contractors in Busan?

Korean is the working language at most sites, though the port area is used to foreign crews and visitors. Plan interpreters or Korean-language safety materials for local staff, and check that device interfaces and instructions can be provided in Korean.

Which types of site around Busan involve classified areas?

Port tank terminals, ship repair and shipbuilding yards, and industrial plants in the wider south-east region can all have gas or vapour areas. Each operator sets its own zones and permits, so ask for classification drawings and portable-device policy.

Which regulators are relevant to hazardous-area work in Busan?

Busan Port Authority manages the port and the Korea Occupational Safety and Health Agency (KOSHA) covers workplace safety. SAMS does not hold any approval from either. Ask the yard or terminal which rules it applies, and confirm with the site operator.
