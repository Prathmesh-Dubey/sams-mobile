The term · ATEX and IECEx

What “ATEX and IECEx” means for a smartphone

ATEX is the European legal framework for equipment used in explosive atmospheres (Directive 2014/34/EU). IECEx is the international certification scheme built on the IEC 60079 standards. Buyers outside Europe often ask for both: an IECEx certificate is the technical evidence most national regimes build on, while ATEX is the legal route into the European market.

For the IS-04Z1 both routes are in progress with TÜV in Germany and neither certificate has been issued. The marking target is II 2 G Ex ib IIC T4: equipment Group II (surface industry), Category 2 (Zone 1 gas), protection concept ib (intrinsic safety), gas group IIC and temperature class T4.

In Grande Prairie. A reviewer in Grande Prairie would expect certificate evidence suited to gas plants, compressor stations and wellheads, where condensate and shale gas are handled. A dual ATEX and IECEx route could help service firms who work for several operators across the region. The IS-04Z1 is in certification and holds nothing at present.

Frequently asked questions

Can we buy an ATEX and IECEx mobile phone in Grande Prairie today?

No. Nothing can be bought or taken into a classified area today: certification is in progress and launch is planned for Q4 2026. What is available now is the datasheet, the written certification schedule and evaluation units for safe-area use.

Is the IS-04Z1 ATEX and IECEx certified?

Not yet. Both routes are in progress with TÜV in Germany and no certificate has been issued. Any listing that says otherwise is wrong.

Which areas at Grande Prairie would use it?

Likely candidates are gas processing plants, well sites and wellheads, compressor stations, condensate handling. Which areas are classified, and to what zone, is set by the site operator's own area classification drawings.

Is intrinsically safe the same as explosion proof?

Not exactly. Explosion proof is a broad everyday term; intrinsic safety is one protection concept, which limits energy so ignition cannot occur. Flameproof (Ex d) is a different concept that contains an explosion. The IS-04Z1 is intrinsically safe by design.

How does winter affect field devices near Grande Prairie?

Northern Alberta has cold winters, so batteries, screens and cables need low-temperature ratings, and warm storage between jobs helps. Confirm the operator's winter equipment guidance and temperature class for classified areas.

Do liquids-rich gas plays change zone classification?

They can, because condensate and NGL streams add heavier flammable vapours alongside methane, which may affect gas group and temperature class choices. Ask the operator for the classification drawing and choose devices to match.

Can a phone cope with the cold in Grande Prairie?

Cold is a major constraint, since winters are very cold and a record low of about -52 degrees C has been reported. Battery performance and glove use are practical concerns for outdoor work. Check the datasheet's operating temperature range and plan insulated storage. The site operator can advise on winter working practice.
