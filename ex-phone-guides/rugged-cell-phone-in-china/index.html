Field notes · China

More about China that matters for portable equipment

China: rules and approvals

Explosion-protected electrical products in China need CCC certification, mandatory since 1 October 2020, to the GB/T 3836 series, which is China's adoption of IEC 60079.

China: rules and approvals

ATEX certificates and Eurasian certificates are not accepted in China, and the applicant for CCC may be a foreign company.

China: rules and approvals

CNCA designated six certification bodies and 13 testing laboratories for Ex products, and an IECEx test report may serve as test evidence but does not replace the CCC certificate.

China: background

The Daqing field in Heilongjiang, discovered in 1959 in the Songhua Jiang-Liao basin, has been the backbone of Chinese oil production for decades, and Shengli began producing in 1968.

The term · rugged

What “rugged” means for a smartphone

A rugged phone is built to survive water, dust, drops and rough handling, usually rated by an IP code and a drop specification. Ruggedness is an environmental property: it says nothing about whether a phone may be used where flammable gas or dust is present. That is decided by explosion-protection certification and marking.

The IS-04Z1 combines both: an IP68 sealed rugged build and a design for Zone 1 and Zone 21 (marking target Ex ib IIC T4). The explosion-protection certificates are in progress and none has been issued.

In China. China's industrial sites cover almost the full climate range, from the cold, dry north to the humid, subtropical south. IP68 sealing and a rugged build are specified for that spread, not one regional extreme. IP68 is a dust and water rating, and it does not provide explosion protection, which comes from NEPSI and IECEx certification.

Questions · China

Frequently asked questions

Can we buy a rugged cell phone in China today?

Not yet. The IS-04Z1 is pre-launch and its certification is still in progress ahead of a Q4 2026 launch. No unit goes into a classified area before the certificate covering it exists. The datasheet, the certification schedule and safe-area evaluation units are available now.

What does a rugged cell phone in China cost?

SAMS has not published a price. Any figure you are offered for an uncertified Ex phone should be treated with caution; written indicative pricing is available for a defined quantity and site.

How rugged is the IS-04Z1?

It is designed to IP68 sealing, with glove-friendly controls. Ruggedness is not explosion protection: that depends on the certificates, which are in progress.

Which sites in China does this cover?

The market guide for China covers Shanxi, Shaanxi and Inner Mongolia, Daqing, Shengli oilfield, among others. Site-level pages are linked below.

Is intrinsically safe the same as explosion proof?

Not exactly. Explosion proof is a broad everyday term; intrinsic safety is one protection concept, which limits energy so ignition cannot occur. Flameproof (Ex d) is a different concept that contains an explosion. The IS-04Z1 is intrinsically safe by design.

What radio and network approvals does a smartphone need in China?

In addition to CCC where applicable, radio devices need SRRC approval and phones connecting to public networks need the MIIT network access licence. The NAL is described as compulsory, valid for three years, with local testing and a local representative. Confirm the current requirements with MIIT or an accredited body.

Do Ex phones need a CCC mark in China?

Products that fall within the explosion-protected electrical category of the CCC catalogue may not be sold, imported or used in business without a CCC certificate and mark, to the GB/T 3836 series. Check with a CNCA-designated body whether a given product falls within that catalogue scope.

Does NEPSI certification mean a Chinese operator will accept the phone?

No: Sinopec, PetroChina and the major coal producers each run their own equipment-approval and procurement process, so a device can be NEPSI-certified and still unlisted. NEPSI and SRRC are being pursued and are not finished. Confirm the listing route with the operator.
