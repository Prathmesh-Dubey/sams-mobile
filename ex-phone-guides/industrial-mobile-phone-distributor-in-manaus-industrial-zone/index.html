Manaus Free Trade Zone: who works there

The Manaus refinery, long known as Reman and the Isaac Sabba refinery on the Rio Negro, was one of two Petrobras refineries sold (it is now Ream), so the site operator is no longer Petrobras.

Manaus Free Trade Zone: climate and equipment wear

Manaus has a tropical monsoon climate with an average temperature of 27.4 degrees Celsius, high air humidity and about 2,300 mm of rain a year, so enclosures and seals face constant moisture.

Manaus Free Trade Zone: logistics and access

Aerial photography of the Isaac Sabba refinery shows it on the Negro River in the Amazon forest with oil tankers alongside, reflecting river-borne supply of crude and products.

Manaus Free Trade Zone: rules and approvals

Decree-Law 288 of 28 February 1967 redefined the zone with an area of 10,000 km2, including industrial and agricultural centres, as a vehicle for regional development in the Amazon.

The term · industrial

What “industrial” means for a smartphone

“Industrial” describes a phone built for plant conditions: sealed, drop-resistant and manageable at fleet scale through Android Enterprise. It does not by itself mean the phone may enter a classified area. Only a device with a valid explosion-protection certificate and marking may do that.

The IS-04Z1 is designed to be both an industrial Android phone (IP68, 4,000 mAh battery, 50 MP camera, Android 16 with EMM and MDM support) and an intrinsically safe one. Its explosion-protection certificates are in progress and have not been issued.

In Manaus Free Trade Zone. Manaus fleets typically belong to manufacturers, who may only need Ex handsets for a small safety or maintenance team. EMM or MDM enrolment keeps such a niche fleet controlled. Limited road links mean deliveries from the south-east take longer, so buyers should plan spares locally.

Frequently asked questions

Can we buy an industrial mobile phone in Manaus Industrial Zone today?

No. Nothing can be bought or taken into a classified area today: certification is in progress and launch is planned for Q4 2026. What is available now is the datasheet, the written certification schedule and evaluation units for safe-area use.

What does an industrial mobile phone in Manaus Industrial Zone cost?

There is no list price yet. SAMS gives indicative pre-launch pricing in writing, against a stated quantity and site, and does not commit to a lead time before certification completes.

Is the IS-04Z1 an industrial phone as well as an Ex phone?

Yes by design: IP68 sealing, a 4,000 mAh battery, a 50 MP camera and Android 16 with EMM and MDM support. The explosion-protection certificates are separate and still in progress.

Which areas at Manaus Free Trade Zone would use it?

Likely candidates are fuel storage and loading, refinery and fuel logistics, port petroleum and chemical handling. Which areas are classified, and to what zone, is set by the site operator's own area classification drawings.

How do I become a SAMS distributor for Manaus Free Trade Zone?

Apply through the distributor page with your company details and the territory you cover. SAMS reviews applications company by company and has not yet granted a territory for Manaus Free Trade Zone.

How does equipment usually reach Manaus?

By air through Eduardo Gomes International Airport or by river and road freight, since Manaus is far from the main southern ports. Allow extra lead time and confirm free-zone customs treatment with a local forwarder.

Does the hot, humid Amazon climate affect handheld devices?

Typically yes, through heat, heavy rain and condensation. Choose sealed housings and keep spare batteries cool and dry. Confirm with the site safety officer the ingress protection and temperature class the plant expects for outdoor classified areas.

Do Free Zone factories need Ex handsets?

Mostly not, because most factories are light manufacturing and classified areas are concentrated around fuel storage and handling. Check each site's classification. Confirm documentation requirements with the site operator, and avoid buying Ex equipment where no hazardous area exists.
