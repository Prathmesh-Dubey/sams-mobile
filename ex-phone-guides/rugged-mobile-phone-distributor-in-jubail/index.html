Field notes · Jubail Industrial City

More about Jubail Industrial City that matters for portable equipment

Jubail Industrial City: rules and approvals

Saudi Arabia's Communications, Space and Technology Commission handles radio type approval, with certificates renewed every two years, and accepts European test documents.

Jubail Industrial City: background

Jubail was a small fishing village until 1975, when the government chose it for a new industrial city, so nearly all plant infrastructure dates from the last 50 years.

Jubail Industrial City: people and language

Jubail had 474,679 residents in 2022, making it the third most populous city in the Eastern Province, so a large local workforce and service sector supports the plants.

Jubail Industrial City: logistics and access

Two main highways, the Dhahran to Jubail Highway and the Abu Hadriyah Highway, link the city to the rest of the Eastern Province.

The term · rugged

What “rugged” means for a smartphone

A rugged phone is built to survive water, dust, drops and rough handling, usually rated by an IP code and a drop specification. Ruggedness is an environmental property: it says nothing about whether a phone may be used where flammable gas or dust is present. That is decided by explosion-protection certification and marking.

The IS-04Z1 combines both: an IP68 sealed rugged build and a design for Zone 1 and Zone 21 (marking target Ex ib IIC T4). The explosion-protection certificates are in progress and none has been issued.

In Jubail Industrial City. Hot desert climate, with summer temperatures that can exceed 50 C, and a Gulf coastal location make heat and humidity relevant. IP68 on the IS-04Z1 addresses dust and water ingress. It does not provide explosion protection, and the datasheet temperature range should be compared with expected conditions.

Questions · Jubail

Frequently asked questions

Can we buy a rugged mobile phone in Jubail today?

No. Nothing can be bought or taken into a classified area today: certification is in progress and launch is planned for Q4 2026. What is available now is the datasheet, the written certification schedule and evaluation units for safe-area use.

What does a rugged mobile phone in Jubail cost?

No price is published. While certification is in progress there is no list price and no committed lead time, and a supplier quoting a firm figure for an uncertified Ex handset is promising something it cannot stand behind. Indicative pre-launch pricing is issued in writing against a stated quantity and site.

How rugged is the IS-04Z1?

It is designed to IP68 sealing, with glove-friendly controls. Ruggedness is not explosion protection: that depends on the certificates, which are in progress.

Which areas at Jubail Industrial City would use it?

Likely candidates are refinery process units, petrochemical and chemical plants, storage and loading terminals, port bulk handling. Which areas are classified, and to what zone, is set by the site operator's own area classification drawings.

How do I become a SAMS distributor for Jubail Industrial City?

Apply through the distributor page with your company details and the territory you cover. SAMS reviews applications company by company and has not yet granted a territory for Jubail Industrial City.

Why do Jubail plants have gas-handling and pipeline hazards?

Associated gas gathered by Aramco is piped to Jubail and Yanbu as petrochemical feedstock, so plants receive hydrocarbon feed by pipeline. That typically means compressor, metering and storage areas with gas classification; the operator defines the zones, so ask for classification drawings.

How far is Jubail from the nearest major airport?

The Dammam airport terminal is about 60 km by road from the Jubail suburbs and around 80 km from the city centre. Visiting teams should allow road transfer time and confirm the transport and gate arrangements with the plant they are visiting.

Is heat above 50 C a problem for handsets?

Summer temperatures in Jubail can exceed 50 C, which is demanding for batteries and electronics. Check the datasheet operating range, avoid leaving handsets in vehicles, and ask the operator about any heat restrictions. IP68 helps against dust and splash but says nothing about heat tolerance or ignition protection.
