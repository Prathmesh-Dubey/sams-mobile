The term · Ex-proof

What “Ex-proof” means for a smartphone

“Ex-proof” is shorthand for equipment carrying an explosion-protection marking (the Ex mark) that matches the area it enters. The marking states the protection concept, gas or dust group and temperature class, and it must be backed by a certificate issued by a recognised body, so a phone described as Ex-proof should come with a certificate number you can check.

The IS-04Z1 is designed to carry the marking II 2 G Ex ib IIC T4. That is a target: certification is in progress, no certificate has been issued, and SAMS will publish the certificate numbers when they exist.

In Manama. A marking check near Mina Salman port fuel handling or LNG regasification at Hidd would start with the equipment protection level and zone suitability, then gas group and temperature class against the operator's area classification. The IS-04Z1 marking target is II 2 G Ex ib IIC T4, which remains a target until certified.

Frequently asked questions

Can we buy an Ex-proof smartphone in Manama today?

Not yet. The IS-04Z1 is pre-launch and its certification is still in progress ahead of a Q4 2026 launch. No unit goes into a classified area before the certificate covering it exists. The datasheet, the certification schedule and safe-area evaluation units are available now.

What does an Ex-proof smartphone in Manama cost?

SAMS has not published a price. Any figure you are offered for an uncertified Ex phone should be treated with caution; written indicative pricing is available for a defined quantity and site.

What Ex marking will the IS-04Z1 carry?

The marking target is II 2 G Ex ib IIC T4. It becomes a marking only when the certificate is issued; until then it is a design target.

Which areas at Manama would use it?

Likely candidates are refinery process units (sitra), port fuel and cargo handling (mina salman), gas processing and lpg handling, lng import and regasification (hidd). Which areas are classified, and to what zone, is set by the site operator's own area classification drawings.

Is intrinsically safe the same as explosion proof?

Not exactly. Explosion proof is a broad everyday term; intrinsic safety is one protection concept, which limits energy so ignition cannot occur. Flameproof (Ex d) is a different concept that contains an explosion. The IS-04Z1 is intrinsically safe by design.

Does ship repair near Manama create gas-hazard work?

Ship repair at yards such as ASRY involves tank entry, painting and hydro blasting, which can create temporary Zone 1 conditions inside vessel spaces. Yard safety teams issue permits and gas-free certificates before work starts.

What happens at Mina Salman port that needs Ex equipment?

Fuel and chemical cargo handling, tank areas and vessel work can create temporary classified zones, while container yards are mostly safe. Port operators set permits for each vessel and berth.

How will Bahrain's summer heat affect an Ex handset?

Summer temperatures reach about 45 C, and dust and salt air add to the load. Ambient temperature limits form part of an Ex marking, so check them against the final certificate when issued. IP68 helps against dust and splashes but does not replace explosion protection. Keep devices out of parked vehicles.
