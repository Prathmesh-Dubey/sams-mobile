State operator MERO finished expanding the Transalpine pipeline at the end of 2024, delivering up to 8 million tonnes of oil a year, with the central oil depot at Nelahozeves as a key storage point.

Czech Republic: industry on the ground

The country has two refineries, both owned by Česká rafinérská, including the Litvínov refinery, so Litvínov and Kralupy are the best-known flammable-liquid processing sites.

The Orlen Unipetrol group includes refineries and plants at Litvínov and Kralupy nad Vltavou, Paramo at Pardubice and Kolín, Spolana at Neratovice, and two research centres, and it is the only crude oil processor in the country.

Czech Republic: infrastructure

The Ingolstadt-Kralupy-Litvínov pipeline can bring crude oil from Germany and can be connected to the Transalpine pipeline, so crude arrives through pipeline terminals rather than seaports.

The term · explosion proof

What “explosion proof” means for a smartphone

“Explosion proof” is the phrase most buyers use for equipment that may be taken into a classified hazardous area. Strictly, it covers several protection concepts: a flameproof enclosure (Ex d) contains an internal explosion, while intrinsic safety (Ex i) limits electrical energy so that an ignition cannot start. Phones are normally intrinsically safe, because a flameproof handset would be too heavy to carry all shift.

The IS-04Z1 uses intrinsic safety, with a marking target of Ex ib IIC T4, and is designed for Zone 1 and Zone 21. It is pre-launch, certification is in progress and no certificate has been issued.

In Czech Republic. At Litvínov, Kralupy or Třinec, the everyday phrase explosion proof covers many things. Flameproof Ex d suits fixed motors and enclosures, while the IS-04Z1 relies on intrinsic safety so a handheld can be used live near coke ovens, crackers and tank farms. The operator classifies each area, not SAMS.

Frequently asked questions

Can we buy an explosion proof cell phone in Czech Republic today?

No. Nothing can be bought or taken into a classified area today: certification is in progress and launch is planned for Q4 2026. What is available now is the datasheet, the written certification schedule and evaluation units for safe-area use.

What does an explosion proof cell phone in Czech Republic cost?

There is no list price yet. SAMS gives indicative pre-launch pricing in writing, against a stated quantity and site, and does not commit to a lead time before certification completes.

Is the IS-04Z1 explosion proof or intrinsically safe?

It is intrinsically safe (marking target Ex ib IIC T4), which is the usual way to make a phone for hazardous areas. It is not a flameproof (Ex d) design, and certification is still in progress.

Which sites in Czech Republic does this cover?

The market guide for Czech Republic covers Litvínov and Záluží, Kralupy nad Vltavou, Neratovice, Valašské Meziříčí, among others. Site-level pages are linked below.

Is intrinsically safe the same as explosion proof?

Not exactly. Explosion proof is a broad everyday term; intrinsic safety is one protection concept, which limits energy so ignition cannot occur. Flameproof (Ex d) is a different concept that contains an explosion. The IS-04Z1 is intrinsically safe by design.

Are Czech refineries covered by ATEX rules for handheld devices?

The Czech Republic is an EU member, so ATEX Directive 2014/34/EU applies to equipment and employers zone workplaces under Directive 1999/92/EC. Refineries such as Litvínov typically also have internal device approval and permit-to-work procedures, so ask the HSE office before bringing phones in.

What language should Czech site documents be in?

Czech is the usual site language, and ATEX user instructions are typically expected in the local language of the destination. A Czech-language instruction sheet with the certificate pack often speeds up acceptance. Ask the operator whether it also accepts English documentation for contractors.

Why does the issuing notified body matter to Czech buyers?

FTZÚ in Ostrava-Radvanice is notified body 1026, and Czech engineers recognise it on an Ex certificate, so buyers often ask which body issued one before anything else. The certificate number, issuing body and marking string will be published for checking. No certificate exists yet, as certification is in progress.
