The term · IECEx

What “IECEx” means for a smartphone

IECEx is the international scheme for certifying equipment used in explosive atmospheres. A certificate of conformity is issued by an accredited certification body against a specific edition of the IEC 60079 standards and carries a schedule of conditions of safe use. It is not a legal requirement in most countries on its own, but many national schemes accept it as the technical basis.

The IECEx certificate for the IS-04Z1 is in progress and has not been issued. The marking target is Ex ib IIC T4, the intrinsic safety concept for Zone 1 gas with the hardest gas group (IIC) and a 135 °C temperature class.

In Muscat. The Mina Al Fahal refinery and Al Rusayl Industrial City host international contractors and suppliers who often work from IECEx documents. The scheme provides a certificate of conformity with a schedule of conditions. IS-04Z1 IECEx certification is in progress, so nothing is issued and nothing can be cited.

Frequently asked questions

Can we buy an IECEx mobile phone in Muscat today?

Not today. Until the certificates are issued the IS-04Z1 stays out of classified areas, and until launch there is nothing to purchase. You can ask for the datasheet and the certification schedule, or book a safe-area evaluation unit.

What does an IECEx mobile phone in Muscat cost?

SAMS has not published a price. Any figure you are offered for an uncertified Ex phone should be treated with caution; written indicative pricing is available for a defined quantity and site.

Does the IS-04Z1 hold an IECEx certificate?

No. The IECEx certificate is in progress and has not been issued. SAMS will publish the number when it exists.

Which areas at Muscat would use it?

Likely candidates are refinery process units, crude export and fuel storage, industrial city factories using solvents or gases. Which areas are classified, and to what zone, is set by the site operator's own area classification drawings.

Is intrinsically safe the same as explosion proof?

Not exactly. Explosion proof is a broad everyday term; intrinsic safety is one protection concept, which limits energy so ignition cannot occur. Flameproof (Ex d) is a different concept that contains an explosion. The IS-04Z1 is intrinsically safe by design.

Does the weather affect site operations?

Muscat is hot and arid, with most rain between December and April, and tropical cyclones such as Gonu in 2007 and Phet in 2010 have affected the coast in June. Plan for heat and storm disruption.

Do phones need local approval?

Yes. Oman's TRA type approval is required for telecom equipment, valid for four years, with a local representative and compliance mark. Confirm the current rules for each model with your supplier.

Is coastal heat a concern at Mina Al Fahal?

Summers are hot and humid along the coast, which affects outdoor equipment and battery life. Check the certified ambient range, avoid direct sun on parked devices and charge only in a safe area. Ask the operator for its device handling rules.
