Field notes · EGAS

More about EGAS that matters for portable equipment

EGAS: projects and investment

The Zohr gas field, found in 2015 about 190 km north of Port Said, is estimated to hold around 30 trillion cubic feet of gas, and production began in December 2017.

EGAS: rules and approvals

EGAS is responsible for issuing natural gas exploration licences in Egypt, so offshore and onshore gas bid rounds run through it rather than through the operating companies.

EGAS: industry on the ground

Gas imports began in April 2015, with regasification supplying the national grid, after domestic consumption reached about the same level as production, so import terminals form part of the system.

EGAS: projects and investment

In 2014 and 2015 EGAS signed six agreements covering three offshore areas in the Mediterranean and three onshore areas in the Nile Delta, and launched a bidding round for twelve offshore blocks.

The term · explosion proof

What “explosion proof” means for a smartphone

“Explosion proof” is the phrase most buyers use for equipment that may be taken into a classified hazardous area. Strictly, it covers several protection concepts: a flameproof enclosure (Ex d) contains an internal explosion, while intrinsic safety (Ex i) limits electrical energy so that an ignition cannot start. Phones are normally intrinsically safe, because a flameproof handset would be too heavy to carry all shift.

The IS-04Z1 uses intrinsic safety, with a marking target of Ex ib IIC T4, and is designed for Zone 1 and Zone 21. It is pre-launch, certification is in progress and no certificate has been issued.

In EGAS. In gas plants and LNG facilities, 'explosion-proof' is everyday shorthand for different concepts. Flameproof Ex d protects fixed equipment, while intrinsic safety limits the energy of a portable device. A phone used at a transmission station or tank farm is a handheld, so the IS-04Z1 is designed around intrinsic safety.

Questions · EGAS

Frequently asked questions

Can EGAS buy the IS-04Z1 today?

Not today. Until the certificates are issued the IS-04Z1 stays out of classified areas, and until launch there is nothing to purchase. You can ask for the datasheet and the certification schedule, or book a safe-area evaluation unit.

What does an explosion proof mobile phone for EGAS cost?

There is no list price yet. SAMS gives indicative pre-launch pricing in writing, against a stated quantity and site, and does not commit to a lead time before certification completes.

Is the IS-04Z1 explosion proof or intrinsically safe?

It is intrinsically safe (marking target Ex ib IIC T4), which is the usual way to make a phone for hazardous areas. It is not a flameproof (Ex d) design, and certification is still in progress.

How does EGAS approve suppliers?

EGAS publishes supplier registration forms and guides, and an LNG supplier register with published criteria, on its website (egas.com.eg); confirm current procedures with EGAS directly.

How will SAMS supply EGAS?

Through authorised distributors once certificates exist. Until then SAMS can send the datasheet and certification schedule and arrange safe-area evaluation units.

How does the Egyptian climate affect phone selection?

Egypt's gas and oil sites range from humid Mediterranean coast and Nile Delta locations to hot, dusty desert areas. For handheld devices check dust and water ingress protection, high temperature rating and corrosion resistance, and confirm each site's own environmental requirements.

Does EGAS buy equipment for gas sites directly?

EGAS is a holding company that owns state stakes and issues licences, so day-to-day equipment purchases are normally made by the operating companies and joint ventures that run each field or plant. Identify the operator of the site and ask its procurement and HSE teams for requirements.

Where does a supplier find EGAS registration requirements?

EGAS publishes supplier registration forms and guides, and an LNG supplier register with published criteria, on egas.com.eg. Procedures change, so confirm them with EGAS directly. The IS-04Z1 is in certification and has no certificate, which any registration must state honestly.
