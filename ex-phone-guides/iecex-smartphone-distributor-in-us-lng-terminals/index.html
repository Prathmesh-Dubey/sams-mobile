Site conditions at US LNG export terminals: what wears and what limits equipment

Scale

The United States shipped 11.9 Bcf a day of LNG in 2024, with Louisiana's four terminals handling three-fifths. Cheniere reports 16 trains in operation across Sabine Pass and Corpus Christi.

Terminals

Cameron LNG has three trains of about 12 mtpa. Calcasieu Pass has 10 mtpa nameplate capacity and began commercial operations on 16 April 2025. Freeport LNG is in Freeport, Texas.

Field notes · US LNG export terminals

More about US LNG export terminals that matters for portable equipment

US LNG export terminals: industry on the ground

In 2024 the United States had seven large LNG liquefaction export terminals with a combined capacity of about 88 million tonnes a year.

US LNG export terminals: climate and equipment wear

LNG is held at about minus 162 degrees Celsius and is not itself flammable; it must first vaporise and mix with air in a range of roughly 5 to 15 percent before it can burn.

US LNG export terminals: infrastructure

The Lake Charles LNG terminal near Lake Charles, Louisiana, has facilities for LNG receipt, storage and regasification and is one of the few such US terminals.

US LNG export terminals: projects and investment

Golden Pass LNG at Sabine Pass, owned 70 percent by QatarEnergy and 30 percent by ExxonMobil, has an 18 million tonne a year capacity and was due to be commissioned in 2025.

The term · IECEx

What “IECEx” means for a smartphone

IECEx is the international scheme for certifying equipment used in explosive atmospheres. A certificate of conformity is issued by an accredited certification body against a specific edition of the IEC 60079 standards and carries a schedule of conditions of safe use. It is not a legal requirement in most countries on its own, but many national schemes accept it as the technical basis.

The IECEx certificate for the IS-04Z1 is in progress and has not been issued. The marking target is Ex ib IIC T4, the intrinsic safety concept for Zone 1 gas with the hardest gas group (IIC) and a 135 °C temperature class.

In US LNG export terminals. Terminals such as Sabine Pass, Corpus Christi, Cameron, Calcasieu Pass, Freeport and Golden Pass involve global contractors and LNG carriers. IECEx gives these teams an international reference: a certificate of conformity with a schedule of conditions. SAMS holds no such certificate yet for the IS-04Z1.

Frequently asked questions

Can we buy an IECEx smartphone in US LNG Terminals today?

No. Nothing can be bought or taken into a classified area today: certification is in progress and launch is planned for Q4 2026. What is available now is the datasheet, the written certification schedule and evaluation units for safe-area use.

What does an IECEx smartphone in US LNG Terminals cost?

There is no list price yet. SAMS gives indicative pre-launch pricing in writing, against a stated quantity and site, and does not commit to a lead time before certification completes.

Does the IS-04Z1 hold an IECEx certificate?

No. The IECEx certificate is in progress and has not been issued. SAMS will publish the number when it exists.

Which areas at US LNG export terminals would use it?

Likely candidates are liquefaction trains, lng storage tanks, ship loading berths, gas compression and pipelines. Which areas are classified, and to what zone, is set by the site operator's own area classification drawings.

How do I become a SAMS distributor for US LNG export terminals?

Apply through the distributor page with your company details and the territory you cover. SAMS reviews applications company by company and has not yet granted a territory for US LNG export terminals.

What hazardous areas should I expect at an LNG terminal?

Typical classified areas are around storage tanks, vaporisers, loading arms, compressor buildings and flare systems. The operator defines these with area classification drawings, so request them before any visit and confirm what approved portable equipment they accept.

Why does LNG need special precautions even though it does not burn as a liquid?

LNG must vaporise and mix with air within a flammable range of roughly 5 to 15 percent before it ignites. Leaks and vents therefore create gas-hazard zones, which is why sites control portable electronics and ignition sources carefully.

Who approves handheld devices at an LNG export terminal?

Terminal operators and EPC contractors normally control equipment approvals, so the approving party depends on whether the site is operating or under construction. The IS-04Z1 has no certificate yet. Confirm requirements with the site operator or EPC before any proposal.
