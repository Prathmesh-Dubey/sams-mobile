The term · hazardous area

What “hazardous area” means for a smartphone

A hazardous area is a place where flammable gas, vapour or combustible dust may be present. Standards divide these into zones: Zones 0, 1 and 2 for gas and vapour, and Zones 20, 21 and 22 for dust. Zone 1 and Zone 21 are the tier where an explosive atmosphere is likely to occur in normal operation, which is where the IS-04Z1 is designed to work. Anything carried in must be marked for the zone, gas group and temperature class of the area.

No certificate has been issued for the IS-04Z1 yet, so it must not be taken into a classified area until the certificate covering it exists and the site operator has accepted it.

In Ras Al Khaimah. Ras Al Khaimah's classified areas include port fuel and bulk-cargo handling, pharmaceutical solvent areas and kiln fuel supply and powder handling that may be Zone 21. Hazardous-area work is smaller in scale than in Abu Dhabi. Each plant or tenant classifies its own areas, not SAMS.

Frequently asked questions

Can we buy a hazardous area smartphone in Ras Al Khaimah today?

Not today. Until the certificates are issued the IS-04Z1 stays out of classified areas, and until launch there is nothing to purchase. You can ask for the datasheet and the certification schedule, or book a safe-area evaluation unit.

What does a hazardous area smartphone in Ras Al Khaimah cost?

SAMS has not published a price. Any figure you are offered for an uncertified Ex phone should be treated with caution; written indicative pricing is available for a defined quantity and site.

Which hazardous areas is the IS-04Z1 designed for?

Zone 1 and Zone 21 (gas and dust) with gas group IIC and temperature class T4, subject to certification. It must not be used in a classified area before its certificate is issued and the site has accepted it.

Which areas at Ras Al Khaimah would use it?

Likely candidates are port fuel and bulk-cargo handling, pharmaceutical solvent areas, kiln fuel gas supply and powder handling (zone 21), gas distribution networks. Which areas are classified, and to what zone, is set by the site operator's own area classification drawings.

How do I become a SAMS distributor for Ras Al Khaimah?

Apply through the distributor page with your company details and the territory you cover. SAMS reviews applications company by company and has not yet granted a territory for Ras Al Khaimah.

Do ceramics plants create hazardous areas?

They can. Kilns and dryers use fuel gas, which may need gas classification around supply points, and tile powders can create dust, which operators assess separately. RAK Ceramics is a large local example; classification is plant-specific, so request the site's documents.

Can visiting engineers fly directly to Ras Al Khaimah?

Yes. Ras Al Khaimah International Airport serves the emirate, so visitors do not have to land in Dubai, although many do and drive. Check current flight schedules and the client's access rules; ask about equipment import paperwork too.

Is a smaller site in Ras Al Khaimah likely to need a classified-area phone?

It depends on what the site handles. Port fuel, pharmaceutical solvents and kiln fuel or powder areas may be classified, while offices are not. The operator classifies its own areas. Ask the site's HSE officer whether any area you visit is classified before choosing a device.
