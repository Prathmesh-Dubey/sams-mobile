Ciudad del Carmen: geography and layout

Ciudad del Carmen: industry on the ground

Pemex's exploration of the Campeche Basin in the early 1970s led to the Cantarell field, about 70 km from Ciudad del Carmen, which supplied 63.2 per cent of Mexico's crude at its 2004 peak before declining.

Ciudad del Carmen: background

Carmen was granted port status in 1841 because of its maritime trade and was made a city in 1856; earlier centuries saw it used as a port for repairing ships.

Ciudad del Carmen: projects and investment

The Ku-Maloob-Zaap area lies about 107 km north-east of Ciudad del Carmen and was described as Mexico's largest oil producer, so offshore activity is centred north-east of the city.

The term · intrinsically safe

What “intrinsically safe” means for a smartphone

Intrinsic safety limits the electrical and thermal energy in a circuit so that neither a spark nor a hot surface can ignite a gas or dust atmosphere, even when a fault occurs. The ib level, used for Zone 1, tolerates one fault. It is the usual way to make a handheld device that a worker carries continuously through a hazardous area.

The IS-04Z1 is designed on this principle for Zone 1 and Zone 21 (marking target II 2 G Ex ib IIC T4). Intrinsic safety is a design basis, not a certificate: certification is in progress and none has been issued yet.

In Ciudad del Carmen. Many staff here work rotations on platforms and vessels, with a shore base on the island. A single intrinsically safe handheld can carry checklists, photos and permits through crew-change bases, terminal storage and platform decks. That mobility is why the IS-04Z1 is designed on that principle, subject to certification and operator acceptance.

Frequently asked questions

Can we buy an intrinsically safe smartphone in Ciudad del Carmen today?

Not today. Until the certificates are issued the IS-04Z1 stays out of classified areas, and until launch there is nothing to purchase. You can ask for the datasheet and the certification schedule, or book a safe-area evaluation unit.

What does an intrinsically safe smartphone in Ciudad del Carmen cost?

SAMS has not published a price. Any figure you are offered for an uncertified Ex phone should be treated with caution; written indicative pricing is available for a defined quantity and site.

What does ‘intrinsically safe’ mean for the IS-04Z1?

The circuits are designed to limit energy so a spark or hot surface cannot ignite a gas or dust atmosphere, even with one fault (level ib, Zone 1). Certification of that design is in progress.

Which areas at Ciudad del Carmen would use it?

Likely candidates are marine terminal and storage, offshore production platforms, supply-vessel and crew-change bases. Which areas are classified, and to what zone, is set by the site operator's own area classification drawings.

How do I become a SAMS distributor for Ciudad del Carmen?

Apply through the distributor page with your company details and the territory you cover. SAMS reviews applications company by company and has not yet granted a territory for Ciudad del Carmen.

Does a tropical coast change device requirements?

Typically yes: heat, humidity and salt air are the main concerns, so sealed, corrosion-resistant devices are sensible. Confirm operator guidance on ingress protection and temperature class.

Which approvals matter for Ex devices in Mexico?

Mexico has its own conformity framework, and operators often also accept IECEx or ATEX marking. Requirements change, so confirm with the operator and a local certification adviser before shipping.

Does the same device work offshore and at the shore base?

Only if its marking suits the zone and gas group at each location, as classified by the operator. Offshore platforms and onshore terminals may differ. Check each area's classification and the certificate conditions before moving a device between them.
