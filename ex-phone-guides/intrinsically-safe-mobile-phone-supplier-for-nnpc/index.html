The term · intrinsically safe

What “intrinsically safe” means for a smartphone

Intrinsic safety limits the electrical and thermal energy in a circuit so that neither a spark nor a hot surface can ignite a gas or dust atmosphere, even when a fault occurs. The ib level, used for Zone 1, tolerates one fault. It is the usual way to make a handheld device that a worker carries continuously through a hazardous area.

The IS-04Z1 is designed on this principle for Zone 1 and Zone 21 (marking target II 2 G Ex ib IIC T4). Intrinsic safety is a design basis, not a certificate: certification is in progress and none has been issued yet.

In NNPC. Operators in refinery units, pipelines and storage areas inspect widely separated equipment, often under demanding conditions. A handheld intrinsically safe phone allows tag scanning, photographs and defect reports inside the classified area. It would suit routine rounds and the heavy contractor activity of rehabilitation or turnaround work at refineries and upstream joint-venture sites.

Questions · NNPC

Frequently asked questions

Can NNPC buy the IS-04Z1 today?

Not today. Until the certificates are issued the IS-04Z1 stays out of classified areas, and until launch there is nothing to purchase. You can ask for the datasheet and the certification schedule, or book a safe-area evaluation unit.

What does an intrinsically safe mobile phone for NNPC cost?

There is no list price yet. SAMS gives indicative pre-launch pricing in writing, against a stated quantity and site, and does not commit to a lead time before certification completes.

What does ‘intrinsically safe’ mean for the IS-04Z1?

The circuits are designed to limit energy so a spark or hot surface cannot ignite a gas or dust atmosphere, even with one fault (level ib, Zone 1). Certification of that design is in progress.

How does NNPC approve suppliers?

Suppliers and contractors register on the Nigerian Petroleum Exchange (NipeX), which is linked to NNPC tendering and vendor pre-qualification; local-content registration with the regulator and NCDMB is also required for work in the sector. Confirm current requirements with NNPC.

How will SAMS supply NNPC?

Through authorised distributors once certificates exist. Until then SAMS can send the datasheet and certification schedule and arrange safe-area evaluation units.

Do joint-venture partners set their own equipment standards on NNPC assets?

Often yes. NNPC manages joint ventures with international oil companies, and the operating partner usually applies its own HSE and contractor management system on site. Check who the operator is for the specific asset, and ask that operator for its approved equipment and certification requirements.

What climate conditions should intrinsically safe phones handle in southern Nigerian oil sites?

The Niger Delta and coastal areas are typically hot and humid with heavy rainfall, which stresses seals, screens and batteries. Look for strong ingress protection, tested operating temperatures and corrosion resistance, and ask the site what environmental limits its own instructions require.

What registration does a supplier need to work with NNPC?

Suppliers register on the Nigerian Petroleum Exchange, which is linked to NNPC tendering and vendor pre-qualification, and local-content registration with the regulator and NCDMB is also reported to be required. Check current rules with NNPC. SAMS has appointed no distributor, and the IS-04Z1 has no certificate yet.
