Field notes · Sohar

More about Sohar that matters for portable equipment

Sohar: background

The first lease at the new port was signed with Sohar Refinery in 2003, and the free zone itself was established in 2010, so many plants are of 2000s to 2010s vintage.

Sohar: infrastructure

Hafeet Rail is building a 238 km line to connect the port with the UAE national network, and its first track shipment arrived through the Steinweg general cargo terminal.

Sohar: who works there

C. Steinweg Oman holds the concession for dry bulk, break-bulk, roll-on/roll-off and container stuffing operations in the port, so cargo-side contractors work under a separate terminal operator.

Sohar: rules and approvals

Oman's Telecommunications Regulatory Authority follows EU-style type-approval procedures for radio equipment and issues a TRA label; a self-declaration route has also been introduced, so confirm the current path.

The term · rugged

What “rugged” means for a smartphone

A rugged phone is built to survive water, dust, drops and rough handling, usually rated by an IP code and a drop specification. Ruggedness is an environmental property: it says nothing about whether a phone may be used where flammable gas or dust is present. That is decided by explosion-protection certification and marking.

The IS-04Z1 combines both: an IP68 sealed rugged build and a design for Zone 1 and Zone 21 (marking target Ex ib IIC T4). The explosion-protection certificates are in progress and none has been issued.

In Sohar. Summers are extremely hot, with a record of 50.8 C, and rainfall is low. IP68 is designed to resist dust and immersion, which helps with fine sand and washdown, but it does not provide explosion protection. Check temperature class and the permitted ambient range for outdoor shifts.

Frequently asked questions

Can we buy a rugged smartphone in Sohar today?

Not today. Until the certificates are issued the IS-04Z1 stays out of classified areas, and until launch there is nothing to purchase. You can ask for the datasheet and the certification schedule, or book a safe-area evaluation unit.

What does a rugged smartphone in Sohar cost?

SAMS has not published a price. Any figure you are offered for an uncertified Ex phone should be treated with caution; written indicative pricing is available for a defined quantity and site.

How rugged is the IS-04Z1?

It is designed to IP68 sealing, with glove-friendly controls. Ruggedness is not explosion protection: that depends on the certificates, which are in progress.

Which areas at Sohar would use it?

Likely candidates are refinery process units, port liquid bulk handling, fuel and chemical storage, industrial plant utilities. Which areas are classified, and to what zone, is set by the site operator's own area classification drawings.

How do I become a SAMS distributor for Sohar?

Apply through the distributor page with your company details and the territory you cover. SAMS reviews applications company by company and has not yet granted a territory for Sohar.

Do I need Omani radio approval before using a phone in Sohar?

Radio equipment sold or used in Oman normally needs Telecommunications Regulatory Authority type approval or a declaration of conformity route, with a TRA label. Requirements change, so check the authority's current rules or ask your local importer before shipment.

Who approves equipment inside SOHAR Port and Freezone?

Typically the tenant or terminal operator, not the port authority alone. Dry-bulk and general cargo areas run under a terminal concession, while refinery and chemical tenants have their own HSE rules. Confirm the approval route with the site operator before shipping any device.

How hot does Sohar get for outdoor work?

Summers are extremely hot, with a record of 50.8 C, and rainfall is low. Check the certified ambient range, keep devices out of direct sun and charge only in a safe area. IP68 helps with dust but is not explosion protection.
