Field notes · Yeosu

More about Yeosu that matters for portable equipment

Yeosu: who works there

GS Caltex is owned in equal shares by Chevron and GS Energy and was founded in 1967 as Korea's first private oil company, so it follows long-established corporate safety systems.

Yeosu: projects and investment

In November 2022 the company completed a 2.7 trillion won mixed feed cracker near the Yeosu No. 2 Complex, with annual capacity of 900,000 tonnes of ethylene and 500,000 tonnes of polyethylene.

Yeosu: logistics and access

The company imports around 80 types of crude from about 30 countries and exported to 57 countries in 2022, so tanker and product shipping through the Yeosu coast is continuous.

Yeosu: infrastructure

The aromatics business started in 1990, with annual capacity of 3.0 million tonnes of aromatics and 180,000 tonnes of polypropylene, so benzene-type vapour hazards exist alongside fuel and olefin units.

The term · industrial

What “industrial” means for a smartphone

“Industrial” describes a phone built for plant conditions: sealed, drop-resistant and manageable at fleet scale through Android Enterprise. It does not by itself mean the phone may enter a classified area. Only a device with a valid explosion-protection certificate and marking may do that.

The IS-04Z1 is designed to be both an industrial Android phone (IP68, 4,000 mAh battery, 50 MP camera, Android 16 with EMM and MDM support) and an intrinsically safe one. Its explosion-protection certificates are in progress and have not been issued.

In Yeosu. A restructuring involving Lotte Chemical's Yeosu operations was approved in July 2026, so purchasing and contractor lists may change. Purchasing is normally handled by the operator or its maintenance contractors. A managed fleet under EMM or MDM should be enrolled to whichever route is confirmed.

Questions · Yeosu

Frequently asked questions

Can we buy an industrial mobile phone in Yeosu today?

No. Nothing can be bought or taken into a classified area today: certification is in progress and launch is planned for Q4 2026. What is available now is the datasheet, the written certification schedule and evaluation units for safe-area use.

What does an industrial mobile phone in Yeosu cost?

There is no list price yet. SAMS gives indicative pre-launch pricing in writing, against a stated quantity and site, and does not commit to a lead time before certification completes.

Is the IS-04Z1 an industrial phone as well as an Ex phone?

Yes by design: IP68 sealing, a 4,000 mAh battery, a 50 MP camera and Android 16 with EMM and MDM support. The explosion-protection certificates are separate and still in progress.

Which areas at Yeosu would use it?

Likely candidates are refinery process units, ethylene cracker and polymer plants, chemical storage tank farms, marine loading jetties. Which areas are classified, and to what zone, is set by the site operator's own area classification drawings.

How do I become a SAMS distributor for Yeosu?

Apply through the distributor page with your company details and the territory you cover. SAMS reviews applications company by company and has not yet granted a territory for Yeosu.

How do I register as a contractor or supplier at a Yeosu plant?

Large operators such as GS Caltex normally run vendor registration and safety induction processes, with permits for work in classified areas. Confirm the current requirements directly with the plant's procurement and safety departments before mobilising equipment.

Does the Yeosu rainy season affect handheld equipment?

Yes. June to August brings more than 200 mm of rain a month and high humidity, followed by cold winters. Typically devices need good water ingress protection and cleaning routines, and battery performance should be checked in cold weather.

Does heavy summer rain affect devices at Yeosu?

Yeosu has heavy rain from June to August, which affects outdoor rounds and jetty work. The IS-04Z1 is designed to IP68 for dust and water, but that does not give explosion protection. Plan handling and charging in dry, safe areas and check with the operator.
