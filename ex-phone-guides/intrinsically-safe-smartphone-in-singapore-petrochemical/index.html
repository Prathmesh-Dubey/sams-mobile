The term · intrinsically safe

What “intrinsically safe” means for a smartphone

Intrinsic safety limits the electrical and thermal energy in a circuit so that neither a spark nor a hot surface can ignite a gas or dust atmosphere, even when a fault occurs. The ib level, used for Zone 1, tolerates one fault. It is the usual way to make a handheld device that a worker carries continuously through a hazardous area.

The IS-04Z1 is designed on this principle for Zone 1 and Zone 21 (marking target II 2 G Ex ib IIC T4). Intrinsic safety is a design basis, not a certificate: certification is in progress and none has been issued yet.

In Singapore petrochemical sector. Operators on Jurong Island cover large integrated sites, with long pipe routes between units. A handheld intrinsically safe phone is designed for the rounds, inspections and turnaround checks that need photographs and readings in process areas. Use depends on certification in progress and on each plant's own permit rules.

Frequently asked questions

Can we buy an intrinsically safe smartphone in Singapore Petrochemical today?

No. Nothing can be bought or taken into a classified area today: certification is in progress and launch is planned for Q4 2026. What is available now is the datasheet, the written certification schedule and evaluation units for safe-area use.

What does an intrinsically safe smartphone in Singapore Petrochemical cost?

SAMS has not published a price. Any figure you are offered for an uncertified Ex phone should be treated with caution; written indicative pricing is available for a defined quantity and site.

What does ‘intrinsically safe’ mean for the IS-04Z1?

The circuits are designed to limit energy so a spark or hot surface cannot ignite a gas or dust atmosphere, even with one fault (level ib, Zone 1). Certification of that design is in progress.

Which areas at Singapore petrochemical sector would use it?

Likely candidates are cracker process units, chemical derivative plants, tank farms, polymer handling and dust areas. Which areas are classified, and to what zone, is set by the site operator's own area classification drawings.

Is intrinsically safe the same as explosion proof?

Not exactly. Explosion proof is a broad everyday term; intrinsic safety is one protection concept, which limits energy so ignition cannot occur. Flameproof (Ex d) is a different concept that contains an explosion. The IS-04Z1 is intrinsically safe by design.

What hazardous areas does a petrochemical complex have?

Common examples include cracker and reactor areas, pump and compressor houses, tank farms, jetties and polymer or powder handling, where dust zones can also arise. Zone 1 is common around seals and vents, but classification is set per plant, so ask for the area drawing.

How is Singapore's petrochemical feedstock supplied?

Singapore has no crude oil or feedstock of its own, so material arrives by sea, is stored in tank farms and caverns, and is sent through pipelines. This creates busy jetty and transfer areas where equipment control is strict.

Can one approval be reused between plants on Jurong Island?

Not safely. Each plant runs its own contractor and equipment approvals, so acceptance at one plant does not carry over to a neighbour. Prepare a datasheet pack for each. Confirm requirements with the site operator at every plant.
