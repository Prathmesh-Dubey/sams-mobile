Field notes · Switzerland

More about Switzerland that matters for portable equipment

Switzerland: projects and investment

The Mantelerlass, passed on 29 September 2023, sets a target of 35 TWh a year from new renewable technologies by 2035, compared with about 6 TWh a year in 2022, which implies major construction work.

Switzerland: background

Hydraulic power was used instead of steam in Swiss industry because of the mountainous terrain and lack of coal, which explains the concentration of hydro stations and tunnels in the Alps.

Switzerland: industry on the ground

Switzerland produces no fossil fuels and imports petroleum, so refining and bulk fuel handling is limited, while storage depots and distribution terminals are the main flammable-liquid sites.

Switzerland: logistics and access

The three Rhine ports around Basel handle about six million tonnes and over 120,000 containers a year, roughly 10% of Swiss imports and a third of the country's mineral oil products.

The term · explosion proof

What “explosion proof” means for a smartphone

“Explosion proof” is the phrase most buyers use for equipment that may be taken into a classified hazardous area. Strictly, it covers several protection concepts: a flameproof enclosure (Ex d) contains an internal explosion, while intrinsic safety (Ex i) limits electrical energy so that an ignition cannot start. Phones are normally intrinsically safe, because a flameproof handset would be too heavy to carry all shift.

The IS-04Z1 uses intrinsic safety, with a marking target of Ex ib IIC T4, and is designed for Zone 1 and Zone 21. It is pre-launch, certification is in progress and no certificate has been issued.

In Switzerland. Explosion-proof is the everyday phrase, but at a Swiss fine chemical plant the real subject is solvent atmospheres in a validated building. Flameproof Ex d suits heavy enclosures, while the IS-04Z1 uses intrinsic safety to limit the energy of a handheld, which a reviewer weighs against cleaning and airlock procedures.

Questions · Switzerland

Frequently asked questions

Can we buy an explosion proof cell phone in Switzerland today?

Not today. Until the certificates are issued the IS-04Z1 stays out of classified areas, and until launch there is nothing to purchase. You can ask for the datasheet and the certification schedule, or book a safe-area evaluation unit.

What does an explosion proof cell phone in Switzerland cost?

No price is published. While certification is in progress there is no list price and no committed lead time, and a supplier quoting a firm figure for an uncertified Ex handset is promising something it cannot stand behind. Indicative pre-launch pricing is issued in writing against a stated quantity and site.

Is the IS-04Z1 explosion proof or intrinsically safe?

It is intrinsically safe (marking target Ex ib IIC T4), which is the usual way to make a phone for hazardous areas. It is not a flameproof (Ex d) design, and certification is still in progress.

Which sites in Switzerland does this cover?

The market guide for Switzerland covers Basel and Schweizerhalle, Visp, Monthey, Kaisten and the Fricktal, among others. Site-level pages are linked below.

Is intrinsically safe the same as explosion proof?

Not exactly. Explosion proof is a broad everyday term; intrinsic safety is one protection concept, which limits energy so ignition cannot occur. Flameproof (Ex d) is a different concept that contains an explosion. The IS-04Z1 is intrinsically safe by design.

Does Switzerland accept ATEX certified devices?

Switzerland is not an EU member, but it generally aligns its equipment rules with European requirements, so ATEX-marked devices are commonly accepted. Confirm the current requirement and any Swiss conformity marking with the site operator or your importer before ordering.

What language should device documents be in for Swiss sites?

German, French and Italian are all national languages depending on the canton, and many industrial sites also work in English. Ask the operator which language it requires for instructions and certificates, and provide the declaration of conformity together with the Ex marking details.

Can the IS-04Z1 be wiped down in a Swiss pharmaceutical building?

IP68 sealing and a rugged build are aimed at a cleaned plant rather than a rained-on one, but your cleaning agents and routine must be checked. Test a unit with your own procedure and confirm compatibility with the site's validation team.
