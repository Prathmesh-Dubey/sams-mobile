The term · rugged

What “rugged” means for a smartphone

A rugged phone is built to survive water, dust, drops and rough handling, usually rated by an IP code and a drop specification. Ruggedness is an environmental property: it says nothing about whether a phone may be used where flammable gas or dust is present. That is decided by explosion-protection certification and marking.

The IS-04Z1 combines both: an IP68 sealed rugged build and a design for Zone 1 and Zone 21 (marking target Ex ib IIC T4). The explosion-protection certificates are in progress and none has been issued.

In Panipat, Haryana. Panipat has hot, dusty summers and cold, foggy winters. Dust ingress in summer is a real concern, and an IP68 rating helps by sealing against dust and water. It is an ingress rating only, and it does not replace the Ex marking needed in a classified area. Cold can reduce battery life.

Frequently asked questions

Can we buy a rugged smartphone in Panipat India today?

Not yet. The IS-04Z1 is pre-launch and its certification is still in progress ahead of a Q4 2026 launch. No unit goes into a classified area before the certificate covering it exists. The datasheet, the certification schedule and safe-area evaluation units are available now.

What does a rugged smartphone in Panipat India cost?

SAMS has not published a price. Any figure you are offered for an uncertified Ex phone should be treated with caution; written indicative pricing is available for a defined quantity and site.

How rugged is the IS-04Z1?

It is designed to IP68 sealing, with glove-friendly controls. Ruggedness is not explosion protection: that depends on the certificates, which are in progress.

Which areas at Panipat, Haryana would use it?

Likely candidates are refinery process units, naphtha cracker and petrochemical units, product tank farms, loading gantries. Which areas are classified, and to what zone, is set by the site operator's own area classification drawings.

Is intrinsically safe the same as explosion proof?

Not exactly. Explosion proof is a broad everyday term; intrinsic safety is one protection concept, which limits energy so ignition cannot occur. Flameproof (Ex d) is a different concept that contains an explosion. The IS-04Z1 is intrinsically safe by design.

What hazardous areas are at Panipat?

The refinery has process units, hydrogen and cracker areas, tank farms and loading facilities, and the naphtha cracker complex adds ethylene and propylene handling. The operator defines classified areas, so request the drawings before visiting.

Is Panipat part of a larger petrochemical complex?

Yes, the refinery has paraxylene and PTA units and a naphtha cracker complex alongside it. The units may have different operators and rules, so ask each site.

How does Panipat's winter fog and cold affect handhelds?

Cold can reduce battery capacity and fog adds moisture, so sealing and warm storage between uses help. An IP68 housing handles moisture ingress. Ask the operator about its rules for portable equipment outdoors in winter and where devices may be kept warm.
