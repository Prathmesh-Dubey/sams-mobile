The term · intrinsically safe

What “intrinsically safe” means for a smartphone

Intrinsic safety limits the electrical and thermal energy in a circuit so that neither a spark nor a hot surface can ignite a gas or dust atmosphere, even when a fault occurs. The ib level, used for Zone 1, tolerates one fault. It is the usual way to make a handheld device that a worker carries continuously through a hazardous area.

The IS-04Z1 is designed on this principle for Zone 1 and Zone 21 (marking target II 2 G Ex ib IIC T4). Intrinsic safety is a design basis, not a certificate: certification is in progress and none has been issued yet.

In Oslo. Terminal staff at Sjursoya move between storage tanks, loading points and fuel depots on routine rounds. An intrinsically safe handheld lets them record readings, take photos and complete permits at the point of work without leaving classified areas. That is the role the IS-04Z1 is designed for, once certified.

Questions · Oslo

Frequently asked questions

Can we buy an intrinsically safe smartphone in Oslo today?

No. Nothing can be bought or taken into a classified area today: certification is in progress and launch is planned for Q4 2026. What is available now is the datasheet, the written certification schedule and evaluation units for safe-area use.

What does an intrinsically safe smartphone in Oslo cost?

No price is published. While certification is in progress there is no list price and no committed lead time, and a supplier quoting a firm figure for an uncertified Ex handset is promising something it cannot stand behind. Indicative pre-launch pricing is issued in writing against a stated quantity and site.

What does ‘intrinsically safe’ mean for the IS-04Z1?

The circuits are designed to limit energy so a spark or hot surface cannot ignite a gas or dust atmosphere, even with one fault (level ib, Zone 1). Certification of that design is in progress.

Which areas at Oslo would use it?

Likely candidates are petroleum product storage and loading, methanol and bulk cargo handling, fuel depots. Which areas are classified, and to what zone, is set by the site operator's own area classification drawings.

How do I become a SAMS distributor for Oslo?

Apply through the distributor page with your company details and the territory you cover. SAMS reviews applications company by company and has not yet granted a territory for Oslo.

How does Oslo's winter affect handheld equipment used outdoors?

Winters are fairly cold under a humid continental climate, so battery performance and display response can fall in low temperatures. Typically users keep spare batteries warm and check the device's rated operating temperature against site conditions.

Can shipping-sector contacts in Oslo help with marine hazardous-area requirements?

Often. Oslo's concentration of shipping companies, brokers and insurers means technical managers and buyers are close together. They can usually tell you which certifications and class requirements apply to portable devices on their fleets, but confirm each requirement in writing.

Which Norwegian rules apply to equipment in explosive atmospheres?

ATEX-based rules apply to equipment, with authorities such as Havtil and DSB involved in oversight in general terms. A supplier should ask the terminal or company which documents it requires. Confirm specifics with the site operator or the relevant authority.
