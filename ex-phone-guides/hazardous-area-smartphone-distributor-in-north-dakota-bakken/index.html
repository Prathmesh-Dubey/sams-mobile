The term · hazardous area

What “hazardous area” means for a smartphone

A hazardous area is a place where flammable gas, vapour or combustible dust may be present. Standards divide these into zones: Zones 0, 1 and 2 for gas and vapour, and Zones 20, 21 and 22 for dust. Zone 1 and Zone 21 are the tier where an explosive atmosphere is likely to occur in normal operation, which is where the IS-04Z1 is designed to work. Anything carried in must be marked for the zone, gas group and temperature class of the area.

No certificate has been issued for the IS-04Z1 yet, so it must not be taken into a classified area until the certificate covering it exists and the site operator has accepted it.

In Bakken, North Dakota (Williston Basin). Zone 1 work in the Bakken arises at drilling rigs, wellsites, tank batteries and gas gathering and processing, plus crude loading facilities. The operator classifies these areas, and the North Dakota Department of Mineral Resources oversees oil and gas activity. SAMS does not classify anything and takes no regulatory role.

Can we buy a hazardous area smartphone in North Dakota Bakken today?

Not yet. The IS-04Z1 is pre-launch and its certification is still in progress ahead of a Q4 2026 launch. No unit goes into a classified area before the certificate covering it exists. The datasheet, the certification schedule and safe-area evaluation units are available now.

What does a hazardous area smartphone in North Dakota Bakken cost?

Which hazardous areas is the IS-04Z1 designed for?

Zone 1 and Zone 21 (gas and dust) with gas group IIC and temperature class T4, subject to certification. It must not be used in a classified area before its certificate is issued and the site has accepted it.

Which areas at Bakken, North Dakota (Williston Basin) would use it?

Likely candidates are drilling rigs, wellsites and tank batteries, gas gathering and processing, crude loading facilities. Which areas are classified, and to what zone, is set by the site operator's own area classification drawings.

How do I become a SAMS distributor for Bakken, North Dakota (Williston Basin)?

How do I get to Williston from overseas?

Williston Basin International Airport opened in 2019 and is the closest airport, usually reached through a larger US hub. Allow for road transfers and winter weather delays, and agree site access in advance.

Are Bakken sites very remote?

Many wellpads and processing plants are spread over rural plains, with Williston acting as the main service base. Confirm network coverage, emergency contact procedures and lone-worker policies with the operator, because coverage and response times differ from city sites.

Which regulator sets the rules for oil and gas work here?

The North Dakota Department of Mineral Resources, Oil and Gas Division oversees oil and gas activity. It does not approve phones. Equipment requirements generally come from operators and field service contractors, and SAMS holds no certificate yet, so ask the relevant operator what it would accept.
