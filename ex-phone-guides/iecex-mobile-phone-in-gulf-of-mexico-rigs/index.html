The term · IECEx

What “IECEx” means for a smartphone

IECEx is the international scheme for certifying equipment used in explosive atmospheres. A certificate of conformity is issued by an accredited certification body against a specific edition of the IEC 60079 standards and carries a schedule of conditions of safe use. It is not a legal requirement in most countries on its own, but many national schemes accept it as the technical basis.

The IECEx certificate for the IS-04Z1 is in progress and has not been issued. The marking target is Ex ib IIC T4, the intrinsic safety concept for Zone 1 gas with the hardest gas group (IIC) and a 135 °C temperature class.

In US Gulf of Mexico offshore platforms and rigs. Deepwater platforms such as Thunder Horse and Perdido involve international rig contractors, service companies and inspectors, many of whom use IECEx paperwork. The aim is a certificate of conformity plus a schedule of conditions that an offshore engineer can read against a specific area. It is still in progress.

Which areas at US Gulf of Mexico offshore platforms and rigs would use it?

Likely candidates are drill floors and well bays, production process decks, wellhead areas, utility and compression areas. Which areas are classified, and to what zone, is set by the site operator's own area classification drawings.

Is intrinsically safe the same as explosion proof?

Not exactly. Explosion proof is a broad everyday term; intrinsic safety is one protection concept, which limits energy so ignition cannot occur. Flameproof (Ex d) is a different concept that contains an explosion. The IS-04Z1 is intrinsically safe by design.

Why do offshore handsets need a long battery and strong charging plan?

Offshore trips run for days at a time with crews rotating on set schedules, and charging points in classified areas are limited. Plan for a full-shift battery, spare cells approved for the area, and charging only in safe areas as the platform rules require.

Is decommissioning work a factor for handset demand in the Gulf?

Decommissioning is a steady activity, with tens of thousands of old wells to plug and structures to remove under regulator-approved plans. Contractors on these jobs still work in areas that may contain hydrocarbons, so the same area-classification checks apply.

How do logistics affect offshore equipment?

Facilities lie well offshore, with Thunder Horse about 150 miles south-east of New Orleans, so access depends on helicopter and vessel schedules. Plan device enrolment, battery charging and safe-area handover onshore, and ask the operator how equipment is checked and approved before it travels.
