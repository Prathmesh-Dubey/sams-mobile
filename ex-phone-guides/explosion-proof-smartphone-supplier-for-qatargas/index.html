The term · explosion proof

What “explosion proof” means for a smartphone

“Explosion proof” is the phrase most buyers use for equipment that may be taken into a classified hazardous area. Strictly, it covers several protection concepts: a flameproof enclosure (Ex d) contains an internal explosion, while intrinsic safety (Ex i) limits electrical energy so that an ignition cannot start. Phones are normally intrinsically safe, because a flameproof handset would be too heavy to carry all shift.

The IS-04Z1 uses intrinsic safety, with a marking target of Ex ib IIC T4, and is designed for Zone 1 and Zone 21. It is pre-launch, certification is in progress and no certificate has been issued.

In Qatargas. Around LNG trains, helium production and offshore facilities, explosion-proof is a general phrase that covers more than one concept. Flameproof Ex d protects fixed electrical equipment. Intrinsic safety limits the energy of a handheld. A phone relies on the second, and the target marking is II 2 G Ex ib IIC T4.

Frequently asked questions

Can Qatargas buy the IS-04Z1 today?

Not today. Until the certificates are issued the IS-04Z1 stays out of classified areas, and until launch there is nothing to purchase. You can ask for the datasheet and the certification schedule, or book a safe-area evaluation unit.

What does an explosion proof smartphone for Qatargas cost?

SAMS has not published a price. Any figure you are offered for an uncertified Ex phone should be treated with caution; written indicative pricing is available for a defined quantity and site.

Is the IS-04Z1 explosion proof or intrinsically safe?

It is intrinsically safe (marking target Ex ib IIC T4), which is the usual way to make a phone for hazardous areas. It is not a flameproof (Ex d) design, and certification is still in progress.

How does Qatargas approve suppliers?

QatarEnergy LNG is part of the QatarEnergy group, whose Supply Management section describes vendor registration through the Mushtaryat Portal (SAP Vendor Code) and a Preferred Manufacturers List; confirm current requirements with its procurement and HSE teams.

How will SAMS supply Qatargas?

Through authorised distributors once certificates exist. Until then SAMS can send the datasheet and certification schedule and arrange safe-area evaluation units.

What environment should be assumed for devices at Ras Laffan?

The site is a coastal desert location with high summer heat, humidity and salt in the air, plus dust. Check enclosure ingress protection, corrosion resistance and the operating temperature range of any device, and confirm area-specific rules with the operator rather than assuming one standard across all facilities.

Which Qatargas areas typically need intrinsically safe devices?

Typically LNG liquefaction trains, gas treatment and sulphur units, helium and condensate handling, jetty and loading areas, and offshore platforms feeding Ras Laffan. The operating company classifies each area, so ask for the zone drawings, gas group and temperature class before specifying a phone or tablet.

Is Qatargas the same company as QatarEnergy LNG?

According to the sheet, Qatargas was rebranded QatarEnergy LNG in September 2023 and is a QatarEnergy company operating LNG ventures at Ras Laffan Industrial City. Procurement and HSE requirements should be confirmed with the company. The IS-04Z1 is pre-launch and has no certificate.
