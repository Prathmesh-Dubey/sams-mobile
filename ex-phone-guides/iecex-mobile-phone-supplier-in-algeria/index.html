The term · IECEx

What “IECEx” means for a smartphone

IECEx is the international scheme for certifying equipment used in explosive atmospheres. A certificate of conformity is issued by an accredited certification body against a specific edition of the IEC 60079 standards and carries a schedule of conditions of safe use. It is not a legal requirement in most countries on its own, but many national schemes accept it as the technical basis.

The IECEx certificate for the IS-04Z1 is in progress and has not been issued. The marking target is Ex ib IIC T4, the intrinsic safety concept for Zone 1 gas with the hardest gas group (IIC) and a 135 °C temperature class.

In Algeria. In Algeria the IECEx scheme is the common language between Sonatrach, its foreign joint-venture partners such as Eni and TotalEnergies, and the visiting contractors who work for all of them. A reviewer would read the certificate of conformity for the marking and the schedule of conditions for any special conditions of use, before the device is listed for a plant.

Frequently asked questions

Can we buy an IECEx mobile phone in Algeria today?

Not today. Until the certificates are issued the IS-04Z1 stays out of classified areas, and until launch there is nothing to purchase. You can ask for the datasheet and the certification schedule, or book a safe-area evaluation unit.

What does an IECEx mobile phone in Algeria cost?

Does the IS-04Z1 hold an IECEx certificate?

Which sites in Algeria does this cover?

The market guide for Algeria covers Hassi Messaoud, Ouargla, Hassi R'Mel, Laghouat, In Amenas, Illizi, In Salah, Tamanrasset, among others. Site-level pages are linked below.

How will SAMS supply Algeria?

Through authorised distributors once certificates exist. Until then SAMS can send the datasheet and certification schedule and arrange safe-area evaluation units.

How long does Algerian type approval typically take?

Providers report about ten weeks for processing and a certificate validity of five years if the device is unchanged, with a March 2026 decree allowing several importers to use one certificate. Treat these as typical figures and confirm the current timing and fees with ARPCE or an agent.

Which Algerian authority approves mobile phones and radios?

ARPCE is described as the main authority issuing type approval for equipment such as mobile phones and Wi-Fi devices, while ANF handles radio spectrum approvals for devices such as short-range radios. A local representative is needed. Confirm which body applies to your specific device before applying.

Will the battery and display cope with Saharan heat and distance from camp?

The 4,000 mAh battery is sized for a working day, and IP68 sealing is aimed at sand and dust. The certificate will state the ambient range, so check it against southern field summers. Away from camps the carrier signal can be patchy, so the handset falls back to plant Wi-Fi. Verify on a test unit.
