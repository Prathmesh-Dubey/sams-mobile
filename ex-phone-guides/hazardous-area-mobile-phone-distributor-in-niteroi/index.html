The term · hazardous area

What “hazardous area” means for a smartphone

A hazardous area is a place where flammable gas, vapour or combustible dust may be present. Standards divide these into zones: Zones 0, 1 and 2 for gas and vapour, and Zones 20, 21 and 22 for dust. Zone 1 and Zone 21 are the tier where an explosive atmosphere is likely to occur in normal operation, which is where the IS-04Z1 is designed to work. Anything carried in must be marked for the zone, gas group and temperature class of the area.

No certificate has been issued for the IS-04Z1 yet, so it must not be taken into a classified area until the certificate covering it exists and the site operator has accepted it.

In Niteroi. Niteroi is mainly a commercial city, so the classified areas here are essentially the shipyard: module fabrication and integration, tank and hull work, and fuel and gas handling at quaysides. The yard operator classifies them for each job; SAMS does not.

Frequently asked questions

Can we buy a hazardous area mobile phone in Niteroi today?

No. Nothing can be bought or taken into a classified area today: certification is in progress and launch is planned for Q4 2026. What is available now is the datasheet, the written certification schedule and evaluation units for safe-area use.

What does a hazardous area mobile phone in Niteroi cost?

There is no list price yet. SAMS gives indicative pre-launch pricing in writing, against a stated quantity and site, and does not commit to a lead time before certification completes.

Which hazardous areas is the IS-04Z1 designed for?

Zone 1 and Zone 21 (gas and dust) with gas group IIC and temperature class T4, subject to certification. It must not be used in a classified area before its certificate is issued and the site has accepted it.

Which areas at Niteroi would use it?

Likely candidates are fpso module fabrication and integration, shipyard tank and hull work, fuel and gas handling at quaysides. Which areas are classified, and to what zone, is set by the site operator's own area classification drawings.

How do I become a SAMS distributor for Niteroi?

Apply through the distributor page with your company details and the territory you cover. SAMS reviews applications company by company and has not yet granted a territory for Niteroi.

Is local content relevant for equipment supply in Niteroi?

Local-content requirements are a decisive factor in Petrobras tenders, and shipyards respond to them. For handheld devices it is usually a contractor-level question, so confirm with the yard or main contractor whether domestic sourcing or registration applies.

How does Niteroi's climate affect handheld phones?

A tropical monsoon climate means warm, humid weather and frequent rain, so sealed cases, drying routines and spare batteries are sensible. Confirm the operator's ingress protection and temperature requirements for outdoor classified areas.

Do handsets need Ex marking for all shipyard work?

Only where the work is classified, such as tank and hull work or fuel and gas handling at quaysides, as set by the yard and its HSE team. Other areas may not be. Confirm the classification for each job with the site operator.
