Supplier approval · Petrobras

How suppliers reach Petrobras

Suppliers register through the Petronect portal: identify the company, select supply families, submit documents and pass technical evaluation to receive a registration certificate (CRC). The Corporate Registry is open to foreign companies; the Simplified Registry is for Brazilian companies only. The Ex certificate and an organisation's own vendor or engineering approval are two separate timelines; SAMS is working towards both and holds neither today.

Field notes · Petrobras

More about Petrobras that matters for portable equipment

Petrobras: geography and layout

The Tupi, Jupiter and Sugar Loaf discoveries of 2007 and 2008 lie in the Santos Basin about 300 km off the coast of Rio de Janeiro, so offshore work depends on long sea and helicopter transfers.

Petrobras: who works there

The company runs six business areas: refining, transport and marketing; exploration and production; distribution; gas and power; international; and biofuels, so a buyer must identify which area controls a given site.

Petrobras: infrastructure

The Cenpes research centre in Rio de Janeiro, opened in 1963, is among the world's largest centres dedicated to petroleum research, and headquarters offices sit in downtown Rio.

Petrobras: projects and investment

In 2023 the company announced the resumption of a new production line at the Abreu e Lima refinery in Ipojuca, Pernambuco, intended to refine heavy crude from the pre-salt region.

The term · ATEX and IECEx

What “ATEX and IECEx” means for a smartphone

ATEX is the European legal framework for equipment used in explosive atmospheres (Directive 2014/34/EU). IECEx is the international certification scheme built on the IEC 60079 standards. Buyers outside Europe often ask for both: an IECEx certificate is the technical evidence most national regimes build on, while ATEX is the legal route into the European market.

For the IS-04Z1 both routes are in progress with TÜV in Germany and neither certificate has been issued. The marking target is II 2 G Ex ib IIC T4: equipment Group II (surface industry), Category 2 (Zone 1 gas), protection concept ib (intrinsic safety), gas group IIC and temperature class T4.

In Petrobras. A Petrobras reviewer assessing a handset for a Campos or Santos Basin platform, an FPSO or a refinery would want the certificate of conformity, schedule of conditions and marking. Dual ATEX and IECEx evidence helps the many international contractors and EPCs serving the pre-salt fields. The IS-04Z1 is in certification and no certificate has been issued.

Frequently asked questions

Can Petrobras buy the IS-04Z1 today?

Not today. Until the certificates are issued the IS-04Z1 stays out of classified areas, and until launch there is nothing to purchase. You can ask for the datasheet and the certification schedule, or book a safe-area evaluation unit.

What does an ATEX and IECEx mobile phone for Petrobras cost?

No price is published. While certification is in progress there is no list price and no committed lead time, and a supplier quoting a firm figure for an uncertified Ex handset is promising something it cannot stand behind. Indicative pre-launch pricing is issued in writing against a stated quantity and site.

Is the IS-04Z1 ATEX and IECEx certified?

Not yet. Both routes are in progress with TÜV in Germany and no certificate has been issued. Any listing that says otherwise is wrong.

How does Petrobras approve suppliers?

Suppliers register through the Petronect portal: identify the company, select supply families, submit documents and pass technical evaluation to receive a registration certificate (CRC). The Corporate Registry is open to foreign companies; the Simplified Registry is for Brazilian companies only.

Is intrinsically safe the same as explosion proof?

Not exactly. Explosion proof is a broad everyday term; intrinsic safety is one protection concept, which limits energy so ignition cannot occur. Flameproof (Ex d) is a different concept that contains an explosion. The IS-04Z1 is intrinsically safe by design.

Are offshore Petrobras platforms different from refineries for phone selection?

Yes. Offshore platforms combine Zone 1 and Zone 2 areas with salt air, vibration and limited space, while refineries have larger plant areas and more fixed infrastructure. Check ingress protection, corrosion resistance and wireless coverage needs separately for each, and confirm area classification with the asset operator.

Does Petrobras still source equipment only from Brazilian suppliers?

The legal monopoly ended in 1997 and the market is open to competition, but large operators usually apply local-content and registration rules and run their own supplier approval. Confirm the current registration requirements and any local-content expectations directly with Petrobras procurement.

Can a foreign company register to supply Petrobras?

Petrobras suppliers register through the Petronect portal and pass technical evaluation to receive a registration certificate. The Corporate Registry is open to foreign companies, while the Simplified Registry is for Brazilian firms only. Confirm current steps with Petrobras. The IS-04Z1 has no certificate yet.
