Field notes · Senoko

More about Senoko that matters for portable equipment

Senoko: infrastructure

Its authorised capacity is 2,800 megawatts from seven operating units, and it runs mainly as a combined-cycle gas plant, so gas-turbine halls and fuel systems form its main classified areas.

Senoko: industry on the ground

Natural gas supplies over 90 percent of Singapore's electricity, up from less than 20 percent in 2000, which is why large gas-fired plants such as Senoko dominate the local hazardous-area picture.

Senoko: infrastructure

Combined-cycle units 6 and 7, rated at 431 megawatts each, were completed by 2012 by repowering the second oil-fired steam stage, while units 3 to 5 at 365 megawatts each followed by 2004.

Senoko: industry on the ground

Natural gas has replaced crude oil as the main fuel since 1992, with fuel oil kept as a back-up, and stage III boilers were modified with gas burners to burn either fuel.

The term · IECEx

What “IECEx” means for a smartphone

IECEx is the international scheme for certifying equipment used in explosive atmospheres. A certificate of conformity is issued by an accredited certification body against a specific edition of the IEC 60079 standards and carries a schedule of conditions of safe use. It is not a legal requirement in most countries on its own, but many national schemes accept it as the technical basis.

The IECEx certificate for the IS-04Z1 is in progress and has not been issued. The marking target is Ex ib IIC T4, the intrinsic safety concept for Zone 1 gas with the hardest gas group (IIC) and a 135 °C temperature class.

In Senoko. Senoko's owners are international groups, so visiting engineers and outage contractors may be used to reading IECEx paperwork. A certificate of conformity with its schedule of conditions is what such a reader would look for. For the IS-04Z1 neither document exists yet, because the IECEx programme is still in progress.

Frequently asked questions

Can we buy an IECEx mobile phone in Senoko Industrial Zone today?

Not yet. The IS-04Z1 is pre-launch and its certification is still in progress ahead of a Q4 2026 launch. No unit goes into a classified area before the certificate covering it exists. The datasheet, the certification schedule and safe-area evaluation units are available now.

What does an IECEx mobile phone in Senoko Industrial Zone cost?

SAMS has not published a price. Any figure you are offered for an uncertified Ex phone should be treated with caution; written indicative pricing is available for a defined quantity and site.

Does the IS-04Z1 hold an IECEx certificate?

No. The IECEx certificate is in progress and has not been issued. SAMS will publish the number when it exists.

Which areas at Senoko would use it?

Likely candidates are power plant gas and fuel systems, fuel storage tanks and terminal services. Which areas are classified, and to what zone, is set by the site operator's own area classification drawings.

Is intrinsically safe the same as explosion proof?

Not exactly. Explosion proof is a broad everyday term; intrinsic safety is one protection concept, which limits energy so ignition cannot occur. Flameproof (Ex d) is a different concept that contains an explosion. The IS-04Z1 is intrinsically safe by design.

When are outages most likely to need extra field devices?

Combined-cycle units are taken offline for planned inspections, and contractor numbers rise during those windows. Dates are set by the operator and the grid, so ask the plant's maintenance planner about the outage calendar and the approved-equipment list.

Does the fuel mix change the equipment requirement?

Natural gas has been the main fuel since 1992, with fuel oil as a back-up. Gas groups and temperature classes differ between natural gas and oil vapour, so devices should be matched to the gas group and temperature class stated in the plant's area classification.

Is the whole Senoko estate a hazardous area?

No. The classified areas are mostly the gas and fuel systems and fuel tanks at the power station, not the estate as a whole. Other tenants have not been checked here. Ask the relevant operator for its classification before assuming a zone, and confirm with the site operator.
