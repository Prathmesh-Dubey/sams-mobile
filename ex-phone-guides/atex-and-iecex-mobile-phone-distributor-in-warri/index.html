The term · ATEX and IECEx

What “ATEX and IECEx” means for a smartphone

ATEX is the European legal framework for equipment used in explosive atmospheres (Directive 2014/34/EU). IECEx is the international certification scheme built on the IEC 60079 standards. Buyers outside Europe often ask for both: an IECEx certificate is the technical evidence most national regimes build on, while ATEX is the legal route into the European market.

For the IS-04Z1 both routes are in progress with TÜV in Germany and neither certificate has been issued. The marking target is II 2 G Ex ib IIC T4: equipment Group II (surface industry), Category 2 (Zone 1 gas), protection concept ib (intrinsic safety), gas group IIC and temperature class T4.

In Warri. Warri hosts many international oil company offices and the Escravos gas and gas-to-liquids plants lie nearby, so reviewers see contractor packs from firms with international standards. ATEX and IECEx evidence together suits them. The IS-04Z1 has no certificate yet, as certification is in progress.

Questions · Warri

Frequently asked questions

Can we buy an ATEX and IECEx mobile phone in Warri today?

Not today. Until the certificates are issued the IS-04Z1 stays out of classified areas, and until launch there is nothing to purchase. You can ask for the datasheet and the certification schedule, or book a safe-area evaluation unit.

What does an ATEX and IECEx mobile phone in Warri cost?

No price is published. While certification is in progress there is no list price and no committed lead time, and a supplier quoting a firm figure for an uncertified Ex handset is promising something it cannot stand behind. Indicative pre-launch pricing is issued in writing against a stated quantity and site.

Is the IS-04Z1 ATEX and IECEx certified?

Not yet. Both routes are in progress with TÜV in Germany and no certificate has been issued. Any listing that says otherwise is wrong.

Which areas at Warri would use it?

Likely candidates are refinery process units, gas plants and gas-to-liquids facilities, seaport and jetty fuel handling. Which areas are classified, and to what zone, is set by the site operator's own area classification drawings.

How do I become a SAMS distributor for Warri?

Apply through the distributor page with your company details and the territory you cover. SAMS reviews applications company by company and has not yet granted a territory for Warri.

What should I plan for during the Warri rainy season?

Rain falls from about May to October with annual totals near 2,770 mm, so expect sustained humidity. Choose devices with a sealed housing and a strap or holster, keep spare batteries dry, and check the operator's induction rules about devices in wet, classified outdoor areas.

Does harmattan dust matter for phones and handhelds used around Warri?

Typically yes. The dry-season harmattan carries fine dust that can clog ports, speaker grilles and cooling paths, so sealed enclosures (dust-tight ratings) and routine cleaning matter. Confirm the dust and ingress requirements with the site operator before specifying equipment.

Is the Warri refinery running at the moment?

The refinery has been shut and restarted repeatedly since 2025, and NNPC signed a memorandum with Chinese firms in April 2026 on restart plans. Check current status first, before planning any visit or quote. Confirm it with the site operator.
