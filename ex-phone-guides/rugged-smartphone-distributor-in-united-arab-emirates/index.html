The term · rugged

What “rugged” means for a smartphone

A rugged phone is built to survive water, dust, drops and rough handling, usually rated by an IP code and a drop specification. Ruggedness is an environmental property: it says nothing about whether a phone may be used where flammable gas or dust is present. That is decided by explosion-protection certification and marking.

The IS-04Z1 combines both: an IP68 sealed rugged build and a design for Zone 1 and Zone 21 (marking target Ex ib IIC T4). The explosion-protection certificates are in progress and none has been issued.

In United Arab Emirates. Coastal humidity, salt air and long offshore rotations wear equipment in ways inland plant does not, and temperatures run above 45 °C. A rugged build is aimed at heat, humidity and salt, and IP68 is what survives a jetty washdown. IP68 is an ingress rating and does not give explosion protection.

Questions · United Arab Emirates

Frequently asked questions

Can we buy a rugged smartphone in United Arab Emirates today?

Not today. Until the certificates are issued the IS-04Z1 stays out of classified areas, and until launch there is nothing to purchase. You can ask for the datasheet and the certification schedule, or book a safe-area evaluation unit.

What does a rugged smartphone in United Arab Emirates cost?

SAMS has not published a price. Any figure you are offered for an uncertified Ex phone should be treated with caution; written indicative pricing is available for a defined quantity and site.

How rugged is the IS-04Z1?

It is designed to IP68 sealing, with glove-friendly controls. Ruggedness is not explosion protection: that depends on the certificates, which are in progress.

Which sites in United Arab Emirates does this cover?

The market guide for United Arab Emirates covers Ruwais, Habshan, Das Island, Zirku Island, among others. Site-level pages are linked below.

How do I become a SAMS distributor for United Arab Emirates?

Apply through the distributor page with your company details and the territory you cover. SAMS reviews applications company by company and has not yet granted a territory for United Arab Emirates.

Which Ministry handles product safety conformity?

The Ministry of Industry and Advanced Technology handles safety declarations of conformity, listed as valid for one year with a local representative required. This is separate from the TDRA telecom approval, so ask your supplier to confirm both.

Where are the main hazardous-area sites in the UAE?

Ruwais and the Abu Dhabi oil and gas fields, Fujairah's oil terminals, Jebel Ali in Dubai, with its power, desalination and aluminium smelter complex. Each belongs to a different operator.

Can the IS-04Z1 be used on an offshore island such as Das or Zirku?

It is designed with a 4,000 mAh battery and a fully sealed body to stay in service until the next rotation, since there is no same-day replacement. Certification is in progress. Confirm area classification and permit rules with the operator before any use.
