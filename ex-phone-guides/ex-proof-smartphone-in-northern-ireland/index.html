The term · Ex-proof

What “Ex-proof” means for a smartphone

“Ex-proof” is shorthand for equipment carrying an explosion-protection marking (the Ex mark) that matches the area it enters. The marking states the protection concept, gas or dust group and temperature class, and it must be backed by a certificate issued by a recognised body, so a phone described as Ex-proof should come with a certificate number you can check.

The IS-04Z1 is designed to carry the marking II 2 G Ex ib IIC T4. That is a target: certification is in progress, no certificate has been issued, and SAMS will publish the certificate numbers when they exist.

In Northern Ireland. A marking check at a gas-fired station or pressure reduction site would concentrate on natural gas, which is normally in a lower gas group than hydrogen, so IIC marking is conservative. The operator's zone map sets the requirement. The IS-04Z1 target marking is II 2 G Ex ib IIC T4, still pending certification.

Questions · Northern Ireland

Frequently asked questions

Can we buy an Ex-proof smartphone in Northern Ireland today?

No. Nothing can be bought or taken into a classified area today: certification is in progress and launch is planned for Q4 2026. What is available now is the datasheet, the written certification schedule and evaluation units for safe-area use.

What does an Ex-proof smartphone in Northern Ireland cost?

SAMS has not published a price. Any figure you are offered for an uncertified Ex phone should be treated with caution; written indicative pricing is available for a defined quantity and site.

What Ex marking will the IS-04Z1 carry?

The marking target is II 2 G Ex ib IIC T4. It becomes a marking only when the certificate is issued; until then it is a design target.

Which areas at Northern Ireland would use it?

Likely candidates are gas-fired power station fuel systems, gas transmission pipeline assets, gas pressure reduction sites, fuel storage. Which areas are classified, and to what zone, is set by the site operator's own area classification drawings.

Is intrinsically safe the same as explosion proof?

Not exactly. Explosion proof is a broad everyday term; intrinsic safety is one protection concept, which limits energy so ignition cannot occur. Flameproof (Ex d) is a different concept that contains an explosion. The IS-04Z1 is intrinsically safe by design.

Is crossing the Irish Sea a logistics issue for equipment supply?

Goods moving between Great Britain and Northern Ireland pass through post-Brexit customs arrangements, which can add paperwork and lead time. For urgent site equipment, plan stock holding in Northern Ireland or the Republic and confirm the current import documentation with your freight forwarder.

How does the damp maritime climate affect phone selection?

Northern Ireland has a mild but wet and windy climate, with frequent rain and salt air near the coast, so sealing, corrosion resistance and condensation tolerance matter more than extreme temperature ratings. Check ingress protection and gasket quality, and ask the site about its own requirements.

Which marking applies to equipment in Northern Ireland?

Under the Windsor Framework, relevant EU product rules apply in Northern Ireland, so equipment is typically CE marked, with UKNI added where a UK notified body is used. Because rules can change, confirm the current position with the site operator and HSENI guidance.
