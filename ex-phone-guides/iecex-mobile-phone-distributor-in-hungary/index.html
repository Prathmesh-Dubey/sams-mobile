More about Hungary that matters for portable equipment

Hungary: industry on the ground

MOL's Danube Refinery at Százhalombatta started up in 1965 and has a capacity of about 8.1 million tonnes a year, making it one of the largest refineries in Central and Eastern Europe.

Hungary: industry on the ground

The Matra power plant, the last coal electricity producer, supplied about 9% of Hungary's electricity in 2020 and was served by coal mines in Visonta and Bükkábrány, with a gas combined-cycle replacement planned.

Hungary: rules and approvals

Under ATEX, Category 3 equipment may be self-certified by the manufacturer with a technical dossier, while Category 1 and 2 usually involve a notified body, which affects what Hungarian buyers should ask for.

Hungary: infrastructure

The Arad-Szeged natural gas pipeline connects Romania and Hungary, so border compressor and metering stations belong to the national gas network.

The term · IECEx

What “IECEx” means for a smartphone

IECEx is the international scheme for certifying equipment used in explosive atmospheres. A certificate of conformity is issued by an accredited certification body against a specific edition of the IEC 60079 standards and carries a schedule of conditions of safe use. It is not a legal requirement in most countries on its own, but many national schemes accept it as the technical basis.

The IECEx certificate for the IS-04Z1 is in progress and has not been issued. The marking target is Ex ib IIC T4, the intrinsic safety concept for Zone 1 gas with the hardest gas group (IIC) and a 135 °C temperature class.

In Hungary. Hungarian sites such as Százhalombatta, Tiszaújváros and Kazincbarcika use contractors who also work in other countries. For them the IECEx scheme is a familiar reference, with a certificate of conformity naming the equipment and a schedule of conditions setting conditions of use. IECEx certification for the IS-04Z1 is in progress; none can be cited yet.

Can we buy an IECEx mobile phone in Hungary today?

Not yet. The IS-04Z1 is pre-launch and its certification is still in progress ahead of a Q4 2026 launch. No unit goes into a classified area before the certificate covering it exists. The datasheet, the certification schedule and safe-area evaluation units are available now.

What does an IECEx mobile phone in Hungary cost?

Does the IS-04Z1 hold an IECEx certificate?

Which sites in Hungary does this cover?

The market guide for Hungary covers Százhalombatta, Tiszaújváros, Kazincbarcika, Dorog, among others. Site-level pages are linked below.

How do I become a SAMS distributor for Hungary?

Apply through the distributor page with your company details and the territory you cover. SAMS reviews applications company by company and has not yet granted a territory for Hungary.

How does Hungary's winter weather affect Ex handheld devices?

Continental winters can bring sub-zero temperatures, which reduce battery capacity. Check the lowest rated ambient temperature on the certificate and whether touch input works with gloves. Plan spare batteries and warm storage for outdoor work at refineries and pipeline stations.

Which Hungarian companies run the main classified-area sites?

MOL is the largest integrated oil, gas and petrochemical group, and the Paks plant and gas network operators also have hazardous areas. Each company runs its own supplier approval, so contact its procurement and HSE teams for current device and contractor requirements.

Can one device cover both a refinery and a pharmaceutical plant?

Gas groups, dust and temperature needs differ between a refinery and a solvent suite. A device would need a marking that suits each classification. Check each plant's zone documents, since the answer depends on the operator's classification rather than the device alone.
