The term · explosion proof

What “explosion proof” means for a smartphone

“Explosion proof” is the phrase most buyers use for equipment that may be taken into a classified hazardous area. Strictly, it covers several protection concepts: a flameproof enclosure (Ex d) contains an internal explosion, while intrinsic safety (Ex i) limits electrical energy so that an ignition cannot start. Phones are normally intrinsically safe, because a flameproof handset would be too heavy to carry all shift.

The IS-04Z1 uses intrinsic safety, with a marking target of Ex ib IIC T4, and is designed for Zone 1 and Zone 21. It is pre-launch, certification is in progress and no certificate has been issued.

In Salalah. At methanol and ammonia production and LPG storage, explosion-proof is common shorthand. Flameproof Ex d suits fixed equipment in strong housings, whereas a handheld used on plant rounds and port liquid bulk handling suits intrinsic safety. The IS-04Z1 is designed around intrinsic safety.

Frequently asked questions

Can we buy an explosion proof smartphone in Salalah today?

No. Nothing can be bought or taken into a classified area today: certification is in progress and launch is planned for Q4 2026. What is available now is the datasheet, the written certification schedule and evaluation units for safe-area use.

What does an explosion proof smartphone in Salalah cost?

There is no list price yet. SAMS gives indicative pre-launch pricing in writing, against a stated quantity and site, and does not commit to a lead time before certification completes.

Is the IS-04Z1 explosion proof or intrinsically safe?

It is intrinsically safe (marking target Ex ib IIC T4), which is the usual way to make a phone for hazardous areas. It is not a flameproof (Ex d) design, and certification is still in progress.

Which areas at Salalah would use it?

Likely candidates are methanol and ammonia production, lpg extraction and storage, port liquid bulk handling. Which areas are classified, and to what zone, is set by the site operator's own area classification drawings.

How do I become a SAMS distributor for Salalah?

Apply through the distributor page with your company details and the territory you cover. SAMS reviews applications company by company and has not yet granted a territory for Salalah.

How does the monsoon season affect work?

From June to September the khareef monsoon brings cloud and fog, especially in July and August, so conditions are cooler and damper than in northern Oman. Protect portable equipment against moisture and condensation during this season.

Do phones need approval?

Yes, Oman TRA type approval is needed for telecom equipment and is valid for four years. Your distributor should confirm the approval for the model.

Does the khareef monsoon affect equipment?

The monsoon runs from June to September and brings mist and rain, unlike most of Oman. IP68 is designed to withstand water and dust, but it is not explosion protection. Check the certified ambient range and keep devices dry. Ask the operator about wet-weather practice.
