The term · IECEx

What “IECEx” means for a smartphone

IECEx is the international scheme for certifying equipment used in explosive atmospheres. A certificate of conformity is issued by an accredited certification body against a specific edition of the IEC 60079 standards and carries a schedule of conditions of safe use. It is not a legal requirement in most countries on its own, but many national schemes accept it as the technical basis.

The IECEx certificate for the IS-04Z1 is in progress and has not been issued. The marking target is Ex ib IIC T4, the intrinsic safety concept for Zone 1 gas with the hardest gas group (IIC) and a 135 °C temperature class.

In Offshore Indonesia. Fields in the Natuna, Java Sea and Kutai Basin involve international partners and fabrication yards, so IECEx documents travel well. A certificate of conformity with a schedule of conditions would be reviewed. For the IS-04Z1 they are targets, not issued papers.

Frequently asked questions

Can we buy an IECEx mobile phone in Offshore Indonesia today?

Not today. Until the certificates are issued the IS-04Z1 stays out of classified areas, and until launch there is nothing to purchase. You can ask for the datasheet and the certification schedule, or book a safe-area evaluation unit.

What does an IECEx mobile phone in Offshore Indonesia cost?

No price is published. While certification is in progress there is no list price and no committed lead time, and a supplier quoting a firm figure for an uncertified Ex handset is promising something it cannot stand behind. Indicative pre-launch pricing is issued in writing against a stated quantity and site.

Does the IS-04Z1 hold an IECEx certificate?

No. The IECEx certificate is in progress and has not been issued. SAMS will publish the number when it exists.

Which areas at Offshore Indonesia would use it?

Likely candidates are wellhead and production platforms, fpsos and floating production units, drilling and workover areas, gas processing modules. Which areas are classified, and to what zone, is set by the site operator's own area classification drawings.

Is intrinsically safe the same as explosion proof?

Not exactly. Explosion proof is a broad everyday term; intrinsic safety is one protection concept, which limits energy so ignition cannot occur. Flameproof (Ex d) is a different concept that contains an explosion. The IS-04Z1 is intrinsically safe by design.

Do offshore platforms in Indonesia require certified phones in hazardous areas?

Usually yes for classified areas. Operators normally require equipment certified for the zone, gas group and temperature class of the location, supported by a permit to work. Confirm the exact certification marks the platform accepts for portable devices.

What language support is available for contractors offshore Indonesia?

Indonesian is the national language and Malay is widely spoken in areas such as Natuna, while operators typically run safety briefings in English as well. Check that device interfaces and any emergency procedures can be used in the languages your crew needs.

Does the sea affect handset choice offshore Indonesia?

Heat, humidity and salt spray are constant, and monsoon seasons affect sea state and crew changes. Check seals, ports and charging contacts for corrosion. IP68 is useful but is not explosion protection. Confirm approved devices with the operator.
