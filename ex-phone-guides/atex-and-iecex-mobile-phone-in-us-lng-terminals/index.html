The term · ATEX and IECEx

What “ATEX and IECEx” means for a smartphone

ATEX is the European legal framework for equipment used in explosive atmospheres (Directive 2014/34/EU). IECEx is the international certification scheme built on the IEC 60079 standards. Buyers outside Europe often ask for both: an IECEx certificate is the technical evidence most national regimes build on, while ATEX is the legal route into the European market.

For the IS-04Z1 both routes are in progress with TÜV in Germany and neither certificate has been issued. The marking target is II 2 G Ex ib IIC T4: equipment Group II (surface industry), Category 2 (Zone 1 gas), protection concept ib (intrinsic safety), gas group IIC and temperature class T4.

In US LNG export terminals. US LNG export terminals are built and run by international developers, EPCs and shipping interests, who commonly expect certificate evidence that works across jurisdictions. A dual ATEX and IECEx route is designed to meet that expectation. For the IS-04Z1 certification is in progress and no certificate has been issued.

Frequently asked questions

Can we buy an ATEX and IECEx mobile phone in US LNG Terminals today?

Not today. Until the certificates are issued the IS-04Z1 stays out of classified areas, and until launch there is nothing to purchase. You can ask for the datasheet and the certification schedule, or book a safe-area evaluation unit.

What does an ATEX and IECEx mobile phone in US LNG Terminals cost?

There is no list price yet. SAMS gives indicative pre-launch pricing in writing, against a stated quantity and site, and does not commit to a lead time before certification completes.

Is the IS-04Z1 ATEX and IECEx certified?

Not yet. Both routes are in progress with TÜV in Germany and no certificate has been issued. Any listing that says otherwise is wrong.

Which areas at US LNG export terminals would use it?

Likely candidates are liquefaction trains, lng storage tanks, ship loading berths, gas compression and pipelines. Which areas are classified, and to what zone, is set by the site operator's own area classification drawings.

Is intrinsically safe the same as explosion proof?

Not exactly. Explosion proof is a broad everyday term; intrinsic safety is one protection concept, which limits energy so ignition cannot occur. Flameproof (Ex d) is a different concept that contains an explosion. The IS-04Z1 is intrinsically safe by design.

Are LNG terminals busy all year?

Export terminals run continuously, with ships loading regularly and trains shut down only for planned maintenance. Teams visiting for service or inspection need to coordinate with operations and book access well ahead.

How do hurricanes affect Gulf Coast LNG sites?

Several US LNG sites named in federal filings, including Sabine Pass, Cameron and Corpus Christi, are on the Gulf Coast, where storm season makes shutdown and evacuation planning normal. Keep spares and agree storm-season support arrangements with the operator.

Does the same device record work for every US terminal?

Not automatically. Each terminal has its own classification drawings and approval process, so a marking accepted at one may need separate review at another. The IS-04Z1 target is II 2 G Ex ib IIC T4, and certification is in progress. Ask each operator what documents it expects.
