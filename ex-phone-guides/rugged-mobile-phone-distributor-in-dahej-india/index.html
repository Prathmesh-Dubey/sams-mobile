The term · rugged

What “rugged” means for a smartphone

A rugged phone is built to survive water, dust, drops and rough handling, usually rated by an IP code and a drop specification. Ruggedness is an environmental property: it says nothing about whether a phone may be used where flammable gas or dust is present. That is decided by explosion-protection certification and marking.

The IS-04Z1 combines both: an IP68 sealed rugged build and a design for Zone 1 and Zone 21 (marking target Ex ib IIC T4). The explosion-protection certificates are in progress and none has been issued.

In Dahej, Gujarat. Dahej is a coastal site with hot, humid weather, salty air and monsoon rain, all of which wear on exposed equipment. IP68 sealing helps with water and dust, and a sealed housing suits corrosion-conscious buying. That rating is about ingress only, so Ex protection still depends on the marking and certification.

Which areas at Dahej, Gujarat would use it?

How big is the tidal range at Dahej and does it matter?

High water can rise 10 to 11 metres, which affects vessel movements, jetty operations and marine support work. Schedules usually follow tide tables, so build flexibility into plans for jetty visits.

What are the main hazardous areas at the Dahej LNG terminal?

LNG sites typically classify areas around storage tanks, vaporisation units, jetty loading arms and truck loading bays, because vapour can form a flammable mixture with air. The operator defines the zones, so request the drawings and accepted approvals before entering.

What should be checked for monsoon and coastal conditions at Dahej?

Check ingress rating, corrosion resistance of connectors and seals, and how the device is stored between shifts. Heavy rain, salty air and heat all matter. IP68 helps with water, but explosion protection comes only from the marking. Ask the operator about its portable equipment rules in wet weather.
