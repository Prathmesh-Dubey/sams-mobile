The term · ATEX and IECEx

What “ATEX and IECEx” means for a smartphone

ATEX is the European legal framework for equipment used in explosive atmospheres (Directive 2014/34/EU). IECEx is the international certification scheme built on the IEC 60079 standards. Buyers outside Europe often ask for both: an IECEx certificate is the technical evidence most national regimes build on, while ATEX is the legal route into the European market.

For the IS-04Z1 both routes are in progress with TÜV in Germany and neither certificate has been issued. The marking target is II 2 G Ex ib IIC T4: equipment Group II (surface industry), Category 2 (Zone 1 gas), protection concept ib (intrinsic safety), gas group IIC and temperature class T4.

In United Arab Emirates. A UAE procurement or HSE reviewer at ADNOC companies would expect certificate evidence that names the scheme, marking and ambient range, and a dual ATEX and IECEx route helps international contractors and EPCs working across Abu Dhabi and the northern emirates. The IS-04Z1 certification is in progress and no certificate has been issued.

Can we buy an ATEX and IECEx mobile phone in United Arab Emirates today?

No. Nothing can be bought or taken into a classified area today: certification is in progress and launch is planned for Q4 2026. What is available now is the datasheet, the written certification schedule and evaluation units for safe-area use.

What does an ATEX and IECEx mobile phone in United Arab Emirates cost?

How do I become a SAMS distributor for United Arab Emirates?

Apply through the distributor page with your company details and the territory you cover. SAMS reviews applications company by company and has not yet granted a territory for United Arab Emirates.

Do phones need TDRA approval in the UAE?

Yes. Telecom equipment needs TDRA type approval, valid for three years, with a compliance mark and supplier registration; no local testing is required. Confirm the latest rules per model.

How does the coastal environment affect equipment?

Salt pans along the coast can accelerate corrosion, and summer heat is intense inland and on the coast. Choose devices with corrosion-resistant housings, sealed connectors and high operating-temperature ratings, and rinse and dry them after exposure to salt air.

Can the handset be managed from our own console?

Android 16 with EMM and MDM support is designed to let IT enrol, restrict and update the unit from the operator's own console. Check compatibility with your specific EMM product during an evaluation, and agree the policy for contractors who return devices at demobilisation.
