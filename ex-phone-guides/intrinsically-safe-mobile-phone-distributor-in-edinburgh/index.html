The term · intrinsically safe

What “intrinsically safe” means for a smartphone

Intrinsic safety limits the electrical and thermal energy in a circuit so that neither a spark nor a hot surface can ignite a gas or dust atmosphere, even when a fault occurs. The ib level, used for Zone 1, tolerates one fault. It is the usual way to make a handheld device that a worker carries continuously through a hazardous area.

The IS-04Z1 is designed on this principle for Zone 1 and Zone 21 (marking target II 2 G Ex ib IIC T4). Intrinsic safety is a design basis, not a certificate: certification is in progress and none has been issued yet.

In Edinburgh, Leith and the Forth. Inspection rounds on a petrochemical site cover pumps, valves, flare and tank areas, often with many stops. A handheld designed for intrinsic safety lets an operator log readings and photographs on the spot. At Leith, much quayside work is not classified, so the benefit appears mainly for crews who then cross into Grangemouth.

Frequently asked questions

Can we buy an intrinsically safe mobile phone in Edinburgh today?

Not today. Until the certificates are issued the IS-04Z1 stays out of classified areas, and until launch there is nothing to purchase. You can ask for the datasheet and the certification schedule, or book a safe-area evaluation unit.

What does an intrinsically safe mobile phone in Edinburgh cost?

There is no list price yet. SAMS gives indicative pre-launch pricing in writing, against a stated quantity and site, and does not commit to a lead time before certification completes.

What does ‘intrinsically safe’ mean for the IS-04Z1?

The circuits are designed to limit energy so a spark or hot surface cannot ignite a gas or dust atmosphere, even with one fault (level ib, Zone 1). Certification of that design is in progress.

Which areas at Edinburgh, Leith and the Forth would use it?

Likely candidates are port and offshore wind foundation handling, petrochemical process units at grangemouth, fuel import and storage terminal at grangemouth, ethane import and storage. Which areas are classified, and to what zone, is set by the site operator's own area classification drawings.

How do I become a SAMS distributor for Edinburgh, Leith and the Forth?

Apply through the distributor page with your company details and the territory you cover. SAMS reviews applications company by company and has not yet granted a territory for Edinburgh, Leith and the Forth.

Is Edinburgh itself a hazardous-area worksite?

Mostly it is an administrative and financial centre, with limited heavy industry. Whisky, brewing, fuel storage and port activity exist in and around the Forth, and larger process sites are elsewhere in Scotland, so confirm the site and operator before assuming a classification.

How do I reach Edinburgh for supplier meetings or site work?

Edinburgh Airport lies within the green belt and the city has rail links across the Central Belt, so travel from other UK and European cities is straightforward. Plan equipment delivery by road freight and confirm gate procedures with each site.

Is Edinburgh itself a hazardous-area location?

Mostly no. The city has few classified areas, and the Port of Leith is being developed for offshore wind foundation handling. The petrochemical complex and fuel import terminal are at Grangemouth, up the Forth. Confirm with the site operator which area a given task falls into before choosing equipment.
