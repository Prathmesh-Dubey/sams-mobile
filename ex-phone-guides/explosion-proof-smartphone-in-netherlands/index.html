More about Netherlands that matters for portable equipment

Netherlands: who works there

Rotterdam has five port concessions, operated by separate companies under the overall authority of the port, so access, induction and device rules typically differ from terminal to terminal.

Netherlands: infrastructure

In 2020, 64.2% of Dutch power came from gas-fired thermal stations, and the last of fourteen gas power stations was commissioned in 2013, so gas plant maintenance is a recurring workload.

Netherlands: geography and layout

The Port of Rotterdam is Europe's largest seaport, covering 105 square kilometres over about 40 km, from the historic harbours through Botlek and Europoort to the reclaimed Maasvlakte on the North Sea.

Netherlands: logistics and access

The port of Amsterdam handled 63 million tonnes of cargo in 2023, is the second largest port in the Netherlands, and comprises 620 hectares of waterways and 1,995 hectares of land along the North Sea Canal.

The term · explosion proof

What “explosion proof” means for a smartphone

“Explosion proof” is the phrase most buyers use for equipment that may be taken into a classified hazardous area. Strictly, it covers several protection concepts: a flameproof enclosure (Ex d) contains an internal explosion, while intrinsic safety (Ex i) limits electrical energy so that an ignition cannot start. Phones are normally intrinsically safe, because a flameproof handset would be too heavy to carry all shift.

The IS-04Z1 uses intrinsic safety, with a marking target of Ex ib IIC T4, and is designed for Zone 1 and Zone 21. It is pre-launch, certification is in progress and no certificate has been issued.

In Netherlands. Dutch operators use explosieveilig as the everyday word, but the concepts differ. Flameproof Ex d suits pumps and motors, whereas the IS-04Z1 uses intrinsic safety, Ex ib. That is the concept that suits a handset on a Pernis refinery round, a Vopak tank terminal or a Chemelot cracker, where one person carries it through many areas.

Questions · Netherlands

Frequently asked questions

Can we buy an explosion proof smartphone in Netherlands today?

Not today. Until the certificates are issued the IS-04Z1 stays out of classified areas, and until launch there is nothing to purchase. You can ask for the datasheet and the certification schedule, or book a safe-area evaluation unit.

What does an explosion proof smartphone in Netherlands cost?

SAMS has not published a price. Any figure you are offered for an uncertified Ex phone should be treated with caution; written indicative pricing is available for a defined quantity and site.

Is the IS-04Z1 explosion proof or intrinsically safe?

It is intrinsically safe (marking target Ex ib IIC T4), which is the usual way to make a phone for hazardous areas. It is not a flameproof (Ex d) design, and certification is still in progress.

Which sites in Netherlands does this cover?

The market guide for Netherlands covers Pernis, Botlek, Europoort, Maasvlakte, among others. Site-level pages are linked below.

Is intrinsically safe the same as explosion proof?

Not exactly. Explosion proof is a broad everyday term; intrinsic safety is one protection concept, which limits energy so ignition cannot occur. Flameproof (Ex d) is a different concept that contains an explosion. The IS-04Z1 is intrinsically safe by design.

What is it like to work at a Dutch port site with handheld devices?

The Rhine-Meuse delta coast is windy and often wet, so check the water ingress rating, glove-friendly touch and cold-temperature battery performance. Terminals have separate induction processes, so plan device registration with each concession's HSE team in advance.

Which Rotterdam areas have the most hazardous-area activity?

Petrochemical and refinery sites are concentrated at Botlek and Europoort and in the area west of the city, with further bulk terminals at Maasvlakte. Exact classifications are held by each concession holder, so request the zoning drawings for your terminal.

Can the IS-04Z1 be used on sites where cameras are banned?

Camera policy is a site decision. Android 16 with EMM and MDM allows the camera, location services and apps to be restricted centrally, which lets a telecoms team show what is permitted. Hold the evidence for the site's security and HSE teams before bringing any device through the gate.
