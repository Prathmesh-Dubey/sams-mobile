Site conditions at Kandla (Deendayal Port), Gujarat: what wears and what limits equipment

Oil jetties

Wikipedia describes oil jetties on the western bank of Kandla creek handling products from LPG and chemicals to edible oils. Oil Jetty No. 8 at Old Kandla, rated at 3.5 million tonnes a year, was inaugurated on 26 May 2025.

Tank farms

Private and public tank farms sit near the port; Indian Oil reports crude storage at Kandla, and Parker Agrochem and Aegis rent liquid storage capacity. Several operators therefore work in one area.

The term · Ex-proof

What “Ex-proof” means for a smartphone

“Ex-proof” is shorthand for equipment carrying an explosion-protection marking (the Ex mark) that matches the area it enters. The marking states the protection concept, gas or dust group and temperature class, and it must be backed by a certificate issued by a recognised body, so a phone described as Ex-proof should come with a certificate number you can check.

The IS-04Z1 is designed to carry the marking II 2 G Ex ib IIC T4. That is a target: certification is in progress, no certificate has been issued, and SAMS will publish the certificate numbers when they exist.

In Kandla (Deendayal Port), Gujarat. A marking check at Kandla should consider the range of cargo, from LPG and chemicals to edible oils, and the areas at jetties, tank farms and loading gantries. LPG and chemicals can sit in different gas groups. The IS-04Z1 target marking is II 2 G Ex ib IIC T4, which the operator must compare with its classification.

How is Vadinar linked to Kandla?

When is the best time for marine work at Kandla?
