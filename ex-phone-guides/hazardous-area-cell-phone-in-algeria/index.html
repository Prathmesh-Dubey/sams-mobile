The term · hazardous area

What “hazardous area” means for a smartphone

A hazardous area is a place where flammable gas, vapour or combustible dust may be present. Standards divide these into zones: Zones 0, 1 and 2 for gas and vapour, and Zones 20, 21 and 22 for dust. Zone 1 and Zone 21 are the tier where an explosive atmosphere is likely to occur in normal operation, which is where the IS-04Z1 is designed to work. Anything carried in must be marked for the zone, gas group and temperature class of the area.

No certificate has been issued for the IS-04Z1 yet, so it must not be taken into a classified area until the certificate covering it exists and the site operator has accepted it.

In Algeria. Classified areas in Algeria fall into two families: the Saharan oil and gas fields and processing hubs at Hassi Messaoud, Hassi R'Mel and In Amenas, and the coastal LNG, refining and petrochemical complexes at Arzew and Skikda. The operator, whether Sonatrach or a joint venture, classifies these areas. SAMS does not, and the device is designed for Zone 1 and Zone 21.

Frequently asked questions

Can we buy a hazardous area cell phone in Algeria today?

Not yet. The IS-04Z1 is pre-launch and its certification is still in progress ahead of a Q4 2026 launch. No unit goes into a classified area before the certificate covering it exists. The datasheet, the certification schedule and safe-area evaluation units are available now.

What does a hazardous area cell phone in Algeria cost?

Which hazardous areas is the IS-04Z1 designed for?

Zone 1 and Zone 21 (gas and dust) with gas group IIC and temperature class T4, subject to certification. It must not be used in a classified area before its certificate is issued and the site has accepted it.

Which sites in Algeria does this cover?

The market guide for Algeria covers Hassi Messaoud, Ouargla, Hassi R'Mel, Laghouat, In Amenas, Illizi, In Salah, Tamanrasset, among others. Site-level pages are linked below.

Is intrinsically safe the same as explosion proof?

Not exactly. Explosion proof is a broad everyday term; intrinsic safety is one protection concept, which limits energy so ignition cannot occur. Flameproof (Ex d) is a different concept that contains an explosion. The IS-04Z1 is intrinsically safe by design.

How long does Algerian type approval typically take?

Providers report about ten weeks for processing and a certificate validity of five years if the device is unchanged, with a March 2026 decree allowing several importers to use one certificate. Treat these as typical figures and confirm the current timing and fees with ARPCE or an agent.

Which Algerian authority approves mobile phones and radios?

ARPCE is described as the main authority issuing type approval for equipment such as mobile phones and Wi-Fi devices, while ANF handles radio spectrum approvals for devices such as short-range radios. A local representative is needed. Confirm which body applies to your specific device before applying.

Will the battery and display cope with Saharan heat and distance from camp?

The 4,000 mAh battery is sized for a working day, and IP68 sealing is aimed at sand and dust. The certificate will state the ambient range, so check it against southern field summers. Away from camps the carrier signal can be patchy, so the handset falls back to plant Wi-Fi. Verify on a test unit.
