The term · intrinsically safe

What “intrinsically safe” means for a smartphone

Intrinsic safety limits the electrical and thermal energy in a circuit so that neither a spark nor a hot surface can ignite a gas or dust atmosphere, even when a fault occurs. The ib level, used for Zone 1, tolerates one fault. It is the usual way to make a handheld device that a worker carries continuously through a hazardous area.

The IS-04Z1 is designed on this principle for Zone 1 and Zone 21 (marking target II 2 G Ex ib IIC T4). Intrinsic safety is a design basis, not a certificate: certification is in progress and none has been issued yet.

In EGPC. Rounds at El-Nasr in Suez, Mostorod near Cairo and the other refineries involve reading gauges, photographing equipment and filing permits, with turnarounds adding extra crews. A handheld designed as intrinsically safe could carry those tasks on one device. The IS-04Z1 is designed for this use, though certification is still in progress.

Frequently asked questions

Can EGPC buy the IS-04Z1 today?

Not today. Until the certificates are issued the IS-04Z1 stays out of classified areas, and until launch there is nothing to purchase. You can ask for the datasheet and the certification schedule, or book a safe-area evaluation unit.

What does an intrinsically safe mobile phone for EGPC cost?

There is no list price yet. SAMS gives indicative pre-launch pricing in writing, against a stated quantity and site, and does not commit to a lead time before certification completes.

What does ‘intrinsically safe’ mean for the IS-04Z1?

The circuits are designed to limit energy so a spark or hot surface cannot ignite a gas or dust atmosphere, even with one fault (level ib, Zone 1). Certification of that design is in progress.

How does EGPC approve suppliers?

Supplier registration and equipment approval are run by the organisation itself; confirm current requirements with its procurement and HSE teams.

Is intrinsically safe the same as explosion proof?

Not exactly. Explosion proof is a broad everyday term; intrinsic safety is one protection concept, which limits energy so ignition cannot occur. Flameproof (Ex d) is a different concept that contains an explosion. The IS-04Z1 is intrinsically safe by design.

Which Egyptian sites typically contain Zone 1 areas?

Typical areas include refinery process units, tank farms and loading terminals on the Suez Gulf and Mediterranean coasts, oil and gas production facilities in the Western Desert and Gulf of Suez, and fuel stations. Classification is site-specific, so request area drawings from the operator.

How do suppliers get approved by EGPC companies?

Egyptian state-owned companies generally buy through registered-vendor lists and tenders, with technical review by the user department, and joint ventures may apply their international partner's system. There is no public approved list, so confirm the route with the relevant company's procurement office.

How should dust and heat be handled for refinery rounds in Egypt?

Check the device's stated operating temperature range, plan battery use for long shifts and keep chargers in a safe area. IP68 is designed to keep dust out but is not explosion protection. The IS-04Z1 is in certification. Confirm conditions with the refinery operator.
