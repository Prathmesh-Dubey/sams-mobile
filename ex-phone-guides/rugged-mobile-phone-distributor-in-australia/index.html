Field notes · Australia

More about Australia that matters for portable equipment

Australia: rules and approvals

Australia works on the IECEx scheme and the AS/NZS 60079 series, and the national ANZEx scheme is harmonised with IECEx, so IECEx-certified equipment can be used almost without separate national testing.

Australia: rules and approvals

The Australian Communications and Media Authority regulates the supply of radiocommunications equipment and made rule changes in 2023 to simplify and streamline that regulation.

Australia: rules and approvals

An ATEX marking alone is not automatically accepted, because ATEX is an EU directive that allows self-declaration for some categories, and such equipment usually needs a conformity assessment.

Australia: people and language

The Pilbara has more than 45,000 residents, most living in the western third in towns such as Port Hedland, Karratha and Wickham, while the eastern third is almost entirely desert.

The term · rugged

What “rugged” means for a smartphone

A rugged phone is built to survive water, dust, drops and rough handling, usually rated by an IP code and a drop specification. Ruggedness is an environmental property: it says nothing about whether a phone may be used where flammable gas or dust is present. That is decided by explosion-protection certification and marking.

The IS-04Z1 combines both: an IP68 sealed rugged build and a design for Zone 1 and Zone 21 (marking target Ex ib IIC T4). The explosion-protection certificates are in progress and none has been issued.

In Australia. Pilbara summers push site ambients past 45 degrees, and a haul-road vehicle adds drop, shock and vibration. IP68 sealing and a rugged build are designed for gloved use and that duty. Neither IP68 nor ruggedness gives explosion protection, and underground coal is a different regime, Group I, from the Group II surface certification targeted here.

Frequently asked questions

Can we buy a rugged mobile phone in Australia today?

Not today. Until the certificates are issued the IS-04Z1 stays out of classified areas, and until launch there is nothing to purchase. You can ask for the datasheet and the certification schedule, or book a safe-area evaluation unit.

What does a rugged mobile phone in Australia cost?

SAMS has not published a price. Any figure you are offered for an uncertified Ex phone should be treated with caution; written indicative pricing is available for a defined quantity and site.

How rugged is the IS-04Z1?

It is designed to IP68 sealing, with glove-friendly controls. Ruggedness is not explosion protection: that depends on the certificates, which are in progress.

Which sites in Australia does this cover?

The market guide for Australia covers Karratha and the Burrup Peninsula, Onslow, Western Australia, Darwin, Gladstone and Curtis Island, among others. Site-level pages are linked below.

How do I become a SAMS distributor for Australia?

Apply through the distributor page with your company details and the territory you cover. SAMS reviews applications company by company and has not yet granted a territory for Australia.

What conditions affect devices in the Pilbara?

The Pilbara is hot, dry and dusty, with long distances between sites and towns. Check the operating temperature range, dust ingress rating and battery life in heat, and confirm with the operator any site rules on permits, inductions and mobile devices in classified areas.

What is the Hazardous Area Verification Dossier?

It is the installation documentation expected under AS/NZS 60079.14, showing that the equipment, its certificate and its installation suit the classified area. Operators and inspectors look for it before energising equipment, so ask the site electrical team what must be supplied with portable and fixed equipment.

Will a handset cope with Pilbara heat and a long roster day?

The target marking is T4 with the ambient range to be fixed on the certificate, which is not yet issued. The 4,000 mAh battery is sized for a roster day with radio, camera and GNSS running. Check the certified range against your site's worst summer ambient and trial a unit before fleet orders.
