The term · explosion proof

What “explosion proof” means for a smartphone

“Explosion proof” is the phrase most buyers use for equipment that may be taken into a classified hazardous area. Strictly, it covers several protection concepts: a flameproof enclosure (Ex d) contains an internal explosion, while intrinsic safety (Ex i) limits electrical energy so that an ignition cannot start. Phones are normally intrinsically safe, because a flameproof handset would be too heavy to carry all shift.

The IS-04Z1 uses intrinsic safety, with a marking target of Ex ib IIC T4, and is designed for Zone 1 and Zone 21. It is pre-launch, certification is in progress and no certificate has been issued.

In Jazan City for Primary and Downstream Industries. Refinery process units, gasification and syngas areas all fall under the casual heading of explosion-proof. In practice the protection concept differs: flameproof Ex d for fixed equipment, and intrinsic safety for a handheld. The IS-04Z1 is designed for intrinsic safety, so state which concept each Jazan area needs.

Frequently asked questions

Can we buy an explosion proof smartphone in Jizan today?

Not yet. The IS-04Z1 is pre-launch and its certification is still in progress ahead of a Q4 2026 launch. No unit goes into a classified area before the certificate covering it exists. The datasheet, the certification schedule and safe-area evaluation units are available now.

What does an explosion proof smartphone in Jizan cost?

There is no list price yet. SAMS gives indicative pre-launch pricing in writing, against a stated quantity and site, and does not commit to a lead time before certification completes.

Is the IS-04Z1 explosion proof or intrinsically safe?

It is intrinsically safe (marking target Ex ib IIC T4), which is the usual way to make a phone for hazardous areas. It is not a flameproof (Ex d) design, and certification is still in progress.

Which areas at Jazan City for Primary and Downstream Industries would use it?

Likely candidates are refinery process units, gasification and syngas, hydrogen production, power generation. Which areas are classified, and to what zone, is set by the site operator's own area classification drawings.

How do I become a SAMS distributor for Jazan City for Primary and Downstream Industries?

Apply through the distributor page with your company details and the territory you cover. SAMS reviews applications company by company and has not yet granted a territory for Jazan City for Primary and Downstream Industries.

How is Jizan different from Saudi Arabia's Eastern Province for equipment planning?

It is on the humid Red Sea coast in the south-west, far from the Eastern Province hubs, so freight and service visits take longer. The industrial city is newer, with refinery, gasification and power plants, so check each plant operator's own approval rules.

Will environmental rules affect work near the Jazan industrial city?

Possibly. The downtown area of the development is planned beside mangroves and near the Farasan Islands, and the city has pursued LEED Platinum certification for its plan. Contractors should expect environmental conditions in permits; confirm with the Royal Commission and the plant operator.

Does hydrogen change the device requirement?

It can. Hydrogen production and syngas areas at Jazan may need a more demanding gas group than ordinary fuel areas. The operator sets the classification, so ask for the gas group on the area drawing. The IS-04Z1 target marking is IIC, but certification is still in progress and no certificate exists yet.
