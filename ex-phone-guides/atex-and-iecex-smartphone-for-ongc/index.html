The term · ATEX and IECEx

What “ATEX and IECEx” means for a smartphone

ATEX is the European legal framework for equipment used in explosive atmospheres (Directive 2014/34/EU). IECEx is the international certification scheme built on the IEC 60079 standards. Buyers outside Europe often ask for both: an IECEx certificate is the technical evidence most national regimes build on, while ATEX is the legal route into the European market.

For the IS-04Z1 both routes are in progress with TÜV in Germany and neither certificate has been issued. The marking target is II 2 G Ex ib IIC T4: equipment Group II (surface industry), Category 2 (Zone 1 gas), protection concept ib (intrinsic safety), gas group IIC and temperature class T4.

In ONGC. An ONGC procurement or HSE reviewer would expect certificate evidence before any handheld goes onto an offshore platform or into a gas processing plant such as Hazira or Uran. A dual ATEX and IECEx route also suits international contractors and EPCs bidding for work on Mumbai High, Assam or Cambay. No certificate has yet been issued for the IS-04Z1; certification is in progress.

Questions · ONGC

Frequently asked questions

Can ONGC buy the IS-04Z1 today?

No. Nothing can be bought or taken into a classified area today: certification is in progress and launch is planned for Q4 2026. What is available now is the datasheet, the written certification schedule and evaluation units for safe-area use.

What does an ATEX and IECEx smartphone for ONGC cost?

No price is published. While certification is in progress there is no list price and no committed lead time, and a supplier quoting a firm figure for an uncertified Ex handset is promising something it cannot stand behind. Indicative pre-launch pricing is issued in writing against a stated quantity and site.

Is the IS-04Z1 ATEX and IECEx certified?

Not yet. Both routes are in progress with TÜV in Germany and no certificate has been issued. Any listing that says otherwise is wrong.

How does ONGC approve suppliers?

ONGC buyers register through its e-tender portal (user ID obtained via the new-bidder access authorisation link), and bidders need a digital signature certificate; confirm current rules with ONGC.

Is intrinsically safe the same as explosion proof?

Not exactly. Explosion proof is a broad everyday term; intrinsic safety is one protection concept, which limits energy so ignition cannot occur. Flameproof (Ex d) is a different concept that contains an explosion. The IS-04Z1 is intrinsically safe by design.

How do monsoon and heat affect devices on Indian sites?

Western offshore and Gujarat sites are hot and humid, with a heavy monsoon season, while Assam is very humid and rainy. For handheld devices that means good sealing, corrosion resistance and thermal limits; ask the asset about its environmental requirements.

Where would intrinsically safe phones be used at ONGC?

Typical uses are offshore platforms off Mumbai, onshore well sites and group gathering stations in Assam and Gujarat, and the gas processing plants at Hazira and Uran. Classification is set per installation, so ask each asset's HSE team for area drawings and its rules on portable electronics.

Do I need to register with ONGC before offering a device?

ONGC buyers are reported to register through its e-tender portal, and bidders need a digital signature certificate. That process is separate from product certification. The IS-04Z1 has no certificate yet, so any approach should begin with the datasheet and a safe-area evaluation. Check current rules with ONGC procurement before bidding.
