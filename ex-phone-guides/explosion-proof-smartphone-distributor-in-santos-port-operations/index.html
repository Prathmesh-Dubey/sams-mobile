The term · explosion proof

What “explosion proof” means for a smartphone

“Explosion proof” is the phrase most buyers use for equipment that may be taken into a classified hazardous area. Strictly, it covers several protection concepts: a flameproof enclosure (Ex d) contains an internal explosion, while intrinsic safety (Ex i) limits electrical energy so that an ignition cannot start. Phones are normally intrinsically safe, because a flameproof handset would be too heavy to carry all shift.

The IS-04Z1 uses intrinsic safety, with a marking target of Ex ib IIC T4, and is designed for Zone 1 and Zone 21. It is pre-launch, certification is in progress and no certificate has been issued.

In Port of Santos. In the port, explosion-proof is used for liquid-bulk fuel and chemical terminals, tank farms, tanker piers with loading arms and LPG handling. Fixed gear may be flameproof Ex d; a handheld relies on intrinsic safety, as the IS-04Z1 is designed to do. Certification is pending.

Frequently asked questions

Can we buy an explosion proof smartphone in Santos Port Operations today?

No. Nothing can be bought or taken into a classified area today: certification is in progress and launch is planned for Q4 2026. What is available now is the datasheet, the written certification schedule and evaluation units for safe-area use.

What does an explosion proof smartphone in Santos Port Operations cost?

There is no list price yet. SAMS gives indicative pre-launch pricing in writing, against a stated quantity and site, and does not commit to a lead time before certification completes.

Is the IS-04Z1 explosion proof or intrinsically safe?

It is intrinsically safe (marking target Ex ib IIC T4), which is the usual way to make a phone for hazardous areas. It is not a flameproof (Ex d) design, and certification is still in progress.

Which areas at Port of Santos would use it?

Likely candidates are liquid-bulk fuel and chemical terminals, tank farms, tanker piers and loading arms, lpg handling. Which areas are classified, and to what zone, is set by the site operator's own area classification drawings.

How do I become a SAMS distributor for Port of Santos?

Apply through the distributor page with your company details and the territory you cover. SAMS reviews applications company by company and has not yet granted a territory for Port of Santos.

Which Santos port activities involve flammable hazards?

Liquid bulk terminals handling fuels, chemicals and ethanol, tank farms, pipelines and vessel loading arms typically have classified zones. Container yards are mostly non-hazardous, so ask each terminal for its area classification before specifying devices.

How does Santos affect the delivery of equipment to Brazil?

Santos is the main entry for imported goods, with about 30 percent of national trade by value in some months. Customs clearance, import duties and radio type approval take time, so plan lead time and use a local clearing agent.

Do all Santos terminals accept the same equipment?

Not necessarily. Terminals are operated by separate companies, such as Transpetro, Ultracargo, Vopak and Stolthaven, each with its own procedures. Confirm equipment approval and procurement requirements with each terminal operator before planning a fleet.
