Field notes · Oman

More about Oman that matters for portable equipment

Oman: geography and layout

Duqm, about 550 km from Muscat, hosts a Special Economic Zone with a refinery on 900 hectares and a port, and Duqm Airport opened in 2014.

Oman: industry on the ground

Petroleum Development Oman delivers most of the country's crude and gas, owned 60 percent by the government, 34 percent by Shell, 4 percent by TotalEnergies and 2 percent by Partex.

Oman: climate and equipment wear

Muscat has a hot arid climate with about 10 centimetres of rain a year, falling mostly from December to April, so heat rather than rain is the main stress.

Oman: rules and approvals

Oman's Telecommunications Regulatory Authority type approval is valid for four years, previously it had no expiry, and a local representative and compliance mark are required.

The term · IECEx

What “IECEx” means for a smartphone

IECEx is the international scheme for certifying equipment used in explosive atmospheres. A certificate of conformity is issued by an accredited certification body against a specific edition of the IEC 60079 standards and carries a schedule of conditions of safe use. It is not a legal requirement in most countries on its own, but many national schemes accept it as the technical basis.

The IECEx certificate for the IS-04Z1 is in progress and has not been issued. The marking target is Ex ib IIC T4, the intrinsic safety concept for Zone 1 gas with the hardest gas group (IIC) and a 135 °C temperature class.

In Oman. Oman combines a dominant national operator with international operators, such as Occidental Oman and CC Energy Development, plus visiting contractors. IECEx is the shared language between them. Ask for the certificate of conformity and its schedule of conditions, which fix the marking, ambient range and any special conditions of use. The programme is in progress.

Questions · Oman

Frequently asked questions

Can we buy an IECEx mobile phone in Oman today?

No. Nothing can be bought or taken into a classified area today: certification is in progress and launch is planned for Q4 2026. What is available now is the datasheet, the written certification schedule and evaluation units for safe-area use.

What does an IECEx mobile phone in Oman cost?

There is no list price yet. SAMS gives indicative pre-launch pricing in writing, against a stated quantity and site, and does not commit to a lead time before certification completes.

Does the IS-04Z1 hold an IECEx certificate?

No. The IECEx certificate is in progress and has not been issued. SAMS will publish the number when it exists.

Which sites in Oman does this cover?

The market guide for Oman covers Fahud, Yibal, Marmul, Qarn Alam, among others. Site-level pages are linked below.

How will SAMS supply Oman?

Through authorised distributors once certificates exist. Until then SAMS can send the datasheet and certification schedule and arrange safe-area evaluation units.

Do 3G phones still work in Oman?

No. Oman announced in July 2025 that it had completed the shutdown of 3G networks, upgrading to 4G and 5G. Choose devices with LTE support.

What approval do phones need in Oman?

The TRA issues type approval certificates, now valid for four years, with a local representative and a compliance mark required. A new self-declaration route for importers started in 2026, so confirm the current route for each model.

How does the Dhofar khareef affect equipment?

From June to September the monsoon leaves Salalah in persistent damp and fog, and Sohar and Sur are humid and salt laden all year. A rugged build and IP68 sealing are aimed at that, but IP68 is not explosion protection. Check the certified ambient range once published.
