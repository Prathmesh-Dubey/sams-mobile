The term · explosion proof

What “explosion proof” means for a smartphone

“Explosion proof” is the phrase most buyers use for equipment that may be taken into a classified hazardous area. Strictly, it covers several protection concepts: a flameproof enclosure (Ex d) contains an internal explosion, while intrinsic safety (Ex i) limits electrical energy so that an ignition cannot start. Phones are normally intrinsically safe, because a flameproof handset would be too heavy to carry all shift.

The IS-04Z1 uses intrinsic safety, with a marking target of Ex ib IIC T4, and is designed for Zone 1 and Zone 21. It is pre-launch, certification is in progress and no certificate has been issued.

In OQ. At Sohar, Mina Al Fahal and Salalah explosion-proof is everyday shorthand for different concepts. Flameproof Ex d protects fixed motors and enclosures, while intrinsic safety suits handheld phones. LPG extraction and storage bring a flammable gas hazard, so a portable device around those areas is judged on intrinsic safety, which the IS-04Z1 is designed around.

Questions · OQ

Frequently asked questions

Can OQ buy the IS-04Z1 today?

Not today. Until the certificates are issued the IS-04Z1 stays out of classified areas, and until launch there is nothing to purchase. You can ask for the datasheet and the certification schedule, or book a safe-area evaluation unit.

What does an explosion proof smartphone for OQ cost?

There is no list price yet. SAMS gives indicative pre-launch pricing in writing, against a stated quantity and site, and does not commit to a lead time before certification completes.

Is the IS-04Z1 explosion proof or intrinsically safe?

It is intrinsically safe (marking target Ex ib IIC T4), which is the usual way to make a phone for hazardous areas. It is not a flameproof (Ex d) design, and certification is still in progress.

How does OQ approve suppliers?

OQ reports more than 13,000 registered vendors and suppliers and has a Vendors page on its website; confirm current registration and equipment approval requirements with its procurement and HSE teams.

Is intrinsically safe the same as explosion proof?

Not exactly. Explosion proof is a broad everyday term; intrinsic safety is one protection concept, which limits energy so ignition cannot occur. Flameproof (Ex d) is a different concept that contains an explosion. The IS-04Z1 is intrinsically safe by design.

Which OQ locations most often need Zone 1 rated mobile devices?

Typically the refinery process areas at Sohar and Mina Al Fahal, petrochemical and polymer plants, the LPG and ammonia facilities at Salalah and the large crude and product terminals. OQ or the site operator issues the area classification, so request the hazardous-area drawing before specifying equipment.

How does the Duqm refinery joint venture affect equipment suppliers?

The Duqm Refinery and Petrochemical Complex is a joint venture between OQ and Kuwait Petroleum International, so contractors may deal with the joint venture company rather than OQ directly. Ask the project or operating team who issues site access and equipment approvals before shipping devices.

Does OQ's Duqm joint venture follow the same approval route?

Not necessarily. Duqm is a 50:50 joint venture, so its operating company may have its own procurement and HSE rules. Confirm the operating entity and its requirements. The IS-04Z1 has no certificate yet, and SAMS has appointed no distributor for Oman.
