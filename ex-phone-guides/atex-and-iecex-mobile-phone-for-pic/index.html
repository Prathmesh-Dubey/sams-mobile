The term · ATEX and IECEx

What “ATEX and IECEx” means for a smartphone

ATEX is the European legal framework for equipment used in explosive atmospheres (Directive 2014/34/EU). IECEx is the international certification scheme built on the IEC 60079 standards. Buyers outside Europe often ask for both: an IECEx certificate is the technical evidence most national regimes build on, while ATEX is the legal route into the European market.

For the IS-04Z1 both routes are in progress with TÜV in Germany and neither certificate has been issued. The marking target is II 2 G Ex ib IIC T4: equipment Group II (surface industry), Category 2 (Zone 1 gas), protection concept ib (intrinsic safety), gas group IIC and temperature class T4.

In PIC. A Petrochemical Industries Company reviewer would expect certificate evidence before a handheld enters polymer, aromatics or fertiliser plants. With joint ventures and international contractors involved, a dual ATEX and IECEx route lets one product answer both families of paperwork. The IS-04Z1 is in certification and no certificate has been issued.

Questions · PIC

Frequently asked questions

Can PIC buy the IS-04Z1 today?

No. Nothing can be bought or taken into a classified area today: certification is in progress and launch is planned for Q4 2026. What is available now is the datasheet, the written certification schedule and evaluation units for safe-area use.

What does an ATEX and IECEx mobile phone for PIC cost?

No price is published. While certification is in progress there is no list price and no committed lead time, and a supplier quoting a firm figure for an uncertified Ex handset is promising something it cannot stand behind. Indicative pre-launch pricing is issued in writing against a stated quantity and site.

Is the IS-04Z1 ATEX and IECEx certified?

Not yet. Both routes are in progress with TÜV in Germany and no certificate has been issued. Any listing that says otherwise is wrong.

How does PIC approve suppliers?

Supplier registration and equipment approval are run by the organisation itself; confirm current requirements with its procurement and HSE teams.

Is intrinsically safe the same as explosion proof?

Not exactly. Explosion proof is a broad everyday term; intrinsic safety is one protection concept, which limits energy so ignition cannot occur. Flameproof (Ex d) is a different concept that contains an explosion. The IS-04Z1 is intrinsically safe by design.

Do PIC joint ventures register suppliers separately?

Often yes. EQUATE and the other joint ventures run their own plants and procurement, while PIC itself is a Kuwait Petroleum Corporation subsidiary with its own vendor process. Check with each company's procurement team, as registration with one organisation does not normally entitle you to bid with the others.

What hazardous-area conditions do petrochemical units at Shuaiba usually involve?

Aromatics, olefins and styrene units handle flammable liquids and gases, so Zone 1 and Zone 2 areas with gas groups IIA to IIB, and in some cases IIC, are typical. Dusty polymer and fertiliser handling can add Zone 21 or 22 areas. Always request the classification drawing for the specific unit.

Are fertiliser plants treated like petrochemical plants for hazardous areas?

Not necessarily. Fertiliser plants can have different gases and dusts from polymer or aromatics units, and the operator's classification decides. The IS-04Z1 targets gas atmospheres only. Ask PIC's HSE team for the area drawings and the substances involved before judging any device.
