Field notes · Romania

More about Romania that matters for portable equipment

Romania: projects and investment

OMV Petrom completed an offshore campaign in the Lebăda Est and Lebăda Vest fields on the Histria block in the Black Sea in 2010, showing that offshore work is an established part of Romanian upstream activity.

Romania: who works there

Petrom was privatised in late 2004 and sold to the Austrian company OMV, the largest privatisation deal in Romania's history at the time, and has been named OMV Petrom since 2010.

Romania: geography and layout

Gas production is around 25 million cubic metres a day, with about 100 billion cubic metres of onshore reserves and an estimated 40 billion offshore, so both Carpathian-area fields and Black Sea sites matter.

Romania: infrastructure

Two reactors at Cernavodă generate about 18-20% of Romanian electricity, and the country also has substantial coal deposits and considerable hydroelectric capacity, so mines, dams and power plants all feature.

The term · ATEX and IECEx

What “ATEX and IECEx” means for a smartphone

ATEX is the European legal framework for equipment used in explosive atmospheres (Directive 2014/34/EU). IECEx is the international certification scheme built on the IEC 60079 standards. Buyers outside Europe often ask for both: an IECEx certificate is the technical evidence most national regimes build on, while ATEX is the legal route into the European market.

For the IS-04Z1 both routes are in progress with TÜV in Germany and neither certificate has been issued. The marking target is II 2 G Ex ib IIC T4: equipment Group II (surface industry), Category 2 (Zone 1 gas), protection concept ib (intrinsic safety), gas group IIC and temperature class T4.

In Romania. A Romanian procurement or HSE reviewer, particularly at the refineries around Ploiești and Brazi, would expect ATEX evidence tied to the EU framework, with the national transposition in view. The IS-04Z1 certification programme is in progress, so no certificate exists yet. Running IECEx alongside ATEX would also help international contractors and EPCs who arrive for a shutdown with their own approval paperwork.

Frequently asked questions

Can we buy an ATEX and IECEx smartphone in Romania today?

Not today. Until the certificates are issued the IS-04Z1 stays out of classified areas, and until launch there is nothing to purchase. You can ask for the datasheet and the certification schedule, or book a safe-area evaluation unit.

Is the IS-04Z1 ATEX and IECEx certified?

Not yet. Both routes are in progress with TÜV in Germany and no certificate has been issued. Any listing that says otherwise is wrong.

Which sites in Romania does this cover?

The market guide for Romania covers Ploiești and Brazi, Năvodari, Constanța, Vadu and Corbu, among others. Site-level pages are linked below.

Is intrinsically safe the same as explosion proof?

Not exactly. Explosion proof is a broad everyday term; intrinsic safety is one protection concept, which limits energy so ignition cannot occur. Flameproof (Ex d) is a different concept that contains an explosion. The IS-04Z1 is intrinsically safe by design.

How do Romanian winters affect Ex handheld devices?

Continental winters can bring sub-zero temperatures, which cut battery capacity and can make touchscreens harder to use with gloves. Check the lowest rated ambient temperature on the certificate and plan spare batteries and warm storage for outdoor work at pipeline stations and refineries.

Where is hazardous-area work concentrated in Romania?

Refineries, gas fields and compressor stations, Black Sea offshore fields, chemical works and power plants are the main examples. Five refineries remained by 2018 with spare capacity for export. Confirm site lists with each operator and ask for its zone drawings.

Can the IS-04Z1 keep a position fix at an isolated Romanian well site?

It is designed with four-constellation GNSS, 4G LTE and Wi-Fi so a lone worker at a well site or compressor station can be located and stay connected. Real coverage depends on the mobile network at the site, so test it on your own field route during an evaluation.
