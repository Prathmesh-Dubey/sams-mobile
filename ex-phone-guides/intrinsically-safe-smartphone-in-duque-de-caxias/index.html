Duque de Caxias: projects and investment

The Reduc delayed coking unit started operating in July 2008 after an investment of about one billion dollars, converting heavy feedstock into diesel and naphtha and producing petroleum coke.

Duque de Caxias: infrastructure

A natural-gas power unit of 63 MW has served the refinery since 1976, so the site generates part of its own electricity as well as processing fuels and lubricants.

Duque de Caxias: industry on the ground

The Duque de Caxias refinery (Reduc) has operated since 1961, ranks among Brazil's largest by crude capacity, and supplies about 80 percent of the country's lubricants while leading natural gas processing.

Duque de Caxias: geography and layout

The Duque de Caxias refinery complex covers about 13 square kilometres beside Rodovia Washington Luis in the Campos Eliseos district, making it a very large industrial site inside the Rio de Janeiro metropolitan area.

The term · intrinsically safe

What “intrinsically safe” means for a smartphone

Intrinsic safety limits the electrical and thermal energy in a circuit so that neither a spark nor a hot surface can ignite a gas or dust atmosphere, even when a fault occurs. The ib level, used for Zone 1, tolerates one fault. It is the usual way to make a handheld device that a worker carries continuously through a hazardous area.

The IS-04Z1 is designed on this principle for Zone 1 and Zone 21 (marking target II 2 G Ex ib IIC T4). Intrinsic safety is a design basis, not a certificate: certification is in progress and none has been issued yet.

In Duque de Caxias. Maintenance shutdowns of the coking and hydrotreating units are scheduled in 2026, and shutdown walkdowns need handhelds that can safely enter process units. Intrinsic safety suits those inspections. The IS-04Z1 is designed for such use and is awaiting certification.

Frequently asked questions

Can we buy an intrinsically safe smartphone in Duque de Caxias today?

Not yet. The IS-04Z1 is pre-launch and its certification is still in progress ahead of a Q4 2026 launch. No unit goes into a classified area before the certificate covering it exists. The datasheet, the certification schedule and safe-area evaluation units are available now.

What does an intrinsically safe smartphone in Duque de Caxias cost?

There is no list price yet. SAMS gives indicative pre-launch pricing in writing, against a stated quantity and site, and does not commit to a lead time before certification completes.

What does ‘intrinsically safe’ mean for the IS-04Z1?

The circuits are designed to limit energy so a spark or hot surface cannot ignite a gas or dust atmosphere, even with one fault (level ib, Zone 1). Certification of that design is in progress.

Which areas at Duque de Caxias would use it?

Likely candidates are refinery process units, tank farms and pipelines, loading and dispatch areas, biofuel (saf and renewable diesel) processing. Which areas are classified, and to what zone, is set by the site operator's own area classification drawings.

Is intrinsically safe the same as explosion proof?

Not exactly. Explosion proof is a broad everyday term; intrinsic safety is one protection concept, which limits energy so ignition cannot occur. Flameproof (Ex d) is a different concept that contains an explosion. The IS-04Z1 is intrinsically safe by design.

Is Duque de Caxias refinery work usually tied to shutdown cycles?

Large refineries run planned turnarounds, and Reduc is in Petrobras' revamp programme. Exact dates are set by the operator, so confirm shutdown windows, contractor induction rules and permit-to-work practice before scheduling visits with portable equipment.

Which Reduc areas typically need Ex-rated mobile equipment?

Process units, lubricant and gas processing areas, tank farms and loading points commonly contain classified zones. Each area's gas group and temperature class is defined in the refinery's classification drawings, so ask the safety department for them before specifying a handheld device.

Does the new biofuel processing change classification?

Possibly. SAF and renewable diesel processing is a different product stream from the original refinery units, so its area classification may differ. Do not assume one marking fits all. Ask Petrobras for the classification of the exact unit before choosing a device.
