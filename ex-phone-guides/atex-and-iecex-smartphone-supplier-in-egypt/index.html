More about Egypt that matters for portable equipment

Egypt: rules and approvals

The National Telecom Regulatory Authority must approve any equipment that uses radio frequencies or connects to public networks, including mobile phones, before it can be imported or sold in Egypt.

Egypt: projects and investment

Egypt has set a target of 42 per cent renewable electricity by 2030 and is building nuclear capacity at El Dabaa, so new classified-area work will include non-fossil projects too.

Egypt: industry on the ground

Apache, using assets acquired from BP in 2010, is the major operator in the Western Desert, often through joint ventures with EGPC such as Khalda Petroleum and Qarun Petroleum.

The term · ATEX and IECEx

What “ATEX and IECEx” means for a smartphone

ATEX is the European legal framework for equipment used in explosive atmospheres (Directive 2014/34/EU). IECEx is the international certification scheme built on the IEC 60079 standards. Buyers outside Europe often ask for both: an IECEx certificate is the technical evidence most national regimes build on, while ATEX is the legal route into the European market.

For the IS-04Z1 both routes are in progress with TÜV in Germany and neither certificate has been issued. The marking target is II 2 G Ex ib IIC T4: equipment Group II (surface industry), Category 2 (Zone 1 gas), protection concept ib (intrinsic safety), gas group IIC and temperature class T4.

In Egypt. Egyptian operators and EGPC work from an IECEx Certificate of Conformity, so a reviewer would ask for that first, with EOS conformity marking being pursued alongside. An ATEX certificate beside it helps international operators such as Eni and the LNG and EPC contractors at Idku and Damietta. Certification is in progress.

Questions · Egypt

Frequently asked questions

Can we buy an ATEX and IECEx smartphone in Egypt today?

No. Nothing can be bought or taken into a classified area today: certification is in progress and launch is planned for Q4 2026. What is available now is the datasheet, the written certification schedule and evaluation units for safe-area use.

What does an ATEX and IECEx smartphone in Egypt cost?

SAMS has not published a price. Any figure you are offered for an uncertified Ex phone should be treated with caution; written indicative pricing is available for a defined quantity and site.

Is the IS-04Z1 ATEX and IECEx certified?

Not yet. Both routes are in progress with TÜV in Germany and no certificate has been issued. Any listing that says otherwise is wrong.

Which sites in Egypt does this cover?

The market guide for Egypt covers Gulf of Suez, Zohr field, Idku, Damietta, among others. Site-level pages are linked below.

How will SAMS supply Egypt?

Through authorised distributors once certificates exist. Until then SAMS can send the datasheet and certification schedule and arrange safe-area evaluation units.

Where do Zone 1 areas typically arise in Egypt?

Typically at Western Desert and Gulf of Suez oil and gas facilities, Mediterranean offshore gas processing, LNG plants at Damietta and Idku, refineries and the petrochemical clusters near the Suez Canal. Ask each operator for its classification drawings before specifying equipment.

What approval does a mobile phone need in Egypt?

NTRA type approval is described as mandatory before telecom or wireless equipment can be imported or sold, usually through a local importer, with CE or FCC test reports and technical documents. Certificates are typically valid for a limited period such as a year. Confirm current rules with NTRA or an agent.

Will the camera be usable around LNG and export infrastructure?

Photography around LNG and export infrastructure runs on strict permit control. Android 16 with EMM and MDM lets camera policy be pushed centrally, so it can be limited per site. The device has a 50 MP camera, but use is subject to site rules. Check with the operator's security team.
