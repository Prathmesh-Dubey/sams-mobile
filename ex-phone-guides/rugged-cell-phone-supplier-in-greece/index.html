Field notes · Greece

More about Greece that matters for portable equipment

Greece: who works there

HelleniQ Energy, formerly Hellenic Petroleum, operates three refineries at Thessaloniki, Elefsina and Aspropyrgos with 57% of national refining capacity; Motor Oil Hellas holds the remaining 43%.

Greece: logistics and access

Greece imported about 496,600 barrels of oil a day against domestic production of 7,946, according to the cited 2011 data, so imported crude moves through port terminals and tank farms.

Greece: who works there

Motor Oil Hellas, headquartered in Marousi, operates the Corinth refinery, which gives it about 35% of Greek refining, and its naphtha complex of 22,000 barrels a day was completed in 2022.

Greece: industry on the ground

Petrochemicals, including plastics, PVC, polypropylene, aliphatic solvents, chlorine and sodium hydroxide, are produced at a unit forming part of the Thessaloniki refinery.

The term · rugged

What “rugged” means for a smartphone

A rugged phone is built to survive water, dust, drops and rough handling, usually rated by an IP code and a drop specification. Ruggedness is an environmental property: it says nothing about whether a phone may be used where flammable gas or dust is present. That is decided by explosion-protection certification and marking.

The IS-04Z1 combines both: an IP68 sealed rugged build and a design for Zone 1 and Zone 21 (marking target Ex ib IIC T4). The explosion-protection certificates are in progress and none has been issued.

In Greece. Greek refineries and yards sit in heat and salt air, close to the sea. IP68 sealing helps with water, dust and salt-laden spray ingress, and washdown. It is not explosion protection, which must come from certification and the Ex marking. Heat exposure and battery behaviour on hot days should be tested on a trial unit.

Frequently asked questions

Can we buy a rugged cell phone in Greece today?

Not today. Until the certificates are issued the IS-04Z1 stays out of classified areas, and until launch there is nothing to purchase. You can ask for the datasheet and the certification schedule, or book a safe-area evaluation unit.

What does a rugged cell phone in Greece cost?

There is no list price yet. SAMS gives indicative pre-launch pricing in writing, against a stated quantity and site, and does not commit to a lead time before certification completes.

How rugged is the IS-04Z1?

It is designed to IP68 sealing, with glove-friendly controls. Ruggedness is not explosion protection: that depends on the certificates, which are in progress.

Which sites in Greece does this cover?

The market guide for Greece covers Aspropyrgos, Elefsina, Agioi Theodoroi, Thessaloniki, among others. Site-level pages are linked below.

How will SAMS supply Greece?

Through authorised distributors once certificates exist. Until then SAMS can send the datasheet and certification schedule and arrange safe-area evaluation units.

Do Greek sites accept ATEX certified devices?

Greece is an EU member, so ATEX Directive 2014/34/EU applies and CE plus Ex marking is the normal basis for acceptance, with zoning under Directive 1999/92/EC. Refineries typically add their own permit-to-work and device registration. Ask the safety office for its current process.

Where are Greece's main refineries?

HelleniQ Energy runs refineries at Thessaloniki, Elefsina and Aspropyrgos, and Motor Oil Hellas operates the remaining capacity. These are the best-known large classified-area sites. Operators run their own contractor induction and device approval, so confirm requirements before arrival.

Will a handset cope with Greek summer heat and salt air?

The IS-04Z1 is designed with IP68 sealing for industrial use, but heat affects any battery. Ask for the datasheet's temperature range and test a unit on a real summer round near the coast. Site rules on charging and storing devices still apply.
