The term · industrial

What “industrial” means for a smartphone

“Industrial” describes a phone built for plant conditions: sealed, drop-resistant and manageable at fleet scale through Android Enterprise. It does not by itself mean the phone may enter a classified area. Only a device with a valid explosion-protection certificate and marking may do that.

The IS-04Z1 is designed to be both an industrial Android phone (IP68, 4,000 mAh battery, 50 MP camera, Android 16 with EMM and MDM support) and an intrinsically safe one. Its explosion-protection certificates are in progress and have not been issued.

In Paradip, Odisha. With moorings about 25 km offshore and the refinery several kilometres from the port, device fleets span several operational locations. MDM or EMM enrolment, which the IS-04Z1 supports, helps maintain control. Charging should be in safe areas such as control rooms and workshops.

Frequently asked questions

Can we buy an industrial smartphone in Paradip India today?

No. Nothing can be bought or taken into a classified area today: certification is in progress and launch is planned for Q4 2026. What is available now is the datasheet, the written certification schedule and evaluation units for safe-area use.

What does an industrial smartphone in Paradip India cost?

SAMS has not published a price. Any figure you are offered for an uncertified Ex phone should be treated with caution; written indicative pricing is available for a defined quantity and site.

Is the IS-04Z1 an industrial phone as well as an Ex phone?

Yes by design: IP68 sealing, a 4,000 mAh battery, a 50 MP camera and Android 16 with EMM and MDM support. The explosion-protection certificates are separate and still in progress.

Which areas at Paradip, Odisha would use it?

Likely candidates are refinery process units, crude receipt via single point moorings, polypropylene unit, product storage and loading. Which areas are classified, and to what zone, is set by the site operator's own area classification drawings.

How do I become a SAMS distributor for Paradip, Odisha?

Apply through the distributor page with your company details and the territory you cover. SAMS reviews applications company by company and has not yet granted a territory for Paradip, Odisha.

How do I reach Paradip?

The nearest commercial airport is Biju Patnaik International Airport, and Paradip is on National Highway 53, about 90 kilometres from Cuttack. Allow time for road transfer and gate passes.

Why is construction work still ongoing at Paradip?

A petrochemical complex including polypropylene, ethylene glycol and PX-PTA units is being added in stages. Construction contractors and operators may follow different access rules, so confirm with each.

How should cyclone and monsoon exposure influence device selection?

Plan for heavy rain, humidity and storm disruption, with secure storage and charging in safe indoor areas. Sealed IP68 housings help with water. Ask the operator about its severe-weather procedures for portable equipment and how devices are secured during warnings.
