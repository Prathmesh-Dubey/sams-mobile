The term · IECEx

What “IECEx” means for a smartphone

IECEx is the international scheme for certifying equipment used in explosive atmospheres. A certificate of conformity is issued by an accredited certification body against a specific edition of the IEC 60079 standards and carries a schedule of conditions of safe use. It is not a legal requirement in most countries on its own, but many national schemes accept it as the technical basis.

The IECEx certificate for the IS-04Z1 is in progress and has not been issued. The marking target is Ex ib IIC T4, the intrinsic safety concept for Zone 1 gas with the hardest gas group (IIC) and a 135 °C temperature class.

In Kuwait. Kuwait's refineries and the Al-Zour LNG import terminal draw on international EPCs and visiting contractors, and IECEx is the shared language between them. A buyer would ask for the certificate of conformity together with its schedule of conditions, which records the exact marking, ambient range and any special conditions of use. None is issued yet for the IS-04Z1.

Frequently asked questions

Can we buy an IECEx cell phone in Kuwait today?

Not yet. The IS-04Z1 is pre-launch and its certification is still in progress ahead of a Q4 2026 launch. No unit goes into a classified area before the certificate covering it exists. The datasheet, the certification schedule and safe-area evaluation units are available now.

What does an IECEx cell phone in Kuwait cost?

SAMS has not published a price. Any figure you are offered for an uncertified Ex phone should be treated with caution; written indicative pricing is available for a defined quantity and site.

Does the IS-04Z1 hold an IECEx certificate?

No. The IECEx certificate is in progress and has not been issued. SAMS will publish the number when it exists.

Which sites in Kuwait does this cover?

The market guide for Kuwait covers Ahmadi, Greater Burgan, Mina Al-Ahmadi, Mina Abdullah, among others. Site-level pages are linked below.

How do I become a SAMS distributor for Kuwait?

Apply through the distributor page with your company details and the territory you cover. SAMS reviews applications company by company and has not yet granted a territory for Kuwait.

What is the main field area in Kuwait?

Greater Burgan in the south-east, with Magwa and Ahmadi sub-fields, around Al Ahmadi. Refineries at Mina Al-Ahmadi and Mina Abdullah are in the same coastal belt south of Kuwait City.

Does extreme heat affect electronics?

Yes, summer highs average 42 to 46 degrees Celsius and record 54. Use devices rated for high ambient temperature, and avoid leaving them in vehicles or direct sun.

Can the IS-04Z1 cope with Kuwaiti summer heat and dust storms?

IP68 sealing is designed to keep dust out, and the build is rugged. Heat is a separate question: the certified ambient range and T4 class are fixed by the certificate, which is not yet issued. Compare the published range with your site maximum, and confirm with the operator before planning summer use.
