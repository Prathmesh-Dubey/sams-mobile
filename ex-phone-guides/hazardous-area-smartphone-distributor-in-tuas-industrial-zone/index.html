Field notes · Tuas

More about Tuas that matters for portable equipment

Tuas: geography and layout

Tuas is bounded by the Western Water Catchment to the north, Pioneer to the east and the Straits of Johor to the west, and it has six subzones including Tuas Bay and Tuas View.

Tuas: logistics and access

PSA plans to move its city terminals by 2027 and Pasir Panjang by 2040 into the Tuas Megaport, shifting Singapore's container operations to the west.

Tuas: infrastructure

The Malaysia-Singapore Second Link opened on 2 January 1998 via Tuas, and the AYE and PIE expressways connect Tuas to the rest of Singapore.

Tuas: background

Tuas was swamp and then a fishing village before being resettled in the 1970s and developed for industry, with land reclaimed off the coast from the 1980s and still expanding.

The term · hazardous area

What “hazardous area” means for a smartphone

A hazardous area is a place where flammable gas, vapour or combustible dust may be present. Standards divide these into zones: Zones 0, 1 and 2 for gas and vapour, and Zones 20, 21 and 22 for dust. Zone 1 and Zone 21 are the tier where an explosive atmosphere is likely to occur in normal operation, which is where the IS-04Z1 is designed to work. Anything carried in must be marked for the zone, gas group and temperature class of the area.

No certificate has been issued for the IS-04Z1 yet, so it must not be taken into a classified area until the certificate covering it exists and the site operator has accepted it.

In Tuas. Which Tuas tenants hold classified areas has not been verified individually, and the estate is far from uniformly hazardous. Areas arise at shipyard and drydock work, fuel and chemical handling and fabrication and coating shops. The tenant classifies them, not SAMS, so ask each one before assuming a zone.

Questions · Tuas Industrial Zone

Frequently asked questions

Can we buy a hazardous area smartphone in Tuas Industrial Zone today?

Not yet. The IS-04Z1 is pre-launch and its certification is still in progress ahead of a Q4 2026 launch. No unit goes into a classified area before the certificate covering it exists. The datasheet, the certification schedule and safe-area evaluation units are available now.

What does a hazardous area smartphone in Tuas Industrial Zone cost?

SAMS has not published a price. Any figure you are offered for an uncertified Ex phone should be treated with caution; written indicative pricing is available for a defined quantity and site.

Which hazardous areas is the IS-04Z1 designed for?

Zone 1 and Zone 21 (gas and dust) with gas group IIC and temperature class T4, subject to certification. It must not be used in a classified area before its certificate is issued and the site has accepted it.

Which areas at Tuas would use it?

Likely candidates are shipyard and drydock work, dangerous-goods chemical blending and storage, fabrication and coating shops. Which areas are classified, and to what zone, is set by the site operator's own area classification drawings.

How do I become a SAMS distributor for Tuas?

Apply through the distributor page with your company details and the territory you cover. SAMS reviews applications company by company and has not yet granted a territory for Tuas.

What hazardous areas are found in Tuas?

Mostly shipyards, chemical and fuel handling sites, a renewable diesel plant and waste-to-energy facilities. Classified areas are limited to specific plants rather than the whole zone, so ask each tenant for its area classification.

Is Tuas Port expected to change access and logistics?

Yes. The port will grow in phases through the 2040s and PSA plans to relocate city terminals into it, so traffic and construction will increase. Contractors should check gate and escort rules with the host site.

Is all of Tuas treated as a hazardous area?

No. Classified areas arise mainly at shipyards, fuel and chemical handling and process plants, and which tenants have them has not been verified individually. Ask each tenant for its area classification, and confirm with the site operator.
