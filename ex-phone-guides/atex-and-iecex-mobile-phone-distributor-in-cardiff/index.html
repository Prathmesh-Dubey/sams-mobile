The term · ATEX and IECEx

What “ATEX and IECEx” means for a smartphone

ATEX is the European legal framework for equipment used in explosive atmospheres (Directive 2014/34/EU). IECEx is the international certification scheme built on the IEC 60079 standards. Buyers outside Europe often ask for both: an IECEx certificate is the technical evidence most national regimes build on, while ATEX is the legal route into the European market.

For the IS-04Z1 both routes are in progress with TÜV in Germany and neither certificate has been issued. The marking target is II 2 G Ex ib IIC T4: equipment Group II (surface industry), Category 2 (Zone 1 gas), protection concept ib (intrinsic safety), gas group IIC and temperature class T4.

In Cardiff and Roath Dock. An HSE-minded reviewer in Cardiff would ask for ATEX-style evidence because the employer's DSEAR assessment drives equipment choice at the Roath Dock fuel terminal. A dual ATEX and IECEx route, both in progress for SAMS, would also help visiting inspection contractors who carry international paperwork. Neither certificate has been issued, and nothing should be specified from this page.

Frequently asked questions

Can we buy an ATEX and IECEx mobile phone in Cardiff today?

Not today. Until the certificates are issued the IS-04Z1 stays out of classified areas, and until launch there is nothing to purchase. You can ask for the datasheet and the certification schedule, or book a safe-area evaluation unit.

What does an ATEX and IECEx mobile phone in Cardiff cost?

SAMS has not published a price. Any figure you are offered for an uncertified Ex phone should be treated with caution; written indicative pricing is available for a defined quantity and site.

Is the IS-04Z1 ATEX and IECEx certified?

Not yet. Both routes are in progress with TÜV in Germany and no certificate has been issued. Any listing that says otherwise is wrong.

Which areas at Cardiff and Roath Dock would use it?

Likely candidates are fuel storage and distribution terminal, marine jetty and tanker discharge, road tanker loading. Which areas are classified, and to what zone, is set by the site operator's own area classification drawings.

How do I become a SAMS distributor for Cardiff and Roath Dock?

Apply through the distributor page with your company details and the territory you cover. SAMS reviews applications company by company and has not yet granted a territory for Cardiff and Roath Dock.

Do UK hazardous-area rules apply in Wales?

Yes. Health and safety law, including the Dangerous Substances and Explosive Atmospheres Regulations, applies across Great Britain, and the Health and Safety Executive regulates it. Employers must classify areas and control ignition sources, so ask the site for its hazardous-area register.

Is Welsh language support needed for site documents in Cardiff?

Rarely for industrial safety documents, which are normally in English, though public bodies provide Welsh versions and more than one in eight Cardiff residents speak Welsh. Check any contract or public-sector client requirements for bilingual materials.

Does Cardiff have a refinery that would need Ex equipment?

No. Fuel arrives at Roath Dock by coastal tanker from Pembroke, so Zone 1 work in Cardiff arises at fuel handling and storage rather than refining. Wales' refining is at Pembroke. A buyer wanting refinery-scale demand should look to the Milford Haven Waterway instead.
