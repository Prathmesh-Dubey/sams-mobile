The term · hazardous area

What “hazardous area” means for a smartphone

A hazardous area is a place where flammable gas, vapour or combustible dust may be present. Standards divide these into zones: Zones 0, 1 and 2 for gas and vapour, and Zones 20, 21 and 22 for dust. Zone 1 and Zone 21 are the tier where an explosive atmosphere is likely to occur in normal operation, which is where the IS-04Z1 is designed to work. Anything carried in must be marked for the zone, gas group and temperature class of the area.

No certificate has been issued for the IS-04Z1 yet, so it must not be taken into a classified area until the certificate covering it exists and the site operator has accepted it.

In London and the Thames. London itself has no refinery. The classified areas are at Thames-side fuel terminals, namely Thames Oilport on the former Coryton site and West Thurrock in Essex, with marine jetties, road tanker loading and gas network sites. The terminals lie on the estuary in Essex, not inside Greater London, and the operators classify the zones.

Frequently asked questions

Can we buy a hazardous area mobile phone in London today?

Not today. Until the certificates are issued the IS-04Z1 stays out of classified areas, and until launch there is nothing to purchase. You can ask for the datasheet and the certification schedule, or book a safe-area evaluation unit.

What does a hazardous area mobile phone in London cost?

There is no list price yet. SAMS gives indicative pre-launch pricing in writing, against a stated quantity and site, and does not commit to a lead time before certification completes.

Which hazardous areas is the IS-04Z1 designed for?

Zone 1 and Zone 21 (gas and dust) with gas group IIC and temperature class T4, subject to certification. It must not be used in a classified area before its certificate is issued and the site has accepted it.

Which areas at London and the Thames would use it?

Likely candidates are fuel storage terminals, marine jetties, road tanker loading, gas network sites. Which areas are classified, and to what zone, is set by the site operator's own area classification drawings.

Is intrinsically safe the same as explosion proof?

Not exactly. Explosion proof is a broad everyday term; intrinsic safety is one protection concept, which limits energy so ignition cannot occur. Flameproof (Ex d) is a different concept that contains an explosion. The IS-04Z1 is intrinsically safe by design.

Does London's weather matter for handheld devices?

A temperate oceanic climate means mild temperatures but frequent light rain, on about 110 days a year. Typically devices used outdoors need good water ingress protection and users check ratings against site conditions, especially on the tidal Thames.

How should I plan equipment deliveries to London?

Deliveries typically arrive by road or air, and the congested metropolitan area calls for scheduled slots and extra time. Confirm customs treatment for imported electronics and any site access or gate checks with the client.

Why does travel matter for terminal work near London?

The terminals are in Essex, so crews and equipment normally travel by road from the capital, and site access follows each operator's induction and permit rules. Plan time for sign-in, safe-area handover and device checks. Confirm the current access arrangements with the terminal operator.
