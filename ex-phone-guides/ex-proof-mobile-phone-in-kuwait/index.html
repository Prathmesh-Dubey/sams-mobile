Field notes · Kuwait

More about Kuwait that matters for portable equipment

Kuwait: geography and layout

Kuwait covers 17,820 square kilometres, about 200 km north to south and 170 km east to west, with 195 km of coastline.

Kuwait: rules and approvals

Kuwait's Public Authority for Industry handles the safety registration route for electrical products, with a product registration that typically takes about a week.

Kuwait: rules and approvals

CITRA type approval for wired and wireless equipment accepts CE marking and EU test standards, needs no local representative, normally takes about six weeks and lasts three years.

Kuwait: climate and equipment wear

Summer daily highs average 42 to 46 degrees Celsius and the record is 54 degrees at Mitribah in July 2016, so heat is a constant factor for outdoor equipment.

The term · Ex-proof

What “Ex-proof” means for a smartphone

“Ex-proof” is shorthand for equipment carrying an explosion-protection marking (the Ex mark) that matches the area it enters. The marking states the protection concept, gas or dust group and temperature class, and it must be backed by a certificate issued by a recognised body, so a phone described as Ex-proof should come with a certificate number you can check.

The IS-04Z1 is designed to carry the marking II 2 G Ex ib IIC T4. That is a target: certification is in progress, no certificate has been issued, and SAMS will publish the certificate numbers when they exist.

In Kuwait. A marking check in Kuwait starts with the target II 2 G Ex ib IIC T4 read against the area classification drawing. T4 matters in a country with some of the hottest shade temperatures on earth, and the Zone 21 design target is relevant to fertiliser and minerals handling at Shuaiba. Gas group and detection practice for the northern sour gas facilities belong to the operator's drawings.

Frequently asked questions

Can we buy an Ex-proof mobile phone in Kuwait today?

Not yet. The IS-04Z1 is pre-launch and its certification is still in progress ahead of a Q4 2026 launch. No unit goes into a classified area before the certificate covering it exists. The datasheet, the certification schedule and safe-area evaluation units are available now.

What does an Ex-proof mobile phone in Kuwait cost?

There is no list price yet. SAMS gives indicative pre-launch pricing in writing, against a stated quantity and site, and does not commit to a lead time before certification completes.

What Ex marking will the IS-04Z1 carry?

The marking target is II 2 G Ex ib IIC T4. It becomes a marking only when the certificate is issued; until then it is a design target.

Which sites in Kuwait does this cover?

The market guide for Kuwait covers Ahmadi, Greater Burgan, Mina Al-Ahmadi, Mina Abdullah, among others. Site-level pages are linked below.

Is intrinsically safe the same as explosion proof?

Not exactly. Explosion proof is a broad everyday term; intrinsic safety is one protection concept, which limits energy so ignition cannot occur. Flameproof (Ex d) is a different concept that contains an explosion. The IS-04Z1 is intrinsically safe by design.

What is the main field area in Kuwait?

Greater Burgan in the south-east, with Magwa and Ahmadi sub-fields, around Al Ahmadi. Refineries at Mina Al-Ahmadi and Mina Abdullah are in the same coastal belt south of Kuwait City.

Does extreme heat affect electronics?

Yes, summer highs average 42 to 46 degrees Celsius and record 54. Use devices rated for high ambient temperature, and avoid leaving them in vehicles or direct sun.

Can the IS-04Z1 cope with Kuwaiti summer heat and dust storms?

IP68 sealing is designed to keep dust out, and the build is rugged. Heat is a separate question: the certified ambient range and T4 class are fixed by the certificate, which is not yet issued. Compare the published range with your site maximum, and confirm with the operator before planning summer use.
