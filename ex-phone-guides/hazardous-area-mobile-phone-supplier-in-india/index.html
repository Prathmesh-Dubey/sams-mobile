Field notes · India

More about India that matters for portable equipment

India: geography and layout

The largest crude reserves are in the Western Offshore region at 37 per cent and Assam at 27 per cent, so both offshore platforms and remote north-eastern fields matter.

India: rules and approvals

Imports are applied for by an Indian service provider or distributor, and there is no separate PESO mark; the Ex marking and the approval letter show compliance.

India: industry on the ground

India imports 82 per cent of its oil needs, so import terminals, tank farms and coastal refineries are central to supply.

India: background

India's first oil deposits were found near Digboi in Assam in 1889, and its gas industry began in the 1960s with discoveries in Assam and the Mumbai High field.

The term · hazardous area

What “hazardous area” means for a smartphone

A hazardous area is a place where flammable gas, vapour or combustible dust may be present. Standards divide these into zones: Zones 0, 1 and 2 for gas and vapour, and Zones 20, 21 and 22 for dust. Zone 1 and Zone 21 are the tier where an explosive atmosphere is likely to occur in normal operation, which is where the IS-04Z1 is designed to work. Anything carried in must be marked for the zone, gas group and temperature class of the area.

No certificate has been issued for the IS-04Z1 yet, so it must not be taken into a classified area until the certificate covering it exists and the site operator has accepted it.

In India. Indian classified areas fall into two groups: the hydrocarbon chain, from coastal refineries to gas processing, LNG terminals and offshore platforms, and pharmaceutical plants where solvents classify rooms inside buildings. The operator classifies the areas, not SAMS. The device is designed for Zone 1 and Zone 21.

Questions · India

Frequently asked questions

Can we buy a hazardous area mobile phone in India today?

Not today. Until the certificates are issued the IS-04Z1 stays out of classified areas, and until launch there is nothing to purchase. You can ask for the datasheet and the certification schedule, or book a safe-area evaluation unit.

What does a hazardous area mobile phone in India cost?

No price is published. While certification is in progress there is no list price and no committed lead time, and a supplier quoting a firm figure for an uncertified Ex handset is promising something it cannot stand behind. Indicative pre-launch pricing is issued in writing against a stated quantity and site.

Which hazardous areas is the IS-04Z1 designed for?

Zone 1 and Zone 21 (gas and dust) with gas group IIC and temperature class T4, subject to certification. It must not be used in a classified area before its certificate is issued and the site has accepted it.

Which sites in India does this cover?

The market guide for India covers Jamnagar, Gujarat, Dahej and the Gujarat PCPIR, Hazira and Surat, Vadodara, Koyali, Ankleshwar, among others. Site-level pages are linked below.

How will SAMS supply India?

Through authorised distributors once certificates exist. Until then SAMS can send the datasheet and certification schedule and arrange safe-area evaluation units.

Is PESO approval needed for Ex phones in India?

PESO approval is required for Ex electrical apparatus at petroleum, gas cylinder and pressure vessel premises, and imports are applied for through an Indian service provider or distributor. Whether it applies to a given site depends on the licensing category, so ask the site's safety team and a PESO consultant.

Where does hazardous-area work concentrate in India?

Typically the large coastal refineries such as Jamnagar, offshore platforms in the Western Offshore region, petrochemical and fertiliser complexes, and gas fields in Assam and Gujarat. Each operator issues its own area classification, so request the drawing, gas group and temperature class first.

Which Indian approvals apply to this handset?

The market guide lists PESO, BIS and WPC approvals as in progress, alongside IECEx. None has been granted yet. Which are needed for a given site depends on the operator and the regulator, so ask the site operator and check the current status before ordering.
