The term · IECEx

What “IECEx” means for a smartphone

IECEx is the international scheme for certifying equipment used in explosive atmospheres. A certificate of conformity is issued by an accredited certification body against a specific edition of the IEC 60079 standards and carries a schedule of conditions of safe use. It is not a legal requirement in most countries on its own, but many national schemes accept it as the technical basis.

The IECEx certificate for the IS-04Z1 is in progress and has not been issued. The marking target is Ex ib IIC T4, the intrinsic safety concept for Zone 1 gas with the hardest gas group (IIC) and a 135 °C temperature class.

In Pioneer, Jurong Industrial Estate. Shipyards in the Pioneer area serve international vessel owners, so IECEx is a convenient reference for gas-free and coating work. A certificate of conformity and schedule of conditions would be reviewed. For the IS-04Z1 these remain targets, as certification is in progress.

Frequently asked questions

Can we buy an IECEx mobile phone in Pioneer Industrial Area today?

Not yet. The IS-04Z1 is pre-launch and its certification is still in progress ahead of a Q4 2026 launch. No unit goes into a classified area before the certificate covering it exists. The datasheet, the certification schedule and safe-area evaluation units are available now.

What does an IECEx mobile phone in Pioneer Industrial Area cost?

SAMS has not published a price. Any figure you are offered for an uncertified Ex phone should be treated with caution; written indicative pricing is available for a defined quantity and site.

Does the IS-04Z1 hold an IECEx certificate?

No. The IECEx certificate is in progress and has not been issued. SAMS will publish the number when it exists.

Which areas at Pioneer, Jurong Industrial Estate would use it?

Likely candidates are shipyard and marine work areas, paint and coating shops, fabrication yards. Which areas are classified, and to what zone, is set by the site operator's own area classification drawings.

How do I become a SAMS distributor for Pioneer, Jurong Industrial Estate?

Apply through the distributor page with your company details and the territory you cover. SAMS reviews applications company by company and has not yet granted a territory for Pioneer, Jurong Industrial Estate.

Do all three Seatrium yards in this belt share the same access rules?

Not necessarily. Each yard has its own address, switchboard and gate, and contractor induction and permit steps are normally run per yard and per project. Ask your sponsor for the rules for the specific yard and the vessel you will board.

What kind of vessels are repaired near Pioneer?

Seatrium describes repair and upgrade work on LNG and LPG carriers, floating production units, jack-up rigs, tankers, container ships and heavy-lift vessels. Gas carriers and floating production units generally bring the strictest hazardous-area controls.

Is every yard in Pioneer classified the same way?

No. Each tenant has a different hazard profile, and classification is set tenant by tenant. A coating shop, a dry dock and a fabrication yard can differ. Confirm with the site operator which areas, if any, are classified before choosing a handheld for the task.
