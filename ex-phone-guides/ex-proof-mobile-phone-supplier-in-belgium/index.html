Field notes · Belgium

More about Belgium that matters for portable equipment

Belgium: industry on the ground

Chemicals, pharmaceuticals, petroleum products, metals and motor vehicle assembly feature among Belgium's industrial base, and chemicals are also a leading export, which means many process plants with flammable solvents and gases.

Belgium: infrastructure

Fluxys is the main natural gas transmission operator, and gas imports came mainly from the Netherlands and Norway in 2021, so compressor and metering stations are part of the classified-area network.

Belgium: people and language

Dutch-speaking Flanders and French-speaking Wallonia have different industrial histories and languages, and Belgium has a multilingual, well-trained workforce, so Dutch and French documents are often requested.

Belgium: geography and layout

Industry is concentrated in Flanders in the north, around Brussels, and along the Sillon industriel through Liège and Charleroi, so hazardous-area work is split between a coastal north and an older steel and chemicals south.

The term · Ex-proof

What “Ex-proof” means for a smartphone

“Ex-proof” is shorthand for equipment carrying an explosion-protection marking (the Ex mark) that matches the area it enters. The marking states the protection concept, gas or dust group and temperature class, and it must be backed by a certificate issued by a recognised body, so a phone described as Ex-proof should come with a certificate number you can check.

The IS-04Z1 is designed to carry the marking II 2 G Ex ib IIC T4. That is a target: certification is in progress, no certificate has been issued, and SAMS will publish the certificate numbers when they exist.

In Belgium. A marking check on the Scheldelaan or the left bank would focus on Group IIC, because IIC includes hydrogen and steam crackers, industrial gas networks and new capacity make it the sensible assumption. The target is II 2 G Ex ib IIC T4, with Zone 21 dust under the same programme and a stated ambient range.

Questions · Belgium

Frequently asked questions

Can we buy an Ex-proof mobile phone in Belgium today?

No. Nothing can be bought or taken into a classified area today: certification is in progress and launch is planned for Q4 2026. What is available now is the datasheet, the written certification schedule and evaluation units for safe-area use.

What does an Ex-proof mobile phone in Belgium cost?

No price is published. While certification is in progress there is no list price and no committed lead time, and a supplier quoting a firm figure for an uncertified Ex handset is promising something it cannot stand behind. Indicative pre-launch pricing is issued in writing against a stated quantity and site.

What Ex marking will the IS-04Z1 carry?

The marking target is II 2 G Ex ib IIC T4. It becomes a marking only when the certificate is issued; until then it is a design target.

Which sites in Belgium does this cover?

The market guide for Belgium covers Antwerp right bank, Antwerp left bank, Zeebrugge, Ghent canal zone, among others. Site-level pages are linked below.

How will SAMS supply Belgium?

Through authorised distributors once certificates exist. Until then SAMS can send the datasheet and certification schedule and arrange safe-area evaluation units.

Do Belgian plants require ATEX certified phones?

Belgium is an EU member, so ATEX Directive 2014/34/EU applies to equipment placed on its market, and employers zone workplaces under Directive 1999/92/EC. Zone 1 and Zone 2 devices should carry the Ex and CE markings. Confirm the exact accepted categories with each site operator.

What documentation language is usual for Belgian industrial sites?

Dutch in Flanders, French in Wallonia and sometimes English at international chemical sites. Instructions are typically expected in the user's language for ATEX equipment, so ask the operator which language it expects. Bilingual documentation packs can avoid delays at gate control.

Will an ordinary phone policy at the Seveso gate apply to this device?

Camera control is why ordinary phones are refused at the gate on Seveso sites. Android 16 with EMM and MDM lets the camera, location services and application list be set centrally, so permissions can be shown during evaluation. Whether a site accepts it still depends on its own rules. Confirm with the operator before taking a unit in.
