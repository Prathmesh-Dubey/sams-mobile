Field notes · PTT

More about PTT that matters for portable equipment

PTT: projects and investment

In 2012 PTT Exploration and Production took over Cove Energy, which held an 8.5% share in a large offshore natural gas field in Mozambique, extending PTT's offshore gas work abroad.

PTT: background

PTT was established on 29 December 1978 as the Petroleum Authority of Thailand, and was corporatised and re-registered as PTT Public Company Limited on 1 October 2001, listing on the Stock Exchange of Thailand that December.

PTT: geography and layout

The headquarters is on Vibhavadi Rangsit Road in Chatuchak, Bangkok, while its key assets are offshore in the Gulf of Thailand and at industrial sites, so site contacts are separate from head office.

PTT: industry on the ground

PTT opened the Bangchak oil refinery in Bangkok in 1985 and built a nationwide network of fuel distribution outlets, adding refinery and retail forecourt areas to its estate.

The term · ATEX and IECEx

What “ATEX and IECEx” means for a smartphone

ATEX is the European legal framework for equipment used in explosive atmospheres (Directive 2014/34/EU). IECEx is the international certification scheme built on the IEC 60079 standards. Buyers outside Europe often ask for both: an IECEx certificate is the technical evidence most national regimes build on, while ATEX is the legal route into the European market.

For the IS-04Z1 both routes are in progress with TÜV in Germany and neither certificate has been issued. The marking target is II 2 G Ex ib IIC T4: equipment Group II (surface industry), Category 2 (Zone 1 gas), protection concept ib (intrinsic safety), gas group IIC and temperature class T4.

In PTT. A PTT reviewer would expect certificate evidence that names the model and the marking for its gas separation plants, pipelines and receiving facilities. International contractors and group-company projects may carry ATEX or IECEx papers, so a dual route helps comparison. The IS-04Z1 programme is in progress and no certificate has been issued.

Frequently asked questions

Can PTT buy the IS-04Z1 today?

Not today. Until the certificates are issued the IS-04Z1 stays out of classified areas, and until launch there is nothing to purchase. You can ask for the datasheet and the certification schedule, or book a safe-area evaluation unit.

What does an ATEX and IECEx mobile phone for PTT cost?

SAMS has not published a price. Any figure you are offered for an uncertified Ex phone should be treated with caution; written indicative pricing is available for a defined quantity and site.

Is the IS-04Z1 ATEX and IECEx certified?

Not yet. Both routes are in progress with TÜV in Germany and no certificate has been issued. Any listing that says otherwise is wrong.

How does PTT approve suppliers?

Supplier registration and equipment approval are run by the organisation itself; confirm current requirements with its procurement and HSE teams.

Is intrinsically safe the same as explosion proof?

Not exactly. Explosion proof is a broad everyday term; intrinsic safety is one protection concept, which limits energy so ignition cannot occur. Flameproof (Ex d) is a different concept that contains an explosion. The IS-04Z1 is intrinsically safe by design.

Which PTT group sites typically need intrinsically safe handhelds?

Typical sites include offshore production platforms and gas receiving terminals, LPG terminals, refineries, petrochemical complexes and fuel stations. Zoning is facility-specific, so ask the operating subsidiary's HSE team for area classification drawings and its portable-electronics rules before specifying devices.

Is PTT or a subsidiary the right buyer for Ex equipment?

Because most employees and operations sit in subsidiaries such as PTT Exploration and Production or PTT Global Chemical, each often runs its own procurement and HSE approval. Identify the operating company for the site and confirm its supplier registration and certification requirements.

Does approval at PTT cover its group companies?

Not necessarily. The sheet notes that refining and petrochemicals are run by group companies, not PTT itself, and that supplier registration and equipment approval are run by the organisation. Confirm which entity applies with its procurement and HSE teams. The IS-04Z1 has no certificate yet.
