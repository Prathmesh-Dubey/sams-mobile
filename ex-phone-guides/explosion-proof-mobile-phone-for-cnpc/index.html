The term · explosion proof

What “explosion proof” means for a smartphone

“Explosion proof” is the phrase most buyers use for equipment that may be taken into a classified hazardous area. Strictly, it covers several protection concepts: a flameproof enclosure (Ex d) contains an internal explosion, while intrinsic safety (Ex i) limits electrical energy so that an ignition cannot start. Phones are normally intrinsically safe, because a flameproof handset would be too heavy to carry all shift.

The IS-04Z1 uses intrinsic safety, with a marking target of Ex ib IIC T4, and is designed for Zone 1 and Zone 21. It is pre-launch, certification is in progress and no certificate has been issued.

In CNPC. At oil and gas production facilities and refineries, 'explosion-proof' is everyday shorthand for different concepts. Flameproof Ex d protects fixed motors and enclosures, while intrinsic safety limits the energy of a portable device. A phone used on field or plant rounds is a handheld, so the IS-04Z1 is designed around intrinsic safety.

Questions · CNPC

Frequently asked questions

Can CNPC buy the IS-04Z1 today?

Not yet. The IS-04Z1 is pre-launch and its certification is still in progress ahead of a Q4 2026 launch. No unit goes into a classified area before the certificate covering it exists. The datasheet, the certification schedule and safe-area evaluation units are available now.

What does an explosion proof mobile phone for CNPC cost?

No price is published. While certification is in progress there is no list price and no committed lead time, and a supplier quoting a firm figure for an uncertified Ex handset is promising something it cannot stand behind. Indicative pre-launch pricing is issued in writing against a stated quantity and site.

Is the IS-04Z1 explosion proof or intrinsically safe?

It is intrinsically safe (marking target Ex ib IIC T4), which is the usual way to make a phone for hazardous areas. It is not a flameproof (Ex d) design, and certification is still in progress.

How does CNPC approve suppliers?

Supplier registration and equipment approval are run by the organisation itself; confirm current requirements with its procurement and HSE teams.

Is intrinsically safe the same as explosion proof?

Not exactly. Explosion proof is a broad everyday term; intrinsic safety is one protection concept, which limits energy so ignition cannot occur. Flameproof (Ex d) is a different concept that contains an explosion. The IS-04Z1 is intrinsically safe by design.

How do northern Chinese oilfields affect device choice?

Fields in northern and north-western China can see very cold winters and dusty, dry summers, so battery performance in cold, dust ingress protection and screen visibility in glare matter. Ask the oilfield for its operating temperature limits and any site restrictions on portable electronics.

What explosion-protection certificate do Chinese sites normally require?

China has its own explosion-protection certification scheme and marking, and operators usually expect equipment to carry the Chinese certificate in addition to any international ones. Confirm with the site and its certification body which approvals they accept before offering handheld devices.

Is a handset suited to wide inland fields like Changqing?

It can be, if the marking matches the area classification, battery capacity covers long shifts and the fleet can be managed centrally. Dust and winter cold need checking. IP68 is not explosion protection. The IS-04Z1 is not yet certified, so confirm with the site operator.
