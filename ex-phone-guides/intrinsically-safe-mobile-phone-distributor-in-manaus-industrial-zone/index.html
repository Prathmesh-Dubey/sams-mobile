Field notes · Manaus Free Trade Zone

More about Manaus Free Trade Zone that matters for portable equipment

Manaus Free Trade Zone: industry on the ground

Suframa describes the Manaus Industrial Pole as about 600 high-tech industries in electronics, IT goods, two-wheelers, naval, mechanical, metallurgical and thermoplastics, with 2025 revenue of R$227.67 billion and over half a million jobs.

Manaus Free Trade Zone: infrastructure

Transport links include Eduardo Gomes International Airport and the Rio Negro Bridge, and the city sits on the Rio Negro, making river and air the main freight routes.

Manaus Free Trade Zone: background

The Manaus Free Trade Zone was formalised by Law 3.173 on 6 June 1957 and for its first ten years operated from a rented warehouse in the Port of Manaus.

Manaus Free Trade Zone: rules and approvals

Federal tax exemptions administered by SUFRAMA and SUDAM created the comparative advantage that attracted manufacturers to the zone, so supplier and import procedures follow its own administrative regime.

The term · intrinsically safe

What “intrinsically safe” means for a smartphone

Intrinsic safety limits the electrical and thermal energy in a circuit so that neither a spark nor a hot surface can ignite a gas or dust atmosphere, even when a fault occurs. The ib level, used for Zone 1, tolerates one fault. It is the usual way to make a handheld device that a worker carries continuously through a hazardous area.

The IS-04Z1 is designed on this principle for Zone 1 and Zone 21 (marking target II 2 G Ex ib IIC T4). Intrinsic safety is a design basis, not a certificate: certification is in progress and none has been issued yet.

In Manaus Free Trade Zone. Handheld use in Manaus would be mostly on fuel storage and loading rounds and at port petroleum and chemical handling, not on factory floors. An intrinsically safe device suits those limited areas. The IS-04Z1 is designed for this use once certification is complete.

Questions · Manaus Industrial Zone

Frequently asked questions

Can we buy an intrinsically safe mobile phone in Manaus Industrial Zone today?

Not today. Until the certificates are issued the IS-04Z1 stays out of classified areas, and until launch there is nothing to purchase. You can ask for the datasheet and the certification schedule, or book a safe-area evaluation unit.

What does an intrinsically safe mobile phone in Manaus Industrial Zone cost?

No price is published. While certification is in progress there is no list price and no committed lead time, and a supplier quoting a firm figure for an uncertified Ex handset is promising something it cannot stand behind. Indicative pre-launch pricing is issued in writing against a stated quantity and site.

What does ‘intrinsically safe’ mean for the IS-04Z1?

The circuits are designed to limit energy so a spark or hot surface cannot ignite a gas or dust atmosphere, even with one fault (level ib, Zone 1). Certification of that design is in progress.

Which areas at Manaus Free Trade Zone would use it?

Likely candidates are fuel storage and loading, refinery and fuel logistics, port petroleum and chemical handling. Which areas are classified, and to what zone, is set by the site operator's own area classification drawings.

How do I become a SAMS distributor for Manaus Free Trade Zone?

Apply through the distributor page with your company details and the territory you cover. SAMS reviews applications company by company and has not yet granted a territory for Manaus Free Trade Zone.

How does equipment usually reach Manaus?

By air through Eduardo Gomes International Airport or by river and road freight, since Manaus is far from the main southern ports. Allow extra lead time and confirm free-zone customs treatment with a local forwarder.

Does the hot, humid Amazon climate affect handheld devices?

Typically yes, through heat, heavy rain and condensation. Choose sealed housings and keep spare batteries cool and dry. Confirm with the site safety officer the ingress protection and temperature class the plant expects for outdoor classified areas.

Do Free Zone factories need Ex handsets?

Mostly not, because most factories are light manufacturing and classified areas are concentrated around fuel storage and handling. Check each site's classification. Confirm documentation requirements with the site operator, and avoid buying Ex equipment where no hazardous area exists.
