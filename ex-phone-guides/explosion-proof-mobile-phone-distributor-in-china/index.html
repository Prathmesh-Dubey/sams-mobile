Field notes · China

More about China that matters for portable equipment

China: geography and layout

China's land-based oil resources are mostly in the north, with large fields at Daqing, Shengli, Liaohe, Karamay and Dagang, so cold-climate and arid-climate sites are both common.

China: rules and approvals

The current implementing rule is CNCA-C23-01:2024, in force since 1 May 2024, and certification has been to the 2021 editions of GB/T 3836 since 1 May 2022.

China: rules and approvals

GB 3836.15-2024 on design, selection and erection and GB 3836.16-2024 on inspection and maintenance took effect as mandatory national standards on 1 August 2025, governing installations rather than product certification.

China: rules and approvals

Phones also need the MIIT network access licence (NAL) and SRRC radio approval; the NAL is described as compulsory, valid for three years, with local testing and a local representative required.

The term · explosion proof

What “explosion proof” means for a smartphone

“Explosion proof” is the phrase most buyers use for equipment that may be taken into a classified hazardous area. Strictly, it covers several protection concepts: a flameproof enclosure (Ex d) contains an internal explosion, while intrinsic safety (Ex i) limits electrical energy so that an ignition cannot start. Phones are normally intrinsically safe, because a flameproof handset would be too heavy to carry all shift.

The IS-04Z1 uses intrinsic safety, with a marking target of Ex ib IIC T4, and is designed for Zone 1 and Zone 21. It is pre-launch, certification is in progress and no certificate has been issued.

In China. Explosion proof is the everyday phrase, but a coal mine and a coastal petrochemical plant need different thinking. Flameproof Ex d suits much fixed mining equipment; the IS-04Z1 uses intrinsic safety to limit energy in a handheld. It is designed for Zone 1 gas and Zone 21 dust, not underground classification. Operators classify areas, not SAMS.

Frequently asked questions

Can we buy an explosion proof mobile phone in China today?

Not today. Until the certificates are issued the IS-04Z1 stays out of classified areas, and until launch there is nothing to purchase. You can ask for the datasheet and the certification schedule, or book a safe-area evaluation unit.

What does an explosion proof mobile phone in China cost?

There is no list price yet. SAMS gives indicative pre-launch pricing in writing, against a stated quantity and site, and does not commit to a lead time before certification completes.

Is the IS-04Z1 explosion proof or intrinsically safe?

It is intrinsically safe (marking target Ex ib IIC T4), which is the usual way to make a phone for hazardous areas. It is not a flameproof (Ex d) design, and certification is still in progress.

Which sites in China does this cover?

The market guide for China covers Shanxi, Shaanxi and Inner Mongolia, Daqing, Shengli oilfield, among others. Site-level pages are linked below.

How do I become a SAMS distributor for China?

Apply through the distributor page with your company details and the territory you cover. SAMS reviews applications company by company and has not yet granted a territory for China.

What radio and network approvals does a smartphone need in China?

In addition to CCC where applicable, radio devices need SRRC approval and phones connecting to public networks need the MIIT network access licence. The NAL is described as compulsory, valid for three years, with local testing and a local representative. Confirm the current requirements with MIIT or an accredited body.

Do Ex phones need a CCC mark in China?

Products that fall within the explosion-protected electrical category of the CCC catalogue may not be sold, imported or used in business without a CCC certificate and mark, to the GB/T 3836 series. Check with a CNCA-designated body whether a given product falls within that catalogue scope.

Does NEPSI certification mean a Chinese operator will accept the phone?

No: Sinopec, PetroChina and the major coal producers each run their own equipment-approval and procurement process, so a device can be NEPSI-certified and still unlisted. NEPSI and SRRC are being pursued and are not finished. Confirm the listing route with the operator.
