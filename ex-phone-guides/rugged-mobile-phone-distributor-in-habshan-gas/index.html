The term · rugged

What “rugged” means for a smartphone

A rugged phone is built to survive water, dust, drops and rough handling, usually rated by an IP code and a drop specification. Ruggedness is an environmental property: it says nothing about whether a phone may be used where flammable gas or dust is present. That is decided by explosion-protection certification and marking.

The IS-04Z1 combines both: an IP68 sealed rugged build and a design for Zone 1 and Zone 21 (marking target Ex ib IIC T4). The explosion-protection certificates are in progress and none has been issued.

In Habshan gas complex. Habshan is remote desert, so heat, dust and long distances from the coast define the environment. IP68 helps against dust and water ingress, but it is not a measure of explosion protection. Only the Ex marking and the certificate describe that, and for the IS-04Z1 they are in progress.

Frequently asked questions

Can we buy a rugged mobile phone in Habshan Gas today?

Not yet. The IS-04Z1 is pre-launch and its certification is still in progress ahead of a Q4 2026 launch. No unit goes into a classified area before the certificate covering it exists. The datasheet, the certification schedule and safe-area evaluation units are available now.

What does a rugged mobile phone in Habshan Gas cost?

There is no list price yet. SAMS gives indicative pre-launch pricing in writing, against a stated quantity and site, and does not commit to a lead time before certification completes.

How rugged is the IS-04Z1?

It is designed to IP68 sealing, with glove-friendly controls. Ruggedness is not explosion protection: that depends on the certificates, which are in progress.

Which areas at Habshan gas complex would use it?

Likely candidates are gas processing trains, ngl and condensate handling, sulphur and acid-gas units, gas pipelines. Which areas are classified, and to what zone, is set by the site operator's own area classification drawings.

How do I become a SAMS distributor for Habshan gas complex?

Apply through the distributor page with your company details and the territory you cover. SAMS reviews applications company by company and has not yet granted a territory for Habshan gas complex.

Can goods reach Habshan by rail?

Habshan is the terminal station of the Etihad Rail network, which began commercial operations in December 2015. Rail is commonly used for bulk cargo such as sulphur, but equipment deliveries for a project usually go by road; confirm what is practical with the site operator.

What does a Red Zone mean at Habshan?

Wikipedia notes that much of the Habshan area is marked as a Red Zone because of hydrogen sulphide gas. In practice that usually means extra gas-detection, breathing apparatus and entry controls on top of normal hazardous-area rules, which are set by the operator and taught at induction.

How remote is Habshan, and how does that affect service?

Habshan is remote desert with worker camps, and it is the terminal station of the Etihad Rail network. Deliveries and repairs are arranged through the operator, so allow lead time. Ask ADNOC Gas's procurement team how spares and replacement devices are received on site.
