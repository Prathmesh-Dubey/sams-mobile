The term · explosion proof

What “explosion proof” means for a smartphone

“Explosion proof” is the phrase most buyers use for equipment that may be taken into a classified hazardous area. Strictly, it covers several protection concepts: a flameproof enclosure (Ex d) contains an internal explosion, while intrinsic safety (Ex i) limits electrical energy so that an ignition cannot start. Phones are normally intrinsically safe, because a flameproof handset would be too heavy to carry all shift.

The IS-04Z1 uses intrinsic safety, with a marking target of Ex ib IIC T4, and is designed for Zone 1 and Zone 21. It is pre-launch, certification is in progress and no certificate has been issued.

In Pekanbaru and the Rokan block, Riau. Gathering stations, wellheads, oil treatment and steam facilities mix hazards. Explosion-proof is a loose term. Flameproof Ex d suits fixed motors and panels, while a handheld carried around wellheads and flowlines relies on intrinsic safety. The operator's classification decides where each applies.

Is the IS-04Z1 explosion proof or intrinsically safe?

It is intrinsically safe (marking target Ex ib IIC T4), which is the usual way to make a phone for hazardous areas. It is not a flameproof (Ex d) design, and certification is still in progress.

Which areas at Pekanbaru and the Rokan block, Riau would use it?

Likely candidates are gathering stations, wellheads and flowlines, oil treatment and storage, steam generation and power facilities. Which areas are classified, and to what zone, is set by the site operator's own area classification drawings.

What hazardous areas are common in a mature oil field like Rokan?

Typical areas include wellheads, flowlines, gathering stations, tank farms, pump stations and loading facilities, where flammable vapour can be released. The operator sets the zones, so confirm the classification for each facility rather than assuming one rule for the whole field.

How do I get equipment to the Pekanbaru oil region?

The first road linking Pekanbaru with Dumai was built for the oilfield, and roads and a pipeline connect the Duri area to the Dumai port. Plan for customs clearance in Indonesia and site gate checks on electronics, and confirm lead times and import rules with your freight agent.

Is Pekanbaru itself a hazardous area?

No, Pekanbaru is the regional support city for the block. Hazardous areas are in the field facilities, such as gathering stations and treatment plants. Office and workshop areas usually follow normal rules. Ask the operator which areas are classified.
