Field notes · Jubail Industrial City

More about Jubail Industrial City that matters for portable equipment

Jubail Industrial City: background

Jubail was a small fishing village until 1975, when the government chose it for a new industrial city, so nearly all plant infrastructure dates from the last 50 years.

Jubail Industrial City: people and language

Jubail had 474,679 residents in 2022, making it the third most populous city in the Eastern Province, so a large local workforce and service sector supports the plants.

Jubail Industrial City: logistics and access

Two main highways, the Dhahran to Jubail Highway and the Abu Hadriyah Highway, link the city to the rest of the Eastern Province.

Jubail Industrial City: who works there

The Royal Commission's chairman's office is in Riyadh, with separate directorates general in Jubail and Yanbu, so city-level services are run locally while plants set their own rules.

The term · Ex-proof

What “Ex-proof” means for a smartphone

“Ex-proof” is shorthand for equipment carrying an explosion-protection marking (the Ex mark) that matches the area it enters. The marking states the protection concept, gas or dust group and temperature class, and it must be backed by a certificate issued by a recognised body, so a phone described as Ex-proof should come with a certificate number you can check.

The IS-04Z1 is designed to carry the marking II 2 G Ex ib IIC T4. That is a target: certification is in progress, no certificate has been issued, and SAMS will publish the certificate numbers when they exist.

In Jubail Industrial City. Chemical and petrochemical plants may involve gases from different groups, so a marking check at Jubail should read the gas group and temperature class from each plant's own classification, not from a general assumption. The IS-04Z1 target marking is II 2 G Ex ib IIC T4, still a target.

Frequently asked questions

Can we buy an Ex-proof mobile phone in Jubail today?

Not today. Until the certificates are issued the IS-04Z1 stays out of classified areas, and until launch there is nothing to purchase. You can ask for the datasheet and the certification schedule, or book a safe-area evaluation unit.

What does an Ex-proof mobile phone in Jubail cost?

No price is published. While certification is in progress there is no list price and no committed lead time, and a supplier quoting a firm figure for an uncertified Ex handset is promising something it cannot stand behind. Indicative pre-launch pricing is issued in writing against a stated quantity and site.

What Ex marking will the IS-04Z1 carry?

The marking target is II 2 G Ex ib IIC T4. It becomes a marking only when the certificate is issued; until then it is a design target.

Which areas at Jubail Industrial City would use it?

Likely candidates are refinery process units, petrochemical and chemical plants, storage and loading terminals, port bulk handling. Which areas are classified, and to what zone, is set by the site operator's own area classification drawings.

Is intrinsically safe the same as explosion proof?

Not exactly. Explosion proof is a broad everyday term; intrinsic safety is one protection concept, which limits energy so ignition cannot occur. Flameproof (Ex d) is a different concept that contains an explosion. The IS-04Z1 is intrinsically safe by design.

Is Jubail one operator or many?

Many. The Royal Commission plans and runs the industrial city, but chemical, refining, power and port companies operate their own plants under their own safety and permit rules. Equipment approval for handheld devices is usually decided plant by plant.

Does Jubail's utility infrastructure create classified areas?

Gas-fired power and desalination plants usually include fuel-gas supply areas, so some classified zones are likely. The city's independent water and power project is among the largest in the world, so contractor numbers are high; confirm requirements with the specific plant.

Do approvals carry across plants in Jubail?

Not automatically. Each plant operator sets its own approved-vendor and permit rules, so approval at one plant does not guarantee acceptance at another. Confirm current requirements with the operator or its contractor for each plant. This applies on top of explosion-protection certification.
