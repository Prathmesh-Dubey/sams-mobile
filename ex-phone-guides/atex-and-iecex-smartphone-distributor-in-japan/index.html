Field notes · Japan

More about Japan that matters for portable equipment

Japan: infrastructure

Small rivers, including the Edo River, supply water to the Keiyo industrial region, and the Port of Chiba serves the zone.

Japan: rules and approvals

Explosion-protected electrical equipment must pass type examination, called kentei, under Article 44-2 of the Industrial Safety and Health Act, and equipment without the pass mark may not be used.

Japan: rules and approvals

The type examination involves a sample for structural inspection, with no factory audit in law, and the applicant can be a Japanese or foreign manufacturer or an importer.

Japan: geography and layout

The Keiyo Industrial Zone runs along the north-eastern coast of Tokyo Bay through eight cities in Chiba Prefecture, from Urayasu to Futtsu, including Chiba, Kisarazu and Ichihara.

The term · ATEX and IECEx

What “ATEX and IECEx” means for a smartphone

ATEX is the European legal framework for equipment used in explosive atmospheres (Directive 2014/34/EU). IECEx is the international certification scheme built on the IEC 60079 standards. Buyers outside Europe often ask for both: an IECEx certificate is the technical evidence most national regimes build on, while ATEX is the legal route into the European market.

For the IS-04Z1 both routes are in progress with TÜV in Germany and neither certificate has been issued. The marking target is II 2 G Ex ib IIC T4: equipment Group II (surface industry), Category 2 (Zone 1 gas), protection concept ib (intrinsic safety), gas group IIC and temperature class T4.

In Japan. A Japanese reviewer at a coastal petrochemical complex would expect TIIS evidence, which the sheet lists as in progress, and IECEx. ATEX evidence also helps international contractors and global groups. A dual route lets one pack serve the complex's own review and visitors. No certificate has been issued.

The route · distribution

Distributing the IS-04Z1 in Japan

Distribution of the IS-04Z1 is by authorised partners only, and SAMS has not yet granted a territory for Japan. Each application is reviewed on its merits. Because opening a certified Ex handset in an ordinary workshop invalidates its certificate, the partner must be able to support and service the device locally.

Questions · Japan

Frequently asked questions

Can we buy an ATEX and IECEx smartphone in Japan today?

Not yet. The IS-04Z1 is pre-launch and its certification is still in progress ahead of a Q4 2026 launch. No unit goes into a classified area before the certificate covering it exists. The datasheet, the certification schedule and safe-area evaluation units are available now.

What does an ATEX and IECEx smartphone in Japan cost?

No price is published. While certification is in progress there is no list price and no committed lead time, and a supplier quoting a firm figure for an uncertified Ex handset is promising something it cannot stand behind. Indicative pre-launch pricing is issued in writing against a stated quantity and site.

Is the IS-04Z1 ATEX and IECEx certified?

Not yet. Both routes are in progress with TÜV in Germany and no certificate has been issued. Any listing that says otherwise is wrong.

Which sites in Japan does this cover?

The market guide for Japan covers Kashima, Mizushima, Chiba / Sodegaura, Yokkaichi, among others. Site-level pages are linked below.

How do I become a SAMS distributor for Japan?

Apply through the distributor page with your company details and the territory you cover. SAMS reviews applications company by company and has not yet granted a territory for Japan.

Is Japanese type examination required for Ex phones?

Explosion-protected electrical equipment must pass type examination under Article 44-2 of the Industrial Safety and Health Act, and equipment without the pass mark may not be used. Registered examination bodies, including some outside Japan, carry it out. Check the current process with one of them.

Where do hazardous-area sites sit in Japan?

Typically in coastal industrial zones such as the Keiyo zone along Tokyo Bay, which has refineries and chemical plants, and similar bayside complexes elsewhere. The operator issues the area classification, so request the zone drawing, gas group and temperature class before selecting equipment.

Is TIIS required for use in Japan?

The market guide lists TIIS and IECEx as in progress and neither has been granted. Whether a given site needs TIIS depends on the operator and the regulations it follows. Ask the site operator and check the current certification status before ordering.
