The term · hazardous area

What “hazardous area” means for a smartphone

A hazardous area is a place where flammable gas, vapour or combustible dust may be present. Standards divide these into zones: Zones 0, 1 and 2 for gas and vapour, and Zones 20, 21 and 22 for dust. Zone 1 and Zone 21 are the tier where an explosive atmosphere is likely to occur in normal operation, which is where the IS-04Z1 is designed to work. Anything carried in must be marked for the zone, gas group and temperature class of the area.

No certificate has been issued for the IS-04Z1 yet, so it must not be taken into a classified area until the certificate covering it exists and the site operator has accepted it.

In Algeria. Classified areas in Algeria fall into two families: the Saharan oil and gas fields and processing hubs at Hassi Messaoud, Hassi R'Mel and In Amenas, and the coastal LNG, refining and petrochemical complexes at Arzew and Skikda. The operator, whether Sonatrach or a joint venture, classifies these areas. SAMS does not, and the device is designed for Zone 1 and Zone 21.

Questions · Algeria

Frequently asked questions

Can we buy a hazardous area smartphone in Algeria today?

Not today. Until the certificates are issued the IS-04Z1 stays out of classified areas, and until launch there is nothing to purchase. You can ask for the datasheet and the certification schedule, or book a safe-area evaluation unit.

What does a hazardous area smartphone in Algeria cost?

SAMS has not published a price. Any figure you are offered for an uncertified Ex phone should be treated with caution; written indicative pricing is available for a defined quantity and site.

Which hazardous areas is the IS-04Z1 designed for?

Zone 1 and Zone 21 (gas and dust) with gas group IIC and temperature class T4, subject to certification. It must not be used in a classified area before its certificate is issued and the site has accepted it.

Which sites in Algeria does this cover?

The market guide for Algeria covers Hassi Messaoud, Ouargla, Hassi R'Mel, Laghouat, In Amenas, Illizi, In Salah, Tamanrasset, among others. Site-level pages are linked below.

How do I become a SAMS distributor for Algeria?

Apply through the distributor page with your company details and the territory you cover. SAMS reviews applications company by company and has not yet granted a territory for Algeria.

What heat should devices be rated for in the Algerian Sahara?

Hassi Messaoud averages above 40 degrees Celsius for four months and above 45 degrees in July, so check the operating, charging and storage ranges on the datasheet and keep devices out of direct sun and closed vehicles. Confirm any site limits with the operator or contractor.

Should documents for Algerian field sites be in French or Arabic?

Arabic and Amazigh are the official languages, and French is also commonly used in business, so manuals and safety notices are often supplied in more than one language. Ask the operator or contractor which languages are required for permits, induction and equipment documentation.

Does the phone need Algerian radio approval as well as an Ex certificate?

Radio equipment needs homologation by ARPCE, the communications regulator, before import and use, separately from the Ex certificate. Both are in progress for the IS-04Z1 and neither is finished. Confirm the current requirement with Sonatrach or the partner handling procurement, who will also tell you whether French documentation is expected.
