The term · rugged

What “rugged” means for a smartphone

A rugged phone is built to survive water, dust, drops and rough handling, usually rated by an IP code and a drop specification. Ruggedness is an environmental property: it says nothing about whether a phone may be used where flammable gas or dust is present. That is decided by explosion-protection certification and marking.

The IS-04Z1 combines both: an IP68 sealed rugged build and a design for Zone 1 and Zone 21 (marking target Ex ib IIC T4). The explosion-protection certificates are in progress and none has been issued.

In Pekanbaru and the Rokan block, Riau. Pekanbaru has a tropical rainforest climate with rain every month, so field devices meet heat, humidity and wet conditions. IP68 rates dust and water ingress. It does not provide explosion protection, which must be assessed against the zone where the device is used.

Frequently asked questions

Can we buy a rugged mobile phone in Pekanbaru Oil Field today?

No. Nothing can be bought or taken into a classified area today: certification is in progress and launch is planned for Q4 2026. What is available now is the datasheet, the written certification schedule and evaluation units for safe-area use.

What does a rugged mobile phone in Pekanbaru Oil Field cost?

How rugged is the IS-04Z1?

It is designed to IP68 sealing, with glove-friendly controls. Ruggedness is not explosion protection: that depends on the certificates, which are in progress.

Which areas at Pekanbaru and the Rokan block, Riau would use it?

Likely candidates are gathering stations, wellheads and flowlines, oil treatment and storage, steam generation and power facilities. Which areas are classified, and to what zone, is set by the site operator's own area classification drawings.

Is intrinsically safe the same as explosion proof?

Not exactly. Explosion proof is a broad everyday term; intrinsic safety is one protection concept, which limits energy so ignition cannot occur. Flameproof (Ex d) is a different concept that contains an explosion. The IS-04Z1 is intrinsically safe by design.

Why does steamflood production matter for equipment used at Duri?

Steamflood means hot steam lines and wells operate close to oil and gas handling, so surface temperatures can be higher than at conventional wells. Handheld devices should respect the site's temperature limits and classification, so ask the operator for its rules on portable electronics.

Which company operates the large oil fields around Pekanbaru today?

The Rokan block, including the Minas and Duri fields, has been run by Pertamina Hulu Rokan since August 2021. Contractors and suppliers typically register through Pertamina's vendor processes and follow the site's own induction and permit rules, so confirm current requirements.

Who approves suppliers for the Rokan block?

Suppliers are normally approved by the operator and its contractors, following Pertamina group processes since the 2021 operator change. Ask PT Pertamina Hulu Rokan about current registration requirements before offering any equipment. Confirm with the site operator.
