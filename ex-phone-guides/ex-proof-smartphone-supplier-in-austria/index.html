Field notes · Austria

More about Austria that matters for portable equipment

Austria: industry on the ground

Machinery and equipment, vehicles, chemicals, metals, and iron and steel are leading sectors, so chemical plants, foundries and paint and coating lines add solvent and dust hazards beyond oil and gas.

Austria: who works there

OMV AG, an integrated oil, gas and petrochemical group, is Austria's largest energy business, with a turnover of about 36 billion euros in 2021, and its refining and chemicals sites are typical Zone 1 and Zone 2 workplaces.

Austria: logistics and access

Transport used 31.3% of energy in 2021, the largest share of any sector, and the last coal plant closed in 2020, so fuel logistics and gas networks are the main thermal energy infrastructure.

Austria: industry on the ground

Imported oil supplied 35.4% of energy consumed in 2022 and gas 16.8%, so pipelines, storage and distribution terminals form a large share of Austria's flammable-materials infrastructure.

The term · Ex-proof

What “Ex-proof” means for a smartphone

“Ex-proof” is shorthand for equipment carrying an explosion-protection marking (the Ex mark) that matches the area it enters. The marking states the protection concept, gas or dust group and temperature class, and it must be backed by a certificate issued by a recognised body, so a phone described as Ex-proof should come with a certificate number you can check.

The IS-04Z1 is designed to carry the marking II 2 G Ex ib IIC T4. That is a target: certification is in progress, no certificate has been issued, and SAMS will publish the certificate numbers when they exist.

In Austria. A marking check in Austria would take the certificate number, equipment group, category and temperature class, since the safety officer needs them for the explosion protection document. The gas target is II 2 G Ex ib IIC T4, with Zone 21 dust under the same programme, so one phone could cover a cracker and a starch plant.

Questions · Austria

Frequently asked questions

Can we buy an Ex-proof smartphone in Austria today?

Not yet. The IS-04Z1 is pre-launch and its certification is still in progress ahead of a Q4 2026 launch. No unit goes into a classified area before the certificate covering it exists. The datasheet, the certification schedule and safe-area evaluation units are available now.

What does an Ex-proof smartphone in Austria cost?

SAMS has not published a price. Any figure you are offered for an uncertified Ex phone should be treated with caution; written indicative pricing is available for a defined quantity and site.

What Ex marking will the IS-04Z1 carry?

The marking target is II 2 G Ex ib IIC T4. It becomes a marking only when the certificate is issued; until then it is a design target.

Which sites in Austria does this cover?

The market guide for Austria covers Schwechat, Baumgarten an der March, Linz, Donawitz and Leoben, among others. Site-level pages are linked below.

How will SAMS supply Austria?

Through authorised distributors once certificates exist. Until then SAMS can send the datasheet and certification schedule and arrange safe-area evaluation units.

Does Austria use ATEX for handheld devices in hazardous areas?

Yes, as an EU member Austria applies ATEX Directive 2014/34/EU to equipment, and workplaces are zoned under Directive 1999/92/EC. Devices for Zone 1 or Zone 2 areas normally need the Ex marking, CE marking and documentation. Check the operator's list of accepted categories.

Is OMV the main hazardous-area operator in Austria?

OMV AG is the largest energy business, with integrated oil, gas and petrochemical activity, so it is a major source of classified areas. Other sites include chemical plants and utility sites. Supplier approval is run by each company, so contact its procurement and safety teams.

Does an Austrian approval follow the ATEX certificate?

No: ATEX 2014/34/EU, transposed by the Explosionsschutzverordnung 2015, is the whole product-side requirement and there is no separate Austrian product approval. The employer's duties sit under the VEXAT, which is where evaluation time goes. SAMS holds no ATEX certificate yet. Confirm what your site's explosion protection document needs.
