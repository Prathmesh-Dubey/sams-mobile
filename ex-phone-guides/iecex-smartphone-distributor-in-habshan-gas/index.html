The term · IECEx

What “IECEx” means for a smartphone

IECEx is the international scheme for certifying equipment used in explosive atmospheres. A certificate of conformity is issued by an accredited certification body against a specific edition of the IEC 60079 standards and carries a schedule of conditions of safe use. It is not a legal requirement in most countries on its own, but many national schemes accept it as the technical basis.

The IECEx certificate for the IS-04Z1 is in progress and has not been issued. The marking target is Ex ib IIC T4, the intrinsic safety concept for Zone 1 gas with the hardest gas group (IIC) and a 135 °C temperature class.

In Habshan gas complex. Turnarounds and projects at a complex of this scale draw in international EPCs and specialist contractors. IECEx offers them a common reference, with a certificate of conformity and schedule of conditions to review together. SAMS holds no IECEx certificate for the IS-04Z1; certification is in progress.

Frequently asked questions

Can we buy an IECEx smartphone in Habshan Gas today?

No. Nothing can be bought or taken into a classified area today: certification is in progress and launch is planned for Q4 2026. What is available now is the datasheet, the written certification schedule and evaluation units for safe-area use.

What does an IECEx smartphone in Habshan Gas cost?

Does the IS-04Z1 hold an IECEx certificate?

No. The IECEx certificate is in progress and has not been issued. SAMS will publish the number when it exists.

Which areas at Habshan gas complex would use it?

Likely candidates are gas processing trains, ngl and condensate handling, sulphur and acid-gas units, gas pipelines. Which areas are classified, and to what zone, is set by the site operator's own area classification drawings.

How do I become a SAMS distributor for Habshan gas complex?

Apply through the distributor page with your company details and the territory you cover. SAMS reviews applications company by company and has not yet granted a territory for Habshan gas complex.

Can goods reach Habshan by rail?

Habshan is the terminal station of the Etihad Rail network, which began commercial operations in December 2015. Rail is commonly used for bulk cargo such as sulphur, but equipment deliveries for a project usually go by road; confirm what is practical with the site operator.

What does a Red Zone mean at Habshan?

Wikipedia notes that much of the Habshan area is marked as a Red Zone because of hydrogen sulphide gas. In practice that usually means extra gas-detection, breathing apparatus and entry controls on top of normal hazardous-area rules, which are set by the operator and taught at induction.

How remote is Habshan, and how does that affect service?

Habshan is remote desert with worker camps, and it is the terminal station of the Etihad Rail network. Deliveries and repairs are arranged through the operator, so allow lead time. Ask ADNOC Gas's procurement team how spares and replacement devices are received on site.
