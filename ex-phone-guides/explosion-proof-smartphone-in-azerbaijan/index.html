The term · explosion proof

What “explosion proof” means for a smartphone

“Explosion proof” is the phrase most buyers use for equipment that may be taken into a classified hazardous area. Strictly, it covers several protection concepts: a flameproof enclosure (Ex d) contains an internal explosion, while intrinsic safety (Ex i) limits electrical energy so that an ignition cannot start. Phones are normally intrinsically safe, because a flameproof handset would be too heavy to carry all shift.

The IS-04Z1 uses intrinsic safety, with a marking target of Ex ib IIC T4, and is designed for Zone 1 and Zone 21. It is pre-launch, certification is in progress and no certificate has been issued.

In Azerbaijan. On a Caspian platform explosion proof usually suggests a flameproof Ex d enclosure. The IS-04Z1 instead relies on intrinsic safety, limiting energy so a handheld can go through process modules and terminal areas. Sumgait's polymer plants and the Baku refinery present similar choices. Which concept applies to each area is the operator's classification, not SAMS's.

Questions · Azerbaijan

Frequently asked questions

Can we buy an explosion proof smartphone in Azerbaijan today?

Not yet. The IS-04Z1 is pre-launch and its certification is still in progress ahead of a Q4 2026 launch. No unit goes into a classified area before the certificate covering it exists. The datasheet, the certification schedule and safe-area evaluation units are available now.

What does an explosion proof smartphone in Azerbaijan cost?

SAMS has not published a price. Any figure you are offered for an uncertified Ex phone should be treated with caution; written indicative pricing is available for a defined quantity and site.

Is the IS-04Z1 explosion proof or intrinsically safe?

It is intrinsically safe (marking target Ex ib IIC T4), which is the usual way to make a phone for hazardous areas. It is not a flameproof (Ex d) design, and certification is still in progress.

Which sites in Azerbaijan does this cover?

The market guide for Azerbaijan covers Sangachal terminal, ACG and Shah Deniz, offshore, Baku, Sumgait Chemical Industrial Park, among others. Site-level pages are linked below.

Is intrinsically safe the same as explosion proof?

Not exactly. Explosion proof is a broad everyday term; intrinsic safety is one protection concept, which limits energy so ignition cannot occur. Flameproof (Ex d) is a different concept that contains an explosion. The IS-04Z1 is intrinsically safe by design.

Who sets equipment rules at Sangachal?

The BP-led operating consortium sets site HSE, permit and equipment rules, applying its own standards on top of national requirements. Contractors normally follow the operator's induction and approved-equipment procedures, so ask the operator's HSE team which certificates and registers apply.

Do wireless devices need type approval in Azerbaijan?

A compliance agent describes type approval from the national telecom authority as mandatory for products with wireless components, with an average lead time of about three weeks and no label or sample requirement. Treat this as a guide and confirm current rules with the ministry or a local agent.

Which languages will documentation and the interface support?

Android 16 offers Azerbaijani, Russian and English language settings, and documentation in those three languages is expected on site. Whether a given operator requires one of them specifically is a procurement question. Ask the operator or the locally registered supply partner before the evaluation, so the documentation set matches what site safety teams will request.
