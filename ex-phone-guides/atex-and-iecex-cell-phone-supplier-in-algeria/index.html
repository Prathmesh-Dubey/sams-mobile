Field notes · Algeria

More about Algeria that matters for portable equipment

Algeria: rules and approvals

Telecom equipment approvals are handled by two bodies: ARPCE as telecoms regulator for equipment such as mobile phones and Wi-Fi devices, and ANF for radio spectrum and some short-range devices.

Algeria: rules and approvals

Algerian approval is described as relying largely on international test reports such as EU CE reports, and a local representative is still needed for the application.

Algeria: climate and equipment wear

Hassi Messaoud has a hot desert climate with average highs above 40 degrees Celsius from June to September and over 45 degrees in July, so heat dominates equipment and battery planning.

Algeria: people and language

Algeria's official languages are Arabic and Amazigh, so site signage, permits and training material may need translation or bilingual versions for local crews.

The term · ATEX and IECEx

What “ATEX and IECEx” means for a smartphone

ATEX is the European legal framework for equipment used in explosive atmospheres (Directive 2014/34/EU). IECEx is the international certification scheme built on the IEC 60079 standards. Buyers outside Europe often ask for both: an IECEx certificate is the technical evidence most national regimes build on, while ATEX is the legal route into the European market.

For the IS-04Z1 both routes are in progress with TÜV in Germany and neither certificate has been issued. The marking target is II 2 G Ex ib IIC T4: equipment Group II (surface industry), Category 2 (Zone 1 gas), protection concept ib (intrinsic safety), gas group IIC and temperature class T4.

In Algeria. Sonatrach and its international partners specify Ex equipment to IEC standards, so a procurement reviewer in Algeria would expect an IECEx Certificate of Conformity first, with an ATEX certificate beside it where a specification names the European directive. Carrying both helps joint-venture partners and contractors who bring their own equipment rules into the Saharan fields. Both certifications for the IS-04Z1 are in progress.

Questions · Algeria

Frequently asked questions

Can we buy an ATEX and IECEx cell phone in Algeria today?

No. Nothing can be bought or taken into a classified area today: certification is in progress and launch is planned for Q4 2026. What is available now is the datasheet, the written certification schedule and evaluation units for safe-area use.

What does an ATEX and IECEx cell phone in Algeria cost?

There is no list price yet. SAMS gives indicative pre-launch pricing in writing, against a stated quantity and site, and does not commit to a lead time before certification completes.

Is the IS-04Z1 ATEX and IECEx certified?

Not yet. Both routes are in progress with TÜV in Germany and no certificate has been issued. Any listing that says otherwise is wrong.

Which sites in Algeria does this cover?

The market guide for Algeria covers Hassi Messaoud, Ouargla, Hassi R'Mel, Laghouat, In Amenas, Illizi, In Salah, Tamanrasset, among others. Site-level pages are linked below.

How will SAMS supply Algeria?

Through authorised distributors once certificates exist. Until then SAMS can send the datasheet and certification schedule and arrange safe-area evaluation units.

What heat should devices be rated for in the Algerian Sahara?

Hassi Messaoud averages above 40 degrees Celsius for four months and above 45 degrees in July, so check the operating, charging and storage ranges on the datasheet and keep devices out of direct sun and closed vehicles. Confirm any site limits with the operator or contractor.

Should documents for Algerian field sites be in French or Arabic?

Arabic and Amazigh are the official languages, and French is also commonly used in business, so manuals and safety notices are often supplied in more than one language. Ask the operator or contractor which languages are required for permits, induction and equipment documentation.

Does the phone need Algerian radio approval as well as an Ex certificate?

Radio equipment needs homologation by ARPCE, the communications regulator, before import and use, separately from the Ex certificate. Both are in progress for the IS-04Z1 and neither is finished. Confirm the current requirement with Sonatrach or the partner handling procurement, who will also tell you whether French documentation is expected.
