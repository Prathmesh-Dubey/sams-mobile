More about Singapore that matters for portable equipment

Singapore: projects and investment

SLNG plans a second LNG terminal offshore, slated to operate by the end of the decade, in addition to the existing Jurong Island terminal, so new gas-handling sites are expected over the coming years.

Singapore: rules and approvals

IMDA registers telecommunications equipment under a supplier's declaration approach. Cellular mobile devices, including 5G and LTE, fall under Simplified Equipment Registration, while base stations and similar higher-risk products need a recognised certification body.

Singapore: rules and approvals

An IMDA equipment registration is valid for five years, renewal is filed through the GoBusiness portal at least two months before expiry, and suppliers must keep test reports and technical files for five years.

Singapore: logistics and access

Tuas Port opened on 1 September 2022 and is planned to reach 65 million TEU of annual handling capacity in the 2040s, which concentrates future cargo flows in the far west of the island.

The term · intrinsically safe

What “intrinsically safe” means for a smartphone

Intrinsic safety limits the electrical and thermal energy in a circuit so that neither a spark nor a hot surface can ignite a gas or dust atmosphere, even when a fault occurs. The ib level, used for Zone 1, tolerates one fault. It is the usual way to make a handheld device that a worker carries continuously through a hazardous area.

The IS-04Z1 is designed on this principle for Zone 1 and Zone 21 (marking target II 2 G Ex ib IIC T4). Intrinsic safety is a design basis, not a certificate: certification is in progress and none has been issued yet.

In Singapore. Rounds on Jurong Island, Pulau Bukom and the storage islands such as Sebarok mean long walks and boat journeys, so a handheld that is intrinsically safe saves a second trip back to the control room. A flat handset on an island is the next boat away, which makes battery capacity part of the selection.

Frequently asked questions

Can we buy an intrinsically safe smartphone in Singapore today?

No. Nothing can be bought or taken into a classified area today: certification is in progress and launch is planned for Q4 2026. What is available now is the datasheet, the written certification schedule and evaluation units for safe-area use.

What does an intrinsically safe smartphone in Singapore cost?

There is no list price yet. SAMS gives indicative pre-launch pricing in writing, against a stated quantity and site, and does not commit to a lead time before certification completes.

What does ‘intrinsically safe’ mean for the IS-04Z1?

The circuits are designed to limit energy so a spark or hot surface cannot ignite a gas or dust atmosphere, even with one fault (level ib, Zone 1). Certification of that design is in progress.

Which sites in Singapore does this cover?

The market guide for Singapore covers Jurong Island, Pulau Bukom, Seraya and Merbau, Pulau Sebarok and Pulau Busing, among others. Site-level pages are linked below.

How will SAMS supply Singapore?

Through authorised distributors once certificates exist. Until then SAMS can send the datasheet and certification schedule and arrange safe-area evaluation units.

Which Singapore authorities matter for hazardous-area equipment on site?

The Ministry of Manpower covers workplace safety duties for explosive atmospheres, and the Civil Defence Force covers flammable-material storage and dangerous-goods premises. In practice the site operator's own hazardous-area drawings and equipment standard decide what may enter a zone, so check them before shipping anything.

What should a visitor expect when bringing a handheld device onto Jurong Island?

Visitors normally collect a Jurong Island visitor pass at the checkpoint, then receive a separate site pass from the plant they visit. One refinery's published rules ban phones in process and classified areas and ask visitors to declare electronic equipment. Expect similar screening and ask your sponsor in advance.

Why does gas group IIC matter for Jurong Island?

Hydrogen and acetylene, both gas group IIC, are handled on the island next to ethylene oxide in IIB, so a handset rated only to IIB cannot be used in some areas. The IS-04Z1 target is Ex ib IIC. Certification is in progress, so check the final certificate.
