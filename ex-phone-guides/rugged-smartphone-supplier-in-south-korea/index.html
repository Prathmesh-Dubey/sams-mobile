Field notes · South Korea

More about South Korea that matters for portable equipment

South Korea: logistics and access

South Korea imports nearly all its oil and was the world's third-largest importer of liquefied natural gas in 2024, so LNG terminals and fuel storage are major classified-area sites.

South Korea: climate and equipment wear

Most rainfall is concentrated in the summer months, which matters for outdoor equipment: ingress protection and condensation resistance are worth checking for devices used across the monsoon season.

South Korea: projects and investment

Ulsan opened the country's first hydrogen car charging station in 2009 and the first hydrogen ship charging station at Jangsaengpo Port in 2021, and a Green Hydrogen Town project is in progress, adding hydrogen-classified areas.

South Korea: background

Ulsan was chosen as a Special Industrial Zone in 1962 and became a city in 1963, which is why large refining, petrochemical and shipbuilding sites sit close together on its coast.

The term · rugged

What “rugged” means for a smartphone

A rugged phone is built to survive water, dust, drops and rough handling, usually rated by an IP code and a drop specification. Ruggedness is an environmental property: it says nothing about whether a phone may be used where flammable gas or dust is present. That is decided by explosion-protection certification and marking.

The IS-04Z1 combines both: an IP68 sealed rugged build and a design for Zone 1 and Zone 21 (marking target Ex ib IIC T4). The explosion-protection certificates are in progress and none has been issued.

In South Korea. Korea swings from hot, humid summers to cold, dry winters, and the yards add marine exposure. IP68 sealing and rugged build are specified across the full range rather than one season. IP68 is ingress protection only, and gives no explosion protection by itself.

Questions · South Korea

Frequently asked questions

Can we buy a rugged smartphone in South Korea today?

No. Nothing can be bought or taken into a classified area today: certification is in progress and launch is planned for Q4 2026. What is available now is the datasheet, the written certification schedule and evaluation units for safe-area use.

What does a rugged smartphone in South Korea cost?

SAMS has not published a price. Any figure you are offered for an uncertified Ex phone should be treated with caution; written indicative pricing is available for a defined quantity and site.

How rugged is the IS-04Z1?

It is designed to IP68 sealing, with glove-friendly controls. Ruggedness is not explosion protection: that depends on the certificates, which are in progress.

Which sites in South Korea does this cover?

The market guide for South Korea covers Ulsan, Yeosu, Daesan, Onsan, among others. Site-level pages are linked below.

How will SAMS supply South Korea?

Through authorised distributors once certificates exist. Until then SAMS can send the datasheet and certification schedule and arrange safe-area evaluation units.

Is hydrogen changing hazardous-area requirements in Ulsan?

Ulsan has opened hydrogen charging stations and is developing a Green Hydrogen Town, so hydrogen gas groups may appear in new classified areas. Always check a device's gas group marking against the area classification drawing rather than assuming a general Zone 1 rating is enough.

How does Korean weather affect handheld devices used outdoors?

Summers bring concentrated heavy rainfall and humidity, and winters can be cold, so look at the operating temperature range, water ingress rating and glove-friendly touch operation. Coastal sites at Ulsan also bring salt air, which can affect ports and contacts.

Does the handset support Korean as well as English?

Android 16 carries both on one build, and operations at Korean sites run predominantly in Korean with English for international contractors and documentation. Check the language pack and the keyboard layout you require during an evaluation unit trial.
