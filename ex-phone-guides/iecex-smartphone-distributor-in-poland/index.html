Field notes · Poland

More about Poland that matters for portable equipment

Poland: industry on the ground

The Płock refinery and petrochemical complex is the largest oil refinery in Poland, and Orlen's headquarters are in the same city, with servicing and construction firms clustered around the plant.

Poland: industry on the ground

Coal use splits into 75.6% for electricity and heat, 14.5% for industry and 9.9% for buildings, so industrial coal consumers beyond power plants also have dust and gas hazards.

Poland: background

Poland produced 78 megatonnes of hard coal and 57 megatonnes of brown coal in 2009, and a plan agreed in September 2020 aims to phase out coal by 2049.

Poland: industry on the ground

In 2020 coal made up 69.5% of national energy production and 68.5% of electricity generation, so coal handling, bunkers and mills create combustible dust zones at many power sites.

The term · IECEx

What “IECEx” means for a smartphone

IECEx is the international scheme for certifying equipment used in explosive atmospheres. A certificate of conformity is issued by an accredited certification body against a specific edition of the IEC 60079 standards and carries a schedule of conditions of safe use. It is not a legal requirement in most countries on its own, but many national schemes accept it as the technical basis.

The IECEx certificate for the IS-04Z1 is in progress and has not been issued. The marking target is Ex ib IIC T4, the intrinsic safety concept for Zone 1 gas with the hardest gas group (IIC) and a 135 °C temperature class.

In Poland. Poland hosts large national operators and international contractors working inside operating plant fences. IECEx gives foreign partners a common reference alongside ATEX, the main product route here. Ask for the certificate of conformity and its schedule of conditions, which record the marking and conditions of use. The IS-04Z1 has none yet issued.

The route · distribution

Distributing the IS-04Z1 in Poland

Distribution of the IS-04Z1 is by authorised partners only, and SAMS has not yet granted a territory for Poland. Each application is reviewed on its merits. Questions · Poland

Frequently asked questions

Can we buy an IECEx smartphone in Poland today?

No. Nothing can be bought or taken into a classified area today: certification is in progress and launch is planned for Q4 2026. What is available now is the datasheet, the written certification schedule and evaluation units for safe-area use.

What does an IECEx smartphone in Poland cost?

SAMS has not published a price. Any figure you are offered for an uncertified Ex phone should be treated with caution; written indicative pricing is available for a defined quantity and site.

Does the IS-04Z1 hold an IECEx certificate?

No. The IECEx certificate is in progress and has not been issued. SAMS will publish the number when it exists.

Which sites in Poland does this cover?

The market guide for Poland covers Płock, Gdańsk, Włocławek, Puławy, among others. Site-level pages are linked below.

How do I become a SAMS distributor for Poland?

Apply through the distributor page with your company details and the territory you cover. SAMS reviews applications company by company and has not yet granted a territory for Poland.

What language should Polish sites receive documentation in?

Polish is the working language and ATEX instructions are typically expected in the language of the destination country. A Polish-language instruction sheet with certificate pack usually speeds up site acceptance. Ask the operator whether English documents are also accepted for contractors.

Does Poland follow ATEX for handheld devices in hazardous areas?

Poland is an EU member, so ATEX Directive 2014/34/EU applies to equipment and Directive 1999/92/EC to workplace zoning. Zone 1 and Zone 2 devices should carry CE and Ex markings with instructions in Polish. Operators and mines usually add their own approval rules.

Does one device cover both gas and dust on a fertiliser site?

The gas marking target is II 2 G Ex ib IIC T4, and Zone 21 dust protection belongs to the same programme, so one device is designed to cover both. Certification is in progress, so confirm the final marking and the plant's zone drawing with the operator.
