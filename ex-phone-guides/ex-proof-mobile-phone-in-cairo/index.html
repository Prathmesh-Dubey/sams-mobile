The term · Ex-proof

What “Ex-proof” means for a smartphone

“Ex-proof” is shorthand for equipment carrying an explosion-protection marking (the Ex mark) that matches the area it enters. The marking states the protection concept, gas or dust group and temperature class, and it must be backed by a certificate issued by a recognised body, so a phone described as Ex-proof should come with a certificate number you can check.

The IS-04Z1 is designed to carry the marking II 2 G Ex ib IIC T4. That is a target: certification is in progress, no certificate has been issued, and SAMS will publish the certificate numbers when they exist.

In Greater Cairo. Refinery process units and fuel storage involve hydrocarbon gases, so a marking check would compare gas group and temperature class with the operator's data. Cement kiln fuel areas and factory solvent areas may differ. The IS-04Z1 targets II 2 G Ex ib IIC T4, subject to certification and the operator's acceptance.

Questions · Cairo

Frequently asked questions

Can we buy an Ex-proof mobile phone in Cairo today?

No. Nothing can be bought or taken into a classified area today: certification is in progress and launch is planned for Q4 2026. What is available now is the datasheet, the written certification schedule and evaluation units for safe-area use.

What does an Ex-proof mobile phone in Cairo cost?

SAMS has not published a price. Any figure you are offered for an uncertified Ex phone should be treated with caution; written indicative pricing is available for a defined quantity and site.

What Ex marking will the IS-04Z1 carry?

The marking target is II 2 G Ex ib IIC T4. It becomes a marking only when the certificate is issued; until then it is a design target.

Which areas at Greater Cairo would use it?

Likely candidates are refinery process units, fuel storage, cement kiln fuel areas, factory solvent and gas areas. Which areas are classified, and to what zone, is set by the site operator's own area classification drawings.

Is intrinsically safe the same as explosion proof?

Not exactly. Explosion proof is a broad everyday term; intrinsic safety is one protection concept, which limits energy so ignition cannot occur. Flameproof (Ex d) is a different concept that contains an explosion. The IS-04Z1 is intrinsically safe by design.

Does Cairo's dust and heat matter when choosing Ex phones?

Yes, Cairo is hot in summer, dusty and polluted, so check the dust ingress rating, the maximum ambient temperature on the certificate and the sealing of ports and charging contacts. Regular cleaning of connectors and spare batteries are practical for long outdoor shifts.

How should I plan deliveries to Cairo for a project elsewhere in Egypt?

Equipment typically arrives through Cairo airport or Egyptian ports and is cleared through customs before onward road or rail transport. Allow for traffic and customs time, check radio-equipment import rules and confirm who receives and stores devices at the site.

Can one approach cover refinery and cement sites?

Not necessarily. A refinery has hydrocarbon process units and storage, a cement plant has kiln fuel areas, and factories may have solvent or gas areas. Each operator sets its own classification and approval route. Request zone and gas group data from each site before assuming one device suits all.
