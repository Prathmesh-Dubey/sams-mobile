Field notes · Finland

More about Finland that matters for portable equipment

Finland: who works there

Neste was founded in 1948 as the state petrol company and was the third largest Finnish company by revenue in 2021, with the Finnish state holding 35.91% of shares in 2022.

Finland: background

Neste built Finland's first refinery at Naantali in 1957 and the Porvoo refinery at Sköldvik (Kilpilahti) in 1965, so Naantali and Porvoo are the traditional centres of refining.

Finland: industry on the ground

Finland lacks domestic fossil energy and imports substantial petroleum and natural gas, so refining, fuel terminals and gas-supply sites are mostly import-led rather than field-based.

Finland: industry on the ground

The industrial sector accounted for 53% of total final energy consumption in 2021, well above the IEA average, so process industry sites strongly shape the demand for classified-area equipment.

The term · Ex-proof

What “Ex-proof” means for a smartphone

“Ex-proof” is shorthand for equipment carrying an explosion-protection marking (the Ex mark) that matches the area it enters. The marking states the protection concept, gas or dust group and temperature class, and it must be backed by a certificate issued by a recognised body, so a phone described as Ex-proof should come with a certificate number you can check.

The IS-04Z1 is designed to carry the marking II 2 G Ex ib IIC T4. That is a target: certification is in progress, no certificate has been issued, and SAMS will publish the certificate numbers when they exist.

In Finland. A marking check at a Finnish site would read the gas group, temperature class and equipment category against the area classification. Hydrocarbon and hydrogen service at Kilpilahti may call for a different group than a pulp mill's dust areas. The IS-04Z1 marking target is II 2 G Ex ib IIC T4; it is a target, not a granted marking.

Questions · Finland

Frequently asked questions

Can we buy an Ex-proof smartphone in Finland today?

Not today. Until the certificates are issued the IS-04Z1 stays out of classified areas, and until launch there is nothing to purchase. You can ask for the datasheet and the certification schedule, or book a safe-area evaluation unit.

What does an Ex-proof smartphone in Finland cost?

No price is published. While certification is in progress there is no list price and no committed lead time, and a supplier quoting a firm figure for an uncertified Ex handset is promising something it cannot stand behind. Indicative pre-launch pricing is issued in writing against a stated quantity and site.

What Ex marking will the IS-04Z1 carry?

The marking target is II 2 G Ex ib IIC T4. It becomes a marking only when the certificate is issued; until then it is a design target.

Which sites in Finland does this cover?

The market guide for Finland covers Kilpilahti, Porvoo, Naantali, Kokkola, Harjavalta, among others. Site-level pages are linked below.

How will SAMS supply Finland?

Through authorised distributors once certificates exist. Until then SAMS can send the datasheet and certification schedule and arrange safe-area evaluation units.

Does Finland follow ATEX for handheld devices in hazardous areas?

Finland is an EU member, so ATEX Directive 2014/34/EU applies to equipment placed on the market and employers zone workplaces under Directive 1999/92/EC. Operators usually also apply their own permit and device registration processes. Ask the site safety office for its current list.

Where are Finland's main refining and fuel sites?

Neste built refineries at Naantali in 1957 and at Porvoo, in the Kilpilahti area, in 1965. Fuel terminals and gas-supply sites serve the rest of the country. Individual site rules and current operations should be confirmed with the operator before a visit.

Can a phone be used in cold Finnish winter conditions?

The IS-04Z1 is designed for industrial duty with IP68 sealing, but cold performance of any lithium battery needs testing. Ask for the datasheet's operating temperature range and trial a unit on an actual winter round. Site rules on charging and storage in safe areas still apply.
