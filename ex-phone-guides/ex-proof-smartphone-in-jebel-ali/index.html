Field notes · Jebel Ali

More about Jebel Ali that matters for portable equipment

Jebel Ali: infrastructure

With 67 berths, the harbour is described as the world's largest man-made harbour and the biggest port in the Middle East, so jetty and quay areas are extensive.

Jebel Ali: background

Jebel Ali Port opened in 1979, inaugurated on 26 February that year to supplement Port Rashid, so much of the district's infrastructure dates from the last 45 years.

Jebel Ali: logistics and access

The port handled about 13.7 million TEU in 2021, so container, truck and crane traffic is dense and site rules on vehicles and equipment are strict.

Jebel Ali: logistics and access

The port is linked to Dubai's expressway system and to the airport cargo village, which makes a four-hour ship-to-aircraft transfer possible for urgent spares.

The term · Ex-proof

What “Ex-proof” means for a smartphone

“Ex-proof” is shorthand for equipment carrying an explosion-protection marking (the Ex mark) that matches the area it enters. The marking states the protection concept, gas or dust group and temperature class, and it must be backed by a certificate issued by a recognised body, so a phone described as Ex-proof should come with a certificate number you can check.

The IS-04Z1 is designed to carry the marking II 2 G Ex ib IIC T4. That is a target: certification is in progress, no certificate has been issued, and SAMS will publish the certificate numbers when they exist.

In Jebel Ali. A marking check at Jebel Ali would compare each tenant's area drawings with the device marking. Smelter power-station fuel areas, oil terminals and chemical warehouses may differ in zone and gas group. The IS-04Z1 target is II 2 G Ex ib IIC T4, in progress and not certified.

Frequently asked questions

Can we buy an Ex-proof smartphone in Jebel Ali today?

Not today. Until the certificates are issued the IS-04Z1 stays out of classified areas, and until launch there is nothing to purchase. You can ask for the datasheet and the certification schedule, or book a safe-area evaluation unit.

What does an Ex-proof smartphone in Jebel Ali cost?

There is no list price yet. SAMS gives indicative pre-launch pricing in writing, against a stated quantity and site, and does not commit to a lead time before certification completes.

What Ex marking will the IS-04Z1 carry?

The marking target is II 2 G Ex ib IIC T4. It becomes a marking only when the certificate is issued; until then it is a design target.

Which areas at Jebel Ali would use it?

Likely candidates are gas-fired power station fuel systems, desalination and power plant gas supply, smelter power-station fuel areas, chemical and fuel warehousing. Which areas are classified, and to what zone, is set by the site operator's own area classification drawings.

Is intrinsically safe the same as explosion proof?

Not exactly. Explosion proof is a broad everyday term; intrinsic safety is one protection concept, which limits energy so ignition cannot occur. Flameproof (Ex d) is a different concept that contains an explosion. The IS-04Z1 is intrinsically safe by design.

Why are urgent spares often routed through Jebel Ali?

The port is connected to the airport cargo village, and its operator notes that a four-hour ship-to-aircraft transfer is possible. That helps when a part must reach a site quickly, but hazardous-area equipment still needs its certification documents and customs paperwork in place.

What do large open storage areas mean for equipment at Jebel Ali?

The port has a container yard of over one million square metres and about 960,000 square metres of open storage, so handsets used there face sun, heat and dust. Choose a sealed device with a stated operating temperature range and ingress protection rating.

How does humid coastal heat at Jebel Ali affect a handheld?

Conditions are hot and humid, with summer highs near 40 degrees C. The IS-04Z1 is designed to IP68 with a 4,000 mAh battery, but heat limits have not been published. Ask for the datasheet, and confirm with the site operator whether outdoor use is expected in peak summer.
