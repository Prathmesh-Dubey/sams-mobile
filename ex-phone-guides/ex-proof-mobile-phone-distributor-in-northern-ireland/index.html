More about Northern Ireland that matters for portable equipment

Northern Ireland: infrastructure

The electricity grid is operated by SONI and run by Northern Ireland Electricity, connected to the Republic by interconnectors of up to 1,200 MW and to Great Britain by the 500 MW Moyle interconnector.

Northern Ireland: industry on the ground

Machinery and equipment manufacturing, food processing, textile and electronics manufacturing are the leading industries, concentrated mostly in the eastern parts of Northern Ireland around Belfast.

Northern Ireland: logistics and access

The Belfast yard has sea access through the Victoria Channel with no locks or height restrictions, 24-hour ISPS-approved security, and two airports close by: George Best Belfast City five minutes away and Belfast International under 30 minutes.

Northern Ireland: industry on the ground

Key industries in 2024 include aerospace, agriculture, construction, engineering, health technology and manufacturing, and engineering is the largest manufacturing sub-sector, particularly aerospace and heavy machinery.

The term · Ex-proof

What “Ex-proof” means for a smartphone

“Ex-proof” is shorthand for equipment carrying an explosion-protection marking (the Ex mark) that matches the area it enters. The marking states the protection concept, gas or dust group and temperature class, and it must be backed by a certificate issued by a recognised body, so a phone described as Ex-proof should come with a certificate number you can check.

The IS-04Z1 is designed to carry the marking II 2 G Ex ib IIC T4. That is a target: certification is in progress, no certificate has been issued, and SAMS will publish the certificate numbers when they exist.

In Northern Ireland. A marking check at a gas-fired station or pressure reduction site would concentrate on natural gas, which is normally in a lower gas group than hydrogen, so IIC marking is conservative. The operator's zone map sets the requirement. The IS-04Z1 target marking is II 2 G Ex ib IIC T4, still pending certification.

Questions · Northern Ireland

Frequently asked questions

Can we buy an Ex-proof mobile phone in Northern Ireland today?

Not yet. The IS-04Z1 is pre-launch and its certification is still in progress ahead of a Q4 2026 launch. No unit goes into a classified area before the certificate covering it exists. The datasheet, the certification schedule and safe-area evaluation units are available now.

What does an Ex-proof mobile phone in Northern Ireland cost?

There is no list price yet. SAMS gives indicative pre-launch pricing in writing, against a stated quantity and site, and does not commit to a lead time before certification completes.

What Ex marking will the IS-04Z1 carry?

The marking target is II 2 G Ex ib IIC T4. It becomes a marking only when the certificate is issued; until then it is a design target.

Which areas at Northern Ireland would use it?

Likely candidates are gas-fired power station fuel systems, gas transmission pipeline assets, gas pressure reduction sites, fuel storage. Which areas are classified, and to what zone, is set by the site operator's own area classification drawings.

How do I become a SAMS distributor for Northern Ireland?

Apply through the distributor page with your company details and the territory you cover. SAMS reviews applications company by company and has not yet granted a territory for Northern Ireland.

Do UK ATEX and UKCA rules apply in Northern Ireland?

Northern Ireland remains under EU product rules for many goods, so CE and ATEX marking is generally relevant there, while Great Britain uses UKCA. Requirements can change, so confirm with the operator and the certifying body which marking is accepted for portable Ex equipment on your site.

Where in Northern Ireland are Zone 1 or Zone 2 areas most likely to be found?

Likely locations include fuel and gas terminals at Belfast harbour, gas network compressor and pressure reduction sites, food and feed plants with dust risks, and shipyard and marine repair work. Zoning is site-specific, so ask the operator for area classification drawings before choosing handheld devices.

What would drive demand for Ex equipment in the region?

Gas-fired power stations such as Ballylumford and Coolkeeragh, the Mutual Energy gas pipeline network from Islandmagee, pressure reduction sites and fuel storage. A proposed gas storage cavern project at Islandmagee has not been confirmed in the sources checked, so ask the operators about project timing.
