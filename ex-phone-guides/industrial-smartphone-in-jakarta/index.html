The term · industrial

What “industrial” means for a smartphone

“Industrial” describes a phone built for plant conditions: sealed, drop-resistant and manageable at fleet scale through Android Enterprise. It does not by itself mean the phone may enter a classified area. Only a device with a valid explosion-protection certificate and marking may do that.

The IS-04Z1 is designed to be both an industrial Android phone (IP68, 4,000 mAh battery, 50 MP camera, Android 16 with EMM and MDM support) and an intrinsically safe one. Its explosion-protection certificates are in progress and have not been issued.

In Jakarta (Tanjung Priok fuel terminals). Terminals sit inside a dense port and urban area, so site entry and permits are strictly controlled by each operator. Fleets would need MDM or EMM enrolment, visitor and contractor rules, and charging in safe areas. Connectivity is usually good in the city.

Frequently asked questions

Can we buy an industrial smartphone in Jakarta today?

Not yet. The IS-04Z1 is pre-launch and its certification is still in progress ahead of a Q4 2026 launch. No unit goes into a classified area before the certificate covering it exists. The datasheet, the certification schedule and safe-area evaluation units are available now.

What does an industrial smartphone in Jakarta cost?

SAMS has not published a price. Any figure you are offered for an uncertified Ex phone should be treated with caution; written indicative pricing is available for a defined quantity and site.

Is the IS-04Z1 an industrial phone as well as an Ex phone?

Yes by design: IP68 sealing, a 4,000 mAh battery, a 50 MP camera and Android 16 with EMM and MDM support. The explosion-protection certificates are separate and still in progress.

Which areas at Jakarta (Tanjung Priok fuel terminals) would use it?

Likely candidates are fuel storage terminals, tanker loading and jetties, port fuel handling. Which areas are classified, and to what zone, is set by the site operator's own area classification drawings.

Is intrinsically safe the same as explosion proof?

Not exactly. Explosion proof is a broad everyday term; intrinsic safety is one protection concept, which limits energy so ignition cannot occur. Flameproof (Ex d) is a different concept that contains an explosion. The IS-04Z1 is intrinsically safe by design.

Will the capital move affect suppliers based in Jakarta?

The legal groundwork to move the capital to Nusantara was laid in 2022, but legal capital functions remain in Jakarta until a presidential decree. Practically, most corporate offices and agencies you deal with are still based in Jakarta, so check current arrangements before relocating staff.

Where are the industrial sites around Jakarta?

Industrial estates have developed across Bogor, Depok, Tangerang and Bekasi along major transport corridors, outside the city boundary. Typical hazardous activities there include chemical, fuel and manufacturing plants, so confirm the classification with each plant.

How are visitors and contractors handled at Jakarta fuel terminals?

Entry and device rules are controlled by each operator, and dense urban surroundings mean permits and security checks are strict. Contractors usually follow the terminal's approved equipment list. Ask the operator's HSE team for requirements before bringing any device.
