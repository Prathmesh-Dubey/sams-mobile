The term · explosion proof

What “explosion proof” means for a smartphone

“Explosion proof” is the phrase most buyers use for equipment that may be taken into a classified hazardous area. Strictly, it covers several protection concepts: a flameproof enclosure (Ex d) contains an internal explosion, while intrinsic safety (Ex i) limits electrical energy so that an ignition cannot start. Phones are normally intrinsically safe, because a flameproof handset would be too heavy to carry all shift.

The IS-04Z1 uses intrinsic safety, with a marking target of Ex ib IIC T4, and is designed for Zone 1 and Zone 21. It is pre-launch, certification is in progress and no certificate has been issued.

In Hazira, Surat. At Hazira the phrase explosion-proof covers both gas plant equipment and dust areas. Flameproof Ex d suits fixed gear in LNG regasification and gas processing, while a carried handheld depends on intrinsic safety. Dust areas from heavy engineering and powder handling need their own protection approach.

Frequently asked questions

Can we buy an explosion proof smartphone in Hazira India today?

Not today. Until the certificates are issued the IS-04Z1 stays out of classified areas, and until launch there is nothing to purchase. You can ask for the datasheet and the certification schedule, or book a safe-area evaluation unit.

What does an explosion proof smartphone in Hazira India cost?

There is no list price yet. SAMS gives indicative pre-launch pricing in writing, against a stated quantity and site, and does not commit to a lead time before certification completes.

Is the IS-04Z1 explosion proof or intrinsically safe?

It is intrinsically safe (marking target Ex ib IIC T4), which is the usual way to make a phone for hazardous areas. It is not a flameproof (Ex d) design, and certification is still in progress.

Which areas at Hazira, Surat would use it?

Likely candidates are lng regasification, gas processing, petrochemical plants, marine terminals. Which areas are classified, and to what zone, is set by the site operator's own area classification drawings.

How do I become a SAMS distributor for Hazira, Surat?

Apply through the distributor page with your company details and the territory you cover. SAMS reviews applications company by company and has not yet granted a territory for Hazira, Surat.

Can third parties use the Hazira LNG terminal?

The terminal was opened to other companies in July 2010, which means more operators and contractors can be present on site. Each party's contractors usually follow their own induction, so confirm who controls the area you will visit.

Does humidity matter at a coastal site like Hazira?

Hazira is close to the Arabian Sea and the Tapti River, with a monsoon of about 1,200 millimetres, so humidity and salt air are normal. Choose sealed devices and ask the supplier about corrosion resistance.

Does Hazira have dust hazards as well as gas hazards?

Yes, the area includes both Zone 1 gas areas and Zone 21 dust areas, the latter linked to powder handling and heavy engineering. A gas marking does not cover dust. Check the dust classification with the plant operator before any device is taken into those areas.
