Field notes · Angola

More about Angola that matters for portable equipment

Angola: industry on the ground

Angola LNG at Soyo in Zaire Province is the country's first LNG export plant, a single train rated at about 5.2 million tonnes a year and built to use offshore associated gas.

Angola: industry on the ground

The Soyo plant uses ConocoPhillips' Optimized Cascade liquefaction process and also produces propane, butane and condensate, which means pressurised hydrocarbon storage and loading areas.

Angola: background

Sonangol was created as a state company in 1976 after the nationalisation of ANGOL, and later grew subsidiaries in seismic, engineering, shipping and telecommunications services.

Angola: logistics and access

Sonangol's logistics arm SONILS runs an onshore petroleum supply base of about two million square metres in Luanda Bay with a 2 km quay, inaugurated in 1995 and later extended in 1998, 2004, 2008 and 2012.

The term · intrinsically safe

What “intrinsically safe” means for a smartphone

Intrinsic safety limits the electrical and thermal energy in a circuit so that neither a spark nor a hot surface can ignite a gas or dust atmosphere, even when a fault occurs. The ib level, used for Zone 1, tolerates one fault. It is the usual way to make a handheld device that a worker carries continuously through a hazardous area.

The IS-04Z1 is designed on this principle for Zone 1 and Zone 21 (marking target II 2 G Ex ib IIC T4). Intrinsic safety is a design basis, not a certificate: certification is in progress and none has been issued yet.

In Angola. Offshore rounds in Angola involve helidecks, boat transfers, wet decks and process modules where there is no safe socket mid shift. An intrinsically safe handheld is designed to be taken straight onto the deck for photographs, tag scans and gauge readings, which a standard phone could not do. The 4,000 mAh battery is sized for a working day.

Questions · Angola

Frequently asked questions

Can we buy an intrinsically safe smartphone in Angola today?

Not yet. The IS-04Z1 is pre-launch and its certification is still in progress ahead of a Q4 2026 launch. No unit goes into a classified area before the certificate covering it exists. The datasheet, the certification schedule and safe-area evaluation units are available now.

What does an intrinsically safe smartphone in Angola cost?

SAMS has not published a price. Any figure you are offered for an uncertified Ex phone should be treated with caution; written indicative pricing is available for a defined quantity and site.

What does ‘intrinsically safe’ mean for the IS-04Z1?

The circuits are designed to limit energy so a spark or hot surface cannot ignite a gas or dust atmosphere, even with one fault (level ib, Zone 1). Certification of that design is in progress.

Which sites in Angola does this cover?

The market guide for Angola covers Luanda, Soyo, Zaire province, Cabinda and Malongo, Lobito, Benguela, among others. Site-level pages are linked below.

How will SAMS supply Angola?

Through authorised distributors once certificates exist. Until then SAMS can send the datasheet and certification schedule and arrange safe-area evaluation units.

Who approves mobile phones and radios in Angola?

INACOM, the Instituto Angolano das Comunicações, is the telecom regulator whose homologation applies to wireless and telecom equipment. Local agents often handle the filing and fees. Check with INACOM or an agent which documents and test reports are currently required for your product.

Should documentation for Angolan projects be in Portuguese?

Portuguese is the official language, so local authorities and crews will normally expect manuals, permits and safety notices in Portuguese. International operators often work in English as well, so ask the operator or contractor which languages are required for induction and equipment documentation.

Do I need INACOM approval and Portuguese documentation?

Radio equipment needs type approval from INACOM before it is used on local networks, and documentation in Portuguese is expected. The handset supports Portuguese language profiles. Local content rules also favour supply through an Angolan company. Check the current position with your operator's procurement team and with the partner who would handle importing.
