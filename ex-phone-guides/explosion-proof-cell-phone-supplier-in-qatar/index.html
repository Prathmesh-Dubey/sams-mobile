More about Qatar that matters for portable equipment

Qatar: rules and approvals

Qatar's Communications Regulatory Authority issues a certificate of type approval for radio and telecom equipment based on EU and FCC standards, with no local representative and no fixed expiry.

Qatar: infrastructure

Ras Laffan Port encloses about 4,500 hectares of water and is described as the largest artificial harbour in the world, with the world's largest LNG export facility.

Qatar: geography and layout

The North Field, found in 1971, is the world's biggest natural gas field, covering about 6,000 square kilometres offshore, and it supplies the plants at Ras Laffan.

Qatar: climate and equipment wear

Qatar is flat, dry desert with about 70 millimetres of rain a year, mild winters and summers that often reach 40 degrees Celsius.

The term · explosion proof

What “explosion proof” means for a smartphone

“Explosion proof” is the phrase most buyers use for equipment that may be taken into a classified hazardous area. Strictly, it covers several protection concepts: a flameproof enclosure (Ex d) contains an internal explosion, while intrinsic safety (Ex i) limits electrical energy so that an ignition cannot start. Phones are normally intrinsically safe, because a flameproof handset would be too heavy to carry all shift.

The IS-04Z1 uses intrinsic safety, with a marking target of Ex ib IIC T4, and is designed for Zone 1 and Zone 21. It is pre-launch, certification is in progress and no certificate has been issued.

In Qatar. At Ras Laffan and Mesaieed, explosion proof is shorthand, but the concepts differ. Flameproof Ex d suits fixed motors and fittings, while the IS-04Z1 uses intrinsic safety, Ex ib, which suits a handset carried through liquefaction trains, GTL plants and QAFCO or QAPCO units where one person visits many areas.

Questions · Qatar

Frequently asked questions

Can we buy an explosion proof cell phone in Qatar today?

Not today. Until the certificates are issued the IS-04Z1 stays out of classified areas, and until launch there is nothing to purchase. You can ask for the datasheet and the certification schedule, or book a safe-area evaluation unit.

What does an explosion proof cell phone in Qatar cost?

There is no list price yet. SAMS gives indicative pre-launch pricing in writing, against a stated quantity and site, and does not commit to a lead time before certification completes.

Is the IS-04Z1 explosion proof or intrinsically safe?

It is intrinsically safe (marking target Ex ib IIC T4), which is the usual way to make a phone for hazardous areas. It is not a flameproof (Ex d) design, and certification is still in progress.

Which sites in Qatar does this cover?

The market guide for Qatar covers Ras Laffan Industrial City, Mesaieed Industrial Area, North Field, Dukhan, among others. Site-level pages are linked below.

How will SAMS supply Qatar?

Through authorised distributors once certificates exist. Until then SAMS can send the datasheet and certification schedule and arrange safe-area evaluation units.

Is access to Ras Laffan restricted?

Industrial cities typically have gate and permit systems run by their administration and plant operators. Contractors normally need sponsorship and induction, so ask your host.

Where are Qatar's main hazardous-area sites?

Ras Laffan Industrial City in the north for LNG, gas-to-liquids and refining, and Mesaieed Industrial City in the south, a prime industrial zone and tanking centre. Both are administered by QatarEnergy subsidiaries.

Does the radio need separate approval in Qatar?

The radio inside the handset needs separate CRA type approval, and conformity for the imported product is administered by the Ministry of Commerce and Industry. Both sit inside the launch programme and neither has been granted. Confirm with the site operator which evidence is needed before bringing a device in.
