Field notes · Egypt

More about Egypt that matters for portable equipment

Egypt: rules and approvals

Electrical safety and general product conformity are overseen separately by the Egyptian Organization for Standardization and Quality and the General Organization for Export and Import Control.

Egypt: geography and layout

The Suez Canal Economic Zone, established in 2015, covers about 461 square kilometres on both banks of the canal and includes dry port projects at Ismailia and 10th Ramadan.

Egypt: rules and approvals

NTRA approval typically needs a local legal representative or importer and accepts international CE or FCC test reports, with certificates valid for a limited period, often one year.

Egypt: rules and approvals

The National Telecom Regulatory Authority must approve any equipment that uses radio frequencies or connects to public networks, including mobile phones, before it can be imported or sold in Egypt.

The term · Ex-proof

What “Ex-proof” means for a smartphone

“Ex-proof” is shorthand for equipment carrying an explosion-protection marking (the Ex mark) that matches the area it enters. The marking states the protection concept, gas or dust group and temperature class, and it must be backed by a certificate issued by a recognised body, so a phone described as Ex-proof should come with a certificate number you can check.

The IS-04Z1 is designed to carry the marking II 2 G Ex ib IIC T4. That is a target: certification is in progress, no certificate has been issued, and SAMS will publish the certificate numbers when they exist.

In Egypt. A marking check in Egypt would read temperature class first, as an instrumentation engineer does, then gas group and ambient range. The Western Desert and Gulf of Suez run extreme dry heat while Idku and Damietta add Mediterranean humidity. The target is II 2 G Ex ib IIC T4, with Zone 21 under the same programme.

Frequently asked questions

Can we buy an Ex-proof cell phone in Egypt today?

No. Nothing can be bought or taken into a classified area today: certification is in progress and launch is planned for Q4 2026. What is available now is the datasheet, the written certification schedule and evaluation units for safe-area use.

What does an Ex-proof cell phone in Egypt cost?

No price is published. While certification is in progress there is no list price and no committed lead time, and a supplier quoting a firm figure for an uncertified Ex handset is promising something it cannot stand behind. Indicative pre-launch pricing is issued in writing against a stated quantity and site.

What Ex marking will the IS-04Z1 carry?

The marking target is II 2 G Ex ib IIC T4. It becomes a marking only when the certificate is issued; until then it is a design target.

Which sites in Egypt does this cover?

The market guide for Egypt covers Gulf of Suez, Zohr field, Idku, Damietta, among others. Site-level pages are linked below.

How do I become a SAMS distributor for Egypt?

Apply through the distributor page with your company details and the territory you cover. SAMS reviews applications company by company and has not yet granted a territory for Egypt.

How should devices be specified for Egypt's desert sites?

Western Desert and Gulf of Suez sites typically combine high summer heat, dust and wind-blown sand, with salt air on the coast. Check the operating temperature range, dust ingress rating and enclosure corrosion resistance, and confirm any site limits with the operator or contractor.

Does Egypt separate electrical safety from telecom approval?

Yes. NTRA covers radio and telecom approval, while the Egyptian Organization for Standardization and Quality and the General Organization for Export and Import Control cover electrical safety and general product conformity. A device with batteries or chargers may need to satisfy both, so check each body's scope.

Is EOS conformity enough for EGPC or Eni sites?

No: EGPC and its operating affiliates, along with international operators like Eni, each run vendor-registration and engineering-approval processes, so conformity alone does not guarantee listing. EOS and NTRA approvals are being pursued and are not finished. Confirm the route with the operator's procurement team.
