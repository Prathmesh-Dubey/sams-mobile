Timika and the Grasberg mining district: geography and layout

The Grasberg mine lies in Mimika Regency, Central Papua, near Puncak Jaya, and the original mine was built at about 4,100 metres above sea level, with the open pit rim at roughly 4,270 metres.

Timika and the Grasberg mining district: logistics and access

Early construction included a 116 km road and pipeline, a port, an airstrip, a power plant and a new town, so the operation is largely self-contained and supplied by sea and air.

Timika and the Grasberg mining district: logistics and access

Mozes Kilangin Airport at Timika has direct domestic flights to Jayapura, Nabire, Sorong, Makassar and Bali, plus short flights to interior districts of Mimika such as Jila, Tsinga and Alama.

Timika and the Grasberg mining district: industry on the ground

Grasberg has a large open pit completed in 2019, three operating underground mines, the Block Cave, Deep Mill Level Zone and Big Gossan, and four concentrators, milling more than 72 million tonnes of ore in 2023.

The term · rugged

What “rugged” means for a smartphone

A rugged phone is built to survive water, dust, drops and rough handling, usually rated by an IP code and a drop specification. Ruggedness is an environmental property: it says nothing about whether a phone may be used where flammable gas or dust is present. That is decided by explosion-protection certification and marking.

The IS-04Z1 combines both: an IP68 sealed rugged build and a design for Zone 1 and Zone 21 (marking target Ex ib IIC T4). The explosion-protection certificates are in progress and none has been issued.

In Timika and the Grasberg mining district. Timika averages about 5,690 mm of rain a year, and Grasberg sits at about 4,100 metres, cooler and very wet. IP68 helps against water and dust but is not explosion protection. Check the rated temperature range and condensation behaviour at altitude.

Can we buy a rugged smartphone in Timika Mining today?

Not today. Until the certificates are issued the IS-04Z1 stays out of classified areas, and until launch there is nothing to purchase. You can ask for the datasheet and the certification schedule, or book a safe-area evaluation unit.

How rugged is the IS-04Z1?

It is designed to IP68 sealing, with glove-friendly controls. Ruggedness is not explosion protection: that depends on the certificates, which are in progress.

Which areas at Timika and the Grasberg mining district would use it?

Likely candidates are concentrator and process plant, fuel storage and distribution, concentrate port and dewatering, underground mine areas. Which areas are classified, and to what zone, is set by the site operator's own area classification drawings.

How do I become a SAMS distributor for Timika and the Grasberg mining district?

What makes the Timika and Grasberg site different for handheld devices?

Altitude, heavy rain, long underground haulage and remote supply make it demanding. Underground mines can have gas or dust, and surface plants have flotation chemicals, so confirm area classification with the operator and plan for battery life and spares.

How is equipment normally delivered to Timika?

The operation uses its own port at Amamapare, an airstrip and long access roads built for the mine, and Timika is the regional town. Expect long lead times, customs clearance in Indonesia and site security and induction procedures before devices go underground.

How does altitude and rain at Grasberg affect a handset?

Grasberg sits at about 4,100 metres and the region is very wet, so temperature, condensation and battery behaviour matter. Check the rated operating range and sealing. IP68 is not explosion protection. Confirm site requirements with the operator.
