Field notes · Portugal

More about Portugal that matters for portable equipment

Portugal: projects and investment

Portugal aims to cover 80% of electricity consumption with renewables by 2030 and has developed a hydrogen strategy to reduce gas imports, which may add hydrogen-classified areas.

Portugal: who works there

Galp Energia, formed in 1999 from Petrogal, Gás de Portugal and Transgás, is Portugal's largest oil and gas group, with business units covering upstream, refining and midstream, commercial, and renewables.

Portugal: background

The Sines hard coal power plant operated from 1985-1989 until January 2021 and coal-fired generation ended in 2021, so former coal sites are being redeveloped.

Portugal: industry on the ground

Oil made up 44% of Portugal's total energy supply in 2023, so fuel storage, port terminals and distribution depots form a large part of the classified-area infrastructure.

The term · IECEx

What “IECEx” means for a smartphone

IECEx is the international scheme for certifying equipment used in explosive atmospheres. A certificate of conformity is issued by an accredited certification body against a specific edition of the IEC 60079 standards and carries a schedule of conditions of safe use. It is not a legal requirement in most countries on its own, but many national schemes accept it as the technical basis.

The IECEx certificate for the IS-04Z1 is in progress and has not been issued. The marking target is Ex ib IIC T4, the intrinsic safety concept for Zone 1 gas with the hardest gas group (IIC) and a 135 °C temperature class.

In Portugal. Sines and Estarreja involve international groups such as Repsol and Dow, plus EPC and maintenance contractors, so IECEx offers a shared scheme beside ATEX. Ask for the certificate of conformity and its schedule of conditions, which fix the marking, ambient range and conditions of use. The IS-04Z1 has none yet issued, and the programme is in progress.

Questions · Portugal

Frequently asked questions

Can we buy an IECEx smartphone in Portugal today?

Not today. Until the certificates are issued the IS-04Z1 stays out of classified areas, and until launch there is nothing to purchase. You can ask for the datasheet and the certification schedule, or book a safe-area evaluation unit.

What does an IECEx smartphone in Portugal cost?

SAMS has not published a price. Any figure you are offered for an uncertified Ex phone should be treated with caution; written indicative pricing is available for a defined quantity and site.

Does the IS-04Z1 hold an IECEx certificate?

No. The IECEx certificate is in progress and has not been issued. SAMS will publish the number when it exists.

Which sites in Portugal does this cover?

The market guide for Portugal covers Sines, ZILS Alentejo, Estarreja, Matosinhos and Leça, among others. Site-level pages are linked below.

How do I become a SAMS distributor for Portugal?

Apply through the distributor page with your company details and the territory you cover. SAMS reviews applications company by company and has not yet granted a territory for Portugal.

Does Portugal follow ATEX for handheld devices?

Portugal is an EU member, so ATEX Directive 2014/34/EU applies to equipment and Directive 1999/92/EC to workplace zoning. Zone 1 and Zone 2 devices should carry CE and Ex markings with instructions in Portuguese where required. Operators usually add their own permit and registration procedures.

Why is Sines important for hazardous-area work in Portugal?

Sines hosts Portugal's LNG import terminal, with three storage tanks totalling 390,000 cubic metres and a regasification capacity of 5.6 million tonnes a year. Terminal and port operators set their own induction and device rules, so confirm them before sending equipment.

Is dust protection part of the programme?

Yes, Zone 21 dust protection belongs to the same certification programme as the gas marking. In pulp, cement and mineral processing the powder area is the plant, not a corner of it, so check the final certificate and the site's own dust classification with the operator.
