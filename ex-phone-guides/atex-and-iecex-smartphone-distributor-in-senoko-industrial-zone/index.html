The term · ATEX and IECEx

What “ATEX and IECEx” means for a smartphone

ATEX is the European legal framework for equipment used in explosive atmospheres (Directive 2014/34/EU). IECEx is the international certification scheme built on the IEC 60079 standards. Buyers outside Europe often ask for both: an IECEx certificate is the technical evidence most national regimes build on, while ATEX is the legal route into the European market.

For the IS-04Z1 both routes are in progress with TÜV in Germany and neither certificate has been issued. The marking target is II 2 G Ex ib IIC T4: equipment Group II (surface industry), Category 2 (Zone 1 gas), protection concept ib (intrinsic safety), gas group IIC and temperature class T4.

In Senoko. Reviewers at a power station such as Senoko would expect ATEX and IECEx documents side by side, since gas turbine and fuel-system work draws on equipment suppliers and engineers from several countries. The IS-04Z1 certification is in progress, so only a datasheet is available now. The plant owner decides which certificates and which conformity schedule it will accept.

Questions · Senoko Industrial Zone

Frequently asked questions

Can we buy an ATEX and IECEx smartphone in Senoko Industrial Zone today?

No. Nothing can be bought or taken into a classified area today: certification is in progress and launch is planned for Q4 2026. What is available now is the datasheet, the written certification schedule and evaluation units for safe-area use.

What does an ATEX and IECEx smartphone in Senoko Industrial Zone cost?

There is no list price yet. SAMS gives indicative pre-launch pricing in writing, against a stated quantity and site, and does not commit to a lead time before certification completes.

Is the IS-04Z1 ATEX and IECEx certified?

Not yet. Both routes are in progress with TÜV in Germany and no certificate has been issued. Any listing that says otherwise is wrong.

Which areas at Senoko would use it?

Likely candidates are power plant gas and fuel systems, fuel storage tanks and terminal services. Which areas are classified, and to what zone, is set by the site operator's own area classification drawings.

How do I become a SAMS distributor for Senoko?

Apply through the distributor page with your company details and the territory you cover. SAMS reviews applications company by company and has not yet granted a territory for Senoko.

Is the whole Senoko estate a hazardous area?

No. Classified areas are concentrated at the power station's gas systems and at fuel storage, not across the whole industrial zone. Other tenants may have none at all. Ask the specific site for its area classification drawing before choosing equipment.

What type of hazardous areas does a gas-fired power station have?

Typical examples are gas pressure-reduction and metering stations, gas turbine enclosures, fuel oil storage and pump areas, and battery or hydrogen-related rooms. Most are Zone 2 in normal operation, but Zone 1 can occur around specific equipment, so confirm with the plant.

Who approves contractor devices at the power station?

The plant operator runs its own contractor and equipment approvals, and SAMS has no role in them. Send its procurement and HSE teams the IS-04Z1 datasheet and target marking, noting that certification is in progress and no certificate has been issued.
