Field notes · Canada

More about Canada that matters for portable equipment

Canada: geography and layout

Fort McMurray sits in the middle of the Athabasca oil sands, 435 km north-east of Edmonton on Highway 63, surrounded by boreal forest, so access is largely by one road or by air.

Canada: rules and approvals

Radio and wireless devices sold in Canada need ISED certification against the Radio Standards Specifications, such as RSS-247 for Wi-Fi and Bluetooth and RSS-Gen for general requirements and labelling.

Canada: climate and equipment wear

Fort McMurray averages minus 17.2 degrees Celsius in January, with a recorded low of minus 53.3 degrees, so batteries, touchscreens and enclosures need to work in deep cold.

Canada: rules and approvals

Canada also accepts field certification for custom-made or low-volume equipment, evaluated at the installation site, which is similar to unit verification under the IECEx and ATEX systems.

The term · IECEx

What “IECEx” means for a smartphone

IECEx is the international scheme for certifying equipment used in explosive atmospheres. A certificate of conformity is issued by an accredited certification body against a specific edition of the IEC 60079 standards and carries a schedule of conditions of safe use. It is not a legal requirement in most countries on its own, but many national schemes accept it as the technical basis.

The IECEx certificate for the IS-04Z1 is in progress and has not been issued. The marking target is Ex ib IIC T4, the intrinsic safety concept for Zone 1 gas with the hardest gas group (IIC) and a 135 °C temperature class.

In Canada. IECEx is the scheme visiting international operators and EPCs may bring to Canada, from Kitimat LNG to the Grand Banks, but Rule 18-050 requires equipment certified by a body accredited by the Standards Council of Canada. The certificate of conformity and schedule of conditions would be read alongside that. Nothing has been issued yet.

Questions · Canada

Frequently asked questions

Can we buy an IECEx smartphone in Canada today?

Not today. Until the certificates are issued the IS-04Z1 stays out of classified areas, and until launch there is nothing to purchase. You can ask for the datasheet and the certification schedule, or book a safe-area evaluation unit.

What does an IECEx smartphone in Canada cost?

No price is published. While certification is in progress there is no list price and no committed lead time, and a supplier quoting a firm figure for an uncertified Ex handset is promising something it cannot stand behind. Indicative pre-launch pricing is issued in writing against a stated quantity and site.

Does the IS-04Z1 hold an IECEx certificate?

No. The IECEx certificate is in progress and has not been issued. SAMS will publish the number when it exists.

Which sites in Canada does this cover?

The market guide for Canada covers Athabasca oil sands, Cold Lake and the Lakeland, Alberta Industrial Heartland, Montney and Duvernay, among others. Site-level pages are linked below.

How will SAMS supply Canada?

Through authorised distributors once certificates exist. Until then SAMS can send the datasheet and certification schedule and arrange safe-area evaluation units.

Does a phone for Canadian oil sands sites need Canadian certification?

Hazardous-location electrical equipment in Canada is generally expected to carry certification from a Standards Council of Canada accredited body, and IECEx or ATEX certificates are not accepted as a substitute, although they can speed the evaluation. Confirm the required marking with the site operator and a Canadian certification body.

How cold-tolerant should devices be for northern Alberta?

Fort McMurray averages about minus 17 degrees Celsius in January and has seen extremes below minus 50, so check the datasheet's operating and charging temperature range, battery behaviour in cold, and glove-friendly controls. Confirm any site cold-weather procedures with the operator before deployment.

Will the battery work on cold winter shifts?

Every battery gives up capacity in the cold, and the 4,000 mAh cell is sized for a normal shift, not extreme cold. Unless a certificate says otherwise, hazardous area equipment is assessed for minus 20 to plus 40 degrees Celsius, and the marked range will be published when the certificate issues. Check it first.
