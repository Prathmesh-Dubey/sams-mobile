Field notes · Algeria

More about Algeria that matters for portable equipment

Algeria: background

Oil was discovered at Hassi Messaoud in 1956, and the town sits 85 kilometres south-east of Ouargla in a thinly populated part of the Sahara.

Algeria: geography and layout

Algeria spans 2,381,741 square kilometres, making it the largest country in Africa, with the Sahara covering most of the territory and the population concentrated in the fertile, mountainous north.

Algeria: rules and approvals

Telecom equipment approvals are handled by two bodies: ARPCE as telecoms regulator for equipment such as mobile phones and Wi-Fi devices, and ANF for radio spectrum and some short-range devices.

Algeria: rules and approvals

Algerian approval is described as relying largely on international test reports such as EU CE reports, and a local representative is still needed for the application.

The term · intrinsically safe

What “intrinsically safe” means for a smartphone

Intrinsic safety limits the electrical and thermal energy in a circuit so that neither a spark nor a hot surface can ignite a gas or dust atmosphere, even when a fault occurs. The ib level, used for Zone 1, tolerates one fault. It is the usual way to make a handheld device that a worker carries continuously through a hazardous area.

The IS-04Z1 is designed on this principle for Zone 1 and Zone 21 (marking target II 2 G Ex ib IIC T4). Intrinsic safety is a design basis, not a certificate: certification is in progress and none has been issued yet.

In Algeria. Rounds across wellsites, manifolds and pipeline stations in the Sahara mean long, repeated trips through classified areas, often with no live-line socket nearby. An intrinsically safe handheld is designed to be carried into those areas as it is, to photograph a gauge, scan a tag and log a reading, without a hot-work or phone-surrender routine at every gate.

Questions · Algeria

Frequently asked questions

Can we buy an intrinsically safe cell phone in Algeria today?

Not today. Until the certificates are issued the IS-04Z1 stays out of classified areas, and until launch there is nothing to purchase. You can ask for the datasheet and the certification schedule, or book a safe-area evaluation unit.

What does an intrinsically safe cell phone in Algeria cost?

No price is published. While certification is in progress there is no list price and no committed lead time, and a supplier quoting a firm figure for an uncertified Ex handset is promising something it cannot stand behind. Indicative pre-launch pricing is issued in writing against a stated quantity and site.

What does ‘intrinsically safe’ mean for the IS-04Z1?

The circuits are designed to limit energy so a spark or hot surface cannot ignite a gas or dust atmosphere, even with one fault (level ib, Zone 1). Certification of that design is in progress.

Which sites in Algeria does this cover?

The market guide for Algeria covers Hassi Messaoud, Ouargla, Hassi R'Mel, Laghouat, In Amenas, Illizi, In Salah, Tamanrasset, among others. Site-level pages are linked below.

How do I become a SAMS distributor for Algeria?

Apply through the distributor page with your company details and the territory you cover. SAMS reviews applications company by company and has not yet granted a territory for Algeria.

What heat should devices be rated for in the Algerian Sahara?

Hassi Messaoud averages above 40 degrees Celsius for four months and above 45 degrees in July, so check the operating, charging and storage ranges on the datasheet and keep devices out of direct sun and closed vehicles. Confirm any site limits with the operator or contractor.

Should documents for Algerian field sites be in French or Arabic?

Arabic and Amazigh are the official languages, and French is also commonly used in business, so manuals and safety notices are often supplied in more than one language. Ask the operator or contractor which languages are required for permits, induction and equipment documentation.

Does the phone need Algerian radio approval as well as an Ex certificate?

Radio equipment needs homologation by ARPCE, the communications regulator, before import and use, separately from the Ex certificate. Both are in progress for the IS-04Z1 and neither is finished. Confirm the current requirement with Sonatrach or the partner handling procurement, who will also tell you whether French documentation is expected.
