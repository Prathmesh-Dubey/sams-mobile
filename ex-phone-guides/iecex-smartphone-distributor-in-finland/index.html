IECEx Smartphone Distributor in Finland

The IS-04Z1 is an intrinsically safe Android handset designed for Zone 1 and Zone 21, built for the refinery and polymer plants at Kilpilahti, the metal and chemical parks on the Bothnian coast and the mill and mine sites where a winter round is measured in kilometres. The Finnish Safety and Chemicals Agency, Tukes, monitors safety in technology, chemicals and hazardous materials, with divisions for facilities and mines and for products and equipment, and is Finland's competent authority for REACH, CLP and Seveso III. An IECEx smartphone distributor in Finland would carry the IS-04Z1 to these buyers once certification completes.

Field notes · Finland

More about Finland that matters for portable equipment

Finland: industry on the ground

Finland lacks domestic fossil energy and imports substantial petroleum and natural gas, so refining, fuel terminals and gas-supply sites are mostly import-led rather than field-based.

Finland: industry on the ground

The industrial sector accounted for 53% of total final energy consumption in 2021, well above the IEA average, so process industry sites strongly shape the demand for classified-area equipment.

Finland: infrastructure

About 2.8 million Finns rely on district heating, so combined heat and power plants and fuel handling sites exist in nearly every town.

Finland: industry on the ground

Bioenergy and waste make up 33.6% of total energy supply and peat 2.7%, with Finland one of the last countries still burning peat, which brings fuel-dust handling into power and heat plants.

The term · IECEx

What “IECEx” means for a smartphone

IECEx is the international scheme for certifying equipment used in explosive atmospheres. A certificate of conformity is issued by an accredited certification body against a specific edition of the IEC 60079 standards and carries a schedule of conditions of safe use. It is not a legal requirement in most countries on its own, but many national schemes accept it as the technical basis.

The IECEx certificate for the IS-04Z1 is in progress and has not been issued. The marking target is Ex ib IIC T4, the intrinsic safety concept for Zone 1 gas with the hardest gas group (IIC) and a 135 °C temperature class.

In Finland. Finland hosts operators such as Neste and Borealis Polymers, and large plants draw in international contractors for shutdowns. For them the international scheme is a familiar reference: a certificate of conformity names the equipment, and its schedule of conditions lists any special conditions of use. IECEx certification for the IS-04Z1 is in progress, so nothing can be cited yet.

Questions · Finland

Frequently asked questions

Can we buy an IECEx smartphone in Finland today?

Not today. Until the certificates are issued the IS-04Z1 stays out of classified areas, and until launch there is nothing to purchase. You can ask for the datasheet and the certification schedule, or book a safe-area evaluation unit.

What does an IECEx smartphone in Finland cost?

There is no list price yet. SAMS gives indicative pre-launch pricing in writing, against a stated quantity and site, and does not commit to a lead time before certification completes.

Does the IS-04Z1 hold an IECEx certificate?

No. The IECEx certificate is in progress and has not been issued. SAMS will publish the number when it exists.

Which sites in Finland does this cover?

The market guide for Finland covers Kilpilahti, Porvoo, Naantali, Kokkola, Harjavalta, among others. Site-level pages are linked below.

How do I become a SAMS distributor for Finland?

Apply through the distributor page with your company details and the territory you cover. SAMS reviews applications company by company and has not yet granted a territory for Finland.

Does Finland follow ATEX for handheld devices in hazardous areas?

Finland is an EU member, so ATEX Directive 2014/34/EU applies to equipment placed on the market and employers zone workplaces under Directive 1999/92/EC. Operators usually also apply their own permit and device registration processes. Ask the site safety office for its current list.

Where are Finland's main refining and fuel sites?

Neste built refineries at Naantali in 1957 and at Porvoo, in the Kilpilahti area, in 1965. Fuel terminals and gas-supply sites serve the rest of the country. Individual site rules and current operations should be confirmed with the operator before a visit.

Can a phone be used in cold Finnish winter conditions?

The IS-04Z1 is designed for industrial duty with IP68 sealing, but cold performance of any lithium battery needs testing. Ask for the datasheet's operating temperature range and trial a unit on an actual winter round. Site rules on charging and storage in safe areas still apply.
