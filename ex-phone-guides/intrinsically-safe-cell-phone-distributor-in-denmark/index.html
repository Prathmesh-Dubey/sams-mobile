Field notes · Denmark

More about Denmark that matters for portable equipment

Denmark: logistics and access

Denmark imports about 12% of its energy but is also an exporter, and its electricity grid is connected by transmission lines to other European countries.

Denmark: background

North Sea natural gas projects began in 1984 and North Sea production made Denmark self-sufficient in energy in 1997, which explains the established offshore supply chain on the west coast.

Denmark: rules and approvals

Manufacturers who apply the ATEX provisions and affix CE and Ex markings may sell equipment anywhere in the EU without further requirements for those risks, so Danish buyers can use EU-wide certificates.

Denmark: projects and investment

Denmark expects to remain self-sufficient in oil until 2050 according to the cited article, so existing offshore and onshore oil facilities are likely to need maintenance work for years to come.

The term · intrinsically safe

What “intrinsically safe” means for a smartphone

Intrinsic safety limits the electrical and thermal energy in a circuit so that neither a spark nor a hot surface can ignite a gas or dust atmosphere, even when a fault occurs. The ib level, used for Zone 1, tolerates one fault. It is the usual way to make a handheld device that a worker carries continuously through a hazardous area.

The IS-04Z1 is designed on this principle for Zone 1 and Zone 21 (marking target II 2 G Ex ib IIC T4). Intrinsic safety is a design basis, not a certificate: certification is in progress and none has been issued yet.

In Denmark. Biogas plants and remote terminals are often worked by one technician with the nearest colleague a drive away, and offshore rounds start from Esbjerg. An intrinsically safe handheld is designed for these rounds, with four-constellation GNSS and 4G LTE holding a position and a link, and a 4,000 mAh battery for the shift.

Questions · Denmark

Frequently asked questions

Can we buy an intrinsically safe cell phone in Denmark today?

No. Nothing can be bought or taken into a classified area today: certification is in progress and launch is planned for Q4 2026. What is available now is the datasheet, the written certification schedule and evaluation units for safe-area use.

What does an intrinsically safe cell phone in Denmark cost?

No price is published. While certification is in progress there is no list price and no committed lead time, and a supplier quoting a firm figure for an uncertified Ex handset is promising something it cannot stand behind. Indicative pre-launch pricing is issued in writing against a stated quantity and site.

What does ‘intrinsically safe’ mean for the IS-04Z1?

The circuits are designed to limit energy so a spark or hot surface cannot ignite a gas or dust atmosphere, even with one fault (level ib, Zone 1). Certification of that design is in progress.

Which sites in Denmark does this cover?

The market guide for Denmark covers Esbjerg, The Danish North Sea, Fredericia, Kalundborg, among others. Site-level pages are linked below.

How do I become a SAMS distributor for Denmark?

Apply through the distributor page with your company details and the territory you cover. SAMS reviews applications company by company and has not yet granted a territory for Denmark.

Do Danish offshore platforms require ATEX equipment?

Denmark applies ATEX Directive 2014/34/EU as an EU member, and the directive explicitly covers equipment used on fixed offshore platforms. Operators typically add their own approval, helicopter and survival-suit procedures. Confirm the platform operator's device rules well ahead of a trip.

Is the Danish North Sea still an active market for hazardous-area devices?

Yes, though the sector is mature. Oil and gas production began scaling in the 1980s and continues, with maintenance and decommissioning work. Other hazardous-area work comes from biogas, power and industrial plants. Confirm current projects with operators rather than relying on past production figures.

Is the phone suitable for biogas plants and offshore support work?

It is designed for Zone 1 gas and Zone 21 dust, with four-constellation GNSS and 4G LTE for lone working, but certification is in progress and no certificate exists. Suitability depends on the area classification and gas group at each plant. Check with the plant operator and request the datasheet.
