The term · intrinsically safe

What “intrinsically safe” means for a smartphone

Intrinsic safety limits the electrical and thermal energy in a circuit so that neither a spark nor a hot surface can ignite a gas or dust atmosphere, even when a fault occurs. The ib level, used for Zone 1, tolerates one fault. It is the usual way to make a handheld device that a worker carries continuously through a hazardous area.

The IS-04Z1 is designed on this principle for Zone 1 and Zone 21 (marking target II 2 G Ex ib IIC T4). Intrinsic safety is a design basis, not a certificate: certification is in progress and none has been issued yet.

In Sitra, Bahrain. Turnaround and commissioning work at a refinery means many people walking process units, tank farms and the gas pipeline terminus with phones for photos, permits and checklists. An intrinsically safe handheld avoids carrying an ordinary phone into classified areas. The IS-04Z1 is designed for that role once certified.

Questions · Sitra

Frequently asked questions

Can we buy an intrinsically safe mobile phone in Sitra today?

Not yet. The IS-04Z1 is pre-launch and its certification is still in progress ahead of a Q4 2026 launch. No unit goes into a classified area before the certificate covering it exists. The datasheet, the certification schedule and safe-area evaluation units are available now.

What does an intrinsically safe mobile phone in Sitra cost?

There is no list price yet. SAMS gives indicative pre-launch pricing in writing, against a stated quantity and site, and does not commit to a lead time before certification completes.

What does ‘intrinsically safe’ mean for the IS-04Z1?

The circuits are designed to limit energy so a spark or hot surface cannot ignite a gas or dust atmosphere, even with one fault (level ib, Zone 1). Certification of that design is in progress.

Which areas at Sitra, Bahrain would use it?

Likely candidates are refinery process units, crude and product storage, hydrogen and hydrocracking units, sulphur recovery units. Which areas are classified, and to what zone, is set by the site operator's own area classification drawings.

How do I become a SAMS distributor for Sitra, Bahrain?

Apply through the distributor page with your company details and the territory you cover. SAMS reviews applications company by company and has not yet granted a territory for Sitra, Bahrain.

Are dust hazards present on Sitra?

Aluminium extrusion and metal-working can produce metal dust in some processes, so some areas may have dust classification; the refinery is mostly gas and vapour. Ask the operator for its dust zone survey.

How does Sitra connect to the rest of Bahrain?

A bridge built in the 1930s links the island to Bahrain Island, and Manama is about 5 km away. Contractors normally go through each operator's gate with induction and permits.

Can contractors bring their own handsets into the Sitra refinery?

Usually only if the operator's permit-to-work and equipment rules allow it, and for classified areas only if the device carries a suitable Ex marking. Refinery contractors generally follow the operator's registration systems. Confirm current requirements with the site operator before mobilising any fleet.
