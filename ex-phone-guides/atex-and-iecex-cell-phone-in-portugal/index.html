The term · ATEX and IECEx

What “ATEX and IECEx” means for a smartphone

ATEX is the European legal framework for equipment used in explosive atmospheres (Directive 2014/34/EU). IECEx is the international certification scheme built on the IEC 60079 standards. Buyers outside Europe often ask for both: an IECEx certificate is the technical evidence most national regimes build on, while ATEX is the legal route into the European market.

For the IS-04Z1 both routes are in progress with TÜV in Germany and neither certificate has been issued. The marking target is II 2 G Ex ib IIC T4: equipment Group II (surface industry), Category 2 (Zone 1 gas), protection concept ib (intrinsic safety), gas group IIC and temperature class T4.

In Portugal. A Portuguese HSE or procurement reviewer at Galp, Bondalti or REN Atlântico would expect ATEX evidence, with the certification programme running now, and IECEx alongside for international contractors on Sines and Estarreja projects. Both are in progress and no certificate is issued. Ask for written evidence and the precise marking string.

Frequently asked questions

Which sites in Portugal does this cover?

The market guide for Portugal covers Sines, ZILS Alentejo, Estarreja, Matosinhos and Leça, among others. Site-level pages are linked below.

Is intrinsically safe the same as explosion proof?

Not exactly. Explosion proof is a broad everyday term; intrinsic safety is one protection concept, which limits energy so ignition cannot occur. Flameproof (Ex d) is a different concept that contains an explosion. The IS-04Z1 is intrinsically safe by design.

Does Portugal follow ATEX for handheld devices?

Portugal is an EU member, so ATEX Directive 2014/34/EU applies to equipment and Directive 1999/92/EC to workplace zoning. Zone 1 and Zone 2 devices should carry CE and Ex markings with instructions in Portuguese where required. Operators usually add their own permit and registration procedures.

Why is Sines important for hazardous-area work in Portugal?

Sines hosts Portugal's LNG import terminal, with three storage tanks totalling 390,000 cubic metres and a regasification capacity of 5.6 million tonnes a year. Terminal and port operators set their own induction and device rules, so confirm them before sending equipment.

Is dust protection part of the programme?

Yes, Zone 21 dust protection belongs to the same certification programme as the gas marking. In pulp, cement and mineral processing the powder area is the plant, not a corner of it, so check the final certificate and the site's own dust classification with the operator.
