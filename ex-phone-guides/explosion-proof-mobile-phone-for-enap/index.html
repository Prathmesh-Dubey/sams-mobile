The term · explosion proof

What “explosion proof” means for a smartphone

“Explosion proof” is the phrase most buyers use for equipment that may be taken into a classified hazardous area. Strictly, it covers several protection concepts: a flameproof enclosure (Ex d) contains an internal explosion, while intrinsic safety (Ex i) limits electrical energy so that an ignition cannot start. Phones are normally intrinsically safe, because a flameproof handset would be too heavy to carry all shift.

The IS-04Z1 uses intrinsic safety, with a marking target of Ex ib IIC T4, and is designed for Zone 1 and Zone 21. It is pre-launch, certification is in progress and no certificate has been issued.

In ENAP. In ENAP refineries, 'explosion-proof' is a casual phrase for several concepts. Flameproof Ex d protects fixed motors and enclosures in process units, while intrinsic safety limits the energy of a portable device. A phone used on refinery rounds or at a marine terminal is a handheld, so the IS-04Z1 is designed around intrinsic safety.

Questions · ENAP

Frequently asked questions

Can ENAP buy the IS-04Z1 today?

No. Nothing can be bought or taken into a classified area today: certification is in progress and launch is planned for Q4 2026. What is available now is the datasheet, the written certification schedule and evaluation units for safe-area use.

What does an explosion proof mobile phone for ENAP cost?

There is no list price yet. SAMS gives indicative pre-launch pricing in writing, against a stated quantity and site, and does not commit to a lead time before certification completes.

Is the IS-04Z1 explosion proof or intrinsically safe?

It is intrinsically safe (marking target Ex ib IIC T4), which is the usual way to make a phone for hazardous areas. It is not a flameproof (Ex d) design, and certification is still in progress.

How does ENAP approve suppliers?

ENAP's website offers a supplier management portal (gestion de proveedores) for joining its vendor community and registering for procurement opportunities.

Is intrinsically safe the same as explosion proof?

Not exactly. Explosion proof is a broad everyday term; intrinsic safety is one protection concept, which limits energy so ignition cannot occur. Flameproof (Ex d) is a different concept that contains an explosion. The IS-04Z1 is intrinsically safe by design.

Do Chilean refineries face particular seismic or coastal conditions for equipment?

Chile is seismically active and two of the refineries are coastal, so sites typically apply robust fixing, corrosion protection and emergency procedures. For handheld devices this means checking drop resistance and sealing. Confirm each site's conditions and rules with the refinery directly.

Which ENAP sites are likely to need Ex-rated mobile devices?

The three refineries at Aconcagua, Bio Bio and Gregorio are the main process sites, with storage and loading areas, and the southern production areas are another. Zoning is decided per installation, so ask ENAP's HSE or maintenance teams for area classification drawings before specifying devices.

How does ENAP's supplier portal affect a new device supplier?

ENAP's website offers a supplier management portal for joining its vendor community and registering for procurement opportunities. A new supplier would use it, then meet ENAP's technical and HSE requirements. Confirm current steps with ENAP. The IS-04Z1 has no certificate yet, so no approval can be claimed.
