Field notes · Thailand

More about Thailand that matters for portable equipment

Thailand: logistics and access

Around 79.5% of crude oil supply is imported; domestic oil production peaked in 2016 at 486,000 barrels a day, so port, tank farm and pipeline terminals handle much of the flow.

Thailand: industry on the ground

Map Ta Phut in Rayong, opened in 1990 and run by the Industrial Estate Authority of Thailand, is the largest industrial estate in the country, with 151 factories including petrochemical plants and refineries across about 166 square kilometres.

Thailand: infrastructure

The Erawan gas field in the Gulf of Thailand supplies about 20% of the country's gas production, which makes it an important reference point for offshore device and transport planning.

Thailand: logistics and access

Gas supply is topped up with LNG imports and pipeline gas from Myanmar (6.2 and 7.8 billion cubic metres respectively in 2018), so LNG regasification and receiving stations are part of the hazardous-area picture.

The term · Ex-proof

What “Ex-proof” means for a smartphone

“Ex-proof” is shorthand for equipment carrying an explosion-protection marking (the Ex mark) that matches the area it enters. The marking states the protection concept, gas or dust group and temperature class, and it must be backed by a certificate issued by a recognised body, so a phone described as Ex-proof should come with a certificate number you can check.

The IS-04Z1 is designed to carry the marking II 2 G Ex ib IIC T4. That is a target: certification is in progress, no certificate has been issued, and SAMS will publish the certificate numbers when they exist.

In Thailand. A Thai marking check hinges on gas group: hydrogen at reformers and hydrotreaters, ethylene through the cracker network and acetylene during maintenance all sit within the same estates, so a device rated only to IIB is unusable across much of Map Ta Phut. The target Ex ib IIC T4 is read with Zone 21 evidence for polymer powder.

Frequently asked questions

Can we buy an Ex-proof cell phone in Thailand today?

Not today. Until the certificates are issued the IS-04Z1 stays out of classified areas, and until launch there is nothing to purchase. You can ask for the datasheet and the certification schedule, or book a safe-area evaluation unit.

What does an Ex-proof cell phone in Thailand cost?

What Ex marking will the IS-04Z1 carry?

Which sites in Thailand does this cover?

The market guide for Thailand covers Map Ta Phut Industrial Estate, Rayong industrial estates, Sriracha, Chonburi, Laem Chabang, among others. Site-level pages are linked below.

How will SAMS supply Thailand?

Through authorised distributors once certificates exist. Until then SAMS can send the datasheet and certification schedule and arrange safe-area evaluation units.

Does Thailand's heat and humidity change how I choose an intrinsically safe phone?

Yes, because tropical heat and humidity put stress on batteries and seals. Check the maximum ambient rating on the certificate, the water and dust ingress rating and how the device behaves in direct sun. Spare batteries are typically worthwhile on long outdoor shifts.

Which part of Thailand has the most offshore hazardous-area work?

The Gulf of Thailand, with 34 active petroleum projects across 47 fields as of March 2024, including the Erawan field. Crew change and supply bases on the coast also hold classified areas. Confirm each platform's rules with its operator and helicopter or boat provider.

Is the IS-04Z1 suited to the Rayong monsoon?

IP68 sealing and a rugged build are the design floor for heavy rain on open pipe racks and humid heat. The certificate ambient range is the compliance figure and is still in progress. Test a unit in the wet season and confirm the range with the site.
