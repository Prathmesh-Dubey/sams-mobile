Field notes · Jebel Ali

More about Jebel Ali that matters for portable equipment

Jebel Ali: infrastructure

The port has more than one million square metres of container yard and about 960,000 square metres of open storage, so wide open areas are exposed to sun and dust.

Jebel Ali: infrastructure

With 67 berths, the harbour is described as the world's largest man-made harbour and the biggest port in the Middle East, so jetty and quay areas are extensive.

Jebel Ali: background

Jebel Ali Port opened in 1979, inaugurated on 26 February that year to supplement Port Rashid, so much of the district's infrastructure dates from the last 45 years.

Jebel Ali: logistics and access

The port handled about 13.7 million TEU in 2021, so container, truck and crane traffic is dense and site rules on vehicles and equipment are strict.

The term · Ex-proof

What “Ex-proof” means for a smartphone

“Ex-proof” is shorthand for equipment carrying an explosion-protection marking (the Ex mark) that matches the area it enters. The marking states the protection concept, gas or dust group and temperature class, and it must be backed by a certificate issued by a recognised body, so a phone described as Ex-proof should come with a certificate number you can check.

The IS-04Z1 is designed to carry the marking II 2 G Ex ib IIC T4. That is a target: certification is in progress, no certificate has been issued, and SAMS will publish the certificate numbers when they exist.

In Jebel Ali. A marking check at Jebel Ali would compare each tenant's area drawings with the device marking. Smelter power-station fuel areas, oil terminals and chemical warehouses may differ in zone and gas group. The IS-04Z1 target is II 2 G Ex ib IIC T4, in progress and not certified.

Questions · Jebel Ali

Frequently asked questions

Can we buy an Ex-proof mobile phone in Jebel Ali today?

No. Nothing can be bought or taken into a classified area today: certification is in progress and launch is planned for Q4 2026. What is available now is the datasheet, the written certification schedule and evaluation units for safe-area use.

What does an Ex-proof mobile phone in Jebel Ali cost?

No price is published. While certification is in progress there is no list price and no committed lead time, and a supplier quoting a firm figure for an uncertified Ex handset is promising something it cannot stand behind. Indicative pre-launch pricing is issued in writing against a stated quantity and site.

What Ex marking will the IS-04Z1 carry?

The marking target is II 2 G Ex ib IIC T4. It becomes a marking only when the certificate is issued; until then it is a design target.

Which areas at Jebel Ali would use it?

Likely candidates are gas-fired power station fuel systems, desalination and power plant gas supply, smelter power-station fuel areas, chemical and fuel warehousing. Which areas are classified, and to what zone, is set by the site operator's own area classification drawings.

How do I become a SAMS distributor for Jebel Ali?

Apply through the distributor page with your company details and the territory you cover. SAMS reviews applications company by company and has not yet granted a territory for Jebel Ali.

Does the Jebel Ali refinery add hazardous areas to the port district?

Yes, a refinery adds process units, storage and loading areas, usually with gas classification. ENOC Processing Company runs it, so its own safety and approval rules apply. Contractors should request area classification drawings and the company's equipment requirements before bringing devices in.

How far is Jebel Ali from central Dubai?

The port is about 35 km south-west of Dubai, linked by expressway. Service teams based in the city should allow transfer time and check gate and security procedures, as the port and free zone are controlled areas with their own access passes.

Do access rules differ between tenants at Jebel Ali?

Yes. JAFZA hosts over 8,700 companies, so access and rules vary by tenant, and port, utility and tenant operators set their own approval rules. Ask the operator of the specific site what a phone needs before entering a classified area.
