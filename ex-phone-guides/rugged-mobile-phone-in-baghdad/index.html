Local conditions · Baghdad

What Baghdad means for portable equipment

Hot summers

Summer maximum temperatures average around 44 C with dry sunshine, which matters for the thermal limits of handheld devices used outdoors.

Head-office hub

The Ministry of Oil and SOMO are headquartered in Baghdad, so many contractor and procurement offices sit here even though the large producing fields are in the south and north.

Baghdad: people and language

The term · rugged

What “rugged” means for a smartphone

A rugged phone is built to survive water, dust, drops and rough handling, usually rated by an IP code and a drop specification. Ruggedness is an environmental property: it says nothing about whether a phone may be used where flammable gas or dust is present. That is decided by explosion-protection certification and marking.

The IS-04Z1 combines both: an IP68 sealed rugged build and a design for Zone 1 and Zone 21 (marking target Ex ib IIC T4). The explosion-protection certificates are in progress and none has been issued.

In Baghdad. Summers here are very hot and dry, and handheld devices used outdoors on refinery grounds face real thermal limits. IP68 means the IS-04Z1 is designed to resist dust and water immersion, which helps with fine desert dust and washdown, but it says nothing about explosion protection. Temperature class and zone suitability are separate checks.

Questions · Baghdad

Frequently asked questions

Can we buy a rugged mobile phone in Baghdad today?

Not yet. The IS-04Z1 is pre-launch and its certification is still in progress ahead of a Q4 2026 launch. No unit goes into a classified area before the certificate covering it exists. The datasheet, the certification schedule and safe-area evaluation units are available now.

What does a rugged mobile phone in Baghdad cost?

No price is published. While certification is in progress there is no list price and no committed lead time, and a supplier quoting a firm figure for an uncertified Ex handset is promising something it cannot stand behind. Indicative pre-launch pricing is issued in writing against a stated quantity and site.

How rugged is the IS-04Z1?

It is designed to IP68 sealing, with glove-friendly controls. Ruggedness is not explosion protection: that depends on the certificates, which are in progress.

Which areas at Baghdad would use it?

Likely candidates are refinery process units, fuel and lpg storage, pipeline pumping stations. Which areas are classified, and to what zone, is set by the site operator's own area classification drawings.

Is intrinsically safe the same as explosion proof?

Not exactly. Explosion proof is a broad everyday term; intrinsic safety is one protection concept, which limits energy so ignition cannot occur. Flameproof (Ex d) is a different concept that contains an explosion. The IS-04Z1 is intrinsically safe by design.

Why do many suppliers base themselves in Baghdad?

Ministry and state company offices are concentrated there, so tender documents and approvals are often processed in Baghdad even when the work is at fields in the south or north.

Is Baghdad an industrial hazardous-area site?

The city itself is mainly commercial and administrative, with refineries and fuel depots on the outskirts. Ask the operator which sites you need to enter and what area classification they use.

Can I bring an intrinsically safe phone to the Daura refinery?

Visits and deliveries to refinery sites normally need operator-issued permits and advance planning, so the device question is part of that process. The IS-04Z1 is not yet certified, so it could not be admitted today. Ask the site operator about its device policy and area classification first.
