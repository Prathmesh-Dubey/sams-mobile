The term · hazardous area

What “hazardous area” means for a smartphone

A hazardous area is a place where flammable gas, vapour or combustible dust may be present. Standards divide these into zones: Zones 0, 1 and 2 for gas and vapour, and Zones 20, 21 and 22 for dust. Zone 1 and Zone 21 are the tier where an explosive atmosphere is likely to occur in normal operation, which is where the IS-04Z1 is designed to work. Anything carried in must be marked for the zone, gas group and temperature class of the area.

No certificate has been issued for the IS-04Z1 yet, so it must not be taken into a classified area until the certificate covering it exists and the site operator has accepted it.

In Houston and the Houston Ship Channel. Classified areas here include refinery process units, petrochemical plants, tank farms and dock or terminal loading areas. The facility operator, not SAMS, carries out the area classification and decides which equipment may enter each zone. A supplier datasheet only helps the operator compare the marking with that classification; it does not classify anything.

Frequently asked questions

Can we buy a hazardous area mobile phone in Houston today?

Not yet. The IS-04Z1 is pre-launch and its certification is still in progress ahead of a Q4 2026 launch. No unit goes into a classified area before the certificate covering it exists. The datasheet, the certification schedule and safe-area evaluation units are available now.

What does a hazardous area mobile phone in Houston cost?

No price is published. While certification is in progress there is no list price and no committed lead time, and a supplier quoting a firm figure for an uncertified Ex handset is promising something it cannot stand behind. Indicative pre-launch pricing is issued in writing against a stated quantity and site.

Which hazardous areas is the IS-04Z1 designed for?

Zone 1 and Zone 21 (gas and dust) with gas group IIC and temperature class T4, subject to certification. It must not be used in a classified area before its certificate is issued and the site has accepted it.

Which areas at Houston and the Houston Ship Channel would use it?

Likely candidates are refinery process units, petrochemical plants, tank farms, dock and terminal loading areas. Which areas are classified, and to what zone, is set by the site operator's own area classification drawings.

How do I become a SAMS distributor for Houston and the Houston Ship Channel?

Apply through the distributor page with your company details and the territory you cover. SAMS reviews applications company by company and has not yet granted a territory for Houston and the Houston Ship Channel.

How hot does it get for outdoor equipment in Houston?

Houston typically sees highs of 90 degrees Fahrenheit or more on over 100 days a year, with high humidity from the Gulf. Check a handset's stated operating range and humidity rating on its datasheet, and avoid leaving devices in closed vehicles.

Which areas along the Houston Ship Channel are the main industrial clusters?

The channel is lined with refineries and chemical plants, with major private complexes at Baytown and Deer Park and public terminals at the Turning Basin, Barbours Cut and Bayport. Always confirm which operator controls the specific gate you are visiting.

Can the IS-04Z1 be used at Houston Ship Channel refineries today?

No. The IS-04Z1 is pre-launch and certification is in progress, so no certificate exists. Each refinery and chemical operator along the channel sets its own contractor and equipment approvals. A buyer can evaluate the datasheet and a safe-area use now, and should confirm requirements with the site operator.
