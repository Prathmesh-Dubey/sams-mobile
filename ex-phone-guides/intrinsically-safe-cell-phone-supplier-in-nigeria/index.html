The term · intrinsically safe

What “intrinsically safe” means for a smartphone

Intrinsic safety limits the electrical and thermal energy in a circuit so that neither a spark nor a hot surface can ignite a gas or dust atmosphere, even when a fault occurs. The ib level, used for Zone 1, tolerates one fault. It is the usual way to make a handheld device that a worker carries continuously through a hazardous area.

The IS-04Z1 is designed on this principle for Zone 1 and Zone 21 (marking target II 2 G Ex ib IIC T4). Intrinsic safety is a design basis, not a certificate: certification is in progress and none has been issued yet.

In Nigeria. Flow stations in the Niger Delta, FPSOs on Bonga, Egina, Akpo and Agbami, and export terminals at Escravos and Qua Iboe involve rounds on foot, by boat and by helicopter. An intrinsically safe handheld lets an operator photograph and log findings without gathering permits at each gate. It is a simple tool for a hard footprint.

Questions · Nigeria

Frequently asked questions

Can we buy an intrinsically safe cell phone in Nigeria today?

Not today. Until the certificates are issued the IS-04Z1 stays out of classified areas, and until launch there is nothing to purchase. You can ask for the datasheet and the certification schedule, or book a safe-area evaluation unit.

What does an intrinsically safe cell phone in Nigeria cost?

SAMS has not published a price. Any figure you are offered for an uncertified Ex phone should be treated with caution; written indicative pricing is available for a defined quantity and site.

What does ‘intrinsically safe’ mean for the IS-04Z1?

The circuits are designed to limit energy so a spark or hot surface cannot ignite a gas or dust atmosphere, even with one fault (level ib, Zone 1). Certification of that design is in progress.

Which sites in Nigeria does this cover?

The market guide for Nigeria covers Port Harcourt, Rivers State, Warri and Escravos, Bonny Island, Lekki Free Zone, Lagos, among others. Site-level pages are linked below.

How will SAMS supply Nigeria?

Through authorised distributors once certificates exist. Until then SAMS can send the datasheet and certification schedule and arrange safe-area evaluation units.

What conditions should be assumed for devices in the Niger Delta?

The Delta is typically a low-lying, hot and humid region with heavy seasonal rain, so corrosion and moisture are common concerns. Check ingress protection, corrosion resistance and the operating temperature range, and ask the operator about site transport, such as boat or helicopter, and device rules.

Does the Dangote refinery set its own rules for contractors?

A large new refinery is operated by its owner, which applies its own site HSE, permit and equipment requirements alongside Nigerian law. Contractors should ask the operator for its induction, permit-to-work and approved-equipment procedures rather than assuming the practices of other Nigerian sites.

What approvals will a Nigerian buyer need?

The programme lists IECEx for hazardous area use and SONCAP and NCC steps for national conformity and the radio, all in progress. Nothing is yet issued. Ask your supplier and the site operator which documents your procurement and HSE teams will require before purchase.
