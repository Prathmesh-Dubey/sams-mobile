The term · Ex-proof

What “Ex-proof” means for a smartphone

“Ex-proof” is shorthand for equipment carrying an explosion-protection marking (the Ex mark) that matches the area it enters. The marking states the protection concept, gas or dust group and temperature class, and it must be backed by a certificate issued by a recognised body, so a phone described as Ex-proof should come with a certificate number you can check.

The IS-04Z1 is designed to carry the marking II 2 G Ex ib IIC T4. That is a target: certification is in progress, no certificate has been issued, and SAMS will publish the certificate numbers when they exist.

In Balikpapan, East Kalimantan. A marking check at Balikpapan would read the zone assigned to the cracking unit, tank area or marine mooring, then compare gas group and temperature class. Hydrocarbon vapours are usually within IIA or IIB, so IIC covers them conservatively. The IS-04Z1 target marking, II 2 G Ex ib IIC T4, is not approved.

What Ex marking will the IS-04Z1 carry?

Which areas at Balikpapan, East Kalimantan would use it?

Which operator should I contact for equipment approval at the Balikpapan refinery?

Refinery Unit V is run by PT Kilang Pertamina Balikpapan, part of the Pertamina group. Contractors and suppliers normally register through Pertamina's vendor processes and follow the site's own permit and induction rules, so confirm the current route with the plant.

Will the RDMP upgrade change what handsets are accepted at Balikpapan?
