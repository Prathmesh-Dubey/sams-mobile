Abqaiq: infrastructure

The Abqaiq to Yanbu natural gas liquids pipeline is described as Saudi Arabia's largest NGL line, carrying product west to Red Sea petrochemical plants.

Abqaiq: logistics and access

Abqaiq has a station on the Dammam to Riyadh railway line, and a small Aramco-operated airstrip that is closed to commercial flights.

Abqaiq: people and language

Abqaiq had about 45,000 residents in the 2022 census, of whom roughly 1,500 lived in the Aramco residential community in 2012, so most of the workforce commutes from the wider town.

Abqaiq: infrastructure

The 1,202 km Petroline crude pipeline, completed in the early 1980s, takes oil from the Abqaiq and Ghawar fields overland to Yanbu on the Red Sea.

The term · explosion proof

What “explosion proof” means for a smartphone

“Explosion proof” is the phrase most buyers use for equipment that may be taken into a classified hazardous area. Strictly, it covers several protection concepts: a flameproof enclosure (Ex d) contains an internal explosion, while intrinsic safety (Ex i) limits electrical energy so that an ignition cannot start. Phones are normally intrinsically safe, because a flameproof handset would be too heavy to carry all shift.

The IS-04Z1 uses intrinsic safety, with a marking target of Ex ib IIC T4, and is designed for Zone 1 and Zone 21. It is pre-launch, certification is in progress and no certificate has been issued.

In Abqaiq. Around crude stabilisation units and the natural gas liquids facility, explosion-proof is a casual word. The real distinction is between flameproof enclosures, Ex d, for fixed equipment and intrinsic safety for handheld devices. The IS-04Z1 is designed for intrinsic safety, so a buyer should state which concept the Abqaiq area calls for.

Questions · Abqaiq

Frequently asked questions

Can we buy an explosion proof smartphone in Abqaiq today?

Not yet. The IS-04Z1 is pre-launch and its certification is still in progress ahead of a Q4 2026 launch. No unit goes into a classified area before the certificate covering it exists. The datasheet, the certification schedule and safe-area evaluation units are available now.

What does an explosion proof smartphone in Abqaiq cost?

There is no list price yet. SAMS gives indicative pre-launch pricing in writing, against a stated quantity and site, and does not commit to a lead time before certification completes.

Is the IS-04Z1 explosion proof or intrinsically safe?

It is intrinsically safe (marking target Ex ib IIC T4), which is the usual way to make a phone for hazardous areas. It is not a flameproof (Ex d) design, and certification is still in progress.

Which areas at Abqaiq would use it?

Likely candidates are crude stabilisation units, natural gas liquids facility, utilities units, hydrogen sulphide removal. Which areas are classified, and to what zone, is set by the site operator's own area classification drawings.

How do I become a SAMS distributor for Abqaiq?

Apply through the distributor page with your company details and the territory you cover. SAMS reviews applications company by company and has not yet granted a territory for Abqaiq.

How far is Abqaiq from the nearest commercial airport?

King Fahd International Airport at Dammam is about 90 km away. Abqaiq's own airstrip is run by Saudi Aramco and is not open to commercial traffic, so visiting service teams normally fly to Dammam and continue by road under the host's transport rules.

Is a handset used at Abqaiq subject to Saudi radio approval?

Radio equipment supplied in Saudi Arabia is normally covered by Communications, Space and Technology Commission type approval, with certificates renewed periodically. Aramco sites may add their own device rules, so confirm both the national and the site requirement with your client.

Does the Saudi telecoms regime affect a handset?

Telecoms equipment conformity in Saudi Arabia has been handled through the SABER platform of SASO since February 2025, and the Communications, Space and Technology Commission is the telecoms regulator. These are separate from Ex certification. Ask the supplier for both, and check scope with the relevant authority.
