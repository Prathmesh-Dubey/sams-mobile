The term · explosion proof

What “explosion proof” means for a smartphone

“Explosion proof” is the phrase most buyers use for equipment that may be taken into a classified hazardous area. Strictly, it covers several protection concepts: a flameproof enclosure (Ex d) contains an internal explosion, while intrinsic safety (Ex i) limits electrical energy so that an ignition cannot start. Phones are normally intrinsically safe, because a flameproof handset would be too heavy to carry all shift.

The IS-04Z1 uses intrinsic safety, with a marking target of Ex ib IIC T4, and is designed for Zone 1 and Zone 21. It is pre-launch, certification is in progress and no certificate has been issued.

In Yeosu. At a cracker and polymer complex, explosion-proof is loose shorthand. Flameproof Ex d protects fixed equipment, while a handheld phone around process units and tank farms depends on intrinsic safety. The IS-04Z1 is designed around that concept, with certification in progress and operator rules applying.

Questions · Yeosu

Frequently asked questions

Can we buy an explosion proof smartphone in Yeosu today?

No. Nothing can be bought or taken into a classified area today: certification is in progress and launch is planned for Q4 2026. What is available now is the datasheet, the written certification schedule and evaluation units for safe-area use.

What does an explosion proof smartphone in Yeosu cost?

There is no list price yet. SAMS gives indicative pre-launch pricing in writing, against a stated quantity and site, and does not commit to a lead time before certification completes.

Is the IS-04Z1 explosion proof or intrinsically safe?

It is intrinsically safe (marking target Ex ib IIC T4), which is the usual way to make a phone for hazardous areas. It is not a flameproof (Ex d) design, and certification is still in progress.

Which areas at Yeosu would use it?

Likely candidates are refinery process units, ethylene cracker and polymer plants, chemical storage tank farms, marine loading jetties. Which areas are classified, and to what zone, is set by the site operator's own area classification drawings.

Is intrinsically safe the same as explosion proof?

Not exactly. Explosion proof is a broad everyday term; intrinsic safety is one protection concept, which limits energy so ignition cannot occur. Flameproof (Ex d) is a different concept that contains an explosion. The IS-04Z1 is intrinsically safe by design.

What are the main hazardous-area risks at the Yeosu complex?

Refining units, a heavy-oil upgrading facility, an olefins cracker, aromatics and polypropylene plants together create flammable vapour and gas areas, including ethylene and aromatic hydrocarbons. The operator sets zones and gas groups, so ask for classification drawings before bringing devices.

Is Yeosu convenient for equipment delivery?

The city has an industrial port coast and rail and road links to Gwangju and other Korean cities, so delivery is feasible, though customs and approvals take time for imported electronics. Confirm lead times with your forwarder and the site's gate rules.

Will the restructuring at Yeosu change who approves equipment?

It may. A restructuring plan involving Lotte Chemical's Yeosu operations was approved, and its status could change. Procurement and HSE contacts may be in transition. Confirm requirements with the site operator before sending any documents.
