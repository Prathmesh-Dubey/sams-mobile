Field notes · Sweden

More about Sweden that matters for portable equipment

Sweden: who works there

Preem sells fuels, heating oil and lubricating oils to companies and individuals in Sweden and Norway, and since 2026 its parent company has been part of VARO Energy, so site procedures may follow the new group.

Sweden: industry on the ground

Preem's refineries provide roughly half of all petroleum products consumed in Sweden and nearly 65% of its production is sold abroad, mainly in north-west Europe, so its refineries are large and busy classified-area sites.

Sweden: logistics and access

The Port of Gothenburg is the largest in the Nordic countries and handles nearly 30% of Sweden's foreign trade, about 39 million tonnes a year, so most imported equipment can enter through one port.

Sweden: background

The Lysekil refinery plan began in 1945 when the consumer-owned oil company OK was formed, but the plant was only inaugurated in 1975, and it remains one of the area's main employers.

The term · IECEx

What “IECEx” means for a smartphone

IECEx is the international scheme for certifying equipment used in explosive atmospheres. A certificate of conformity is issued by an accredited certification body against a specific edition of the IEC 60079 standards and carries a schedule of conditions of safe use. It is not a legal requirement in most countries on its own, but many national schemes accept it as the technical basis.

The IECEx certificate for the IS-04Z1 is in progress and has not been issued. The marking target is Ex ib IIC T4, the intrinsic safety concept for Zone 1 gas with the hardest gas group (IIC) and a 135 °C temperature class.

In Sweden. IECEx matters in Sweden through multinational owners and visiting contractors at the Stenungsund cracker cluster, at SSAB and at LKAB, who read the international scheme first. A certificate of conformity and its schedule of conditions would give them a common reference. The IS-04Z1 certification is in progress and not yet issued.

Frequently asked questions

Can we buy an IECEx smartphone in Sweden today?

Not yet. The IS-04Z1 is pre-launch and its certification is still in progress ahead of a Q4 2026 launch. No unit goes into a classified area before the certificate covering it exists. The datasheet, the certification schedule and safe-area evaluation units are available now.

What does an IECEx smartphone in Sweden cost?

There is no list price yet. SAMS gives indicative pre-launch pricing in writing, against a stated quantity and site, and does not commit to a lead time before certification completes.

Does the IS-04Z1 hold an IECEx certificate?

No. The IECEx certificate is in progress and has not been issued. SAMS will publish the number when it exists.

Which sites in Sweden does this cover?

The market guide for Sweden covers Lysekil, Gothenburg river bank, Stenungsund, Nynäshamn, among others. Site-level pages are linked below.

How do I become a SAMS distributor for Sweden?

Apply through the distributor page with your company details and the territory you cover. SAMS reviews applications company by company and has not yet granted a territory for Sweden.

Do Swedish refineries require ATEX certified phones?

Sweden is an EU member, so ATEX Directive 2014/34/EU applies to equipment and Directive 1999/92/EC to zoning. Zone 1 and Zone 2 devices should carry CE and Ex markings. Refineries such as Lysekil typically add their own permit and device registration rules, so ask the HSE office.

What language should device documents be in for Swedish sites?

Swedish is the working language but English is widely used at international industrial sites. ATEX instructions are typically expected in the language of the destination country, so confirm with the operator whether Swedish documents are needed. Bring the declaration of conformity and certificate.

How cold can the IS-04Z1 be used at a northern Swedish site?

A rugged build covers low temperature and thermal shock, but the certified ambient range is a certificate parameter, published with the datasheet. Certification is in progress. Check the lower limit against the coldest conditions at the mine or steelworks before specifying.
