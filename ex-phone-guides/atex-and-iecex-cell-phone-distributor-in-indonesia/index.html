More about Indonesia that matters for portable equipment

Indonesia: infrastructure

Pertamina operates six oil refineries with a combined capacity of around 1 million barrels a day, as of 2013, and has not built a new refinery since Balongan opened in West Java in the mid-1990s.

Indonesia: rules and approvals

Radio and telecom equipment certification by SDPPI is compulsory in Indonesia, with a three-year validity, local testing and a local representative required, and a marking requirement.

Indonesia: rules and approvals

Technical requirements for 5G devices follow Perdirgen No. 5/2021, so 5G-capable phones are tested against that rule as part of SDPPI certification.

Indonesia: rules and approvals

A market-access database lists CE and FCC among other schemes recognised in Indonesia, and notes that expired certificates cannot be renewed and must be tested again as a new model.

The term · ATEX and IECEx

What “ATEX and IECEx” means for a smartphone

ATEX is the European legal framework for equipment used in explosive atmospheres (Directive 2014/34/EU). IECEx is the international certification scheme built on the IEC 60079 standards. Buyers outside Europe often ask for both: an IECEx certificate is the technical evidence most national regimes build on, while ATEX is the legal route into the European market.

For the IS-04Z1 both routes are in progress with TÜV in Germany and neither certificate has been issued. The marking target is II 2 G Ex ib IIC T4: equipment Group II (surface industry), Category 2 (Zone 1 gas), protection concept ib (intrinsic safety), gas group IIC and temperature class T4.

In Indonesia. An Indonesian reviewer would expect IECEx evidence plus the local route, including SNI, Komdigi type approval and TKDN, which the sheet lists as in progress. ATEX evidence helps international contractors working on LNG and offshore-support sites. A dual route lets one pack serve the operator and visiting teams. No certificate has been issued.

Frequently asked questions

Can we buy an ATEX and IECEx cell phone in Indonesia today?

Not yet. The IS-04Z1 is pre-launch and its certification is still in progress ahead of a Q4 2026 launch. No unit goes into a classified area before the certificate covering it exists. The datasheet, the certification schedule and safe-area evaluation units are available now.

What does an ATEX and IECEx cell phone in Indonesia cost?

SAMS has not published a price. Any figure you are offered for an uncertified Ex phone should be treated with caution; written indicative pricing is available for a defined quantity and site.

Is the IS-04Z1 ATEX and IECEx certified?

Not yet. Both routes are in progress with TÜV in Germany and no certificate has been issued. Any listing that says otherwise is wrong.

Which sites in Indonesia does this cover?

The market guide for Indonesia covers Cilacap, Central Java, Balikpapan, East Kalimantan, Dumai, Riau, Balongan, West Java, among others. Site-level pages are linked below.

How do I become a SAMS distributor for Indonesia?

Apply through the distributor page with your company details and the territory you cover. SAMS reviews applications company by company and has not yet granted a territory for Indonesia.

What conditions should devices handle at Indonesian industrial sites?

Tropical sites typically combine heat, high humidity, heavy rain and, near the coast, salt air, so check ingress protection, corrosion resistance and operating temperature range. Confirm site-specific requirements with the operator, including any area classification and approved-device lists.

How does Indonesia's island geography affect equipment logistics?

With thousands of islands, many sites are reached by sea or air, and delays are common. Plan extra lead time and spares for devices, chargers and batteries, and ask the operator or contractor about shipping, customs and site approval rules for each island location.

How would a remote island site get support?

Supply after certification would be through authorised distributors, and none has been appointed yet. For remote sites, ask about spares, replacement time and training before purchase, and agree the logistics route with the operator. Trial units can be tested to check connectivity and battery use first.
