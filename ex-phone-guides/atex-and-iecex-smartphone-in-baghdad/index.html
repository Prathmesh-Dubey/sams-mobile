The term · ATEX and IECEx

What “ATEX and IECEx” means for a smartphone

ATEX is the European legal framework for equipment used in explosive atmospheres (Directive 2014/34/EU). IECEx is the international certification scheme built on the IEC 60079 standards. Buyers outside Europe often ask for both: an IECEx certificate is the technical evidence most national regimes build on, while ATEX is the legal route into the European market.

For the IS-04Z1 both routes are in progress with TÜV in Germany and neither certificate has been issued. The marking target is II 2 G Ex ib IIC T4: equipment Group II (surface industry), Category 2 (Zone 1 gas), protection concept ib (intrinsic safety), gas group IIC and temperature class T4.

In Baghdad. A procurement or HSE reviewer at the Ministry of Oil, a refinery company or a contractor working on the Daura refinery would expect to see both ATEX and IECEx evidence for a handheld used in process units. A dual route helps international contractors and EPCs who bring their own certification files into Baghdad-run tenders. For the IS-04Z1, certification is in progress and no certificate has been issued.

Frequently asked questions

Can we buy an ATEX and IECEx smartphone in Baghdad today?

Not today. Until the certificates are issued the IS-04Z1 stays out of classified areas, and until launch there is nothing to purchase. You can ask for the datasheet and the certification schedule, or book a safe-area evaluation unit.

What does an ATEX and IECEx smartphone in Baghdad cost?

SAMS has not published a price. Any figure you are offered for an uncertified Ex phone should be treated with caution; written indicative pricing is available for a defined quantity and site.

Is the IS-04Z1 ATEX and IECEx certified?

Not yet. Both routes are in progress with TÜV in Germany and no certificate has been issued. Any listing that says otherwise is wrong.

Which areas at Baghdad would use it?

Likely candidates are refinery process units, fuel and lpg storage, pipeline pumping stations. Which areas are classified, and to what zone, is set by the site operator's own area classification drawings.

Is intrinsically safe the same as explosion proof?

Not exactly. Explosion proof is a broad everyday term; intrinsic safety is one protection concept, which limits energy so ignition cannot occur. Flameproof (Ex d) is a different concept that contains an explosion. The IS-04Z1 is intrinsically safe by design.

Why do many suppliers base themselves in Baghdad?

Ministry and state company offices are concentrated there, so tender documents and approvals are often processed in Baghdad even when the work is at fields in the south or north.

Is Baghdad an industrial hazardous-area site?

The city itself is mainly commercial and administrative, with refineries and fuel depots on the outskirts. Ask the operator which sites you need to enter and what area classification they use.

Can I bring an intrinsically safe phone to the Daura refinery?

Visits and deliveries to refinery sites normally need operator-issued permits and advance planning, so the device question is part of that process. The IS-04Z1 is not yet certified, so it could not be admitted today. Ask the site operator about its device policy and area classification first.
