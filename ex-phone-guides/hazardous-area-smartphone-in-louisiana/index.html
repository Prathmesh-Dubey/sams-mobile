The term · hazardous area

What “hazardous area” means for a smartphone

A hazardous area is a place where flammable gas, vapour or combustible dust may be present. Standards divide these into zones: Zones 0, 1 and 2 for gas and vapour, and Zones 20, 21 and 22 for dust. Zone 1 and Zone 21 are the tier where an explosive atmosphere is likely to occur in normal operation, which is where the IS-04Z1 is designed to work. Anything carried in must be marked for the zone, gas group and temperature class of the area.

No certificate has been issued for the IS-04Z1 yet, so it must not be taken into a classified area until the certificate covering it exists and the site operator has accepted it.

In Louisiana. Classified areas in Louisiana include refinery process units, petrochemical plants, LNG liquefaction and loading, and tank farms and terminals. Each operator, such as Cheniere, Sempra or Phillips 66, classifies its own areas. SAMS does not classify anything; it can only describe the marking and limits of its product.

Frequently asked questions

Can we buy a hazardous area smartphone in Louisiana today?

Not yet. The IS-04Z1 is pre-launch and its certification is still in progress ahead of a Q4 2026 launch. No unit goes into a classified area before the certificate covering it exists. The datasheet, the certification schedule and safe-area evaluation units are available now.

What does a hazardous area smartphone in Louisiana cost?

There is no list price yet. SAMS gives indicative pre-launch pricing in writing, against a stated quantity and site, and does not commit to a lead time before certification completes.

Which hazardous areas is the IS-04Z1 designed for?

Zone 1 and Zone 21 (gas and dust) with gas group IIC and temperature class T4, subject to certification. It must not be used in a classified area before its certificate is issued and the site has accepted it.

Which areas at Louisiana would use it?

Likely candidates are refinery process units, petrochemical plants, lng liquefaction and loading, tank farms and terminals. Which areas are classified, and to what zone, is set by the site operator's own area classification drawings.

Is intrinsically safe the same as explosion proof?

Not exactly. Explosion proof is a broad everyday term; intrinsic safety is one protection concept, which limits energy so ignition cannot occur. Flameproof (Ex d) is a different concept that contains an explosion. The IS-04Z1 is intrinsically safe by design.

Where are Louisiana's main refining and chemical clusters?

They concentrate along the Mississippi River between New Orleans and Baton Rouge, around Lake Charles in the southwest, and in the Norco and Chalmette areas. Each has its own operators, so confirm the exact plant and gate procedures before sending staff or equipment.

What should I confirm with a Louisiana operator before bringing portable devices on site?

Ask for the area classification for the units you will visit, the equipment approvals they accept, and any rules on cameras, radios and charging. Requirements vary by operator, and your safety contact on site is the authority.

Is one approval enough for refineries and LNG terminals across the state?

Not necessarily. Operators set their own contractor and equipment approvals, so a refinery and an LNG terminal may ask for different documents. The IS-04Z1 has no certificate yet. Confirm requirements with the site operator for each plant before planning a fleet.
