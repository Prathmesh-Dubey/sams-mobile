Field notes · Dhahran

More about Dhahran that matters for portable equipment

Dhahran: geography and layout

Dhahran lies about 15 km south of Dammam and forms part of the Dammam Metropolitan Area, home to an estimated 2.2 million people; the city itself had 143,936 residents in 2022.

Dhahran: background

Aramco's headquarters has been in Dhahran for around 90 years, following a 1933 concession agreement and the first commercial oil output in 1938.

Dhahran: people and language

KFUPM supplies a talent base of more than 13,000 students and 800 researchers and professors, which supports a deep local pool of petroleum and engineering graduates.

Dhahran: infrastructure

A large part of the city is gated, including Aramco's headquarters and residential camp, King Fahd University of Petroleum and Minerals and the King Abdulaziz Air Base, so entry rules differ by compound.

The term · IECEx

What “IECEx” means for a smartphone

IECEx is the international scheme for certifying equipment used in explosive atmospheres. A certificate of conformity is issued by an accredited certification body against a specific edition of the IEC 60079 standards and carries a schedule of conditions of safe use. It is not a legal requirement in most countries on its own, but many national schemes accept it as the technical basis.

The IECEx certificate for the IS-04Z1 is in progress and has not been issued. The marking target is Ex ib IIC T4, the intrinsic safety concept for Zone 1 gas with the hardest gas group (IIC) and a 135 °C temperature class.

In Dhahran. Because engineering and procurement decisions are concentrated in Dhahran, international EPCs and visiting contractors often present IECEx documents here before work starts at a field site. The certificate of conformity and schedule of conditions would be reviewed. For the IS-04Z1 they are targets only, as certification is in progress.

Questions · Dhahran

Frequently asked questions

Can we buy an IECEx smartphone in Dhahran today?

Not yet. The IS-04Z1 is pre-launch and its certification is still in progress ahead of a Q4 2026 launch. No unit goes into a classified area before the certificate covering it exists. The datasheet, the certification schedule and safe-area evaluation units are available now.

What does an IECEx smartphone in Dhahran cost?

There is no list price yet. SAMS gives indicative pre-launch pricing in writing, against a stated quantity and site, and does not commit to a lead time before certification completes.

Does the IS-04Z1 hold an IECEx certificate?

No. The IECEx certificate is in progress and has not been issued. SAMS will publish the number when it exists.

Which areas at Dhahran would use it?

Likely candidates are oil and gas facilities served from dhahran, fuel storage and handling. Which areas are classified, and to what zone, is set by the site operator's own area classification drawings.

How do I become a SAMS distributor for Dhahran?

Apply through the distributor page with your company details and the territory you cover. SAMS reviews applications company by company and has not yet granted a territory for Dhahran.

Is Dhahran itself a hazardous-area site?

Mostly no. It is an administrative and engineering centre, with Aramco's headquarters and research facilities, while the classified areas it serves are at oil, gas and refining sites elsewhere in the Eastern Province. Buyers and specifiers are based in Dhahran even when the Zone 1 work is not.

Is it practical to cover Bahrain from Dhahran?

Often yes. Bahrain is about 32 km east of Khobar across the King Fahd Causeway, so Dhahran-based teams can reach it by road. Border formalities, equipment import rules and site approvals still apply to each country, so plan them in advance.

Where does supplier registration happen?

Aramco's Dhahran office handles registration of in-Kingdom suppliers, and registration normally runs through Aramco's e-Marketplace Platform with technical and financial qualification. Rules change, so ask Aramco or the contractor for current requirements. This is a commercial step and does not replace explosion-protection certification.
