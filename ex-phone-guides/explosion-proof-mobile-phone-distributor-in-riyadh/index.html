The term · explosion proof

What “explosion proof” means for a smartphone

“Explosion proof” is the phrase most buyers use for equipment that may be taken into a classified hazardous area. Strictly, it covers several protection concepts: a flameproof enclosure (Ex d) contains an internal explosion, while intrinsic safety (Ex i) limits electrical energy so that an ignition cannot start. Phones are normally intrinsically safe, because a flameproof handset would be too heavy to carry all shift.

The IS-04Z1 uses intrinsic safety, with a marking target of Ex ib IIC T4, and is designed for Zone 1 and Zone 21. It is pre-launch, certification is in progress and no certificate has been issued.

In Riyadh. Riyadh's factories produce machinery, chemicals, construction materials and food, so hazard profiles differ by plant. Explosion-proof may mean flameproof Ex d for a chemical plant's gas area, while dust areas need other concepts. The IS-04Z1 is designed for intrinsic safety in gas atmospheres.

Questions · Riyadh

Frequently asked questions

Can we buy an explosion proof mobile phone in Riyadh today?

Not today. Until the certificates are issued the IS-04Z1 stays out of classified areas, and until launch there is nothing to purchase. You can ask for the datasheet and the certification schedule, or book a safe-area evaluation unit.

What does an explosion proof mobile phone in Riyadh cost?

SAMS has not published a price. Any figure you are offered for an uncertified Ex phone should be treated with caution; written indicative pricing is available for a defined quantity and site.

Is the IS-04Z1 explosion proof or intrinsically safe?

It is intrinsically safe (marking target Ex ib IIC T4), which is the usual way to make a phone for hazardous areas. It is not a flameproof (Ex d) design, and certification is still in progress.

Which areas at Riyadh would use it?

Likely candidates are refinery process units, chemical and manufacturing plants, industrial plant dust areas (zone 21). Which areas are classified, and to what zone, is set by the site operator's own area classification drawings.

How do I become a SAMS distributor for Riyadh?

Apply through the distributor page with your company details and the territory you cover. SAMS reviews applications company by company and has not yet granted a territory for Riyadh.

Is Riyadh a hazardous-area site or a buying centre?

Mainly a buying and engineering centre. Aramco's Riyadh refinery and many factories have classified areas, but most large oil and gas sites are in the Eastern Province. Procurement teams, regional headquarters and contractors are in Riyadh even when plants are elsewhere.

Who runs the industrial cities near Riyadh?

MODON, headquartered in Riyadh, develops and oversees industrial cities across the Kingdom, but each factory operator decides its own safety and equipment rules. Check the specific tenant's HSE team for hazardous-area device approval before delivery.

What about dust areas in Riyadh factories?

Industrial plant dust areas classified Zone 21 are present. The IS-04Z1 target marking, II 2 G Ex ib IIC T4, refers to gas atmospheres, and certification is in progress. Do not assume dust suitability; ask the supplier and the plant's safety officer which marking the dust area requires.
