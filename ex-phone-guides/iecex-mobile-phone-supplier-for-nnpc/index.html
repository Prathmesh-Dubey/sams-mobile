The term · IECEx

What “IECEx” means for a smartphone

IECEx is the international scheme for certifying equipment used in explosive atmospheres. A certificate of conformity is issued by an accredited certification body against a specific edition of the IEC 60079 standards and carries a schedule of conditions of safe use. It is not a legal requirement in most countries on its own, but many national schemes accept it as the technical basis.

The IECEx certificate for the IS-04Z1 is in progress and has not been issued. The marking target is Ex ib IIC T4, the intrinsic safety concept for Zone 1 gas with the hardest gas group (IIC) and a 135 °C temperature class.

In NNPC. NNPC manages joint ventures with international oil companies, and those partners and their contractors commonly work from IECEx documents. They look for a certificate of conformity and its schedule of conditions. IECEx is a target for the IS-04Z1 and remains in progress, so treat it as a planned route and not a held approval.

How does NNPC approve suppliers?

Suppliers and contractors register on the Nigerian Petroleum Exchange (NipeX), which is linked to NNPC tendering and vendor pre-qualification; local-content registration with the regulator and NCDMB is also required for work in the sector. Confirm current requirements with NNPC.

How will SAMS supply NNPC?

Through authorised distributors once certificates exist. Until then SAMS can send the datasheet and certification schedule and arrange safe-area evaluation units.

Do joint-venture partners set their own equipment standards on NNPC assets?

Often yes. NNPC manages joint ventures with international oil companies, and the operating partner usually applies its own HSE and contractor management system on site. Check who the operator is for the specific asset, and ask that operator for its approved equipment and certification requirements.

What climate conditions should intrinsically safe phones handle in southern Nigerian oil sites?

The Niger Delta and coastal areas are typically hot and humid with heavy rainfall, which stresses seals, screens and batteries. Look for strong ingress protection, tested operating temperatures and corrosion resistance, and ask the site what environmental limits its own instructions require.

What registration does a supplier need to work with NNPC?

Suppliers register on the Nigerian Petroleum Exchange, which is linked to NNPC tendering and vendor pre-qualification, and local-content registration with the regulator and NCDMB is also reported to be required.
