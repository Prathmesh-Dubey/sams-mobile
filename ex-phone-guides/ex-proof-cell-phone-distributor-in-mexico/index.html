Field notes · Mexico

More about Mexico that matters for portable equipment

Mexico: rules and approvals

Mexico has two approval schemes, telecom homologation and NOM certification, and neither is mandatory for every product; which applies depends on what is being sold.

Mexico: industry on the ground

Oil was first refined into kerosene around 1876 near Tampico, and Pemex's Tula refinery in Hidalgo is among its major inland refining sites.

Mexico: rules and approvals

NOM certificates of conformity, issued by bodies such as NYCE and ANCE, allow customs clearance into Mexico, and the Secretaria de Economia oversees NOM enforcement.

Mexico: industry on the ground

The offshore Cantarell field was discovered in 1976, started production in 1979 and peaked in 2004, so ageing offshore infrastructure is a feature of Mexican upstream work.

The term · Ex-proof

What “Ex-proof” means for a smartphone

“Ex-proof” is shorthand for equipment carrying an explosion-protection marking (the Ex mark) that matches the area it enters. The marking states the protection concept, gas or dust group and temperature class, and it must be backed by a certificate issued by a recognised body, so a phone described as Ex-proof should come with a certificate number you can check.

The IS-04Z1 is designed to carry the marking II 2 G Ex ib IIC T4. That is a target: certification is in progress, no certificate has been issued, and SAMS will publish the certificate numbers when they exist.

In Mexico. NOM-001-SEDE follows the American structure, so a Mexican drawing may read Clase I División 1 where an international one reads Zone 1. A marking check therefore compares II 2 G Ex ib IIC T4 with whichever language the plant drawings use. The IIC group covers the hydrogen and ethylene found on Gulf coast units, and T4 should be confirmed.

Questions · Mexico

Frequently asked questions

Can we buy an Ex-proof cell phone in Mexico today?

Not yet. The IS-04Z1 is pre-launch and its certification is still in progress ahead of a Q4 2026 launch. No unit goes into a classified area before the certificate covering it exists. The datasheet, the certification schedule and safe-area evaluation units are available now.

What does an Ex-proof cell phone in Mexico cost?

There is no list price yet. SAMS gives indicative pre-launch pricing in writing, against a stated quantity and site, and does not commit to a lead time before certification completes.

What Ex marking will the IS-04Z1 carry?

The marking target is II 2 G Ex ib IIC T4. It becomes a marking only when the certificate is issued; until then it is a design target.

Which sites in Mexico does this cover?

The market guide for Mexico covers Dos Bocas, Tabasco, Bay of Campeche, Ciudad del Carmen, Coatzacoalcos and Minatitlán, among others. Site-level pages are linked below.

How do I become a SAMS distributor for Mexico?

Apply through the distributor page with your company details and the territory you cover. SAMS reviews applications company by company and has not yet granted a territory for Mexico.

Which Mexican body approves telecom equipment now?

Following the 2025 changes, the Comision Reguladora de Telecomunicaciones within the Agencia de Transformacion Digital y Telecomunicaciones issues Certificates of Homologation, replacing the IFT. Check the current procedures with the CRT or an accredited body, as supplier documents may still cite IFT.

What conditions should devices handle on the Gulf of Mexico coast?

Gulf coast and offshore sites typically combine heat, high humidity, salt air and seasonal storms, so check ingress protection, corrosion resistance and operating temperature range. Confirm platform transfer rules, such as helicopter battery restrictions, with the operator before deployment.

Does the IS-04Z1 need homologación in Mexico?

Radio equipment needs homologación separately from hazardous area approval, and that step is in progress alongside the explosion protection programme. No approval has been granted yet. Check with the site and your telecoms team which documents they will require before any device is enrolled.
