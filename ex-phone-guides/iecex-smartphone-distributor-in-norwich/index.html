The term · IECEx

What “IECEx” means for a smartphone

IECEx is the international scheme for certifying equipment used in explosive atmospheres. A certificate of conformity is issued by an accredited certification body against a specific edition of the IEC 60079 standards and carries a schedule of conditions of safe use. It is not a legal requirement in most countries on its own, but many national schemes accept it as the technical basis.

The IECEx certificate for the IS-04Z1 is in progress and has not been issued. The marking target is Ex ib IIC T4, the intrinsic safety concept for Zone 1 gas with the hardest gas group (IIC) and a 135 °C temperature class.

In Norwich and the Norfolk coast. The Interconnector and BBL terminals at Bacton connect the UK gas system to continental Europe, so visiting engineers may expect IECEx-based paperwork. The aim is a certificate of conformity and a schedule of conditions that a terminal engineer can review against a given area. At present that paperwork is still in progress.

Frequently asked questions

Can we buy an IECEx smartphone in Norwich today?

No. Nothing can be bought or taken into a classified area today: certification is in progress and launch is planned for Q4 2026. What is available now is the datasheet, the written certification schedule and evaluation units for safe-area use.

What does an IECEx smartphone in Norwich cost?

No price is published. While certification is in progress there is no list price and no committed lead time, and a supplier quoting a firm figure for an uncertified Ex handset is promising something it cannot stand behind. Indicative pre-launch pricing is issued in writing against a stated quantity and site.

Does the IS-04Z1 hold an IECEx certificate?

Which areas at Norwich and the Norfolk coast would use it?

Likely candidates are gas terminal process areas, gas receipt and blending, interconnector terminals, offshore support port facilities. Which areas are classified, and to what zone, is set by the site operator's own area classification drawings.

How do I become a SAMS distributor for Norwich and the Norfolk coast?

Apply through the distributor page with your company details and the territory you cover. SAMS reviews applications company by company and has not yet granted a territory for Norwich and the Norfolk coast.

How does the coastal climate on the Norfolk coast affect phones?

The Norfolk coast is relatively dry by UK standards but exposed to wind and salt air, which can corrode connectors and fixings. For offshore wind and port work, look for good ingress protection and corrosion resistance, and ask the site about its own requirements.

Is Norwich itself a hazardous-area site or are the Ex sites elsewhere in Norfolk?

Norwich is mainly a service, research and distribution city, so most classified areas lie on the coast, around the energy port at Great Yarmouth, gas terminals and offshore wind and gas support bases, as well as in fuel depots and food or feed plants. Confirm the site classification with each operator.

Who approves equipment at Bacton?

Bacton is a complex of six gas terminals run by Shell, Eni, Perenco and National Grid, plus the Interconnector and BBL terminals. Equipment for terminal areas is normally approved by each terminal operator, so approvals may differ between neighbouring sites. Ask each operator for its own requirements.
