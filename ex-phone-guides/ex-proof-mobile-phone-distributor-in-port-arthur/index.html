The term · Ex-proof

What “Ex-proof” means for a smartphone

“Ex-proof” is shorthand for equipment carrying an explosion-protection marking (the Ex mark) that matches the area it enters. The marking states the protection concept, gas or dust group and temperature class, and it must be backed by a certificate issued by a recognised body, so a phone described as Ex-proof should come with a certificate number you can check.

The IS-04Z1 is designed to carry the marking II 2 G Ex ib IIC T4. That is a target: certification is in progress, no certificate has been issued, and SAMS will publish the certificate numbers when they exist.

In Port Arthur, Texas. A marking check at a Port Arthur marine terminal or process unit would compare zone, gas group and temperature class with the operator's drawings. The IS-04Z1 target is II 2 G Ex ib IIC T4, for Zone 1 gas atmospheres. Refinery operators decide which groups apply, and dust areas need separate assessment.

Why is humidity a bigger issue at Port Arthur than at other Texas sites?

Port Arthur is among the most humid places in the contiguous United States, with morning humidity near 90 percent. Condensation inside poorly sealed devices is a real risk, so choose sealed housings and follow the maker's care instructions.

How do turnarounds affect equipment demand at Port Arthur?

Large refineries run planned turnarounds that bring thousands of contractors on site for a limited period. Operators typically plan equipment and induction well in advance, so contact the site's maintenance planners early and confirm approval requirements.

How do hurricanes affect handheld planning on the Gulf Coast?

Port Arthur is hot and humid with hurricane exposure, which shapes turnaround scheduling. A rugged IP68 phone helps with water and dust, and managed enrolment lets a contractor lock or recover devices when a site closes. Store and charge devices in safe areas away from classified plant.
