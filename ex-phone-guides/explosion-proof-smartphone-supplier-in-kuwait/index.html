The term · explosion proof

What “explosion proof” means for a smartphone

“Explosion proof” is the phrase most buyers use for equipment that may be taken into a classified hazardous area. Strictly, it covers several protection concepts: a flameproof enclosure (Ex d) contains an internal explosion, while intrinsic safety (Ex i) limits electrical energy so that an ignition cannot start. Phones are normally intrinsically safe, because a flameproof handset would be too heavy to carry all shift.

The IS-04Z1 uses intrinsic safety, with a marking target of Ex ib IIC T4, and is designed for Zone 1 and Zone 21. It is pre-launch, certification is in progress and no certificate has been issued.

In Kuwait. People at Ahmadi and Shuaiba say explosion proof for anything used near hydrocarbons, but the real protection concepts differ. Flameproof Ex d contains an internal explosion and suits heavy fixed equipment. The IS-04Z1 instead limits energy with intrinsic safety, Ex ib, which is the concept that suits a handset carried through gathering centres and fertiliser plants.

Questions · Kuwait

Frequently asked questions

Can we buy an explosion proof smartphone in Kuwait today?

Not today. Until the certificates are issued the IS-04Z1 stays out of classified areas, and until launch there is nothing to purchase. You can ask for the datasheet and the certification schedule, or book a safe-area evaluation unit.

What does an explosion proof smartphone in Kuwait cost?

No price is published. While certification is in progress there is no list price and no committed lead time, and a supplier quoting a firm figure for an uncertified Ex handset is promising something it cannot stand behind. Indicative pre-launch pricing is issued in writing against a stated quantity and site.

Is the IS-04Z1 explosion proof or intrinsically safe?

It is intrinsically safe (marking target Ex ib IIC T4), which is the usual way to make a phone for hazardous areas. It is not a flameproof (Ex d) design, and certification is still in progress.

Which sites in Kuwait does this cover?

The market guide for Kuwait covers Ahmadi, Greater Burgan, Mina Al-Ahmadi, Mina Abdullah, among others. Site-level pages are linked below.

How will SAMS supply Kuwait?

Through authorised distributors once certificates exist. Until then SAMS can send the datasheet and certification schedule and arrange safe-area evaluation units.

What is the main field area in Kuwait?

Greater Burgan in the south-east, with Magwa and Ahmadi sub-fields, around Al Ahmadi. Refineries at Mina Al-Ahmadi and Mina Abdullah are in the same coastal belt south of Kuwait City.

Does extreme heat affect electronics?

Yes, summer highs average 42 to 46 degrees Celsius and record 54. Use devices rated for high ambient temperature, and avoid leaving them in vehicles or direct sun.

Can the IS-04Z1 cope with Kuwaiti summer heat and dust storms?

IP68 sealing is designed to keep dust out, and the build is rugged. Heat is a separate question: the certified ambient range and T4 class are fixed by the certificate, which is not yet issued. Compare the published range with your site maximum, and confirm with the operator before planning summer use.
