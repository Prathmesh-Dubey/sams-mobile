The term · IECEx

What “IECEx” means for a smartphone

IECEx is the international scheme for certifying equipment used in explosive atmospheres. A certificate of conformity is issued by an accredited certification body against a specific edition of the IEC 60079 standards and carries a schedule of conditions of safe use. It is not a legal requirement in most countries on its own, but many national schemes accept it as the technical basis.

The IECEx certificate for the IS-04Z1 is in progress and has not been issued. The marking target is Ex ib IIC T4, the intrinsic safety concept for Zone 1 gas with the hardest gas group (IIC) and a 135 °C temperature class.

In Kazakhstan. Kazakhstan's large fields, such as Tengiz, Kashagan and Karachaganak, work with international consortia and contractors who know IECEx. A certificate of conformity names the equipment, and its schedule of conditions sets conditions of use. IECEx certification for the IS-04Z1 is in progress; EAC Ex follows it.

How will SAMS supply Kazakhstan?

Through authorised distributors once certificates exist. Until then SAMS can send the datasheet and certification schedule and arrange safe-area evaluation units.

What climate range should devices cope with at Caspian sites?

Sites such as Kashagan see temperatures from about minus 35 to 40 degrees Celsius, with winter sea ice, so check the operating temperature range, battery behaviour in cold and heat, and enclosure ingress protection. Confirm site limits with the operator before shipping devices.

Why is hydrogen sulphide relevant to phone selection in Kazakh fields?

High hydrogen sulphide levels at fields such as Kashagan mean sites usually apply strict gas-detection, personal monitor and escape-equipment rules, and may restrict what can be carried into process areas. Ask the operator for its induction requirements and approved portable-device list before arrival.

Can a handset cope with steppe winters?

The IS-04Z1 has IP68 sealing, but cold affects any lithium battery. Ask for the datasheet's operating range and trial a unit on a winter round at a remote site. Site rules on charging and storing devices in safe areas still apply.
