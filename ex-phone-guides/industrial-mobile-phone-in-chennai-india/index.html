The term · industrial

What “industrial” means for a smartphone

“Industrial” describes a phone built for plant conditions: sealed, drop-resistant and manageable at fleet scale through Android Enterprise. It does not by itself mean the phone may enter a classified area. Only a device with a valid explosion-protection certificate and marking may do that.

The IS-04Z1 is designed to be both an industrial Android phone (IP68, 4,000 mAh battery, 50 MP camera, Android 16 with EMM and MDM support) and an intrinsically safe one. Its explosion-protection certificates are in progress and have not been issued.

In Chennai (Manali and Ennore industrial area). Refinery and port shifts here involve resident staff, contract crews and marine personnel with different data needs. MDM or EMM enrolment keeps one managed fleet across those groups, and the IS-04Z1 supports it. Charging and software updates would take place in safe areas such as control rooms or workshops, not on process units.

Frequently asked questions

Can we buy an industrial mobile phone in Chennai India today?

Not today. Until the certificates are issued the IS-04Z1 stays out of classified areas, and until launch there is nothing to purchase. You can ask for the datasheet and the certification schedule, or book a safe-area evaluation unit.

What does an industrial mobile phone in Chennai India cost?

SAMS has not published a price. Any figure you are offered for an uncertified Ex phone should be treated with caution; written indicative pricing is available for a defined quantity and site.

Is the IS-04Z1 an industrial phone as well as an Ex phone?

Yes by design: IP68 sealing, a 4,000 mAh battery, a 50 MP camera and Android 16 with EMM and MDM support. The explosion-protection certificates are separate and still in progress.

Which areas at Chennai (Manali and Ennore industrial area) would use it?

Likely candidates are refinery process units, lube base stock and wax plants, crude and product storage, marine liquid terminal. Which areas are classified, and to what zone, is set by the site operator's own area classification drawings.

Is intrinsically safe the same as explosion proof?

Not exactly. Explosion proof is a broad everyday term; intrinsic safety is one protection concept, which limits energy so ignition cannot occur. Flameproof (Ex d) is a different concept that contains an explosion. The IS-04Z1 is intrinsically safe by design.

What hazardous areas does the Manali complex include?

A refinery and petrochemical complex has process units, tank farms, loading gantries and feedstock lines, all with classified areas set by the operators. Confirm each operator's area classification and accepted equipment approvals before a visit.

How does Chennai's northeast monsoon affect plant work?

Most rain and cyclones arrive between October and December, and low-lying areas can flood. Plan equipment deliveries and shutdown work outside that window where possible, and keep devices in sealed cases during transport.

How does Chennai's humid, salty air affect a handheld device?

Salt-laden humid air accelerates corrosion on ports, seals and contacts. An IP68 sealed design helps, and rinsing and routine inspection of seals remain good practice. Battery life also depends on heat. Ask the site's inspection team what cleaning and storage routines they expect for portable equipment.
