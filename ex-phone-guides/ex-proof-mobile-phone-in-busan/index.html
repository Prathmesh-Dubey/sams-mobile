The term · Ex-proof

What “Ex-proof” means for a smartphone

“Ex-proof” is shorthand for equipment carrying an explosion-protection marking (the Ex mark) that matches the area it enters. The marking states the protection concept, gas or dust group and temperature class, and it must be backed by a certificate issued by a recognised body, so a phone described as Ex-proof should come with a certificate number you can check.

The IS-04Z1 is designed to carry the marking II 2 G Ex ib IIC T4. That is a target: certification is in progress, no certificate has been issued, and SAMS will publish the certificate numbers when they exist.

In Busan. Zone 1 conditions arise in vessel tank spaces, ship repair yards, fuel and chemical storage and port cargo handling. A marking check would compare the label's gas group and temperature class with the product or cargo in question. The IS-04Z1 target marking is II 2 G Ex ib IIC T4, not yet issued.

Frequently asked questions

Can we buy an Ex-proof mobile phone in Busan today?

Not today. Until the certificates are issued the IS-04Z1 stays out of classified areas, and until launch there is nothing to purchase. You can ask for the datasheet and the certification schedule, or book a safe-area evaluation unit.

What does an Ex-proof mobile phone in Busan cost?

No price is published. While certification is in progress there is no list price and no committed lead time, and a supplier quoting a firm figure for an uncertified Ex handset is promising something it cannot stand behind. Indicative pre-launch pricing is issued in writing against a stated quantity and site.

What Ex marking will the IS-04Z1 carry?

The marking target is II 2 G Ex ib IIC T4. It becomes a marking only when the certificate is issued; until then it is a design target.

Which areas at Busan would use it?

Likely candidates are vessel tank spaces, ship repair yards, fuel and chemical storage, port cargo handling. Which areas are classified, and to what zone, is set by the site operator's own area classification drawings.

Is intrinsically safe the same as explosion proof?

Not exactly. Explosion proof is a broad everyday term; intrinsic safety is one protection concept, which limits energy so ignition cannot occur. Flameproof (Ex d) is a different concept that contains an explosion. The IS-04Z1 is intrinsically safe by design.

Why is Busan useful for sourcing or shipping hazardous-area equipment into Korea?

It is the country's busiest port, with frequent container services and a large forwarding and customs sector. Typical import checks include safety and radio approvals, so confirm requirements with your forwarder before shipping handheld devices.

Does Busan's climate cause problems for phones used outdoors?

It is relatively mild, with a humid subtropical climate, cool dry winters and a monsoon-influenced summer, so extremes are rare. Salt air near the port and summer humidity are the main concerns, so sealed connectors and regular cleaning are typical.

Are typhoons a concern for devices used at Busan quaysides?

Busan is among the most typhoon-prone Korean cities, so rain, wind and salt air are routine. The IS-04Z1 is designed to IP68 for dust and water, which helps, but that is not explosion protection. Check battery and charging plans for storm periods with the yard.
