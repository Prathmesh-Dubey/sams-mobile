Field notes · Spain

More about Spain that matters for portable equipment

Spain: industry on the ground

In 2020 Spain's primary energy came from petroleum (42.3%), natural gas (19.8%), coal (11.6%), nuclear (12%) and renewables (14.3%), so refining, gas and power sites all matter.

Spain: projects and investment

Renewables supplied 46.7% of power consumption in 2021 and Spain was the first country to rely on wind as its top energy source for a full year, so wind and solar construction is a large workload.

Spain: infrastructure

The Port of Tarragona has doubled its bulk-liquid storage area to 36 hectares, holds over 900,000 cubic metres of storage and serves chemical plants through a common pipeline rack, the Dixquimics Rack, with 71 lines.

Spain: logistics and access

Algeria is Spain's largest natural gas supplier, and since November 2021 gas has mainly arrived through the Medgaz pipeline, with LNG shipping as a possible supplement, so terminals and entry points are key sites.

The term · ATEX and IECEx

What “ATEX and IECEx” means for a smartphone

ATEX is the European legal framework for equipment used in explosive atmospheres (Directive 2014/34/EU). IECEx is the international certification scheme built on the IEC 60079 standards. Buyers outside Europe often ask for both: an IECEx certificate is the technical evidence most national regimes build on, while ATEX is the legal route into the European market.

For the IS-04Z1 both routes are in progress with TÜV in Germany and neither certificate has been issued. The marking target is II 2 G Ex ib IIC T4: equipment Group II (surface industry), Category 2 (Zone 1 gas), protection concept ib (intrinsic safety), gas group IIC and temperature class T4.

In Spain. A Spanish procurement or HSE reviewer at Repsol, Petronor or Moeve would expect ATEX evidence under the EU framework, with certificate, marking and ambient range checked first. A dual ATEX and IECEx route also helps international EPCs and contractors working across the Spanish complexes. The IS-04Z1 certification is in progress and no certificate exists yet.

Frequently asked questions

Can we buy an ATEX and IECEx mobile phone in Spain today?

No. Nothing can be bought or taken into a classified area today: certification is in progress and launch is planned for Q4 2026. What is available now is the datasheet, the written certification schedule and evaluation units for safe-area use.

What does an ATEX and IECEx mobile phone in Spain cost?

No price is published. While certification is in progress there is no list price and no committed lead time, and a supplier quoting a firm figure for an uncertified Ex handset is promising something it cannot stand behind. Indicative pre-launch pricing is issued in writing against a stated quantity and site.

Is the IS-04Z1 ATEX and IECEx certified?

Not yet. Both routes are in progress with TÜV in Germany and no certificate has been issued. Any listing that says otherwise is wrong.

Which sites in Spain does this cover?

The market guide for Spain covers Tarragona, Cartagena and Escombreras, Huelva, Campo de Gibraltar, among others. Site-level pages are linked below.

How do I become a SAMS distributor for Spain?

Apply through the distributor page with your company details and the territory you cover. SAMS reviews applications company by company and has not yet granted a territory for Spain.

Where are Spain's main refining and chemical sites?

Cartagena's Escombreras valley is among the oldest refinery locations, and Repsol and other operators run refining and chemical sites on the coasts. Details change, so confirm the current site list and each operator's contractor requirements before planning work.

How do LNG and pipeline gas sites affect device choice in Spain?

Gas entry points, regasification plants and compressor stations are likely Zone 1 or Zone 2 areas, so check the device's gas group and temperature class against the site's classification drawings. Operators typically also require device registration and induction, so allow time before a shutdown.

Will the IS-04Z1 handle Spanish summer heat at a coastal refinery?

It is designed with IP68 sealing and a rugged build for heat, salt and wash-down. The T4 target is judged against the ambient range on the certificate, which is in progress. Check that range against peak August temperatures at your plant before specifying.
