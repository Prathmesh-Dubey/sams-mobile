More about Oslo that matters for portable equipment

Oslo: geography and layout

Oslo sits at the northern end of the Oslofjord, with a municipal population of 724,290 in 2025, a greater urban area of about 1.1 million, and a metropolitan area of roughly 1.5 million.

Oslo: climate and equipment wear

Oslo has a humid continental climate with fairly cold winters and some of the warmest summers in Norway, and the driest seasons are winter and spring while summer and autumn are wettest.

Oslo: background

The waterfalls of the Akerselva powered some of Norway's first modern industry in the 1840s, and the city's population grew from about 10,000 in 1814 to 230,000 by 1900.

Oslo: industry on the ground

The city is a hub of Norwegian trade, banking, industry and shipping, and an important centre for maritime industries, with many of the world's largest shipping companies, shipbrokers and marine insurers based there.

The term · hazardous area

What “hazardous area” means for a smartphone

A hazardous area is a place where flammable gas, vapour or combustible dust may be present. Standards divide these into zones: Zones 0, 1 and 2 for gas and vapour, and Zones 20, 21 and 22 for dust. Zone 1 and Zone 21 are the tier where an explosive atmosphere is likely to occur in normal operation, which is where the IS-04Z1 is designed to work. Anything carried in must be marked for the zone, gas group and temperature class of the area.

No certificate has been issued for the IS-04Z1 yet, so it must not be taken into a classified area until the certificate covering it exists and the site operator has accepted it.

In Oslo. Oslo's main classified areas are petroleum product storage and loading, methanol and bulk cargo handling and fuel depots, mainly at Sjursoya. The terminal operator classifies them, not SAMS. Offices such as those at Fornebu lie outside Oslo's municipality and are generally not classified areas.

Questions · Oslo

Frequently asked questions

Can we buy a hazardous area smartphone in Oslo today?

Not today. Until the certificates are issued the IS-04Z1 stays out of classified areas, and until launch there is nothing to purchase. You can ask for the datasheet and the certification schedule, or book a safe-area evaluation unit.

What does a hazardous area smartphone in Oslo cost?

SAMS has not published a price. Any figure you are offered for an uncertified Ex phone should be treated with caution; written indicative pricing is available for a defined quantity and site.

Which hazardous areas is the IS-04Z1 designed for?

Zone 1 and Zone 21 (gas and dust) with gas group IIC and temperature class T4, subject to certification. It must not be used in a classified area before its certificate is issued and the site has accepted it.

Which areas at Oslo would use it?

Likely candidates are petroleum product storage and loading, methanol and bulk cargo handling, fuel depots. Which areas are classified, and to what zone, is set by the site operator's own area classification drawings.

How do I become a SAMS distributor for Oslo?

Apply through the distributor page with your company details and the territory you cover. SAMS reviews applications company by company and has not yet granted a territory for Oslo.

How does Oslo's winter affect handheld equipment used outdoors?

Winters are fairly cold under a humid continental climate, so battery performance and display response can fall in low temperatures. Typically users keep spare batteries warm and check the device's rated operating temperature against site conditions.

Can shipping-sector contacts in Oslo help with marine hazardous-area requirements?

Often. Oslo's concentration of shipping companies, brokers and insurers means technical managers and buyers are close together. They can usually tell you which certifications and class requirements apply to portable devices on their fleets, but confirm each requirement in writing.

Which Norwegian rules apply to equipment in explosive atmospheres?

ATEX-based rules apply to equipment, with authorities such as Havtil and DSB involved in oversight in general terms. A supplier should ask the terminal or company which documents it requires. Confirm specifics with the site operator or the relevant authority.
