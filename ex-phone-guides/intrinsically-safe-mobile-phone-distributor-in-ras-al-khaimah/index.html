The term · intrinsically safe

What “intrinsically safe” means for a smartphone

Intrinsic safety limits the electrical and thermal energy in a circuit so that neither a spark nor a hot surface can ignite a gas or dust atmosphere, even when a fault occurs. The ib level, used for Zone 1, tolerates one fault. It is the usual way to make a handheld device that a worker carries continuously through a hazardous area.

The IS-04Z1 is designed on this principle for Zone 1 and Zone 21 (marking target II 2 G Ex ib IIC T4). Intrinsic safety is a design basis, not a certificate: certification is in progress and none has been issued yet.

In Ras Al Khaimah. Many small and mid-sized sites here have only a few classified spots, such as a solvent store or fuel supply. A handheld intrinsically safe device is suited to occasional inspection rounds, because staff can carry one phone rather than fixing terminals at each spot. It is designed for such use.

Frequently asked questions

Can we buy an intrinsically safe mobile phone in Ras Al Khaimah today?

Not yet. The IS-04Z1 is pre-launch and its certification is still in progress ahead of a Q4 2026 launch. No unit goes into a classified area before the certificate covering it exists. The datasheet, the certification schedule and safe-area evaluation units are available now.

What does an intrinsically safe mobile phone in Ras Al Khaimah cost?

There is no list price yet. SAMS gives indicative pre-launch pricing in writing, against a stated quantity and site, and does not commit to a lead time before certification completes.

What does ‘intrinsically safe’ mean for the IS-04Z1?

The circuits are designed to limit energy so a spark or hot surface cannot ignite a gas or dust atmosphere, even with one fault (level ib, Zone 1). Certification of that design is in progress.

Which areas at Ras Al Khaimah would use it?

Likely candidates are port fuel and bulk-cargo handling, pharmaceutical solvent areas, kiln fuel gas supply and powder handling (zone 21), gas distribution networks. Which areas are classified, and to what zone, is set by the site operator's own area classification drawings.

How do I become a SAMS distributor for Ras Al Khaimah?

Apply through the distributor page with your company details and the territory you cover. SAMS reviews applications company by company and has not yet granted a territory for Ras Al Khaimah.

Do ceramics plants create hazardous areas?

They can. Kilns and dryers use fuel gas, which may need gas classification around supply points, and tile powders can create dust, which operators assess separately. RAK Ceramics is a large local example; classification is plant-specific, so request the site's documents.

Can visiting engineers fly directly to Ras Al Khaimah?

Yes. Ras Al Khaimah International Airport serves the emirate, so visitors do not have to land in Dubai, although many do and drive. Check current flight schedules and the client's access rules; ask about equipment import paperwork too.

Is a smaller site in Ras Al Khaimah likely to need a classified-area phone?

It depends on what the site handles. Port fuel, pharmaceutical solvents and kiln fuel or powder areas may be classified, while offices are not. The operator classifies its own areas. Ask the site's HSE officer whether any area you visit is classified before choosing a device.
