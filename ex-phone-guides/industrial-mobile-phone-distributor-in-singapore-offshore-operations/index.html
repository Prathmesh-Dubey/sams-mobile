The term · industrial

What “industrial” means for a smartphone

“Industrial” describes a phone built for plant conditions: sealed, drop-resistant and manageable at fleet scale through Android Enterprise. It does not by itself mean the phone may enter a classified area. Only a device with a valid explosion-protection certificate and marking may do that.

The IS-04Z1 is designed to be both an industrial Android phone (IP68, 4,000 mAh battery, 50 MP camera, Android 16 with EMM and MDM support) and an intrinsically safe one. Its explosion-protection certificates are in progress and have not been issued.

In Singapore offshore support sector. Activity depends on the timing of rig and FPSO projects, so headcount swings between quiet periods and peaks. A managed fleet under EMM or MDM can be sized up and down, with enrolment before devices reach the yard. Charging stays in safe areas, and the project sets network and app rules.

Frequently asked questions

Can we buy an industrial mobile phone in Singapore Offshore Operations today?

Not today. Until the certificates are issued the IS-04Z1 stays out of classified areas, and until launch there is nothing to purchase. You can ask for the datasheet and the certification schedule, or book a safe-area evaluation unit.

What does an industrial mobile phone in Singapore Offshore Operations cost?

There is no list price yet. SAMS gives indicative pre-launch pricing in writing, against a stated quantity and site, and does not commit to a lead time before certification completes.

Is the IS-04Z1 an industrial phone as well as an Ex phone?

Yes by design: IP68 sealing, a 4,000 mAh battery, a 50 MP camera and Android 16 with EMM and MDM support. The explosion-protection certificates are separate and still in progress.

Which areas at Singapore offshore support sector would use it?

Likely candidates are rig and floating-unit conversion, offshore equipment repair workshops, vessel mobilisation quays, hazardous materials storage. Which areas are classified, and to what zone, is set by the site operator's own area classification drawings.

How do I become a SAMS distributor for Singapore offshore support sector?

Apply through the distributor page with your company details and the territory you cover. SAMS reviews applications company by company and has not yet granted a territory for Singapore offshore support sector.

How can I plan equipment for project cycles in offshore yards?

Newbuild and conversion projects ramp up in phases, with contractor numbers peaking during outfitting and commissioning. Dates are set by owners and yards, so contact the project team early to learn the approved-equipment list and induction requirements.

Do offshore support bases have strict access rules?

The Loyang base describes 24-hour manned security, strict access requirements and video surveillance. Expect advance registration and gate screening, and declare any electronic equipment. Confirm details with the host company before travel.

Who sets the equipment rules for offshore conversion projects?

Buyers here are typically yards, service companies and operators with their own approvals, and a project may follow the unit owner's rules as well. SAMS cannot grant approval. Ask each project's safety team for its device requirements, and confirm with the site operator.
