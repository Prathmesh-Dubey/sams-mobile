Offshore Indonesia: geography and layout

Natuna Regency, the northernmost part of Riau Islands Province, has only 1,984 sq km of land within a total area of about 264,198 sq km, so almost all of it is open sea.

Offshore Indonesia: industry on the ground

The Tangguh field in Bintuni Bay, West Papua, was estimated to hold 4.4 trillion cubic feet of proven gas, with annual LNG output expected to reach about 7.6 million tonnes.

Offshore Indonesia: infrastructure

Indonesia produced about 450.8 million tonnes of oil equivalent of energy in 2019, and its total primary energy supply rose by nearly 60 percent between 2000 and 2021.

Offshore Indonesia: background

The Arun field in Aceh produced gas from the 1970s and is now largely depleted, and nearby operations included the North Sumatra offshore gas field, with assets passing to Pertamina in 2015.

The term · ATEX and IECEx

What “ATEX and IECEx” means for a smartphone

ATEX is the European legal framework for equipment used in explosive atmospheres (Directive 2014/34/EU). IECEx is the international certification scheme built on the IEC 60079 standards. Buyers outside Europe often ask for both: an IECEx certificate is the technical evidence most national regimes build on, while ATEX is the legal route into the European market.

For the IS-04Z1 both routes are in progress with TÜV in Germany and neither certificate has been issued. The marking target is II 2 G Ex ib IIC T4: equipment Group II (surface industry), Category 2 (Zone 1 gas), protection concept ib (intrinsic safety), gas group IIC and temperature class T4.

In Offshore Indonesia. An offshore HSE reviewer in the Natuna area, Java Sea or Kutai Basin would expect certificate evidence matched to the marking, with its schedule of conditions. The IS-04Z1 evidence is in progress. A dual route helps operators and contractors who move equipment between fields.

Frequently asked questions

Can we buy an ATEX and IECEx smartphone in Offshore Indonesia today?

Not yet. The IS-04Z1 is pre-launch and its certification is still in progress ahead of a Q4 2026 launch. No unit goes into a classified area before the certificate covering it exists. The datasheet, the certification schedule and safe-area evaluation units are available now.

What does an ATEX and IECEx smartphone in Offshore Indonesia cost?

SAMS has not published a price. Any figure you are offered for an uncertified Ex phone should be treated with caution; written indicative pricing is available for a defined quantity and site.

Is the IS-04Z1 ATEX and IECEx certified?

Not yet. Both routes are in progress with TÜV in Germany and no certificate has been issued. Any listing that says otherwise is wrong.

Which areas at Offshore Indonesia would use it?

Likely candidates are wellhead and production platforms, fpsos and floating production units, drilling and workover areas, gas processing modules. Which areas are classified, and to what zone, is set by the site operator's own area classification drawings.

How do I become a SAMS distributor for Offshore Indonesia?

Apply through the distributor page with your company details and the territory you cover. SAMS reviews applications company by company and has not yet granted a territory for Offshore Indonesia.

Is offshore equipment in Indonesia affected by the monsoon season?

Seas and weather vary through the year, with monsoon periods affecting boat and helicopter schedules, so deliveries of spares can be delayed. Devices should be rated for humidity, salt and rain, and spares should be carried to cover a longer supply gap.

How do crews usually reach offshore gas sites near the Natuna Islands?

Typically by air to Ranai on Natuna Island and then by supply boat or helicopter to the installation, though routes depend on the operator. Check the operator's mobilisation instructions and baggage limits for electronic equipment well before travel.

How long might it take to replace a failed handset offshore?

Because personnel and spares move by helicopter and supply vessel, replacement can take days. Plan spare units and offline-capable apps. The operator may require approval before mobilisation. Confirm logistics with the operator's offshore team.
