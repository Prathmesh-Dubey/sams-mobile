The term · ATEX and IECEx

What “ATEX and IECEx” means for a smartphone

ATEX is the European legal framework for equipment used in explosive atmospheres (Directive 2014/34/EU). IECEx is the international certification scheme built on the IEC 60079 standards. Buyers outside Europe often ask for both: an IECEx certificate is the technical evidence most national regimes build on, while ATEX is the legal route into the European market.

For the IS-04Z1 both routes are in progress with TÜV in Germany and neither certificate has been issued. The marking target is II 2 G Ex ib IIC T4: equipment Group II (surface industry), Category 2 (Zone 1 gas), protection concept ib (intrinsic safety), gas group IIC and temperature class T4.

In CNOOC. A CNOOC reviewer assessing a handset for Bohai or South China Sea platforms, onshore terminals or the Huizhou complex would look for a certificate of conformity, schedule of conditions and marking. ATEX and IECEx evidence helps international partners and EPCs working with CNOOC on offshore and petrochemical projects. The IS-04Z1 is in certification and nothing has been issued.

Frequently asked questions

Can CNOOC buy the IS-04Z1 today?

Not yet. The IS-04Z1 is pre-launch and its certification is still in progress ahead of a Q4 2026 launch. No unit goes into a classified area before the certificate covering it exists. The datasheet, the certification schedule and safe-area evaluation units are available now.

What does an ATEX and IECEx smartphone for CNOOC cost?

Is the IS-04Z1 ATEX and IECEx certified?

Not yet. Both routes are in progress with TÜV in Germany and no certificate has been issued. Any listing that says otherwise is wrong.

How does CNOOC approve suppliers?

Supplier registration and equipment approval are run by the organisation itself; confirm current requirements with its procurement and HSE teams.

How will SAMS supply CNOOC?

Through authorised distributors once certificates exist. Until then SAMS can send the datasheet and certification schedule and arrange safe-area evaluation units.

What language is needed for documentation in China?

Chinese is the working language at Chinese sites, so labelling, manuals and training for hazardous-area devices are normally expected in Chinese, with English accepted for some engineering exchange. Confirm the required documentation language with CNOOC's procurement and safety departments.

How do typhoons and sea conditions affect offshore equipment planning?

Offshore facilities in the South China Sea and Bohai Bay area typically plan around seasonal storms and cold-weather periods, which restrict crew changes and logistics. For handheld devices this means robust sealing and battery planning; confirm operating rules with the platform operator.

What should be checked for typhoon-season offshore use?

Check ingress protection, battery behaviour in heat and humidity, and whether the platform's rules allow handheld devices in a given area. IP68 helps with rain and spray but is not explosion protection. The IS-04Z1 is not yet certified. Confirm conditions with the platform operator.
