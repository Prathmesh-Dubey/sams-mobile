Explosion proof mobile phone for pharmaceuticals and specialty chemicals in Belgium

Looking for an explosion proof mobile phone for pharmaceuticals and specialty chemicals in Belgium? Away from the Antwerp cluster, Belgium has major drug and fine chemical sites: Janssen at Beerse and Geel, Pfizer at Puurs, GSK's vaccine sites in Wavre, Rixensart and Gembloux, and chemical works such as Solvay in Wallonia. This page is for HSE and engineering teams where solvent, powder and GMP rules meet in one room. The IS-04Z1 is designed around intrinsic safety rather than a flameproof enclosure.

The sector · Kempen and Wallonia

Where hazard and GMP overlap

Not every pharmaceutical site has a significant classified area. A fill-finish plant making sterile products may have very little. The classified ones are where active ingredients are made. Public sources describe the Geel plant as a raw-material (API) production site for Janssen, and API synthesis uses solvents such as alcohols and ketones, which give Zone 1 or Zone 2 gas atmospheres at reactors, centrifuges and solvent recovery, and powders that give Zone 21 or 22 dust at milling, blending and packing. These can be a few metres apart.

The extra layer is GMP. A handset carried into a controlled area must be cleanable and must not shed particles, and the site decides what may enter. That is a separate requirement from the Ex rating: a certified device is not automatically accepted into a cleanroom, and the page does not claim it is. Wallonia adds chemical works at Jemeppe-sur-Sambre and Feluy, covered on the country page, and Evonik's Antwerp plants are in the Antwerp cluster.

What a pharma and fine chemical site does to a explosion proof mobile phone in Belgium

Gas and dust in one device

The design target is Zone 1 gas at group IIC T4 and Zone 21 dust, so a single handset suits a solvent area and a powder area close together. Whether a given powder needs its own checks, for example for minimum ignition energy, belongs to the site's zoning file.

Wipe-down and cleaning regimes

Sites clean frequently. IP68 sealing allows washing, but which disinfectants are permitted, and what a given cleaning agent does to the casing, must be confirmed with the site and the datasheet.

Records and data integrity

Batch records are regulated. The handset is a communication and photographic tool; it is not a GMP system, and any use for records must go through the site's quality and IT teams.

Spread-out geography

The sites are scattered across Flanders and Wallonia, so a group often has several. Android 16 supports Dutch and French, and one MDM console can manage units across sites.

What a pharma site will ask for

We do not claim any relationship with any company named. Pharmaceutical groups have strict supplier qualification, and equipment entering a GMP area is usually assessed by a quality department as well as by HSE. Ask each site's procurement, quality and HSE teams for the current requirements. Generally expect the ATEX certificate, CE marking, declaration of conformity, gas group, temperature class and Dutch or French instructions, with SCC or VCA commonly asked of contractors. Certification of the IS-04Z1 to ATEX 2014/34/EU is in progress ahead of the Q4 2026 launch.

Questions · pharmaceuticals and specialty chemicals

Frequently asked questions

Is the IS-04Z1 certified for pharma and fine chemical use yet?

No. The device is pre-launch and ATEX certification is in progress ahead of the Q4 2026 launch. Nothing will be supplied for a classified area before the certificate covering it exists.

Does an Ex certificate mean the device can enter a GMP area?

No. GMP contamination control is a separate decision by the site's quality team. Confirm it directly with the operator.

Does one device cover both solvent and powder hazards?

The design target is Zone 1 gas and Zone 21 dust. Zone 2 and Zone 22 areas are normally covered by equipment suited to the more severe zone. The certificate will state the scope.

How is this different from the Antwerp cluster?

Smaller batches, more varied products, more dust, and a quality regime on top. See the page on an explosion proof phone for BASF Antwerpen for a large integrated site.
