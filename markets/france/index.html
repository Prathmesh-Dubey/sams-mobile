Where it works · France

Where it works in France

French classified-area work is organised around plateformes: shared industrial estates where a refiner, a chemical producer, a gas company and their contractors operate inside one fence under one authorisation. Four corridors carry most of it, the Seine valley below Le Havre, the Loire estuary, the Rhône from Lyon to the sea, and the Étang de Berre, with Lacq and Carling carrying the older gas and coal chemistry.

Operators and contractors such as TotalEnergies, North Atlantic and Rhône Énergies need an explosion proof mobile phone they can carry into classified areas at Gonfreville-l'Orcher, Port-Jérôme and Donges. The IS-04Z1 is designed for Zone 1 and Zone 21 duty there.

Southern summers and the mistral

A plant at Fos, Lavéra or in the Rhône valley works through real summer heat and a wind that carries dust into everything. The ATEX marking target is II 2 G Ex ib IIC T4, and the temperature class is the first thing an instrumentation engineer checks.

Contractors under a plan de prévention

Equipment carried by an entreprise extérieure has to be as documented as your own. Android 16 with EMM and MDM lets a handset be enrolled, restricted and recovered within a shutdown, so a contractor device is controlled rather than tolerated.

French on the device and in the box

Android 16 carries a French interface, and the notice d'instructions and declaration of conformity are produced in French alongside English. On a French site that is a condition of acceptance.

Gas here, powder a few metres away

Zone 21 dust protection sits in the same certification programme as the gas marking. Specialty chemical and pharmaceutical production puts a solvent area and a powder area inside one building more often than a refinery does.

A full shutdown shift

The 4,000 mAh cell is sized for a whole grand arrêt shift with the camera and a work order application in use, because the nearest charging point is outside the classified area.

Locating and recording, under control

France treats employee geolocation as a data protection question with CNIL guidance behind it, and the CSE expects to be consulted. Location services, the camera and individual applications are set by policy, so what is switched off can be demonstrated.

Regulatory route · France

What your HSE and procurement teams will ask for.

On the product side France is straightforward. ATEX 2014/34/EU applies directly, a notified body issues the certificate and the CE marking follows, with no separate French approval afterwards. Buyers do expect a familiar name on the certificate, and the two they read fastest are INERIS, notified body 0080, and LCIE Bureau Veritas, 0081. Two French conditions matter in practice: the notice d'instructions has to be supplied in French, and radio conformity runs under the Radio Equipment Directive with the ANFR responsible for spectrum and radio equipment in France.

The obligations that shape the purchase belong to the employer and the operator. Articles R.4227-52 to R.4227-54 of the Code du travail require a document relatif à la protection contre les explosions, the DRPCE, which records the risk assessment and the protective measures and is carried into the DUERP; the zoning behind it follows the arrêté of 8 July 2003, in zones 0, 1 and 2 for gas and 20, 21 and 22 for dust. INERIS publishes the practical ATEX guidance most French preventionists work from, and INRS the prevention material behind it. Above that sits the site itself, authorised under the ICPE regime and, past the Seveso thresholds, classified seuil bas or seuil haut and inspected by the DREAL.

Typical Zone 1 and Zone 21 duty for the IS-04Z1 explosion proof mobile phone in France includes: Grand arrêt on a Normandy or Loire platform; Shared perimeters in the Vallée de la Chimie; Small crews at terminals and storage sites; Evidence for the DREAL and the POI.

Refining01

Grand arrêt on a Normandy or Loire platform

A French shutdown puts months of work into a fixed window with hundreds of contractor crews on site. A Zone 1 handset lets an isolation be photographed, a query settled by voice and a permit signed where the work is, which is time a schedule built around walking cannot recover.

Chemical platforms02

Shared perimeters in the Vallée de la Chimie

South of Lyon, and again at Roussillon and Carling, several companies operate inside one platform with common utilities and common rules. A device covering Zone 1 and Zone 21 and locked down centrally crosses those boundaries without a second handset waiting at each one.

LNG and gas storage03

Small crews at terminals and storage sites

Montoir, Fos, Dunkerque and the underground storage sites run classified areas with compact teams and long outdoor rounds. The check-in, the position fix and the record all sit on the device already used to call the control room.

Seveso compliance04

Evidence for the DREAL and the POI

On a seuil haut establishment, inspection and internal emergency exercises turn ordinary decisions into evidence. Photographs taken where the observation was made, timestamped and filed against the right equipment, are easier to defend than an account written afterwards.

Ask about France →
Is the IS-04Z1 already ATEX certified for use in France?

No. It is designed to ATEX 2014/34/EU for Zone 1 and Zone 21 and the certification programme is in progress. Launch is planned for Q4 2026, and no unit is supplied for use in a classified area before the certificate covering it is issued.

Does France require a national approval on top of ATEX?

No. ATEX 2014/34/EU applies directly and there is no separate French approval for Ex equipment. Two French conditions do apply: the notice d'instructions, the manual and the declaration of conformity must be in French, all planned into the launch alongside English, and radio conformity sits under the Radio Equipment Directive with the ANFR as the French authority.

What is the price of an ATEX phone in France?

No list price is published before launch, and a firm price for a device still in certification should be treated with suspicion. Pre-launch pricing is quoted against a stated quantity, configuration and support scope. Evaluation units are placed under a trial agreement rather than sold.

Can we reference it in our DRPCE before it launches?

You can prepare the entry, not close it. The datasheet gives the target marking string, the temperature class and the equipment protection level, so the selection reasoning can be drafted internally. It should only be signed as deployed equipment once the certificate number exists.

What does the T4 target mean on a hot southern site?

T4 limits the maximum surface temperature of the equipment to 135 °C, which covers most substances handled on French sites. That figure is about the device, not the weather: the ambient range the certificate fixes is published with the datasheet, and summer behaviour at Fos or Lavéra is what an evaluation should measure.

How do we handle the camera and geolocation with our CSE?

By configuration, and by showing it. Android 16 supports EMM and MDM, so the camera, location services and individual applications can be set to what the site and the consultation allow, and the resulting policy can be demonstrated to the CSE during the trial.
