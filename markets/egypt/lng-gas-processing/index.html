Egypt · LNG & Gas Processing

Explosion proof mobile phone for LNG and gas processing in Egypt

Egypt has two LNG liquefaction sites, Idku and Damietta, and a gas processing and transmission network behind them, which makes the explosion proof mobile phone question in Egypt for LNG and gas processing a chain with several different environments in it. A handset that suits a cold, marine liquefaction train must also suit an inland compressor yard. This page maps that chain for HSE, process safety and telecoms teams, and says what a handset would be asked to do at each link. The IS-04Z1 is designed around intrinsic safety rather than a flameproof enclosure.

The chain · LNG and gas

Explosion proof mobile phone areas along Egypt’s LNG chain

Gas enters the system from offshore Mediterranean and Delta fields and from the Western Desert. GASCO processes and transmits much of it, including NGL work at El Amreya near Alexandria. Some reaches the two coastal export plants. Idku, operated through Egyptian LNG, has two trains and is reported to be Shell-operated. Damietta, on the Mediterranean coast near Port Said, is owned by SEGAS; after a restructuring agreed in 2020 and 2021 the shareholding was reported as Eni 50 per cent, EGAS 40 per cent and EGPC 10 per cent, and the plant resumed exports in 2021 after being idle since 2012. Earlier descriptions naming Union Fenosa Gas as operator relate to the previous ownership. The hazardous-area picture is a gradient: methane in IIA through most of the chain, with ethylene or propane refrigerant and condensate in liquefaction units, and NGL fractionation inland. That is why the specification request is usually Ex ib IIC rather than a gas-by-gas selection.

Cold process, but a warm handset

LNG storage and transfer involve cryogenic temperatures at the equipment, not at the user’s hand. Check the certificate’s ambient range and the marking target of II 2 G Ex ib IIC T4, and do not assume that an Ex mark says anything about contact with cold surfaces.

Coastal terminals versus inland yards

Idku and Damietta add humidity and salt to the gas hazard; inland stations add dust and distance. IP68 sealing and rugged build cover both.

Distance between sites

Maintenance crews cover many stations. A shift-length 4,000 mAh battery and four-constellation GNSS avoid safe-area charging trips and make coordinates part of the record.

Photography controls

Export terminals restrict cameras. The 50 MP camera is handled through Android Enterprise policy rather than by buying a camera-less device.

One console for a long chain

Enrolment into an existing mobile device management system lets an owner apply consistent policy at the plant and at a remote station.

How work is organised · LNG and gas

Shutdown campaigns and shipping schedules

Terminals01

Cargo-driven operations

Liquefaction plants run to the shipping schedule, and loading is a controlled sequence between shore and ship. Rounds and inspections continue around the cargo operation.

Plant outages02

Train turnarounds

A planned train outage brings in specialist contractors for compressors, cold boxes and instrumentation. The more people entering the area, the more value in a managed handset carrying permits and a directory.

Approvals03

Separate sites, separate reviews

Each operator applies its own engineering review. We have not verified specific registration programmes; ask each operator’s procurement and HSE teams. See also explosion proof phone for Egyptian LNG at Idku, the GASCO network page and the upstream oil and gas page.

Questions · LNG and gas

Frequently asked questions

Do Idku and Damietta follow the same approval route?

No. They are separate companies with separate site reviews, so approval at one does not carry to the other automatically.

Is LNG methane in gas group IIA?

Yes, methane is group IIA. Liquefaction can also involve refrigerants and condensate, and a few plant areas may carry hydrogen, so Ex ib IIC is requested for flexibility.

Could the device go onto an LNG carrier?

No claim is made. Ship classification and flag-state rules are outside our certification programme and would need separate evaluation.

Is the IS-04Z1 available for LNG sites now?

No. It launches in Q4 2026 with certification in progress and nothing supplied into a classified area before the certificate exists.
