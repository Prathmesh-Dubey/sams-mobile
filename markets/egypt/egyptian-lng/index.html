Egypt · Egyptian LNG (ELNG)

Explosion proof mobile phone for Egyptian LNG, Idku terminal

Idku, on the Mediterranean coast east of Alexandria, is where Egyptian LNG liquefies gas for export, and it is one of two LNG sites in the country. For anyone specifying an explosion proof mobile phone for Egyptian LNG in Egypt, the site is compact and process-heavy: gas arrives by pipeline, is treated, liquefied and sent to storage and the loading jetty, with each step classified differently. This page is for the HSE, operations and telecoms staff who decide what a technician may carry into a train, a tank area or a berth. The IS-04Z1 is designed around intrinsic safety rather than a flameproof enclosure.

The terminal · Idku

Explosion proof mobile phone areas at the Idku LNG terminal

Industry reporting describes Idku as a two-train plant of about 7.2 million tonnes a year, owned by a consortium that includes Shell, Petronas and the Egyptian state companies EGAS and EGPC, with Shell named as operator; confirm the present structure with the company. What matters for a handset is the process chain. Feed gas comes in by pipeline and passes through inlet separation, acid gas removal and dehydration, then through the liquefaction trains where it is cooled to around minus 162 degrees Celsius. The product runs to insulated storage tanks and out along the jetty to carriers through loading arms. Methane is group IIA, but liquefaction also involves refrigerant inventories and condensate, so the plant’s area classification drawing is the document that defines which parts are Zone 1, Zone 2 or outside. The loading jetty adds a marine boundary that moves with the carrier. Ambient conditions are coastal rather than desert: humid, salt-laden, hot in summer.

What a liquefaction site does to a handset

A liquefaction plant is a place where the cold equipment is insulated from the people, but the humidity and the salt reach everyone.

Cold plant, warm people

The LNG is cold; the technician’s hands and device are not. The temperature class on the certificate and its ambient range are the numbers to check, and the marking target is II 2 G Ex ib IIC T4. A handset is not designed to touch cryogenic surfaces and should never be used that way.

Salt air on the Mediterranean coast

Idku sits on the coast, so spray and humidity reach the handset all year. IP68 sealing and rugged build are the design answer.

Photography control at an export site

Export infrastructure is camera-restricted. The 50 MP camera is useful for logging a leaking flange or a damaged insulation joint, and the owner’s Android Enterprise console can disable it by area or role.

Rounds across a big flat site

Between the trains, storage and jetty a technician covers a long walk with nowhere to charge inside the boundary. A 4,000 mAh battery is specified for a full shift, and four-constellation GNSS shows where on the site an observation was made.

Arabic and English on one shift

The workforce includes Egyptian and expatriate staff. The interface and manual are intended in both languages.

How work is organised · Idku

Rounds, cargo operations and turnarounds

Operations01

Control-room-led rounds

Operators walk set routes through the trains and utilities, reporting readings and anomalies to a central control room. One handset for voice, photographs and notes reduces the equipment signed out at the start of a shift.

Cargo02

Carrier arrival and loading

Loading involves a tightly controlled sequence between ship and shore. Communications are normally through marine radio and fixed systems; a handset is for supporting staff on the jetty area under the loading master’s rules, not a replacement for those.

Turnaround03

Planned outages with contractor surges

Trains are taken down for maintenance in planned campaigns, with inspection and mechanical contractors on site. Questions · Egyptian LNG

Frequently asked questions

Does an LNG plant need a different protection concept from other gas plants?

No. The protection concept for Zone 1 is the same, Ex ib for intrinsic safety. The difference is the cold and marine environment, which is a matter for the certificate’s ambient range and the device’s environmental testing.

Can the handset be used at the Idku loading jetty?

Only if the jetty area’s classification and the loading master’s rules permit it, and only once a certificate covering the area exists. No certificate has been issued yet.

Is Idku the same as the Damietta terminal?

No. They are separate companies on separate sites, each with its own site engineering review; see the LNG page for Damietta.
