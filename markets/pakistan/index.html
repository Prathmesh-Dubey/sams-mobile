Built for local conditions

Built for Pakistani operating conditions

The Pakistani problem is heat: summer ambients in Sindh and Balochistan among the highest anywhere, dust storms inland, monsoon humidity on the coast, and handsets that travel long distances between field and city.

For evaluators in Pakistan, the IS-04Z1 explosion proof mobile phone is designed around these points: Extreme summer heat; Gas group for field and plant atmospheres; Dust storms and rough handling; A full shift away from a charger; Coverage on remote fields; Control over a shared fleet.

Extreme summer heat

Summer ambients in the southern fields regularly pass 45 degrees. The certified ambient range is stated on the certificate and will be published when it is issued, so check it against your site maximum before planning summer use.

Gas group for field and plant atmospheres

Natural gas, LNG and refinery service is mostly Group IIA, with IIB on some process units. A marking target of Ex ib IIC covers those, so one specification runs from a wellsite to a refinery.

Dust storms and rough handling

Dust and grit get into everything, and kit gets dropped on hardstanding. IP68 sealing keeps particulate out and the handset has a rugged build.

A full shift away from a charger

Wellsites, compressor stations and jetties offer no safe area socket mid shift. The 4,000 mAh battery is sized for a working day with camera, torch and GNSS in use.

Coverage on remote fields

In parts of Balochistan and Sindh the carrier signal is thin. The device falls back to plant Wi-Fi, and four constellation GNSS fixes a position away from landmarks.

Control over a shared fleet

Handsets pass between rotations and contractors. Android 16 with EMM and MDM support gives enrolment, remote lock and remote wipe, and the device is registered with PTA so it works on Pakistani networks.

Regulatory route · Pakistan

What your HSE and procurement teams will ask for.

The certificate HSE and engineering teams in Pakistan ask for is the IECEx Certificate of Conformity, with the ATEX certificate where a specification names the European directive. The Oil and Gas Regulatory Authority and the Department of Explosives regulate the sector and its petroleum installations, but the requirement that reaches site is the operator equipment specification.

One Pakistani step is critical for a phone. Mobile devices must be type approved by the Pakistan Telecommunication Authority and registered in its Device Identification, Registration and Blocking System, and an unregistered handset is blocked from Pakistani networks. That approval is handled as part of the launch programme, and supply runs through a locally registered partner.

Ask about Pakistan →
Is the IS-04Z1 approved for use in Pakistan yet?

No. The device is pre-launch and the IECEx certification programme is in progress, as is every other scheme. Nothing should be planned into a classified area until the Certificate of Conformity exists.

Which certificate will a Pakistani operator ask to see?

The IECEx Certificate of Conformity in almost every case, because operators and their partners here specify to IEC standards. An ATEX certificate is issued in parallel where a specification names the European directive.

Will the phone work on Pakistani mobile networks?

Yes, once it is type approved by the Pakistan Telecommunication Authority and registered in DIRBS. Unregistered handsets are blocked, so this is handled as part of the launch programme and delivery.

Will it cope with summer heat in Sindh?

It is designed for hot, dusty operation with IP68 sealing. The certified ambient temperature range is stated on the certificate and will be published when it is issued, so check it against your site maximum.

Can it be used at an LNG terminal?

It is designed for Zone 1 and Zone 21 with a marking target of Ex ib IIC T4, which covers the gas groups found in LNG service. Operator management of change still applies.

When will units be available, and who repairs them?

Launch is Q4 2026. Warranty and service run through the Pakistani partner SAMS is appointing, and repair of certified Ex equipment must be done by a recognised facility.

What is the price of an explosion proof phone in Pakistan?

No list price is published before the certificates exist. Pricing is quoted in writing against quantity and configuration through the Pakistani partner, and landed cost depends on import and PTA steps, so it is set with the channel rather than published.
