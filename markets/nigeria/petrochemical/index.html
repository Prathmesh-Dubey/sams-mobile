The sector · Nigeria

Where an explosion proof mobile phone is needed in Nigerian petrochemicals

Two clusters dominate. At Lekki, east of Lagos, the Dangote complex combines a large refinery with a polypropylene plant and a urea plant. At Eleme, near Port Harcourt, Indorama operates petrochemical and fertiliser units, and the state's Port Harcourt refinery stands at Alesa-Eleme nearby. Notore operates an ammonia and urea business at Onne, and the state refineries at Warri and Kaduna have their own petrochemical history. The hazards are broader than a pure oil site. Hydrogen is present in hydrotreating and reformer units, ethylene is Group IIB and propylene IIA, ammonia is Group IIA with toxic hazards on top, and polymer powder, pellets and urea granules bring combustible dust to silos, bagging halls and conveyors. Plants like these run tight shutdown windows, so the contractor headcount rises sharply for a few weeks.

What a mixed gas and dust plant does to a handset

A petrochemical site brings gas, vapour and dust together. One handset that covers all of them reduces the number of rules operators have to teach.

Hydrogen and a wide gas group

A marking target of Ex ib IIC covers hydrogen as well as the IIA and IIB hydrocarbons, so a single specification suits a cracker, a reformer and a tank farm.

Dust at bagging and silo areas

Polypropylene and urea dust settles on surfaces and equipment. The Zone 21 design target addresses dust areas, and IP68 sealing keeps fine powder out of the housing.

Humidity and heat

Both Lekki and Eleme are hot and humid for most of the year. The temperature class on the certificate decides where the device may be carried, so check it against the area drawings.

Large plots and long walks

Complexes of this kind cover a large plot, and the 4,000 mAh battery is sized for a full shift with camera and GNSS active.

Contractor surges during shutdowns

Short windows bring hundreds of contractors. Android Enterprise with an existing MDM console lets one team enrol and later wipe temporary devices.

Approvals · Petrochemical

What plant buyers ask for

Plant owners run their own HSE standards. Ask each plant's procurement and HSE teams which equipment review applies, since the Indorama, Notore and Dangote plants are separate companies with separate rules. They normally expect the IECEx certificate, with marking, gas group, dust protection and temperature class. In Nigeria the SONCAP and NCC steps and Nigerian content paperwork apply too. Questions · Petrochemical

Frequently asked questions

Is ammonia handled the same way as hydrocarbon gas?

Ammonia is Group IIA for explosion protection, but it is toxic, so gas detection and respiratory protection apply on top of the handset's Ex marking.

Does urea dust need Zone 21 protection?

Many fertiliser plants classify granulation and bagging areas for combustible dust. The area classification document decides the zone, and the handset is designed with a Zone 21 target.

Why does hydrogen matter for the handset?

Hydrogen is Group IIC, the most easily ignited common group. A handset marked only IIB would not be permitted in those units.

Can one handset serve a refinery and a fertiliser plant?

Design target allows it, but each plant's classification and rules must be checked separately.
