The site · Bonny Island

Where an explosion proof mobile phone is needed on Bonny Island

The NLNG plant occupies the Finima area of Bonny Island, in Rivers State, where gas arrives by pipeline from the Niger Delta and leaves as LNG, LPG and condensate by ship. The six existing trains give a nameplate of 22 million tonnes a year, and the Train 7 project is intended to take that to about 30 million. Press reports in 2026 put Train 7 above 90 per cent complete, so a handset evaluation now overlaps with a plant that is still being commissioned in one area while the older trains run. The atmospheres are mostly Group IIA: methane, propane and condensate vapour, with refrigerant circuits that typically hold propane and mixed hydrocarbon components. Large flare and relief systems, tank farms and long jetty approaches complete the map. The shareholders are NNPC, Shell, TotalEnergies and Eni, so site rules reflect more than one operator culture.

Heat, humidity and a coastal sun

Bonny Island is a low, wet delta island with year-round heat. The temperature class on the certificate is what an instrumentation engineer checks against the maximum surface temperature the area classification allows, and the marking target for the IS-04Z1 is Ex ib IIC T4.

Rain, mud and construction conditions

Train 7 means active construction beside operating trains, with mud and wet ground. IP68 sealing and rugged build are the design answer to drops and immersion.

Cold surfaces and condensation

LNG pipework and tank bases run very cold, and moving from an air conditioned control room to a wet process area causes condensation. Sealing is meant to keep that out of the handset.

Long walks to safe charging

Between a train, the tank farm and a jetty, the distance to a safe area socket is long. The 4,000 mAh battery is specified to cover a shift with camera, torch and GNSS in use.

Many contractors, one policy

Operating crews, Train 7 contractors and visiting marine staff all pass through. Android Enterprise management from an existing MDM console allows enrolment, app lock and remote wipe for a fleet that changes hands.

Approvals · Bonny Island

What an LNG operator's procurement will ask for

An LNG operator with international shareholders normally specifies electrical equipment to IEC standards, and expects the IECEx Certificate of Conformity with the marking, gas group, temperature class and ambient range stated. Ask Nigeria LNG's procurement and HSE teams for their contractor and equipment registration steps rather than assuming them. The Nigerian layer sits alongside: SONCAP for import clearance, NCC type approval for the radios, and documented local content through a Nigerian entity. The IS-04Z1 is pre-launch with certification in progress, and SAMS has no supply relationship with Nigeria LNG.

Questions · Bonny Island

Frequently asked questions

Is Train 7 already operating?

Press reports in 2026 describe it as more than 90 per cent complete and in systems completion and pre-commissioning, with a first cargo forecast for 2027. Confirm the current position with Nigeria LNG directly, because it decides which areas are classified and live.

Does LNG vapour need a Group IIC handset?

Methane and propane are Group IIA, and the plant also has hydrocarbon refrigerants. An Ex ib IIC target covers all common gas groups, which simplifies a fleet, but the area classification documents set the requirement.

Can the handset be used on a jetty with a ship alongside?

The jetty is classified and ship and shore have their own rules. A device is only allowed where the terminal and the ship's master agree, and the certificate has to support that zone.

Is NLNG buying the IS-04Z1?

No. SAMS has no order, pilot or agreement with Nigeria LNG. This page describes what the device is designed for at a plant like this.
