Where it works · Algeria

Where it is designed to work in Algeria

Classified area work in Algeria splits between the Saharan fields, where oil and gas are produced and processed far from the coast, and the Mediterranean industrial ports of Arzew and Skikda, where gas is liquefied, refined and turned into petrochemicals and fertiliser.

Operators and contractors such as Sonatrach, Eni and TotalEnergies need an explosion proof mobile phone they can carry into classified areas at Hassi Messaoud, Ouargla, Hassi R'Mel, Laghouat and In Amenas, Illizi. The IS-04Z1 is designed for Zone 1 and Zone 21 duty there.

Built for local conditions

Built for Algerian operating conditions

The Algerian problem is the Sahara: summer heat that pushes ordinary electronics past their limits, sand and dust on the wind, and long distances between camp, plant and wellsite.

For evaluators in Algeria, the IS-04Z1 explosion proof mobile phone is designed around these points: Saharan heat and wide swings; Gas group for plant and LNG atmospheres; Sand, dust and vehicle travel; A full shift away from a charger; Coverage that thins out in the desert; Control over a shared fleet.

Saharan heat and wide swings

Summer ambients in the southern fields can pass 45 degrees, with cold desert nights. IP68 sealing keeps sand out, and the certificate will state the ambient range the device is approved for.

Gas group for plant and LNG atmospheres

Hydrocarbon service here is mostly Group IIA, with IIB on some process units. A marking target of Ex ib IIC covers those, so one specification runs from a wellsite to an LNG jetty.

Sand, dust and vehicle travel

Devices ride in pickups between wellsites and get dropped on hardstanding. The handset has a rugged build, and the Zone 21 design covers combustible dust at the coastal plants.

A full shift away from a charger

Wellsites, manifolds and pipeline stations offer no safe area socket mid shift. The 4,000 mAh battery is sized for a working day with camera, torch and GNSS in use.

Coverage that thins out in the desert

Away from camps the carrier signal is patchy. The device falls back to plant Wi-Fi, and four constellation GNSS fixes a position where there are no landmarks to describe.

Control over a shared fleet

Handsets pass between rotations, contractors and camps. Android 16 with EMM and MDM support gives enrolment, remote lock and remote wipe, with French language profiles where crews need them.

Regulatory route · Algeria

What your HSE and procurement teams will ask for.

The certificate HSE and engineering teams in Algeria ask for is an internationally recognised Ex certificate: the IECEx Certificate of Conformity, and the ATEX certificate where a specification names the European directive. Oversight of hydrocarbon activities sits with the Autorité de Régulation des Hydrocarbures, but the requirement that reaches site is the operator equipment specification, usually written by Sonatrach or a joint venture.

Two Algerian steps sit alongside it. Radio equipment needs homologation by ARPCE, the post and electronic communications regulator, before it is imported and used. Procurement by Sonatrach and its partners favours suppliers working through a locally registered company, and documentation in French is expected on site.

Ask about Algeria →
Is the IS-04Z1 approved for use in Algeria yet?

No. The device is pre-launch and the IECEx certification programme is in progress, as is every other scheme. Nothing should be planned into a classified area until the Certificate of Conformity exists.

Which certificate will Sonatrach or a joint venture ask to see?

The IECEx Certificate of Conformity in almost every case, because the Algerian operators and their partners specify to IEC standards. An ATEX certificate is issued in parallel where a specification names the European directive.

Does the phone need ARPCE approval?

Yes. It carries 4G LTE, Wi-Fi, Bluetooth and NFC radios, so homologation by ARPCE is required and forms part of the launch programme alongside the Ex certification.

Is documentation available in French?

Yes. The user manual and safe use instructions will be supplied in French and English, and MDM profiles can be configured in French for crews who need them.

Will it cope with Saharan summer heat?

It is designed for desert operation with IP68 sealing. The certified ambient temperature range is stated on the certificate and will be published when it is issued, so check it against your site maximum before planning summer use.

Can it be used at the LNG plants?

It is designed for Zone 1 and Zone 21 with a marking target of Ex ib IIC T4, which covers the gas groups found in LNG service. Operator management of change still applies.

When will units be available, and who repairs them?

Launch is Q4 2026. Warranty and service run through the Algerian partner SAMS is appointing, and repair of certified Ex equipment must be done by a recognised facility.

What is the price of an explosion proof phone in Algeria?

No list price is published before the certificates exist. Pricing is quoted in writing against quantity and configuration through the Algerian partner, and landed cost depends on import and homologation steps, so it is set with the channel rather than published.
