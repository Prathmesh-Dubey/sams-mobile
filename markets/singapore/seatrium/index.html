The site · Seatrium

An explosion proof mobile phone for Seatrium’s Singapore yards

Seatrium was formed when Sembcorp Marine completed its acquisition of Keppel’s offshore and marine division in February 2023 and took the new name later that year. In Singapore it brings the former Keppel yards at Tuas Boulevard, Tuas and Benoi together with the Sembawang yard. A yard is not a classified plant. It becomes one, temporarily and in patches, when something flammable is on the hull. A tanker arrives for repair with cargo residue in its tanks and has to be gas-freed before anyone can weld. An FPSO conversion reaches hydrocarbon commissioning and then has live gas and oil systems in a quayside berth. Coating and painting inside tanks release solvent vapour. Each of these creates a Zone 1 or Zone 2 area that exists for a shift or a month and is then declared gone. The question for a handset is not whether the yard is classified, but whether the person carrying it can enter the space that is.

What a shipyard does to a handset

A yard moves. The job today is not where the job was last week, and the classified area follows the hull rather than the map.

Permits that change daily

Each job needs its own permit, and a gas-free certificate may expire at the end of a shift. A handset that stays with the person lets the permit, the test record and the photograph live together rather than on separate pieces of paper.

Steel, spray and heat

Hot decks, spray, dust and constant handling are normal. IP68 sealing and rugged build suit that, and the certificate states the temperature class and ambient range the marking holds for.

Large sites on foot

A yard is long and a ship is taller than it looks. A 4,000 mAh battery covers a full shift of walking, calls, photographs and position fixes without a trip back to a safe-area charger.

Subcontractor crews from many places

Yards run many contractor teams on one site. Android Enterprise enrolment from an existing MDM console lets Seatrium’s IT team issue handsets by job, limit the apps and withdraw them at handover.

Supplier approval is Seatrium’s process

How Seatrium assesses equipment suppliers is for its procurement and HSE teams to say. We do not claim any listing or relationship; send us their requirements and we will respond in writing.

Questions · Seatrium

Frequently asked questions

A yard is not a hazardous area. Why would it need an Ex phone?

Parts of it are, temporarily. Cargo tanks being gas-freed, hydrocarbon commissioning on a topsides module and solvent coating in a tank are all classified conditions. The phone is for those conditions, not for the offices.

Does the device replace the gas detector?

No. A handset is not a gas detection instrument, and the permit and gas-free certificate still rely on the correct testing equipment. The phone carries the records, calls and photographs around it.

Is there a Seatrium agreement with SAMS?

No. This page describes the conditions in the yards and what the device is designed for. It is not an endorsement or supply agreement.

What is claimed about the merger on this page?

Only that Sembcorp Marine completed its acquisition of Keppel’s offshore and marine division in 2023 and became Seatrium, which is how company announcements describe it.
