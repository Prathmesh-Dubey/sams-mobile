The site · Daesan

Explosion proof mobile phone for Hyundai Oilbank at Daesan, South Korea

Hyundai Oilbank's refinery is at Daesan in Chungcheongnam-do, on the Yellow Sea coast, within a petrochemical complex shared with other Korean producers. Trade press describes a base-oil joint venture with Shell at Daesan, and the company's petrochemical units sit alongside the refinery. For a handset the picture is a conventional integrated site: crude and vacuum distillation, conversion units, hydrogen-bearing treating loops, and aromatics and olefins units, together with tank farms and a marine terminal. Hydrocarbon gas and vapour are the main classification drivers, with hydrogen present in treating and conversion, and a conservative operator will treat IIC as the relevant gas group wherever hydrogen is in play. Which areas are Zone 1 or Zone 2 comes from the site's classification drawings.

Why it fits

What Daesan asks of a Zone 1 handset

Daesan has its own character: a newer site, a coastal climate and a layered ownership. These are the points we expect an engineer to raise.

A site that is newer than Ulsan or Yeosu

Newer plants tend to have more complete and consistent area classification drawings, which makes it easier to state where a device may go. The IS-04Z1 design target of II 2 G Ex ib IIC T4 is intended to map straight onto a Zone 1 gas area on such a drawing.

Cold west-coast winters and wind

Daesan sits on an exposed coast, where winter wind and cold are the issue rather than heat. IP68 sealing and rugged build are specified across Korea's seasonal range, and the 4,000 mAh battery is sized for a full outdoor shift.

Group relationships

The operator belongs to a group that also owns a major shipbuilder. That may shape group-level vendor conversations, but site equipment approval at Daesan remains the refinery's own decision.

Refining and petrochemicals on one handset

A technician moving between a crude unit and a petrochemical unit should not need a different device. The Zone 1 gas target covers both, and the camera and GNSS features are the same wherever the person is.

One console for operators and contractors

Android Enterprise enrolment and MDM allow work profiles per team, camera policy by area, and a clean retirement of devices at the end of a shutdown contract.

How work is organised

Shifts, shutdowns and a remote coastal location

A refinery of this kind runs continuous shifts with operators on rounds and permit-to-work controlling maintenance. Planned shutdowns bring in contractors in large numbers, so gate processing and device handover happen at scale. Daesan is a coastal industrial site rather than a city centre, so local spares and a defined swap process for a failed device matter. Ask the site team what the current rule is for personal phones, photography and radios.

Frequently asked questions

Is the IS-04Z1 certified for use at Hyundai Oilbank yet?

No. The device is pre-launch and certification is in progress ahead of the Q4 2026 launch. No evaluation, listing or order exists at Hyundai Oilbank.

Does the group's shipbuilding business influence refinery approvals?

Not directly. Equipment approval at Daesan is a site decision by the refinery's own engineering and HSE staff, although group-level relationships may be visible in procurement conversations.

Does the device suit a newer refinery site?

A more uniform, better documented classification usually makes it easier to say where a device with a defined rating may be used, but the final certificate scope, not the age of the plant, decides.

Does a partner's base-oil or petrochemical unit follow the same approval?

Not necessarily. Units operated by joint-venture partners may run their own rules, so confirm which organisation controls each area.
