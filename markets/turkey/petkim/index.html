Explosion proof mobile phone for PETKIM in Turkiye

Petkim is Türkiye’s integrated petrochemical producer, and its Aliağa complex, built from 1985, is next door to a refinery that supplies it with naphtha. This page is for the process safety and HSE teams who need an explosion proof mobile phone for Petkim in Türkiye to carry through olefin, polymer and port areas. The IS-04Z1 is designed for Zone 1 and Zone 21 and is still being certified. The IS-04Z1 is designed around intrinsic safety rather than a flameproof enclosure.

The complex · Petkim

What an explosion proof mobile phone meets at Petkim Aliağa

Petkim was founded in 1965 by Turkish Petroleum and was privatised in 2008. SOCAR is its majority shareholder, and the original plant is at Yarımca in Kocaeli. The Aliağa complex, near İzmir, began to be built from 1985 and carries most of the group’s production. Published descriptions give about 15 main production facilities and roughly 3.6 million tonnes a year of gross capacity, led by ethylene, with polyethylene, polypropylene, PVC, vinyl chloride monomer and xylenes among the products. Feed arrives from the neighbouring STAR refinery under a long-term offtake agreement for naphtha and mixed xylene, and the Petlim container port next to the site opened in December 2016. What a petrochemical complex does to a handset

A petrochemical site gives a handset two jobs at once: gas near the cracker and dust near the polymer plant. The points below are specific to that mix.

Gas and dust in the same footprint

A cracker is a gas job, while a polyolefin silo or a PVC dryer is a dust job. The IS-04Z1 is designed for Zone 1 gas and Zone 21 dust under one certification programme, so an operator does not swap devices at a boundary.

Ethylene and hydrogen set the group

Ethylene is group IIB and hydrogen is IIC. The marking target of II 2 G Ex ib IIC T4 is chosen so a single handset is not excluded from either unit.

Crews who cross the site all day

A shift engineer visits the cracker, the polymer lines and the quay. Four-constellation GNSS, a 50 MP camera and a 4,000 mAh battery are specified for a day of that, without returning to a safe-area charger.

Documents that come with a turnaround

Planned stops bring contractor crews who need permits, site maps and procedures. Android Enterprise lets the site push only the applications it approves and recover the device when the crew leaves.

Questions · Petkim

Frequently asked questions

Does Petkim publish a supplier or HSE requirement?

Petkim publishes a document on ESG practices in procurement and HSE on its website. It is the right place to start for the company’s expectations. Registration steps for a hazardous-area equipment supplier are agreed directly with the procurement and HSE teams, and this page does not claim any SAMS registration.

Can one handset be used at the refinery next door?

The certificate and marking belong to the device, but each site classifies its own areas. The Petkim and STAR HSE teams define zones separately, and each would check the marking against its own drawings. See the SOCAR Türkiye page for the refinery side.

Why mention gas groups on a petrochemical page?

Different gases ignite at different energies. Ethylene is IIB and hydrogen is IIC, which is why a handset marked only for a lower group may be refused at some units. The IS-04Z1’s marking target covers IIC; the certificate will confirm it.
