Turkey · SOCAR Turkiye

Explosion proof mobile phone for SOCAR Turkiye in Turkiye

SOCAR Türkiye is the Turkish business of Azerbaijan’s state oil company, and its Aliağa holdings are among the largest single hazardous-area estates in the country. This page is for the HSE managers, shift supervisors and telecoms teams who need an explosion proof mobile phone for SOCAR Türkiye in Türkiye that works from the STAR refinery to the terminal jetties. The IS-04Z1 is designed for Zone 1 and Zone 21 and its certification is in progress. The IS-04Z1 is designed around intrinsic safety rather than a flameproof enclosure.

The site · Aliağa

Classified areas for an explosion proof mobile phone at STAR and Aliağa

The STAR refinery was inaugurated on 19 October 2018 after its foundation was laid in 2011, and was designed for 10 million tonnes of crude a year, about 214,000 barrels a day. The company now quotes a higher figure of 13 million tonnes, so ask which number a given drawing uses. Its units include a hydrocracker, a delayed coker and naphtha, kerosene and diesel hydrotreaters, and it makes diesel, jet fuel, naphtha, LPG, mixed xylene and petroleum coke. The refinery was built to feed the neighbouring petrochemical complex at Petkim, and the Aliağa terminal, with 200,000 m³ of oil storage and 45,000 m³ of LPG storage, handles the shipping. That integration means a single day’s round can cross refinery, tank farm and jetty without leaving the same operator’s boundary.

What the Aliağa coast does to a handset

Aliağa is flat, coastal and busy. The first three conditions decide how long a handset lasts; the last decides whether anyone is allowed to carry one.

Coastal air and a long summer

The refinery and terminal sit on the Aegean shore, so salt and heat arrive together. IP68 sealing is aimed at spray and wash-down, and the temperature range on the eventual certificate is what an instrument engineer will check against the area classification.

Carbon dust next to gas

Coke handling puts a dust area close to hydrocarbon gas. The IS-04Z1 is designed for Zone 21 dust as well as Zone 1 gas under one certification programme, so crews are not issued two devices.

Distances between units and the jetty

A round from the process area to the jetty is a long outdoor walk with no charger on it. The 4,000 mAh cell is sized to cover a full round, and four-constellation GNSS gives a coordinate for a defect on a pipe rack.

A contractor workforce with different devices

Construction, maintenance and service firms all carry phones of their own. Managing the IS-04Z1 through the operator’s existing Android Enterprise console lets the site apply one policy to handsets it issues.

Questions · SOCAR Türkiye

Frequently asked questions

What must contractors do before working at SOCAR Türkiye?

SOCAR Türkiye states that service providers must give their employees OHS-E training to receive identification cards and must take part in the company’s HSE training, including contractor induction. Beyond that, ask the procurement and HSE teams what equipment evidence they want. This page does not claim any SAMS registration.

Why does petroleum coke matter to a handset?

Coke is conductive carbon dust. Where an area classification marks a dust zone around coke handling, equipment has to carry a dust rating. The IS-04Z1 is designed for Zone 21, and the dust group and marking will be on the certificate when it is issued.

Are the refinery and the terminal classified in the same way?

No. Each unit, tank and jetty has its own area classification, so the zone and temperature class depend on the drawing for that location. The SOCAR Türkiye HSE team defines them.
