Where it works · Hungary

Where it works in Hungary

The map is short and dense. Százhalombatta on the Danube runs the country's main refinery. Tiszaújváros holds the steam cracker, the polyethylene and polypropylene plants and the polyol complex completed there in 2024. Kazincbarcika produces MDI, TDI and PVC on one integrated site. Around these, the pharmaceutical industry works with solvents in Budapest and Dorog, the gas system runs from the Algyő field to the storage caverns of the Great Plain, and bioethanol and starch plants add flammable liquid and combustible dust to the same picture.

Operators and contractors such as MOL, MOL Petrolkémia and BorsodChem need an explosion proof mobile phone they can carry into classified areas at Százhalombatta, Tiszaújváros and Kazincbarcika. The IS-04Z1 is designed for Zone 1 and Zone 21 duty there.

Gas and dust in one certification programme

The gas marking target is II 2 G Ex ib IIC T4, and Zone 21 dust protection belongs to the same programme. On a polyolefin site, a starch plant or a bioethanol plant that is not a refinement, it is the difference between one handset and a rule nobody follows.

Hungarian on the screen and in the file

Android 16 carries a Hungarian interface, and the user manual, the safe-use instructions and the declaration of conformity are produced in Hungarian alongside English. Buyers here treat Hungarian documentation as part of conformity rather than as a courtesy, so it is planned into the launch.

Summer heat and winter rounds

IP68 sealing and rugged build cover high and low temperature, humidity and vibration, which is the actual span of a Hungarian working year on an outdoor unit. The ambient range fixed by the certificate is published with the datasheet rather than claimed beforehand.

One shift, one battery

A classified area has no charging point, and on a site the size of the Tisza complex the walk to one is a real interruption. The 4,000 mAh cell is sized for a full shift with the camera and a maintenance application in use.

Crews that change every few weeks

Android 16 with EMM and MDM lets a device be enrolled, restricted to the applications the site permits, wiped and reissued as one contractor crew leaves and the next arrives, which during a leállás happens continuously.

Evidence captured where it is found

A 50 MP camera with PDAF records a corroded support, a batch observation or a completed isolation clearly enough to be attached to the job, and a camera can be disabled by policy where a site restricts photography.

Regulatory route · Hungary

What your HSE and procurement teams will ask for.

The product route is European and closed. ATEX 2014/34/EU is transposed by the 35/2016. (IX. 27.) NGM rendelet on the examination and certification of equipment and protective systems intended for use in a potentially explosive atmosphere. A notified body certificate, the EU declaration of conformity and CE marking place the device on the Hungarian market, and no additional national Ex approval follows. Market surveillance for this product group is carried out by Budapest Főváros Kormányhivatala through its metrology and technical supervision organisation, and the radio side runs under RED 2014/53/EU with NMHH as the media and communications authority. Hungarian user documentation is expected with the product, and in practice it is the first thing an internal reviewer checks after the certificate number.

The duty side is the operator's. The 3/2003. (III. 11.) FMM-ESzCsM joint rendelet sets the minimum occupational safety and health requirements for workplaces where an explosive atmosphere may occur, and it obliges the employer to prepare and keep the robbanásvédelmi dokumentáció recording the zone classification, the risk assessment and the equipment selection reasoning. Occupational safety inspection is carried out by the employment supervision authority within the government office system. There is a second supervisory line that outsiders often miss: hydrocarbon production, underground gas storage and pipelines fall under mining supervision, and since 1 January 2022 that function has been carried out by SZTFH, the Supervisory Authority for Regulated Activities, which took over the tasks of the former Mining and Geological Survey. On an upstream or storage site the equipment argument is made to that authority, not only to a labour inspector.

Frequently asked questions

Ask about Hungary →
Is the IS-04Z1 already ATEX certified for Hungary?

No. It is designed to ATEX 2014/34/EU for Zone 1 and Zone 21, and the certification programme is in progress. Launch is planned for Q4 2026. Nothing is supplied for use in a classified area before the certificate covering that area exists, and we will not describe the device otherwise while it is still in certification.

Does Hungary add a national approval after ATEX?

No. The 35/2016. (IX. 27.) NGM rendelet transposes the directive, so a notified body certificate with the EU declaration of conformity and CE marking completes the product side. The obligations that follow belong to the employer under the 3/2003. (III. 11.) joint rendelet, and on upstream and gas storage sites to mining supervision by SZTFH.

What is the price of an ATEX mobile phone in Hungary?

No list price is published before launch, and a figure quoted for a device still in certification would not be worth much to a category buyer. Pricing is given in writing against a stated quantity, configuration and support scope. Evaluation units are provided under a trial agreement rather than sold.

Will the documentation and the interface be in Hungarian?

Yes. Android 16 carries a Hungarian interface, and the user manual, the safe-use instructions and the declaration of conformity are produced in Hungarian alongside English. The marking string and temperature class are supplied in a form the robbanásvédelmi dokumentáció can quote directly.

Can it be used at a gas storage site or a wellhead?

Those are among the intended cases, subject to the site's own classification. Zone 1 and Zone 21 coverage, IP68 sealing and four-constellation GNSS suit dispersed installations. The point to plan for is that mining supervision applies there, so the certificate evidence should be prepared for SZTFH scrutiny as well as for the safety file.

Does the target marking cover hydrogen?

Group IIC is the target, and IIC is the group that includes hydrogen. That matters in Hungary because hydrogen is already present in refinery and chemical service, and handheld equipment that stops at IIB tends to be replaced sooner than a procurement cycle allows.

Who handles repair and battery replacement?

The Hungarian partner SAMS is appointing or a facility recognised for repairing Ex equipment. A device like this cannot be opened by a general phone workshop without breaking the protection concept its certificate rests on, so the repair and battery route is defined before launch rather than after the first breakage.
