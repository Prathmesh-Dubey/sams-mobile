Explosion proof mobile phone for chemicals and pharmaceuticals in Hungary

BorsodChem's Kazincbarcika complex produces MDI, TDI and PVC at industrial scale, while Budapest is home to two of Hungary's major pharmaceutical manufacturers, Richter Gedeon and Egis, both working with solvents in active ingredient production. This page is for the HSE, maintenance and engineering teams at those plants who need to know what an explosion proof mobile phone for Hungary has to offer, and what it cannot yet claim: the IS-04Z1 is designed for Zone 1 and Zone 21, and certification is in progress. The sites · Kazincbarcika, Budapest, Dorog and Körmend

Where an explosion proof mobile phone matters in Hungarian chemicals and pharmaceuticals

Kazincbarcika, in Borsod county, is the main production site of BorsodChem, a producer of isocyanates (MDI and TDI), PVC and chlor-alkali products. Process chemistry there runs from chlorine and hydrogen through nitration and aniline-type intermediates to polymer powder, so the plant carries gas, vapour and dust hazards side by side. The pharmaceutical picture is different in kind: Richter Gedeon works in Budapest and Dorog, Egis in Budapest and Körmend, and active ingredient synthesis there depends on organic solvents that are charged, distilled, recovered and stored. The classified areas are smaller than a refinery's but more numerous and more varied.

What isocyanate chemistry and solvent plants ask of a handset

Gas and dust in one programme

A BorsodChem PVC bagging hall and a Richter or Egis powder suite are dust areas; the reactors beside them are gas areas. The IS-04Z1 design target covers Zone 1 and Zone 21, so a technician does not swap equipment to cross the boundary.

Gas group on the datasheet

Chlor-alkali hydrogen is IIC, while most plant solvents are IIA or IIB. The marking target is II 2 G Ex ib IIC T4, so the group does not limit where the unit can go, but the temperature class should be checked against solvents with low autoignition temperatures such as carbon disulphide.

A toxic hazard is not an ignition hazard

Chlorine, phosgene-type chemistry and isocyanates are toxic before they are flammable. A handset does not replace a personal gas detector or the plant's toxic-gas alarms; it sits alongside them for calls, photographs and permits.

Cleanable surfaces and sealed ports

Pharmaceutical areas are washed down and cleaned to procedure. IP68 sealing is designed to tolerate wiping and washdown, though a cleaning agent should always be checked against the housing material with SAMS before it enters a validated cleaning procedure.

Records that stand up to audit

A 50 MP camera and four-constellation GNSS record a defect, a tag and a position. In regulated pharmaceutical work, how records are held and signed is set by the operator's own quality system, and the handset is managed from the existing MDM console.

How work is organised

Turnarounds, contractors and what procurement will ask

Continuous chemical plants such as Kazincbarcika run through planned shutdowns, when contractor numbers rise and device control becomes a daily task. Pharmaceutical sites work differently: production runs in campaigns and batches, with scheduled maintenance between them, and changes to equipment are controlled through the operator's quality system. We have not verified any named supplier-registration scheme for either BorsodChem, Richter Gedeon or Egis, so none is described here. Ask each company's procurement, HSE and quality teams for their approval route, the documents they need and any site induction. Expect questions on the ATEX certificate and marking, Hungarian-language instructions, temperature class, cleaning compatibility and how repairs are controlled.

Questions · Chemicals and pharmaceuticals

Frequently asked questions

Is the IS-04Z1 certified for use at Hungarian chemical or pharmaceutical sites yet?

No. The device is pre-launch and certification, including ATEX, is still in progress ahead of the Q4 2026 launch. Until the certificate exists, no unit should enter a classified area.

Does approval at one pharmaceutical company carry over to another?

No. Each operator runs its own procurement, HSE and quality review, even where two sites are in the same city, so each decision stands alone.

Can the phone be used where toxic chemicals such as chlorine are present?

The Ex marking addresses ignition, not toxicity. Toxic-gas detection and respiratory protection remain separate controls, and the handset is not a substitute for either.

Does the device suit pharmaceutical cleanrooms?

That depends on the cleanroom grade and the operator's procedures. The IS-04Z1 is designed for classified areas rather than for controlled-environment suites, so confirm surface, cleaning and entry rules with the site's quality team before planning use.
