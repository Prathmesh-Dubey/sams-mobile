Thailand · Terminals & Ports

Explosion proof mobile phone for Terminals & Ports in Thailand

Thailand's liquid bulk, LPG and LNG move through a handful of ports on the eastern seaboard, and an explosion proof mobile phone for Thailand's terminals has to work on jetties, in tank farms and at loading arms. This page covers the sector across Map Ta Phut, Sriracha and Laem Chabang. The SAMS IS-04Z1 is designed for Zone 1 and Zone 21; it is pre-launch and certification is in progress. The IS-04Z1 is designed around intrinsic safety rather than a flameproof enclosure.

Terminals and ports · the site

Liquid bulk and gas berths on the eastern seaboard

Map Ta Phut Industrial Port in Rayong is administered by the Industrial Estate Authority of Thailand and is described as the country's largest industrial port. Port listings name liquid bulk terminals operated by Map Ta Phut Tank Terminal, Rayong Refinery, Star Petroleum Refining, Thai Tank Terminal (reported as a PTT Global Chemical and Vopak partnership) and PTTCH. IEAT's Phase 3 project, on about 1,000 rai with a 2,200 metre pier and a 16 metre channel, has six berths: two liquid cargo, three gas carrier and one general cargo. Laem Chabang, owned by the Port Authority of Thailand, is mostly a container port, but its Phase 3 includes Terminal F, awarded to a consortium of Gulf Energy, PTT Tank Terminal and CHEC. Sriracha's refinery jetties sit between the two.

Spray, salt and rain during transfer

Work carries on through the monsoon, and a transfer cannot stop because it is raining. IP68 sealing and rugged build answer water and salt; the Ex marking decides entry.

Cargo changes, classification must not

A tank can hold a vegetable oil one quarter and a solvent the next. The marking target of II 2 G Ex ib IIC T4 is set to the worst case a berth is licensed to handle.

Cameras at a security boundary

Port photography is a security and customs question as well as a safety one. A camera that the operator's console can switch off per site meets both.

Position on a long pier

A 2,200 metre pier gives few landmarks. Four-constellation GNSS puts a fix on the transfer record.

Terminals and ports · working and approvals

How terminal work is organised, and what operators would ask for

A terminal works to a ship schedule, with contractors and surveyors on the same berth in an afternoon.

Ship-driven peaks

Work arrives with the vessel: gauging, hose connection, sampling and surveyor inspection in a few hours. A handset that carries the transfer checklist and photographs reduces the paperwork reconciled afterwards.

Several employers on one berth

Terminal staff, ship crew, surveyors and hauliers share a jetty, so policy should follow the enrolled device and not the person.

Vendor and equipment approval

We have not verified a published approval scheme for portable devices at these terminals, so we do not describe one. Ask each terminal operator and IEAT or the Port Authority for site access rules and the documents expected alongside the Ex certificate.

National file

TISI, the Department of Industrial Works and NBTC apply to the device on top of the IECEx evidence, and all three are in progress.

Questions · Terminals and ports

Frequently asked questions

Can terminal operators buy an explosion proof mobile phone from SAMS today?

No. The IS-04Z1 is pre-launch with a Q4 2026 launch, and IECEx, ATEX and NEC 500 certification are in progress. The datasheet and certification schedule are available now.

Is a handset needed on LNG berths?

Where the area classification includes gas, yes. LNG is mainly methane (group IIA); the IIC design target covers the strictest case and the classification drawings decide each location.

Who sets access rules at Map Ta Phut Port?

IEAT administers the port and estate, and each terminal operator adds its own rules. A device usually has to clear both.
