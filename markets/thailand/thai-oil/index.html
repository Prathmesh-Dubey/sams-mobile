Thailand · Thai Oil

Explosion proof mobile phone for Thai Oil in Thailand

Thai Oil runs the largest refinery in Thailand at Sriracha, on the Chonburi coast, and it is a site where an explosion proof mobile phone has real work to do: hydrogen at pressure, sour service and hot hydrocarbon in the same fenced plot. This page is for the HSE, instrumentation and IT teams who decide what a technician may carry into the Sriracha units. The SAMS IS-04Z1 is designed for Zone 1 and Zone 21, and it is pre-launch, so certification is still in progress. The IS-04Z1 is designed around intrinsic safety rather than a flameproof enclosure.

Thai Oil · the site

What is classified inside the Thai Oil refinery at Sriracha

Thai Oil describes its Sriracha plant as a complex refinery with a crude capacity of 275,000 barrels a day, and the group also runs aromatics, lube base oil, power and marine businesses around it. The Clean Fuel Project announced to lift throughput towards 400,000 barrels a day adds a new crude and vacuum unit, a vacuum gas oil hydrocracker, a residue hydrocracker, a hydrogen plant, a sulphur recovery unit and a power plant fired on residue pitch, according to trade press coverage of the EPC award. For a handset that matters because the heaviest hydrogen and sour-gas service on the site is exactly the new equipment, built beside units that stay in production. Completion was reported for around 2025, so ask Thai Oil for the current status.

What Sriracha does to a handset

Hydrogen sets the gas group

Reformer, hydrotreater and hydrocracker loops all carry hydrogen, so the marking target is II 2 G Ex ib IIC T4. A device assessed only to IIB would be the wrong tool across much of a modern refinery.

Coastal heat and monsoon rain on open structures

Chonburi is hot and humid all year and the southwest monsoon arrives from May. Furnace decks, pipe racks and tank roofs have no shelter, so the IP68 sealing and rugged build are the design floor, with the certificate ambient range as the compliance figure.

A camera that policy can switch off

Refineries control photography tightly. Android 16 with Android Enterprise lets the plant push a camera policy to every enrolled unit from its existing MDM console, and withdraw it for contractors when the job finishes.

Thai Oil · working and approvals

How work is organised at Sriracha, and what Thai Oil teams would ask for

Two things run at once on a refinery site: the permanent shift pattern, and project or shutdown work layered over it. The second is where most unfamiliar handsets turn up.

Rounds, not tasks

Operators work a fixed sequence of readings and valve checks across a large plot, with the control room and a safe-area charging point a drive away. The 4,000 mAh battery is specified to cover a full shift with the camera, GNSS and radio app running, which is how rounds are actually done.

Project and turnaround crews

A construction programme beside live units means many contractor firms sharing the same classified boundaries as operations.

Vendor and equipment approval

We have not verified a published Thai Oil programme for portable electronics, so we do not describe one. The sensible route is to ask Thai Oil procurement and HSE for the equipment approval steps, the documents expected with an Ex certificate, and whether the unit needs to be assessed for the specific zone before it is issued.

Thai Oil · use cases

Where the IS-04Z1 explosion proof mobile phone goes to work.

Operations01

Hydroprocessing unit rounds

An operator walks a hydrocracker or hydrotreater route, records readings and photographs a weeping flange without leaving the classified area. The photograph and the GNSS position go straight onto the notification, so the planner sees the fault and where it is.

Maintenance02

Hot-work permits and gas-test records

A permit receiver on a turnaround job needs the gas test result and the permit in front of them at the vessel, not at the permit office. A managed handset carries the approved permit app and a site directory and can be locked down once the job is signed off.

Marine03

Jetty and offshore receiving points

Transfer crews work far from the control room, on decks and walkways with no landmarks to describe a position by. Four-constellation GNSS puts a fix on the record and the IP68 body copes with spray and rain during a transfer.

Questions · Thai Oil

Frequently asked questions

Can Thai Oil buy an explosion proof mobile phone from SAMS today?

No. The IS-04Z1 is pre-launch with a Q4 2026 launch, and IECEx, ATEX and NEC 500 certification are in progress. A reviewer can have the datasheet and the certification schedule now, but nothing is issued into a classified area before the covering certificate exists.

Which refinery units is the handset designed for?

Zone 1 gas areas and Zone 21 dust areas, gas group IIC, with a temperature class target of T4. That covers hydrogen service, light ends and sour areas. Whether it is accepted in a given unit is for Thai Oil engineering and HSE to decide against the certificate once it exists.

Does a sulphur or catalyst block need anything different?

Sulphur recovery and catalyst handling mix sour gas with fine solids, so both Zone 1 and Zone 21 considerations can apply close together. The design target covers both, but the area classification drawings for the specific block govern which zone applies where.
