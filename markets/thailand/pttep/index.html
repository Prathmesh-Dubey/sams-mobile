Thailand · PTTEP

Explosion proof mobile phone for PTTEP in Thailand

PTTEP is the Thai national upstream operator, and its Gulf of Thailand platforms and the Sirikit onshore field in Kamphaeng Phet are places where a standard phone is simply not allowed past the boarding point. This page is for offshore installation managers, HSE leads and telecoms engineers weighing up an explosion proof mobile phone for Thailand upstream work. The SAMS IS-04Z1 is designed for Zone 1 and Zone 21, it is pre-launch, and certification is in progress. The IS-04Z1 is designed around intrinsic safety rather than a flameproof enclosure.

PTTEP · the site

Where PTTEP works: Gulf platforms and the Sirikit oilfield

Trade press reports that PTTEP took over operatorship of the G1/61 block (Erawan, Platong, Satun and Funan) from Chevron when the new production sharing contract took effect on 24 April 2022, and already operated G2/61, which holds Bongkot. Those two concessions were reported to carry roughly 60 per cent of Thailand's gas supply, with a campaign of new wellhead platforms, subsea pipelines and drilling announced to hold output up. Arthit, further south, is built around a large central processing platform about 230 kilometres off Songkhla in roughly 80 metres of water, with wellhead platforms feeding it and condensate exported to a floating storage unit at Bongkot. Onshore, Sirikit in Lan Krabue District, Kamphaeng Phet, was discovered in 1981 and is still the largest onshore oilfield in the country, with PTTEP as operator since 2004.

Salt, humidity and monsoon spray

Platform decks are wet, salty and warm for most of the year, and the southwest monsoon adds rough seas. IP68 sealing and rugged build are the design floor, and the certificate ambient range is the compliance figure.

Gas group IIC where hydrogen may appear

Offshore gas is mainly methane, which sits in group IIA. The IS-04Z1 targets II 2 G Ex ib IIC T4, which is stricter than the platform needs and means one device also covers any onshore unit with hydrogen. Zone 21 dust is rarely the issue offshore, so Zone 1 gas is the case that matters here.

A fix when there is no horizon to describe

Reporting a leaking flange on a wellbay is easier with a coordinate and a photograph than with a description. Four-constellation GNSS and the 50 MP camera are intended for that use.

A shift that is really a rotation

Offshore crews work a fixed rotation away from shore, so a unit lost or flat on day two stays lost until the next crew change. The 4,000 mAh battery is sized for a long working day, with charging done in the accommodation and not on the deck.

PTTEP · working and approvals

How offshore work is organised, and what PTTEP teams would ask for

The platform is the workplace, the accommodation and the muster point at once, which shapes what a handset must do.

Rotation and permit to work

A small crew works a rotation on or near the platform, with permits issued per job and scope increasing around maintenance campaigns. A managed handset can hold the permit, the platform directory and daily report in one place under the platform operator's own policy.

Contractors and campaigns

New wellhead platforms and drilling campaigns bring rig, installation and commissioning contractors who are not PTTEP staff. Enrolment profiles let the operator decide what each group may run, and withdraw it when the campaign ends.

Vendor and equipment approval

We have not verified a published PTTEP approval scheme for portable devices, so we do not describe one. Ask PTTEP procurement and HSE for the equipment approval steps, the documents expected alongside an Ex certificate, and the offshore transport rules for batteries and radio devices.

Thai national file

The ex evidence underneath is an IECEx Certificate of Conformity, with TISI, Department of Industrial Works and NBTC steps for sale and use in Thailand. All are in progress, and PTTEP would still review the device itself.

Offshore01

Platform rounds and defect reporting

An operator walks the process and compression decks, photographs a seal or gland defect and attaches the position to the notification. The planner onshore sees it before the next supply boat leaves rather than after the crew change.

Drilling02

Rig and workover supervision

A rig brings its classified area with it, and the crew changes with the campaign. An enrolled handset carries the permit app and site directory and can be locked down to the applications the operator approved for that job.

Onshore03

Sirikit wellsites and station visits

Crews travel between dispersed wellsites in Kamphaeng Phet where signal and landmarks are sparse. GNSS and a camera on one intrinsically safe unit record which well, which valve and what was found.

Questions · PTTEP

Frequently asked questions

Can PTTEP buy an explosion proof mobile phone from SAMS today?

No. The IS-04Z1 is pre-launch with a Q4 2026 launch, and IECEx, ATEX and NEC 500 certification are in progress. The datasheet and certification schedule are available now; no unit will be supplied into a classified area before the covering certificate exists.

Does the handset work offshore without a mobile network?

It depends on the platform. Platforms normally run their own Wi-Fi or radio network, and the handset joins it like any Android device. We make no claim about coverage in open water and the connectivity design belongs to PTTEP's telecoms team.

Is Zone 21 relevant on a platform?

Seldom. Offshore risk is overwhelmingly gas, so Zone 1 and Zone 2 apply, with dust zones mainly in specific stores or abrasive blasting. The Zone 21 design target matters more for onshore plants and for contractors who move between sectors.
