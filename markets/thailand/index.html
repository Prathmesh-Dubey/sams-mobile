Heat that never breaks, then the rainy season

Rayong stays warm and humid all year and takes the southwest monsoon from May, so sealing must survive a downpour on an open pipe rack as often as a hot afternoon on a cracker structure. IP68 sealing and a rugged build are the design floor; the certificate ambient range is the compliance figure.

Gas group IIC across the cluster

Hydrogen at the reformers and hydrotreaters, ethylene through the cracker network, acetylene in maintenance work — all inside the same estates. A handset rated only to IIB is unusable across much of Map Ta Phut, hence the Ex ib IIC target.

Polymer dust beside hydrocarbon vapour

Polyethylene and polypropylene powder, catalyst loading and additive blending put Zone 21 dust into the same plant as Zone 1 vapour. One specification covers both, rather than issuing two devices.

A Thai reading workforce

Operators, technicians and contractor crews work in Thai; the drawings and procedures behind them are often English. Android 16 carries both on one hardware build, so a plant issues a single device, a single managed app list and a manual the crew actually reads.

Long shifts and gate discipline

Estate passes are issued for a shift, not a task, and leaving a classified area to charge means clearing a gate twice. The 4,000 mAh battery is sized for a full shift with the radio app, the 50 MP camera and four constellation GNSS running.

Regulatory route · Thailand

What your HSE and procurement teams will ask for.

What a Thai plant engineer and a safety officer will ask to see is the certificate, the marking string and the schedule behind them, and the honest position is that none of them is issued yet. Thai practice references the TIS standards administered by the Thai Industrial Standards Institute under the Ministry of Industry, while factory operation and safety sit with the Department of Industrial Works. The technical evidence underneath is an IECEx Certificate of Conformity, since the Thai treatment of explosive atmospheres follows the international IEC 60079 series rather than a separate national protection concept. Where a plant stands inside an estate run by the Industrial Estate Authority of Thailand, the estate layers its own permit and contractor access regime over that — and at Map Ta Phut it is often the layer that really governs what a technician carries past a gate.

The second route is radio and import. A handset is radio equipment, so it needs type approval from the NBTC before it can be sold or put into service in Thailand, and that approval is filed against a legal entity in country rather than the manufacturer abroad. Hence the Thai partner: one entity to hold the approval, import the fleet and keep spares on the ground. Above both routes sits the operator. As in Malaysia and across the Gulf, two timelines run in parallel — national conformity, and the operator’s own engineering and telecoms review. The second is what decides whether a phone reaches the work front, and a PTT group or SCG package will review the device itself whatever the national file says.

Ask about Thailand →
Is the IS-04Z1 approved for hazardous areas in Thailand today?

No. It is pre-launch and every certification, IECEx and ATEX included, is still running ahead of the Q4 2026 launch. Nothing should enter a classified area before the Certificate of Conformity covering it is issued.

Is this a flameproof phone or an intrinsically safe one?

Thai sites use flameproof loosely for anything usable in a classified area, but the concepts differ. Flameproof is Ex d, an enclosure built to contain an explosion inside itself. This device targets Ex ib, the intrinsically safe concept, where available energy is held below anything that could ignite the atmosphere at all.

What is the explosion proof phone price in Thailand?

None is published, and putting a figure against a device whose certification is unfinished would mislead you. Pre-launch pricing is given on request against a stated quantity and configuration, and the landed cost of a compliant unit carries the NBTC work and the import route with it.

Does Thailand accept IECEx certification, or is a separate Thai Ex certificate needed?

Thai practice references the TIS standards administered by TISI, and those follow the international IEC 60079 series, so an IECEx Certificate of Conformity is the evidence a plant and its electrical authority work from. Your operator or estate may add review on top of it.

Does the phone need NBTC approval before we can use it?

Yes. It is radio equipment, so NBTC type approval is required before it can be sold or put into service in Thailand, and it is held by an entity in country. That sits inside the launch schedule.

What zone and what marking does it target?

Gas and dust together, Zone 1 and Zone 21, against an ATEX marking target of II 2 G Ex ib IIC T4. A NEC 500 Class I Division 1 route runs alongside, for Thai packages written to the American code.

Will it get us through the gate at Map Ta Phut?

National paperwork opens that question rather than closing it. An IEAT estate runs its own permit and contractor access regime, and each operator inside it runs an equipment review of its own, so allow time for a site listing as well as for certification.

Can it pair with our gas detectors, and does it run in Thai?

Bluetooth pairing with portable gas detectors is part of the design, and the interface, managed app list and manual set all run in Thai and English on one hardware build, so a mixed company and contractor crew can be issued the same device.

Who will handle warranty, repair and battery replacement?

The Thai partner SAMS is appointing. Ex equipment may only be repaired by a workshop recognised to do it. Open an intrinsically safe handset in an ordinary phone shop and its certification is void.
