The sector · petrochemicals

Explosion proof mobile phone for petrochemical plants in Malaysia

A petrochemical complex starts with gas or naphtha and ends with solid products. At Kerteh, the PETRONAS Chemicals Group site produces olefins, derivatives, ethylene, polyethylene, ammonia, aromatics, vinyl chloride and acrylic acid. At Gebeng the same group makes polypropylene, MTBE, 2-ethylhexyl acrylate and silicone blends, and BASF PETRONAS Chemicals, a joint venture, produces 2-EHA and refined maleic anhydride. At Pengerang, newer plants make polyethylene, polypropylene, ethylene glycols and nitrile butadiene latex. Each product stage brings a different hazard. Upstream there are flammable gases and liquids, with ethylene in gas group IIB and hydrogen in IIC. Downstream, polymer powder and fine dust create Zone 21 situations that a gas-only device cannot enter. A single complex therefore needs devices rated for both, and a safety officer will ask whether one handset can do both jobs. The IS-04Z1 is designed to Zone 1 and Zone 21 for that reason.

Gas and dust under one roof

A polymer plant moves from Zone 1 reactor areas to Zone 21 silo and bagging areas in a single walk. A handset marked only for gas cannot follow. The IS-04Z1 design target of Zone 1 and Zone 21 avoids carrying a second device, subject to the area classification and the final certificate.

Static as a hazard in its own right

Polymer powder and pellets generate static during transfer, and operators wear antistatic clothing for a reason. Ask the site about its static control policy alongside the handset's own marking, and keep the handset's case free from unapproved accessories.

Corrosive and sticky atmospheres

Acid vapours, caustic washes and acrylate liquids can attack seals and coatings. IP68 sealing and a resistant housing help, but sites should be asked which chemicals reach the area and whether wipe-down procedures apply.

Large sites with many gates

Kerteh and Gebeng are industrial areas with many separate plants. Contractors move between operators in the same estate, so one handset with the same configuration, app list and manual set across employers is easier to administer through Android Enterprise.

Humid heat all year

Ambient temperature barely changes through the year and the monsoon brings heavy rain from November. The T4 temperature class and IP68 sealing apply together, and the 4,000 mAh battery is specified for a full shift outdoors.

Questions · petrochemicals

Frequently asked questions

Why does a petrochemical site need Zone 21 as well as Zone 1?

Because powders and fine dust from polymers, additives and fertiliser can form explosive clouds. Zone 21 means such a cloud is likely in normal operation. Zone 1 is the equivalent for gas and vapour. A plant that makes polymer from gas has both.

Is urea or fertiliser dust a Zone 21 hazard?

It depends on the product and the site's dust hazard study. Some fertiliser dusts are weakly explosible and others are not, so the site's classification drawing is the authority. Do not assume a zone from the product name alone.

Which operators would a petrochemical buyer deal with?

The main groups are covered in the PETRONAS Chemicals Group and Pengerang Refining and Petrochemical pages. Ask each site's procurement team about equipment registration.

How does this differ from the oil and gas pages?

Upstream and LNG sites are mainly gas and liquid hazards, covered on the oil and gas page. Petrochemical sites add dust and a much wider range of substances, which is why gas group and dust zone both feature here.
