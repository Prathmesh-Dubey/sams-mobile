Regulatory route · Malaysia

What your HSE and procurement teams will ask for.

What a Malaysian HSE department and an electrical authority will ask for is the certificate, the marking string and the schedule behind them. The duty comes from the Occupational Safety and Health Act 1994 as amended, in force across all workplaces since 1 June 2024 and enforced by DOSH, and hazardous area practice is written around the MS IEC 60079 series adopted from IEC 60079. That makes an IECEx Certificate of Conformity the evidence a site works from. Where the plant is a registered major hazard installation, the Control of Industrial Major Accident Hazards Regulations 1996 add a safety report and DOSH major hazard oversight on top. Above the national requirement sit the operator standards, and PETRONAS Technical Standards are the ones most Malaysian packages are written against.

The radio side is a separate route with its own label. Communications equipment must be certified under the Communications and Multimedia (Technical Standards) Regulations 2000, with SIRIM QAS International acting as the certifying agency for MCMC, and a certified handset carries the compliance label on the product body rather than on the carton. The mains charger supplied with it falls under the Energy Commission’s Certificate of Approval regime for electrical equipment. Commercially there is a third gate: a company selling into PETRONAS entities needs a valid licence or registration through the PETRONAS Licensing Management System, upstream and downstream being handled differently. None of this is complete for this device.

Ask about Malaysia →
Is the IS-04Z1 certified for hazardous areas in Malaysia?

Not yet. It is pre-launch and every certification, IECEx and ATEX included, is in progress ahead of the Q4 2026 launch. Until then you can request the datasheet or join the evaluation programme.

Is this a flameproof mobile phone?

The phrase is used loosely in Malaysia for anything usable in a hazardous area, but the two protection concepts differ. Flameproof means Ex d, an enclosure that contains an explosion. This device targets Ex ib, intrinsic safety, which limits the energy available in the first place.

Does DOSH accept IECEx and ATEX certification?

Malaysian hazardous area practice is written around the MS IEC 60079 series adopted from IEC 60079, so an IECEx Certificate of Conformity is the technical evidence a site and its electrical authority work from. Your operator may specify more on top.

What is the price of an ATEX phone in Malaysia?

There is no list price to give while certification is unfinished, and we do not publish one. Pre-launch pricing is on request against a quantity and configuration, and the delivered cost of a compliant unit includes the MCMC and SIRIM work behind it.

Does it need SIRIM certification to be sold here?

Yes. Communications equipment sold or used in Malaysia is certified through SIRIM QAS International for MCMC and carries the compliance label on the product body. That certification is part of the launch programme.

Can we buy directly, or must it come through a PETRONAS licensed vendor?

Evaluation quantities can be supplied directly. Supply into PETRONAS entities requires the selling company to hold a valid licence or registration, which is a company status rather than a product one, so the partner matters as much as the device.

Which zones and marking is it designed for?

Zone 1 and Zone 21, gas and dust, with an ATEX marking target of II 2 G Ex ib IIC T4. NEC 500 Class I Division 1 is in the same programme for Malaysian projects written to the American code.

Who handles warranty, repair and battery replacement?

The Malaysian partner SAMS is appointing. Repair of certified Ex equipment has to be done by a facility recognised for that work, and opening an intrinsically safe handset in a general phone shop invalidates its certification.
