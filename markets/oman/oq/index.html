Oman · OQ

Explosion proof mobile phone for OQ's Sohar operations

OQ runs Oman's integrated refining and petrochemical complex at Sohar, and an explosion proof mobile phone for Sohar has to combine hydrocarbon Zone 1, hydrogen service, polymer dust and coastal humidity. The IS-04Z1 is designed for Zone 1 and Zone 21 duty on sites like these in Oman. The IS-04Z1 is designed around intrinsic safety rather than a flameproof enclosure.

The company · OQ

Where an explosion proof mobile phone works across OQ's sites

OQ is Oman's state energy group, and its downstream footprint is concentrated on a few coastal sites. At Sohar the refinery was brought onstream in 2006 with a capacity of 116,000 barrels a day, and the Sohar Refinery Improvement Project added five units (crude and vacuum distillation, a delayed coker, a hydrocracker and bitumen blowing) to lift capacity to 198,000 barrels a day. Beside it are aromatics and the Liwa Plastics complex. OQ also holds a half share of the Duqm refinery joint venture, OQ8, with Kuwait Petroleum International, and runs LPG operations at Salalah. Each of these has its own hazards: hydrogen in the hydrocracker, hot residue and coke in the coker, solvent and aromatic vapour in the petrochemical units, and polymer dust at the plastics plant.

What OQ's plants do to a handset

A refinery and a plastics plant are different kinds of dirty, and coastal humidity runs across both. A single device specification has to serve both.

Hydrogen sets the design case

Hydrocracking and reforming are hydrogen service, which is gas group IIC. The marking target of II 2 G Ex ib IIC T4 is set at the strictest case so a single fleet can cover the refinery, aromatics and plastics.

Humidity and salt off the Gulf of Oman

Sohar is damp and salty all year. A rugged build is aimed at humidity and salt, and IP68 covers the washdown applied around process plants.

Polymer and coke dust

Zone 21 is a design target alongside Zone 1, so the handset can work in silo areas and coke handling as well as in gas areas.

Turnaround populations

A shutdown brings in contractors for the duration of a scope. Android 16 with Android Enterprise enrols a lent fleet, restricts the camera and wipes units at demobilisation.

Records that travel

Four-constellation GNSS and a 50 MP camera turn an observation at a furnace or a seal into a record a corrosion engineer elsewhere can act on.

Questions · OQ

Frequently asked questions

Is the IS-04Z1 approved for OQ sites?

No, not yet. National certification is a prerequisite for OQ's own vendor review, and neither is complete. The device is pre-launch with certification in progress ahead of Q4 2026.

Why is gas group IIC the design case?

Hydrogen is gas group IIC, and the hydrocracker and reformer are hydrogen service. Designing to IIC covers the less demanding groups too, so one handset can serve several units.

Does one device cover both the refinery and the plastics plant?

It is designed for Zone 1 gas and Zone 21 dust, so in principle yes, but whether it covers a specific area depends on your hazardous area classification and the certificate once issued.

Can contractors be issued devices for a shutdown?

Yes by design: enrol them into your MDM, restrict the apps and camera, and wipe them at demobilisation.
