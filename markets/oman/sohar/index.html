The site · Sohar

What is actually at Sohar

Sohar Port and Freezone sits on the Gulf of Oman about 200 km north west of Muscat, outside the Strait of Hormuz, which is a large part of why it was built where it is. The port carries three distinct clusters. The petrochemical one is built around the OQ refinery, its aromatics plant and the Liwa Plastics complex, with methanol production and independent chemical tank terminals alongside. The metals cluster runs an aluminium smelter, a direct reduction steel plant and an iron ore pelletising facility with its own ore terminal. Behind both sits the free zone and the industrial estate. Crews and contractors move between them, and a shutdown pulls in a workforce from across the Sultanate.

What Sohar does to a handset

The Batinah coast is humid and salt laden all year, and it gets hot enough in summer that shade planning is part of a work pack. Set against that, a device here has to handle both a hydrocarbon plant and a minerals operation, which are two very different kinds of dirty, and it has to survive a contractor population that changes with every turnaround.

Mineral dust as well as product dust

Iron ore, coal, coke and alumina are handled in the open and on conveyors, and polymer dust sits on the other side of the estate. A Zone 21 dust design with IP68 sealing is what keeps a unit working through both, rather than through the cleaner of the two.

Group IIC, because the refinery makes hydrogen

Hydrocracking, reforming and direct reduction all involve hydrogen rich service, which is gas group IIC. The ATEX marking target of II 2 G Ex ib IIC T4 is set at the strictest case on the estate so one fleet can cover the refinery, the polymer plants and the metals side.

Humidity and salt off the Gulf of Oman

Sohar is damp and salty year round rather than seasonally, which is harder on sealed equipment than dry desert heat. A rugged build is aimed at humidity and salt, and IP68 covers the washdowns the refinery and the quays both apply.

An estate you drive between and then walk

The refinery, the smelters and the terminals are separate sites inside one port estate, and a shift can touch more than one of them. A 4,000 mAh battery specified for a full shift and a body that never needs opening in the field are what carry a unit through that.

Turnaround crews and JSRS registered contractors

A shutdown at Sohar brings in a contractor population issued equipment for the duration of a scope. Android 16 with enterprise management enrols a lent fleet under one policy, restricts the camera and lets it be wiped and recovered at demobilisation.

Questions · Sohar

Frequently asked questions

Can we order units for Sohar now?

Not yet. The IS-04Z1 is pre-launch with a Q4 2026 launch date, and IECEx, ATEX and NEC 500 certification are all still in progress, alongside Omani product conformity and TRA type approval for the radio. We will not supply a unit into a classified area before the certificate covering it exists.

What is the price of an explosion proof phone in Oman?

We publish no figure while certification is running, because quoting one would imply a certainty we do not have. Send the quantity, the sites and the timing, and pre-launch pricing goes to you in writing against that configuration. Volume supply to Omani operators runs through a partner holding an active JSRS certificate, so pricing and channel are settled together.

Do we need a JSRS certificate to evaluate one?

No. Evaluation and documentation do not require it. JSRS becomes decisive when a supplier bids to an Omani oil and gas operator, which is why volume supply into Sohar is planned through a registered Omani partner rather than direct.

Will one device cover both the refinery and the metals plants?

That is what specifying to the worst case is for: Zone 1 and Zone 21, gas group IIC, temperature class T4. Whether it covers a specific area is decided by your own hazardous area classification against the issued certificate, not by this page.

Will it cope with the humidity and salt on the Batinah coast?

That is what a rugged build and IP68 sealing are for. It is also one of the reasons we would rather place an evaluation unit with a crew at Sohar for a full rotation than settle the argument from a datasheet.

How are repairs and battery replacement handled?

An Ex certified device cannot be opened on site or serviced by a general workshop without invalidating its certification. Repair and battery replacement run through SAMS and the Omani partner it is appointing, with spares held within reach of Sohar and Muscat.
