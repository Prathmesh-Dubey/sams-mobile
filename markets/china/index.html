Regulatory route · China

What your HSE and procurement teams will ask for.

Conformity in China runs through the National Supervision and Inspection Centre for Explosion Protection and Safety of Instrumentation (NEPSI), the country’s Ex certification authority. For hazardous-area equipment the technical evidence behind that certification is typically aligned with an IECEx Certificate of Conformity, and because this product is a phone it also needs SRRC (State Radio Regulatory Committee) type approval, now administered under MIIT. None of the three is finished yet.

Getting past the mine or plant gate is a separate exercise. Sinopec, PetroChina and China’s major coal producers each run their own equipment-approval and procurement process, and supply is normally expected to run through a China-registered partner. A device can be fully NEPSI-certified and still be unlisted on an operator’s own approved-equipment register, so plan for two timelines rather than one.

How to evaluate and buy in China

The IS-04Z1 launches in Q4 2026. None of these steps needs a purchase order, and they are ordered so that your approvals and our certification run in parallel.

  1. 01

    Send us the area classification

    Give us the zone, gas or dust group and temperature class for the areas a device would enter, plus the operator whose approval you work under. That is enough for us to say plainly where certification stands against your requirement.

  2. 02

    Take the datasheet and the certificate schedule

    You receive the full specification and a written list of the certifications in progress with target dates. An HSE officer can start an equipment selection justification with it, and procurement can open a vendor file.

Can we buy an explosion proof mobile phone from SAMS in China today?

Not yet. The IS-04Z1 is pre-launch and every certification behind it, including IECEx, ATEX and the NEPSI route, is still in progress ahead of the Q4 2026 launch. What exists today is the datasheet, the certification schedule and a place in the evaluation programme.

Does it cover coal-mining hazards specifically?

The device is designed for Zone 1 gas and Zone 21 dust together, which is the combined methane-and-coal-dust profile of an underground coal working environment. Coal-sector-specific approvals in addition to NEPSI may apply and will be confirmed with the certificate.

What is the price of an explosion proof phone in China?

We do not publish one. Until certification completes there is no list price, and pre-launch pricing is available on request against a stated quantity and site.

Will Sinopec or PetroChina accept it on site?

That decision belongs to each operator and follows the certificates rather than preceding them. NEPSI certification and the underlying IECEx evidence are what their engineering review is built on.

Does it work in Mandarin?

Yes. Android 16 runs the interface in Mandarin or English on the same handset and the user manual will be issued in both.
