Explosion proof mobile phone for Daqing Oilfield

Daqing was discovered in 1959 in the Songliao Basin of Heilongjiang and is operated by PetroChina. This page is for the HSE, production and telecoms teams who need an explosion proof mobile phone in China that works across a mature oilfield, its gathering stations and the winter that comes with it. The IS-04Z1 is designed around intrinsic safety rather than a flameproof enclosure.

The field · Daqing

What is classified at Daqing, and why an explosion proof mobile phone has to fit it

Daqing is not one plant but a field covering a very large area around Daqing city, with a great many wells connected by flowlines to metering rooms, transfer stations and treatment plants. Production is sustained by chemical enhanced recovery. Published research dates polymer flooding at Daqing to the mid-1990s and alkali-surfactant-polymer flooding to pilots in 1994 and large-scale use from 2007, and polymer flooding has kept the field above 10 million tonnes a year for about 14 years. Most recently the Gulong shale oil block inside the field has passed one million tonnes of annual output, according to Xinhua in December 2025. For a handset the result is a field where old gathering infrastructure, injection stations and new shale wellpads sit in the same area, with associated gas at wellheads and separators, chemical handling at injection stations, and PetroChina refining and petrochemical plants nearby in Daqing.

What Daqing does to a handset

Heilongjiang's winter is the first thing every Daqing engineer mentions. Distance, age of plant and constant contractor traffic come next.

Winter lows below minus 30 degrees

Cold spells in Heilongjiang can pass minus 30 degrees Celsius. Cold cuts lithium battery capacity, and the exact operating range will be stated on the certificate once issued, not promised in advance.

A very large area and few fixed landmarks

Pad and station names repeat across a field this size. Four-constellation GNSS puts a coordinate on a fault record, and the 50 MP camera puts a legible photograph next to it.

Slush, ice and meltwater on every round

Winter rounds are done on iced surfaces and through thaw. IP68 sealing keeps meltwater and slush out of the device, which can be wiped and dried rather than kept inside a coat.

Old and new infrastructure in one estate

Original 1960s-era wellsites share the field with later recovery plant and new shale pads. A Zone 1 design target is meant to work across all of them without separate devices.

Contractors on every shift

Workover and construction crews arrive under permits that change daily. Enrolling handsets in an existing MDM console lets the field restrict applications and switch the camera off in sensitive areas.

How work is organised

Rounds, contractors and approvals at Daqing

Daqing is organised around operating units that each own a block of wells and stations, with operators on regular rounds and specialist crews called out for workover, pipeline and electrical faults. Heavy construction and shutdown work tends to cluster in the warmer months. The field is one of PetroChina's most visible assets, so its HSE teams are used to detailed equipment submissions. We have not verified a published supplier-registration route at Daqing and do not name one. Ask the oilfield company's procurement and HSE teams which Ex certificates, test reports and local approvals they accept, and whether a China-registered supplier is required. NEPSI is among the certification routes in progress ahead of the Q4 2026 launch.

Questions · Daqing

Frequently asked questions

How cold can the IS-04Z1 operate at Daqing?

The operating temperature range will be stated on the certificate and datasheet once testing completes. We do not quote a figure before it exists, so ask for the schedule and check it against your coldest site.

Does chemical flooding create its own classified areas?

Injection stations for polymer and surfactant are mostly a chemical-handling matter, but they sit close to classified gathering plant. The site's own area classification decides, and the handset should follow it.

Are the shale wellpads classified differently from the older field?

They are classified by the same rules, but pads with active drilling and testing have larger temporary zones. Confirm the current drawing for the pad rather than assuming the older field's boundaries.

Is the IS-04Z1 certified for Daqing yet?

No. It is pre-launch and certification, including the NEPSI route, is in progress ahead of Q4 2026. Do not take it into a classified area before the certificate exists.
