The sector · Oil and gas

Explosion proof mobile phone for oil and gas sites in China: who operates what

PetroChina is the largest upstream operator. Its Daqing field in Heilongjiang, Changqing in the Ordos Basin, Tarim in Xinjiang and the Southwest field in Sichuan together account for much of China's output. Sinopec produces from Shengli in Shandong, the Tahe field in the Tarim Basin, the sour gas fields around Puguang in Sichuan and the Fuling shale gas field in Chongqing. CNOOC operates offshore, in Bohai Bay and the South China Sea. A fourth name now matters for midstream: PipeChina, the national pipeline company set up in late 2019, which under agreements announced in July 2020 took over long-distance pipelines, storage and some LNG terminals from PetroChina, Sinopec and CNOOC. Older descriptions of the three groups as pipeline operators are out of date. So an equipment approval for a trunk compressor station may come from PipeChina, while a wellpad, a gas plant or a platform is still approved by its producer.

What China's oil and gas geography does to a handset

One national sector spans desert, tundra-cold winters, humid hills and open sea. The handset has to be chosen against the worst of the sites it will visit.

Distance from help

Tarim, Changqing and many Sichuan sites are long drives from a workshop. IP68 sealing and a 4,000 mAh battery reduce the number of times a unit has to come back.

Cold in the northeast and north

Heilongjiang and northern pads see severe winters. The certificate will state the temperature range, and that figure is the one to check, not a general promise.

Sour gas in Sichuan

Hydrogen sulphide is handled with personal and fixed detectors, which the handset does not replace. It still has to meet the area classification on its own.

Offshore transfers

Platform crews travel by helicopter or vessel with rules on carrying lithium batteries. Ask the operator before shipping.

Many contractors, many consoles

A producer, a drilling contractor and a services company may each use a different MDM. The IS-04Z1 is managed from existing consoles, so each can apply its own policy.

How work is organised

Approvals across the oil and gas groups

Each group, and often each oilfield or refinery company within it, runs its own procurement and equipment review. Drilling and well service work is usually contracted and works under the owner's permit-to-work system, and offshore and plant work is packed into planned shutdown windows. Sinopec, PetroChina and CNOOC do not share one approval list. We have not verified a published supplier-registration programme at any of them for handsets and do not name one. The practical route is to ask the owner of the specific site which Ex certificates and test reports it accepts, whether the domestic NEPSI route is required, and whether supply must run through a China-registered partner. NEPSI is among the certification routes in progress ahead of the Q4 2026 launch.

Questions · Oil and gas

Frequently asked questions

Do PetroChina, Sinopec and CNOOC still operate China's pipelines?

Not the long-distance trunk lines. Under 2020 agreements those moved to PipeChina, with the producers keeping stakes. Producers still run field, plant and platform systems.

Is IECEx enough on its own in China?

Generally it supports rather than replaces the domestic route. Operators decide what they accept, so ask each site owner and confirm in writing.

Does one handset cover onshore and offshore?

The design target is Zone 1 gas and Zone 21 dust. An offshore platform adds transfer, salt and network questions that the operator's teams answer separately.

Is the IS-04Z1 certified for oil and gas sites yet?

No. It is pre-launch and certification is in progress ahead of Q4 2026. It must not enter a classified area before the certificate exists.
