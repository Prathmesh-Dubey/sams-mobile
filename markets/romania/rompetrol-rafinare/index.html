Romania · Rompetrol Rafinare

Explosion proof mobile phone for Rompetrol Rafinare in Romania

Rompetrol Rafinare operates two refineries in Romania: Petromidia on the Black Sea coast at Năvodari and Vega at Ploiești, with a petrochemical division on the Petromidia platform. This page is for the people who have to answer what an explosion proof mobile phone in Romania should be allowed to do on a coastal refinery site, in a turnaround and on a loading jetty. The IS-04Z1 is designed around intrinsic safety rather than a flameproof enclosure.

The sites · Rompetrol Rafinare

What a coastal refinery and an inland one classify

Petromidia sits at Năvodari on the Black Sea coast, close to the port of Midia and not far from Constanța. It is a large fuel refinery, and the petrochemical units on the same platform belong to the same site problem. Vega, in Ploiești, is the older of the two and is described in company and press material as the only Romanian producer of bitumen and hexane. Hexane is a solvent, gas group IIB with a low flash point and a wide flammable range, and bitumen means heated tanks and loading. At the coast, hot gas oil and naphtha are handled next to the sea wind, salt and a long list of contractors for the planned stoppages that refineries run on a fixed cycle. A handset at Petromidia may spend the day in a unit with hydrogen, and an hour later on a loading arm.

Platforms and operations

Petromidia, Năvodari
Crude receipt, distillation, conversion units, fuel blending and loading on the Black Sea coast.
Petromidia petrochemicals
Petrochemical units on the same platform, with powders and gases to classify.
Vega, Ploiești
A smaller refinery producing bitumen, hexane and solvents.
Product storage
Tank farms at both sites with floating-roof and fixed-roof tanks.
Loading
Road and rail gantries, and marine loading where the site has berths.
Utilities
Boilers, steam, hydrogen and nitrogen supply, and flare systems.

Hazards a handset meets

Hydrocarbon vapour
Gas groups IIA and IIB at process, pumps and tank farms.
Hydrogen
Hydrotreating and reforming units, gas group IIC.
Solvent vapour
Hexane and similar light products, gas group IIB.
Hot bitumen
Heated tanks and loading areas; surface temperature matters.
Polymer powders
Dust areas where petrochemical polymers are conveyed or stored, subject to the site’s dust testing.
Hydrogen sulphide
Sour streams and sulphur handling, where the site’s own gas detection governs.

What an explosion proof mobile phone is up against at a Romanian refinery

A refinery’s demands on a device are well known to its instrument engineers. These are the ones that matter for Petromidia and Vega.

Salt, wind and spray

Coastal sites subject electronics to salt-laden air and rain. IP68 sealing and rugged build target that, although the certified ambient range will be published only when the certificate fixes it.

A turnaround is a population event

The planned stoppages at Petromidia and Vega bring many contractor crews into the fence at once. Managed enrolment lets the operator issue, restrict and recover handsets as crews arrive and leave, instead of relying on personal phones.

The IIC and T4 marking

The gas marking target is II 2 G Ex ib IIC T4. Hydrogen units set the gas group; the T4 class is what a refinery engineer compares against the hottest surface and the autoignition of the products nearby.

Records at the point of work

A 50 MP camera and GNSS position let an inspector attach a photograph to a line number and a place, so an inspection finding can be acted on without a search for where it was taken.

Camera control

A refinery needs to restrict photography in some areas. Policy can be pushed from the management console, so the camera can be limited rather than the phone being swapped for another model.

Where the IS-04Z1 goes to work at Petromidia and Vega.

Turnaround01

Overhaul punch lists

During an overhaul a supervisor walks a unit and records open items with photographs and tags. A device carried in the classified area removes the walk back to the cabin to type them up.

Logistics02

Marine and road loading

Loading staff at a jetty or gantry need a handset that tolerates spray, carries a loading checklist and reaches the control room by voice or message.

Maintenance03

Instrument and rotating equipment checks

A technician checking a pump or transmitter needs documents, readings and a camera together, rather than a clipboard and a second device left in the cabin.

Questions · Rompetrol Rafinare

Frequently asked questions

Does one handset suit both Petromidia and Vega?

Both are refineries with hydrocarbon and hydrogen risks, so the IIC target covers either. Each area’s own classification still decides where the handset may be taken, and the final certificate governs the use.

What does the operator need from a supplier?

We have no verified knowledge of a published Rompetrol Rafinare supplier programme and will not invent one. Ask the refinery’s procurement and HSE teams for their requirements for equipment documentation and site access.

Is the camera a problem on a refinery site?

It can be managed. The management console can restrict camera use by policy, which is more practical than buying a phone without one.

Is there any relationship with Rompetrol?

No. SAMS has no customer, pilot or supply agreement with Rompetrol Rafinare, and the IS-04Z1 is not yet certified.
