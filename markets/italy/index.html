Regulatory route · Italy

What your HSE and procurement teams will ask for.

The product route is settled and short. D.Lgs. 85/2016 of 19 May 2016 transposed ATEX 2014/34/EU into Italian law and repealed DPR 126/1998, so equipment placed on the market carries a notified body certificate and the CE marking, with no separate Italian approval afterwards. Notification of certification bodies and market surveillance sit with the ministry now called MIMIT. Two practical points follow: instructions have to be supplied in Italian, and Italian buyers working to international specifications very often ask for the IECEx certificate alongside the ATEX one, particularly on sites run by international groups.

The obligations that decide the purchase are in Titolo XI of D.Lgs. 81/2008, headed protection from explosive atmospheres. The datore di lavoro classifies the areas, following the zone definitions in Allegato XLIX and in practice the CEI EN 60079 series with the CEI 31 guides, and records the assessment and the measures in the documento sulla protezione contro le esplosioni, which belongs with the DVR rather than beside it. Above that, a major hazard establishment falls under D.Lgs. 105/2015, the Italian Seveso III decree, where the national inspection plan for upper-tier sites is prepared centrally with ISPRA and inspections are scheduled by the regional technical committee, the CTR, working with the fire service. INAIL sits behind the whole system as insurer, verifier and the source of much of the technical guidance Italian safety professionals work from.

Ask about Italy →
Is the IS-04Z1 already ATEX certified for Italy?

No. It is designed to ATEX 2014/34/EU for Zone 1 and Zone 21 and the certification programme is in progress. Launch is planned for Q4 2026, and no unit is supplied for use in a classified area before the certificate covering that use exists.

Does Italy add a national approval on top of ATEX?

No. D.Lgs. 85/2016 transposed the directive and repealed DPR 126/1998, so a notified body certificate and the CE marking are the product-side requirement, with notification and market surveillance under the ministry now called MIMIT. Italian instructions and often an IECEx certificate are what buyers add.

How much does an explosion proof mobile phone cost in Italy?

There is no list price before launch, and a firm figure for a device still in certification is a reason to ask harder questions. Pre-launch pricing is quoted against a stated quantity, configuration and support scope, which is also the form a tender needs. Evaluation units are issued under a trial agreement.

People search for an ex proof phone. Is that the same thing?

Usually yes, loosely. Ex proof, exproof and explosion proof are all used for a phone meant for a classified area, but the protection concepts differ: Ex d contains an explosion in a heavy enclosure, while Ex ib limits the energy in the circuit so ignition cannot start. The IS-04Z1 takes the Ex ib route.

Will the interface and the manual be in Italian?

Yes. Android 16 carries an Italian interface, and the manuale d'uso, the safe-use instructions and the declaration of conformity are produced in Italian alongside English, because Italian sites treat documentation in Italian as part of lawful supply.

Can we reference it in our explosion protection document already?

You can draft the entry. The datasheet gives the target marking string, the temperature class and the equipment protection level, so the selection reasoning can be prepared and reviewed. It should not be closed as deployed equipment until the certificate number exists.

How will service work at our sites in Sicily or Sardinia?

Through a partner with real reach, which is why an island service route is a selection criterion for us rather than an afterthought. Repair and battery replacement on an intrinsically safe device have to go through a recognised facility, and exchange time is worth testing during the evaluation.
