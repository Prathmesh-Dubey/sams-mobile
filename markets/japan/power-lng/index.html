Japan · Power & LNG

Explosion proof mobile phone for power and LNG in Japan

Japan runs one of the world's largest LNG import chains, and an explosion proof mobile phone for power and LNG in Japan serves coastal terminals and the gas-fired power stations beside them. This page is for the HSE, maintenance and telecoms teams who decide what may be carried into a terminal or generating station. The IS-04Z1 is designed around intrinsic safety rather than a flameproof enclosure.

Why it fits power and LNG

Built for the world's largest LNG import chain

Japan's power sector depends on LNG imports at a scale unmatched anywhere else, running an extensive network of coastal receiving terminals feeding gas-fired generation.

Zone 1 gas at receiving terminals and generation fuel systems

LNG unloading, storage, regasification and generation fuel-gas systems are classified Zone 1 gas atmospheres, and Ex ib IIC T4 covers the hydrocarbon gas groups present across that chain.

Cryogenic exposure at unloading and regasification

LNG unloading arms and regasification equipment introduce cold-temperature exposure alongside the gas hazard, and the device's environmental testing is specified with that combination in mind rather than a purely hot or temperate-climate assumption.

A dense network of coastal terminals, not one facility

Japan's LNG import infrastructure spans many terminals along the coastline, a distributed footprint where a single device standard simplifies procurement compared with evaluating equipment terminal by terminal.

Seismic and coastal weather resilience

Japan's frequent seismic activity and exposure to typhoons place a premium on equipment ruggedness beyond what a more geologically stable region's power sector needs to consider.

Fleet management across a distributed LNG-to-power chain

EMM and MDM enrolment supports an operator managing devices across multiple coastal terminals and generation sites from one console.

The sites

Explosion proof mobile phone use across Japan's LNG terminals and power stations

Japan imports LNG at terminals around its coast, many sited beside the power stations they feed. JERA's Futtsu site in Chiba, for instance, combines a large thermal power station with an LNG terminal, and JERA also operates Hekinan and Chita in Aichi. JAPEX's Soma terminal in Fukushima Prefecture began operating in 2017. Hazards include natural gas around unloading arms, tank roofs, vaporisers and fuel-gas trains, hydrogen around generator cooling and, at some stations, ammonia or coal handling. Check the classification drawing at each site; it governs what a device may do and where.

Use cases

How work is organised at terminals and stations

Cargo01

Ship discharge

Shore and ship staff coordinate transfer over many hours, with checks at the arms and on the tanks. A handset designed for the jetty area keeps photographs, logs and calls on one managed device.

Outage02

Unit overhaul

Planned outages bring large contractor numbers into turbine halls and fuel-gas areas. Enrolled handsets carry the permit and reporting apps and can be cleared when the outage ends.

Rounds03

Plant walkdowns

Operators record gauge readings on fixed routes through the plant. The handset adds a photograph and a GNSS position to each entry in place of a paper sheet.

Frequently asked questions

Is the IS-04Z1 certified for use at LNG terminals in Japan yet?

No. The device is pre-launch and certification, including IECEx and JIS-referenced marking, is still in progress ahead of the Q4 2026 launch.

Does the device need special certification for cryogenic exposure?

Ex certification covers the gas-hazard classification of the area; separate environmental testing addresses cold-exposure durability, and the certificate will state its exact scope once issued.

Does the device suit Japan's seismic activity?

Ruggedness testing accounts for shock and vibration exposure broadly consistent with seismic and severe-weather conditions, though specific site engineering requirements should always be confirmed.

Does one device cover both LNG terminals and gas-fired power plants?

Yes, the design intent is a single Zone 1 specification covering both the coastal LNG receiving infrastructure and the generation fuel-gas systems it feeds.

Is Japan the world's largest LNG importer?

Japan is one of the largest importers, though China has overtaken it in recent years. The terminals and stations covered here remain significant, and the hazards are the same whatever the ranking.

Which operators run LNG terminals in Japan?

JERA and JAPEX are named on this site, and other utilities and gas companies run their own terminals. Ask each operator what it expects.

Does cold matter to a handset at a terminal?

Surfaces near cryogenic equipment can be very cold, and the handset is not intended to touch them. Check the ambient temperature range on the certificate against the site's conditions.

Is hydrogen present at power stations?

Many stations use hydrogen to cool generators, a Group IIC gas. The IS-04Z1 marking target is Ex ib IIC T4, subject to the certificate once issued.
