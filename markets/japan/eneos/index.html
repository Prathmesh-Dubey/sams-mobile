Japan · ENEOS

Explosion proof mobile phone for ENEOS sites

Searching for an explosion proof mobile phone for ENEOS sites in Japan usually means a refinery where one shift crosses several classified boundaries before lunch. ENEOS is Japan's largest refiner, built from a series of mergers that include the 2017 combination of JX Nippon Oil & Energy and TonenGeneral, so its refineries carry the engineering habits of more than one predecessor. This page is for the HSE, instrumentation and telecoms people who decide what may be carried past the gate. The IS-04Z1 is designed around intrinsic safety rather than a flameproof enclosure.

Why it fits

Designed for a merger-consolidated national refiner

ENEOS's scale is the product of consolidation rather than organic single-company growth, meaning equipment standards and vendor relationships can still vary by site depending on which predecessor company originally built or operated it.

Legacy engineering conventions from multiple predecessor companies

Sites that were formerly part of JX Nippon Oil & Energy, TonenGeneral or other merged companies may retain some of their own historical equipment and vendor-approval conventions, even as ENEOS works to standardise practices across the combined group.

Japan's largest refining and marketing network

As the dominant national refiner, ENEOS's device fleet decisions touch a larger number of sites and a larger workforce than any other single Japanese refiner, a scale where EMM and MDM fleet management delivers the most value.

Zone 1 gas across a nationwide, coastal refinery network

ENEOS refineries span multiple coastal locations, each with Zone 1 classified process units and marine-terminal exposure, and the device's specification targets both consistently.

A company still integrating standards post-merger

Large-scale mergers take years to fully harmonise engineering and procurement standards, meaning ENEOS's own internal standardisation process is itself relevant context for how new equipment gets evaluated at a given site.

Fleet management across a large, multi-site network

EMM and MDM enrolment supports ENEOS managing a consistent device profile across a refining network shaped by its consolidation history.

The sites

Where an explosion proof mobile phone is carried across ENEOS refineries

ENEOS refineries include Negishi in Yokohama, Kawasaki, Chiba at Ichihara, Mizushima in Okayama, Kashima in Ibaraki, Sakai, Sendai, Marifu in Yamaguchi and Oita. Most sit on coastal land beside a tanker berth, so the classified area a shift walks through runs from the process unit to the jetty. Process units handle light hydrocarbon gas and vapour, hydrogen at hydrotreaters and sulphur-removal units, and hydrogen sulphide wherever sour feed is processed, which is why the gas group matters as much as the zone. Tank farms and loading gantries add large, open Zone 1 and Zone 2 vapour areas. The lists below describe typical areas, not a survey of any one plant; the classification drawing for the unit in question always governs.

Use cases

How ENEOS shifts are organised around the equipment

Operations01

Outside operator rounds

A round covers a unit, its pumps and a tank-farm walk, and readings are traditionally written on a sheet. A handset that carries the checklist, the photograph of a weeping flange and the call to the control room replaces the sheet, the radio and the camera that would otherwise be signed out.

Turnaround02

Periodic shutdown campaigns

Refineries stop units on a planned cycle, and a turnaround brings hundreds of contractor staff into the plant at once. Each crew needs a controlled way to carry permits and photographs, and an enrolled handset lets the operator set one policy for all of them.

Marine03

Jetty and tanker operations

Berthing, hose connection and loading put refinery staff, ship crew and surveyors close together. A handset designed for the jetty area and sealed to IP68 against spray removes the argument over which phone may be used at the manifold.

Questions · ENEOS

Frequently asked questions

Is the IS-04Z1 certified for use at ENEOS sites yet?

No. The device is pre-launch, and certification is still in progress ahead of the Q4 2026 launch.

Does ENEOS run one unified vendor-approval process?

Not entirely; sites that were part of different predecessor companies before consolidation can still reflect some of their own legacy conventions.

Does approval at one ENEOS refinery carry to others?

Not automatically, though ENEOS's ongoing standardisation efforts post-merger are gradually aligning practices across its network.

Is ENEOS the same as Idemitsu Kosan?

No, ENEOS and Idemitsu Kosan are separate, competing national refiners with their own procurement processes.

Which ENEOS locations would an explosion proof phone be used at?

That depends on the area classification at each refinery, terminal or plant. Process units, tank farms and jetties are the usual candidates, and the site's classification drawing and permit-to-work rules decide the rest. Check them with the site engineer before specifying a device.

What would ENEOS procurement and HSE teams ask for?

Ask the relevant ENEOS procurement and HSE teams for the supplier registration route and the hazardous-area equipment requirements. Expect questions about the certificate, the marking, the documentation and the service arrangements. SAMS has not verified a specific ENEOS programme and does not claim one.

Will a refinery turnaround change how handsets are issued?

Usually it increases the number of people needing one for a few weeks. Handsets managed from an MDM console can be enrolled, issued and wiped by the operator without a visit to a telecoms workshop.

Do ENEOS refineries involve hydrogen?

Hydrotreating and reforming units use hydrogen-rich streams, which fall in Group IIC. The IS-04Z1 marking target is Ex ib IIC T4, so the gas group is covered in the design intent, subject to the certificate once issued.
