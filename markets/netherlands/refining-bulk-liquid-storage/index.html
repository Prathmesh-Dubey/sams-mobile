The sector · Rotterdam and Zeeland

Where refining and bulk liquid storage is classified

Refining and bulk liquid storage cluster along the Nieuwe Maas and the Nieuwe Waterweg. Shell runs the Pernis complex, bp has Europoort, and the Zeeland Refinery is near Vlissingen. Beside them sit independent terminals including Vopak, which has storage at the Botlek, Europoort and Vlaardingen, and operators such as Odfjell at the Botlek. Our pages on the explosion proof phone for bp Raffinaderij Rotterdam and Shell Nederland cover the two largest operators. The sector is mostly gas and vapour, with dust at the edges, so Zone 1 and Zone 2 dominate and the question for a handset is gas group and temperature class.

Open water and weather

Jetties and tank roofs are exposed to wind, salt and rain. IP68 sealing and rugged build are the first questions a reviewer asks.

A lone worker on a bund

Terminal staff often work alone, at night, on a transfer. Four-constellation GNSS, a data connection and a check-in function on the handset support a lone-working procedure.

Ship schedules do not wait

A transfer follows the vessel, not the shift. The handset needs to carry voice, camera and position, so that the person at the manifold can report without leaving it.

Gas group

Light hydrocarbons are IIA or IIB, and hydrogen is IIC. A marking target of IIC covers the lower groups, which is why the programme is aimed there.

Questions · Refining and Bulk Liquid Storage

Frequently asked questions

What gas group does a terminal need?

Most light hydrocarbons fall in IIA or IIB, but a site that also handles hydrogen needs IIC. The area classification decides, so ask for the zoning.

Can the same phone serve a refinery and a terminal?

The design target is the same, Zone 1 and Zone 21. Whether it fits a given site depends on the certificate and the site’s zoning.

How are visiting contractors handled?

That is for each operator. A device that can be enrolled and wiped is simpler to admit than a personal phone.
