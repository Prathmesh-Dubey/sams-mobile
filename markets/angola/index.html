Where it is designed to work in Angola

Most Angolan production comes from deepwater blocks worked by FPSOs far offshore, supported from Luanda, Soyo and Cabinda. Onshore, the classified areas are the LNG plant at Soyo, the Malongo terminal in Cabinda and the refining and storage around Luanda.

Operators and contractors such as Sonangol, ANPG and TotalEnergies need an explosion proof mobile phone they can carry into classified areas at Luanda, Soyo, Zaire province and Cabinda and Malongo. The IS-04Z1 is designed for Zone 1 and Zone 21 duty there.

Built for local conditions

Built for Angolan operating conditions

In Angola the work is mostly offshore: salt air, humidity that never lets up, crew rotations through Luanda and Soyo, and handsets that have to survive a helideck and a boat transfer.

For evaluators in Angola, the IS-04Z1 explosion proof mobile phone is designed around these points: Heat with constant humidity; Gas group for FPSO and LNG atmospheres; Spray, decks and transfers; A full shift away from a charger; Coverage far offshore; Control over a rotating fleet.

Heat with constant humidity

Year round warmth and humidity, with salt air offshore. IP68 sealing keeps water and particulate out, and the certificate will state the ambient range the device is approved for.

Gas group for FPSO and LNG atmospheres

Hydrocarbon service is typically Group IIA and IIB. A marking target of Ex ib IIC covers those, so one specification runs from an FPSO process deck to the Soyo LNG jetty.

Spray, decks and transfers

Kit gets carried across helidecks, wet decks and boat transfers and knocked against steel. The handset has a rugged build, and IP68 covers immersion rather than splash.

A full shift away from a charger

Process decks and manifolds offer no safe area socket mid shift. The 4,000 mAh battery is sized for a working day with camera, torch and GNSS in use.

Coverage far offshore

Deepwater assets rely on vessel networks rather than carrier signal. The device falls back to installation Wi-Fi, with Bluetooth and NFC for local tools and tags.

Control over a rotating fleet

Handsets pass between rotations, contractors and vessels. Android 16 with EMM and MDM support gives enrolment, remote lock and remote wipe, with Portuguese language profiles.

Regulatory route · Angola

What your HSE and procurement teams will ask for.

The certificate HSE teams on Angolan assets ask for is the IECEx Certificate of Conformity, and the ATEX certificate where a specification names the European directive. The upstream concessionaire, ANPG, oversees operations on the blocks, but the requirement that reaches the deck is the operator equipment specification.

Two Angolan steps sit alongside it. Radio equipment needs type approval from INACOM, the communications regulator, before it is used on local networks. The petroleum sector's local content rules favour goods and services supplied through Angolan companies, so supply runs through a locally registered partner, and documentation in Portuguese is expected.

Is the IS-04Z1 approved for use in Angola yet?

No. The device is pre-launch and the IECEx certification programme is in progress, as is every other scheme. Nothing should be planned into a classified area until the Certificate of Conformity exists.

Which certificate will an Angolan operator ask to see?

The IECEx Certificate of Conformity in almost every case, because the operators here apply the standards they use globally. An ATEX certificate is issued in parallel where a specification names the European directive.

Does the phone need INACOM approval?

Yes. It carries 4G LTE, Wi-Fi, Bluetooth and NFC radios, so INACOM type approval is required and forms part of the launch programme alongside the Ex certification.

Can it be used on an FPSO?

It is designed for Zone 1 and Zone 21, which covers the classified areas on an Angolan FPSO, with a marking target of Ex ib IIC T4. Operator management of change still applies.

Will buying it satisfy local content requirements?

Supply is structured to work inside the petroleum sector's local content rules, through a locally registered Angolan partner holding operator vendor registrations.

Is documentation available in Portuguese?

Yes. The user manual and safe use instructions will be supplied in Portuguese and English, and MDM profiles can be configured in Portuguese.

When will units be available, and who repairs them?

Launch is Q4 2026. Warranty and service run through the Angolan partner SAMS is appointing, and repair of certified Ex equipment must be done by a recognised facility.

What is the price of an explosion proof phone in Angola?

No list price is published before the certificates exist. Pricing is quoted in writing against quantity and configuration through the Angolan partner, and landed cost depends on import and approval steps, so it is set with the channel rather than published.
