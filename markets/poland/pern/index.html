Explosion proof mobile phone for PERN in Poland

PERN moves crude oil and fuel across Poland: pipelines from Płock to Gdańsk and from the east, crude tank farms near Płock, at Adamowo and at Gdańsk, and a chain of fuel bases from Koluszki to Boronów. This page is for PERN’s HSE and operations teams, and the contractors working on its terminals, who want an explosion proof mobile phone in Poland suited to tank farm work. The IS-04Z1 is designed for Zone 1 and Zone 21; certification is in progress ahead of Q4 2026. The IS-04Z1 is designed around intrinsic safety rather than a flameproof enclosure.

The network · PERN

Explosion proof mobile phone for PERN: tank farms, pump stations and fuel bases

A tank farm is a place where vapour is released slowly and constantly, which is why its classification is drawn around rim seals, vents, pumps and manifolds rather than around buildings. PERN’s crude bases are at Miszewko Strzałkowskie near Płock, at Adamowo and at the Gdańsk Oil Terminal; PERN describes close to three million cubic metres of crude storage across the three. Product moves on from Płock through the Płock–Koluszki–Boronów line to fuel bases that include Koluszki, Boronów, Rejowiec, Emilianów, Dębogórze and Małaszewicze. At Gdańsk the tank farm works with the marine terminal operated by Naftoport. Refineries such as the Orlen refineries at Płock and Gdańsk are both PERN’s customers and its neighbours.

Where the zones fall

Floating roof tanks
Vapour at the rim seal and above the roof; the outside of a tank shell is not the same as the inside.
Pump halls and manifolds
Seals and flanges that weep slightly, which is why classification covers them.
Valve pits and pig traps
Launching and receiving pigs opens the line and creates a temporary release.
Loading racks
Vapour displaced from road tankers and rail wagons during filling, gas group IIA with gasoline.
Bunds and drainage
Low points where heavier vapour collects.
Control buildings and laboratories
Outside the zones; the sample and dispatch records are made here.

What a tank farm does to a handset

A tank farm is open ground with a lot of steel on it. The questions are how far people walk, how cold it gets and what the handset may not be taken into.

Not inside the tank

The interior of a crude or fuel tank is Zone 0. The IS-04Z1 is designed for Zone 1 and Zone 21, so it does not go inside a tank, and tank entry stays on the operator’s own procedures.

Rim seals and gauge hatches

Reading a level or taking a sample at the roof is a Zone 1 activity. A handset that can photograph the gauge and the sample label at that point removes a trip back to the office.

A pipeline route that is mostly fields

Valve stations are far from the next building. Four-constellation GNSS gives the line walker a coordinate, and IP68 keeps rain and mud from stopping the handset.

Batches that change the product

Fuel bases receive products in sequence and sample at each change. Dated photographs and readings entered at the point of sampling are harder to dispute later.

Contractors on inspection campaigns

Tank inspection and maintenance bring specialist contractors in for weeks. Enrol the handset when they arrive and wipe it when they go, using your EMM or MDM.

Questions · PERN

Frequently asked questions

Can the handset be used on a floating roof?

The rim seal area is normally treated as a Zone 1 or Zone 2 location, but it is the operator’s classification that decides. The device is designed for Zone 1; the inside of a tank is Zone 0 and outside its design target.

Does crude oil put the temperature class at risk?

The marking target is T4, and many crude and product vapours have ignition temperatures well above that, but check the lowest auto-ignition temperature of anything stored at the site against the marking on the certificate.

How would PERN assess a handset for a terminal?

That is PERN’s procurement and HSE process. Ask for its technical requirements for portable electronic equipment, and whether contractors’ equipment must be registered. SAMS has no relationship with PERN, and this page does not imply one.
