Where it works in Poland

Polish hazardous area work sits in three belts. Refining and petrochemicals concentrate at Płock and on the coast at Gdańsk. Fertilisers, chlorine and polymers run from Włocławek through Puławy and Kędzierzyn-Koźle to Police, where the Polimery Police complex added a large new propylene and polypropylene site. Gas infrastructure has grown fastest, around the Świnoujście import terminal, the Baltic Pipe route and the compressor stations that feed them. Underground coal mining is a fourth world with its own equipment group, and it is described honestly below rather than counted in.

Operators and contractors such as Orlen, Orlen Południe and Anwil need an explosion proof mobile phone they can carry into classified areas at Płock, Gdańsk and Włocławek. The IS-04Z1 is designed for Zone 1 and Zone 21 duty there.

Gas and dust under one programme

The gas marking target is II 2 G Ex ib IIC T4, and Zone 21 dust protection belongs to the same certification programme. On a fertiliser site that means the prilling tower, the bagging hall and the ammonia area can be covered by one handset rather than two.

Group II, and we say so

This is surface equipment. Underground coal mining is Group I, categories M1 and M2, a separate certificate for a methane and coal dust environment, and the IS-04Z1 is not being certified for it. Above ground at a mine, in workshops, on ventilation plant and at surface installations, Group II is what applies.

Cold, wet rounds

IP68 sealing and rugged build, which covers low temperature and humidity, are aimed at a January round on a tank farm or an LNG jetty rather than at office use. The ambient range the certificate fixes is published with the datasheet instead of promised in advance.

A whole shift without leaving the zone

There is no charging point inside a classified area, and the walk back to one costs a plant more than the battery does. The 4,000 mAh cell is sized for a full shift with the camera and a work order application in use.

Polish on the screen and in the file

Android 16 carries a Polish interface, and the manual, the safe-use instructions and the declaration of conformity are produced in Polish alongside English. Polish buyers treat that as a condition of acceptance rather than a nicety.

Contractors in and out

4G LTE, Wi-Fi and NFC let one handset move between the plant network, site Wi-Fi and access control, and Android 16 with EMM and MDM lets it be enrolled, restricted, wiped and reissued as crews rotate through a turnaround.

Regulatory route · Poland

What your HSE and procurement teams will ask for.

On the product side Poland is short and unambiguous. ATEX 2014/34/EU is transposed by the Rozporządzenie Ministra Rozwoju of 6 June 2016 on requirements for equipment and protective systems intended for use in a potentially explosive atmosphere, published as Dz.U. 2016 poz. 817. A notified body certificate, the EU declaration of conformity and CE marking are the basis for placing the device on the Polish market, and there is no additional national Ex approval afterwards. Market surveillance sits with UOKiK through the Inspekcja Handlowa. The radio side runs under RED 2014/53/EU with UKE as the electronic communications authority. Plant teams will also know UDT from pressure and lifting inspections, but a handheld device does not pass through that route, and it is worth saying so early in an internal review so nobody waits for a UDT decision that will never be required.

The duty side is where a Polish evaluation actually happens. The Rozporządzenie Ministra Gospodarki of 8 July 2010 sets the minimum occupational health and safety requirements for workplaces where an explosive atmosphere may occur, and it obliges the employer to produce and maintain the dokument zabezpieczenia przed wybuchem. That document records the zone classification, the risk assessment and the equipment selection reasoning, and PIP, the State Labour Inspectorate, can ask to see it. Underground mining sits outside all of this: it is governed by the geological and mining law and supervised by WUG, the State Mining Authority, and equipment taken below ground has to be Group I, category M1 or M2, certified for methane and coal dust. That is a different certificate from the one this device is pursuing, and no amount of Group II documentation substitutes for it.

Is the IS-04Z1 already ATEX certified for use in Poland?

No. It is designed to ATEX 2014/34/EU for Zone 1 and Zone 21 and the certification programme is in progress. Launch is planned for Q4 2026, and nothing is supplied for use in a classified area before the certificate covering it exists. Any supplier describing an uncertified handset differently is worth a second look.

Does Poland require a national approval on top of ATEX?

No. ATEX 2014/34/EU is transposed by the Rozporządzenie of 6 June 2016, Dz.U. 2016 poz. 817, and a notified body certificate with the EU declaration of conformity and CE marking is the complete product-side requirement. The obligations that follow belong to the employer, under the 2010 workplace regulation that PIP enforces.

Can it be taken into an underground coal mine?

No, and this is the one answer we will not soften. Underground mines need Group I equipment in category M1 or M2, certified for a methane and coal dust environment and supervised by WUG under the mining law. The IS-04Z1 targets Group II surface equipment. It suits mine surface installations, workshops and ventilation plant, not the workings below.

What is the price of an explosion proof mobile phone in Poland?

There is no list price before launch. Pre-launch pricing is quoted against a stated quantity, configuration and support scope, in the currency your contract uses, and it is issued in writing so a tender team can cite it. Evaluation units are issued under a trial agreement rather than sold.

Will there be Polish documentation and a Polish interface?

Yes. Android 16 carries a Polish interface, and the manual, the safe-use instructions and the declaration of conformity are produced in Polish alongside English. The marking string, gas group and temperature class are supplied in a form your explosion protection document can quote directly.

What about radio approval and UKE?

The radio side runs under RED 2014/53/EU, which applies in Poland as it does across the union, with UKE as the national electronic communications authority. There is no separate Polish type approval for a CE marked handset, so the radio evidence sits in the same declaration of conformity as the rest.

How are repair and battery replacement handled?

Through the Polish partner SAMS is appointing or a facility recognised for the purpose. Ex equipment cannot be opened on site or repaired by a general phone workshop, because doing so voids the protection concept the certificate rests on. The repair and battery route is written down as part of the launch rather than improvised afterwards.
