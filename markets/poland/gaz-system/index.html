Explosion proof mobile phone for Gaz-System in Poland

Gaz-System operates Poland’s gas transmission grid, including the compressor stations that carry Norwegian gas after Baltic Pipe, and the Świnoujście LNG terminal on the Baltic coast. This page is for the operator’s HSE, automation and maintenance teams, and the contractors on its stations, who are looking for an explosion proof mobile phone in Poland rated for Zone 1. The IS-04Z1 is designed for that duty; certification is in progress ahead of Q4 2026. The IS-04Z1 is designed around intrinsic safety rather than a flameproof enclosure.

The grid · Gaz-System

Explosion proof mobile phone for Gaz-System: terminal, compressor stations and the grid

Gas transmission is a thin, long business: a few large nodes and a great many small unmanned ones. At the large end the Świnoujście LNG terminal has been in operation since 2016; after a second quay and a third process storage tank were added, Gaz-System describes its regasification capacity as up to 8.3 billion cubic metres a year. Baltic Pipe widened the grid’s capacity, and the compressor stations at Odolanów and Goleniów were expanded while Gustorzyn was built new. At the small end are pressure reduction and metering stations and valve nodes that a technician often visits alone. Natural gas is methane, gas group IIA, but a compressor hall or a vent stack still defines a zone, and a leak check is the moment you most want a camera and a position on the same device.

Where the zones fall

LNG tanks and jetty
Cold methane vapour around tank roofs, loading arms and transfer lines; classification radiates outwards from each source.
Regasification units
Vaporisers and send-out pipework carrying high-pressure natural gas.
Compressor halls
Gas-containing machinery in buildings where the ventilation rating sets the zone.
Vent stacks and blowdown
Venting for maintenance creates temporary zones that operate under permit.
Filter and metering areas
Small enclosures where leak checking is a routine task.
Control and workshop buildings
Outside the zones; where devices are issued and charged.

A grid operator’s people spread thin across a lot of ground, and the handset is often the only link between a technician and the control room.

Alone at an unmanned station

Lone working is routine at small stations. Four-constellation GNSS puts a position on a record, and Android Enterprise lets the device be managed from the same console as the rest of the operator’s fleet.

Wind off the Baltic

The LNG terminal is exposed coast. IP68 sealing and rugged build are aimed at wet, cold and salt-laden outdoor work, with the ambient range to be fixed by the certificate.

Methane is an easier case than hydrogen

Methane sits in gas group IIA, so a handset targeting II 2 G Ex ib IIC T4 has margin. If hydrogen-enriched gas is ever handled, IIC is the group that matters, and the area classification should say so.

Records that follow the asset

A photograph and a coordinate attached to an inspection record are worth more at a valve node than a free-text description. The 50 MP camera is for that.

Contractors on a station outage

Compressor outages bring maintenance contractors onto a small site for a short period. Enrolment, restriction and wiping through EMM and MDM fits that rotation.

Questions · Gaz-System

Frequently asked questions

Is natural gas an easy case for an intrinsically safe phone?

Gas group IIA is the least demanding of the three, but zone type still matters. A compressor hall may be Zone 2 where a vent stack is Zone 1, and the handset must be marked for the more severe of the two it will visit. Always check the operator’s classification drawing.

How does a handset cope with weak coverage along the grid?

The IS-04Z1 has 4G LTE and Wi-Fi. Whether records queue and sync later depends on the application your teams use; ask your application vendor for its offline behaviour.

Who approves a handset on Gaz-System sites?

The operator, through its own procurement and HSE rules. Ask what it requires of portable electronic devices on stations and at the terminal. SAMS has no relationship with Gaz-System, and this page is not an endorsement by it.
