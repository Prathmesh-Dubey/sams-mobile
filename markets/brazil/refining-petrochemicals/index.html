Explosion proof mobile phone for refining and petrochemicals in Brazil

An explosion proof mobile phone for refining and petrochemicals in Brazil has to suit a very different set of owners. Petrobras operates most of the country's refining capacity, Acelen runs the Mataripe refinery in Bahia independently, and Braskem runs the Camaçari and Triunfo petrochemical complexes. This page is for the HSE, turnaround and telecoms teams who plan what is carried into those units, and how it is controlled when contractors are on site. The IS-04Z1 is designed around intrinsic safety rather than a flameproof enclosure.

The sites · Refining and petrochemicals

Explosion proof mobile phone use in Brazilian refineries and petrochemical plants

Refineries in Brazil sit mostly near the coast and the big cities: Paulínia and Cubatão in São Paulo state, Duque de Caxias in Rio de Janeiro, Ipojuca in Pernambuco, and Mataripe in Bahia. Petrochemical complexes grew beside them. The pages on explosion proof phone for Petrobras and explosion proof phone for Braskem go into each company; this page covers what they share. Every unit has a fixed classified area, a permit system and a planned shutdown, the parada, in which contractors outnumber operators.

What a parada does to a handset

A shutdown is the busiest and riskiest period of a refinery's year. It is also the period in which handsets are most likely to be issued in bulk.

IP ratingIP68
Battery4,000 mAh

Hundreds of contractors, weeks at a time

A major shutdown fills each unit with contracted crews. Android Enterprise enrolment makes it possible to issue a handset to a crew, restrict what runs on it and wipe it at demobilisation.

Hydrogen means IIC

Hydrotreaters and crackers have hydrogen in the process, and hydrogen is the most demanding gas group. The marking target for the IS-04Z1 is II 2 G Ex ib IIC T4, subject to the certificate.

Different owners, different reviews

A refinery owned by Petrobras, one owned by Acelen and a petrochemical complex owned by Braskem each run their own engineering review. Plan an evidence pack that each can read.

Heat, salt and sun

Coastal plants combine warm air, salt, and strong sun on pipework. IP68 sealing and the rugged build cover the physical side; the temperature class is checked against the area drawing.

Permits and isolation records

A shutdown runs on permits, isolations and lock-out records. A photograph with a time and a location, taken in the unit, is better evidence than a description written afterwards.

Where the IS-04Z1 goes to work in refineries.

Shutdown01

Parada geral at REPLAN, REDUC or RNEST

A supervisor inside a unit confirms an isolation, checks a permit and reaches the planning room. A handset for the classified area saves the walk to the gate.

Operations02

Operator rounds in a cracker

A round passes furnaces, compressors and tanks. Readings, photographs and a call to the control room come from the same device.

Inspection03

Corrosion and leak surveys

An inspector walks pipework recording defects by position. Four-constellation GNSS gives a coordinate, the 50 MP camera records the defect and the log goes to the integrity team.

Questions · Refining and petrochemicals

Frequently asked questions

Is the IS-04Z1 certified for refining use in Brazil yet?

No. The device is pre-launch, and IECEx, ATEX and the Brazilian INMETRO certification are in progress ahead of the Q4 2026 launch.

Do Petrobras and Acelen have the same vendor process?

No. Acelen is a separate, independently operated company, and each runs its own procurement and engineering review. Ask each directly.

Is hydrogen a problem for a handset?

It is the reason to specify IIC. Hydrogen has a very low ignition energy, so the gas group on the area drawing decides whether a device is suitable.

When is the best time to evaluate a device at a refinery?

Between shutdowns, when safety staff have time. A trial during a parada is possible but needs agreement well before the stop.
