Brazil · Mining, terminals & biofuels

Explosion proof mobile phone for mining, terminals and biofuels in Brazil

An explosion proof mobile phone for mining, terminals and biofuels in Brazil covers three industries with very different hazards. Vale's mines and ports, bulk liquid terminals such as Ultracargo's, and the sugar and ethanol mills run by companies such as Raízen are inland and coastal bulk-materials sites where dust and vapour hazards sit side by side. This page is for the HSE and operations teams who set what a person may carry into those areas. The IS-04Z1 is designed around intrinsic safety rather than a flameproof enclosure.

The sites · Mining, terminals and biofuels

Explosion proof mobile phone use in Brazilian mining, tank terminals and mills

These three sectors share a feature that oil and gas sites do not: the main hazard is often not gas. Mining and port handling carry fuel and coal dust but, for iron ore itself, little combustible dust. Tank terminals handle flammable liquids, and their classified areas are tank roofs, pump bays and loading arms. Sugar and ethanol mills combine combustible dust with ethanol vapour. For the largest operators there are separate pages on explosion proof phone for Vale and explosion proof phone for Raízen.

Dust and washdown

Mills and terminals are cleaned with water and compressed air. IP68 sealing and the rugged build are meant to survive both, but washing routines should be agreed with the site.

Gas and dust in the same site

An ethanol mill has Zone 1 gas in the distillery and Zone 21 dust in the sugar plant. The IS-04Z1 design target covers both, so one device can be issued for the whole round.

Seasonal peaks in the safra

Sugar and ethanol mills run hardest during the harvest and are overhauled between harvests. Enrolment in an MDM console makes seasonal issue and recovery manageable.

Long distances and few specialists

Mines, ports and mills can be far from head office. Four-constellation GNSS records the position of a defect, and remote management through Android Enterprise avoids a site visit for updates.

Portuguese records

Training and inspection records under NR-20, NR-10 and, for mining, NR-22 are expected in the working language. The interface runs in Brazilian Portuguese.

Questions · Mining, terminals and biofuels

Frequently asked questions

Is the IS-04Z1 certified for use in these industries yet?

No. The device is pre-launch, and IECEx, ATEX and the Brazilian INMETRO certification are in progress ahead of the Q4 2026 launch.

Does one device cover iron ore dust and ethanol vapour?

The design target is Zone 1 gas and Zone 21 dust. Iron ore itself is not usually a combustible dust, so the classified areas at a mine are typically fuel, coal and process plant. Each site decides its own classification.

What changes between a tank terminal and a mill?

A terminal is mainly vapour, with its main risk at tank vents and loading arms. A mill adds dust and fibre. The evidence an operator asks for follows from that.

Is Ultracargo covered by this page?

Ultracargo is named here as an operator of tank farms. Any evaluation would be a direct conversation with its own HSE and procurement teams.
