Brazil · Raízen

Explosion proof mobile phone for Raízen sugar-ethanol mills

An explosion proof mobile phone for Raízen in Brazil has to cope with two hazards at once: ethanol vapour in the distillery and combustible dust from sugar and bagasse. Raízen is a joint venture of Shell and Cosan and one of the largest sugarcane processors in the world, with its mills concentrated in São Paulo state and Mato Grosso do Sul. This page is for the safety, industrial and IT teams who decide what a person may carry across a mill floor. The IS-04Z1 is designed around intrinsic safety rather than a flameproof enclosure.

The sites · Raízen

Explosion proof mobile phone use at Raízen's sugar and ethanol mills in Brazil

A sugar and ethanol mill is a small industrial town. Cane arrives by truck, is washed and crushed, and the juice goes to sugar boilers or to fermentation and distillation. The fibre left over, bagasse, fuels the boilers that supply steam and electricity to the mill and often to the grid. Each stage has its own hazard. Raízen's sites are spread across the agricultural interior, so a handset is used far from head office and the safety specialists are a drive away. Mills run through the harvest, the safra, and are overhauled between harvests.

Areas of a mill

Cane reception and milling
Crushing, with bagasse generated and conveyed to the boilers.
Bagasse storage and boilers
Fibre and fine particles that burn easily, with fire risk in storage and on conveyors.
Sugar drying and bagging
Fine sugar dust, a known combustible dust, in dryers, silos and packing.
Fermentation and distillation
Ethanol vapour in columns, with tanks and pumps around them.
Ethanol storage and loading
Tank farms and truck loading where vapour collects at the vents.
Cogeneration and electrical plant
Steam turbines and switchrooms, mostly not classified.
Offices and laboratories
Quality control and administration outside the classified boundary.

Hazard types

Ethanol vapour
Flammable vapour, covered by IIC equipment, found in the distillery and storage.
Sugar dust
Combustible dust in dryers and conveying, with Zone 21 and 22 classification.
Bagasse dust and fibre
Fine organic material around conveyors, with fire and dust risk.
Mixed areas
Places where vapour and dust are both possible, such as loading bays.
Seasonal contractors
Extra crews for the harvest and the overhaul between harvests.
Fuel and agricultural machinery
Field equipment and refuelling outside the process areas.
Fire-fighting systems
Hydrants and foam systems that need regular inspection.

What the safra does to a handset

A mill's year has two parts: a harvest in which everything runs at once, and an interval for maintenance. A fleet of handsets has to suit both.

Dust gets in everywhere

Fine sugar and bagasse dust is hard on seals, speakers and ports. IP68 sealing and the rugged build are the reason a handset can be rinsed after a shift on a mill floor.

Heat and wet washdown

Mill floors are warm and are hosed down regularly. The handset is sealed for immersion and washdown, but the washing routine should be agreed with the site safety officer.

Temporary crews in the harvest

Mills bring in extra staff for the safra. Enrolment in an existing MDM console issues a managed handset to each team and removes it when the season ends.

Distance from head office

Mills are far from the corporate office. Remote management through Android Enterprise means policy and app updates can be applied without a visit.

Both gas and dust in one walk

The design target covers Zone 1 gas and Zone 21 dust, so an operator on a round between the distillery and the sugar plant carries a single device.

Use cases · Raízen

Where the IS-04Z1 goes to work at Raízen.

Distillery01

Fermentation and distillation rounds

An operator records column temperatures and tank levels, photographs a leaking flange and reports it from inside the classified area. The record replaces a round sheet signed at the end of the shift.

Energy02

Boiler house and bagasse conveyor inspection

Conveyors carrying bagasse are inspected for build-up and heat. A handset logs a location and a photograph, and the maintenance team sees where fibre is accumulating.

Maintenance03

Inter-harvest overhaul

Between harvests, contractors overhaul crushers, boilers and distillation columns. A managed fleet gives each crew the same job pack and is cleared when they finish.

Questions · Raízen

Frequently asked questions

Is the IS-04Z1 certified for use at Raízen mills yet?

No. The device is pre-launch, and IECEx, ATEX and the Brazilian INMETRO certification are in progress ahead of the Q4 2026 launch.

Does one handset cover both ethanol vapour and sugar dust?

The design target is Zone 1 gas and Zone 21 dust. Whether a particular area is covered depends on its classification, which the mill's safety team sets.

Does Raízen's shareholder structure change who we speak to?

Raízen is a company in its own right. Its procurement and safety procedures are separate from those of its shareholders, so a supplier deals with Raízen directly.

How does the harvest season affect an evaluation?

A trial is easiest during the inter-harvest period, when plant is stopped and safety staff have time. A trial in the safra is possible, but plan for less access.
