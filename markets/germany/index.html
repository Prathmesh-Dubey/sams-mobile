Where it works · Germany

Where it works in Germany

German classified-area work sits in integrated chemical sites rather than oilfields. One Verbund site can hold hundreds of plants and a contractor population that turns over every few weeks, and an ordinary shift crosses Zone 1 and Zone 21 boundaries several times. Refining, pharmaceutical production and newer gas infrastructure add the rest.

Operators and contractors such as BASF, Currenta and Evonik need an explosion proof mobile phone they can carry into classified areas at Ludwigshafen, Leverkusen and Marl. The IS-04Z1 is designed for Zone 1 and Zone 21 duty there.

Regulatory route · Germany

What your HSE and procurement teams will ask for.

On the product side Germany is refreshingly short. ATEX 2014/34/EU is the legal basis, implemented nationally through the Produktsicherheitsgesetz and its eleventh ordinance, the 11. ProdSV. A notified body issues the certificate, and buyers here expect a familiar name on it: PTB, DEKRA or TÜV. Once that certificate and the EU declaration of conformity exist, no further national product approval is needed, and the radio side runs under RED 2014/53/EU with market surveillance by the Bundesnetzagentur.

The operator side is stricter than in most markets. BetrSichV requires an Explosionsschutzdokument for every workplace with an explosive atmosphere, and TRBS 2152 sets out the zone classification and equipment selection reasoning it must contain. Procurement will ask for the certificate number, the full marking string and a German-language declaration of conformity and manual, then keep all three on file. Equipment arriving with English documentation only does not clear the evaluation.

Where the IS-04Z1 explosion proof mobile phone goes to work.

Typical Zone 1 and Zone 21 duty for the IS-04Z1 explosion proof mobile phone in Germany includes: Shift handover inside a Verbund site; Stillstand work with external contractors; Solvent and powder areas in one building; New gas infrastructure, small teams.

Chemical parks01

Shift handover inside a Verbund site

At Ludwigshafen or a Chempark site the distance from a plant to the shift office is measured in kilometres. A Zone 1 handset lets the operator log a reading, photograph a weeping flange and hand the shift over from the plant, with the site emergency number in the same pocket.

Turnarounds02

Stillstand work with external contractors

A German turnaround brings hundreds of Fremdfirmen through the gate for a few weeks. When each crew carries a hazardous-area handset enrolled in the site MDM, the job pack, the Freigabe and the photographic record stay in one system rather than leaving in a folder.

Pharmaceutical03

Solvent and powder areas in one building

Pharmaceutical and fine-chemical production often puts a Zone 1 solvent area and a Zone 21 dust area a few metres apart. One device covering both zones ends the routine of leaving a phone at one boundary and collecting another at the next.

New gas infrastructure, small teams

The LNG import terminals at Wilhelmshaven and Brunsbüttel, the biogas plants and the hydrogen projects at Lingen and in the Ruhr are recently classified areas run by small crews. They need equipment that works on the jetty, at the compressor and on the walk between them.

Frequently asked questions

Ask about Germany →
Is the IS-04Z1 already ATEX certified?

No. It is designed to ATEX 2014/34/EU for Zone 1 and Zone 21 and certification is in progress. Launch is planned for Q4 2026, and nothing is supplied for use in a classified area before the certificate exists.

Does Germany require a national approval on top of ATEX?

No. ATEX 2014/34/EU, implemented through the Produktsicherheitsgesetz and the 11. ProdSV, is the complete product-side requirement, and there is no separate German approval for Ex equipment. The duties that follow belong to the operator, under BetrSichV and the TRBS rules.

Will the documentation be available in German?

Yes. The declaration of conformity and the manual are produced in German for this market alongside English. German buyers treat that as a condition of acceptance, so it is planned into the launch rather than added afterwards.

Does the target cover hydrogen atmospheres?

Group IIC is the target, and IIC is the group that includes hydrogen. That is deliberate: German operators are adding electrolysers and hydrogen lines to existing sites, and handheld equipment that stops at IIB starts to look short-lived.

How does it fit a Betriebsvereinbarung on mobile devices?

Through configuration rather than assurances. Android 16 supports EMM and MDM, so location services, the camera and individual applications can be set to what the works council agreement permits, and that can be demonstrated during the evaluation.

Can we get a price or a delivery date?

No published price before launch. Pre-launch pricing is quoted against a defined quantity and configuration, and delivery follows the certificate rather than an order date. Evaluation units are issued under a trial agreement rather than sold.
