Germany · Chemicals and petrochemicals

Explosion proof mobile phone for chemicals and petrochemicals in Germany

An explosion proof mobile phone for German chemical plants has to work in two quite different settings: the single-company Verbund of BASF at Ludwigshafen, and the multi-tenant Chempark sites at Leverkusen, Dormagen and Krefeld-Uerdingen, alongside Chemiepark Marl and Industriepark Höchst. This page is for the Ex-Schutz officers, instrumentation engineers and works-council members who decide what may be carried into them, and it describes what the IS-04Z1 is designed for ahead of its Q4 2026 launch. The IS-04Z1 is designed around intrinsic safety rather than a flameproof enclosure.

The sites · Germany

Where a Zone 1 mobile phone is carried on German chemical sites

Ludwigshafen covers around 10 km² and roughly 200 plants, run by one company and tied together by pipelines. The point for a handset is that a single walk from the shift office can pass from a cracker area, through a pipe bridge, into a solvent plant and on to a polymer packing hall, each with its own entry in the Explosionsschutzdokument. Chempark, Chemiepark Marl and Industriepark Höchst are organised differently: an infrastructure operator runs the utilities, security, fire brigade and logistics, while separate companies produce on the same land under their own rules.

What a German chemical site does to a handset

The zone decides whether a device may be carried at all. These are the points that tend to come up when a chemical-park evaluation reads the datasheet.

Hydrogen and hydrocarbons in the same walk

The gas marking target is II 2 G Ex ib IIC T4. Group IIC is the most demanding gas group and covers hydrogen, which is why a handset aimed at a cracker or hydrogenation area is designed to that group rather than to IIA.

Temperature class against the substance

T4 limits surface temperature to 135 °C. Some process substances ignite well below that and call for T5 or T6, so the temperature class in the Explosionsschutzdokument entry is checked per plant, not once for the site.

Dust from polymer and pigment lines

Zone 21 dust protection is part of the same certification programme, so a handset that goes from a solvent plant to a bagging hall does not need swapping at the door.

Distances measured in kilometres

A 4,000 mAh battery has to last a shift where the nearest charging point is outside the zone. Four-constellation GNSS gives a position in open areas; under pipe racks and inside buildings, expect to rely on the site network.

One handset, several companies

Android Enterprise management from an existing MDM console lets a site operator or contractor keep tenant policies separate, and a 50 MP camera can be switched off by policy where a plant restricts photography.

Work organisation · Germany

How work is organised on a German chemical site

Operations01

Rounds and shift handover

Operators walk set routes, log readings and report leaks to a control room that may be a kilometre away. The same handset carries the round list, a photograph of a weeping flange and a line to the park fire brigade.

Turnarounds02

Stillstand and Revision

Units shut down on a planned cycle that runs to years, then hundreds of Fremdfirmen crews arrive for a few weeks. Each crew needs a handset enrolled for the job pack and Freigabe.

Park operations03

Tenant and operator interfaces

In a Chempark or Chemiepark the park operator and the tenant both have a say. A leak at a boundary raises the question of whose alarm number, whose permit and whose policy applies on the device.

Approvals · Germany

What German chemical operators ask of an equipment supplier

On the product side, ATEX 2014/34/EU is the framework, and a certificate from a notified body is the evidence. The operator side is where evaluations spend their time: under BetrSichV and TRBS 2152 the operator decides the zones and records the equipment selection in the Explosionsschutzdokument, so procurement will want the certificate number and full marking string once they exist.

Beyond that, a site operator or large producer usually runs its own supplier and contractor registration. We have not verified any specific park's programme and do not describe one; ask the operator's procurement and HSE teams what they require before any unit enters a plant.

Questions · Chemicals and petrochemicals

Frequently asked questions

Is the IS-04Z1 certified for German chemical plants yet?

No. It is pre-launch and ATEX and IECEx certification are in progress ahead of the Q4 2026 launch. Until a certificate exists it is not to be taken into a classified area, and any unit offered for evaluation is for documentation, network and policy testing in safe areas.

Does one approval from a park operator such as Currenta cover every tenant?

Not automatically. The operator typically sets site-wide access and safety rules, while each tenant company decides what its own people may carry and buys through its own procurement.

Who has to agree to GNSS tracking and camera use?

In Germany the works council normally has co-determination rights over technical systems that can monitor employees, so location features and MDM policies usually need a Betriebsvereinbarung. Draft that agreement alongside the Ex-Schutz review rather than after it.

Can one handset serve every plant at Ludwigshafen?

Only if its marking is at least as demanding as the classification of each plant it enters. The Explosionsschutzdokument for each plant states the zone, gas group and temperature class, and the handset is checked against the strictest of them on the route.
