How the specification meets UAE conditions

Coastal humidity, salt air and long offshore rotations wear equipment in ways inland plant does not. These are the points UAE evaluators press hardest on.

Heat carried in humid air

Coastal sites run above 45 °C with humidity that dry-heat testing does not represent. A rugged build is aimed at heat, humidity and salt, and IP68 is what survives a jetty washdown.

Offshore, where a spare is a helicopter

On Das, Zirku or a Zakum island there is no same-day replacement. A 4,000 mAh battery and a fully sealed body hold a unit in service until the next rotation.

Three tools collapsed into one

Radio, camera and torch inside one handset shrinks what a contractor signs out at the gate and hands back at demobilisation, which is where loaned equipment goes missing.

Evidence worth attaching to a work order

The 50 MP camera with PDAF returns inspection photographs sharp enough to act on, and four-constellation GNSS records where each one was taken.

Connectivity that follows the asset

4G LTE off site, Wi-Fi inside the plant, Bluetooth for gas detectors and headsets, NFC for asset tags and access control. Push-to-talk over cellular runs as an app, not a second radio.

Controlled from your own console

Android 16 with enterprise management enrols into the EMM or MDM platform you already run, so a telecoms team can push the corporate profile, restrict the camera and wipe a lost unit remotely.

Regulatory route · United Arab Emirates

What your HSE and procurement teams will ask for.

Conformity in the Emirates is administered by MoIAT, the Ministry of Industry and Advanced Technology, through the Emirates Conformity Assessment Scheme. For hazardous-area equipment an ECAS registration is built on an IECEx Certificate of Conformity, and the radio inside the handset needs separate TDRA approval. Both sit inside the SAMS launch programme; neither is finished, and we would rather say so than let a specification assume otherwise.

Site access is decided somewhere else entirely. ADNOC supplier registration is a prerequisite for tendering across the group, and the In-Country Value certificate, audited annually and scored into bid evaluation, sets how a registered supplier ranks. ENOC, DP World and Borouge each run approvals of their own. If you are specifying a device for an ADNOC asset, the useful early question is which registered UAE entity will hold the supply.

Can we buy the IS-04Z1 in the UAE today?

Not yet. Launch is Q4 2026 and the certifications, including IECEx, ATEX and MoIAT ECAS registration, are still in progress. What exists today is the datasheet, the certification schedule and a place in the evaluation programme.

Is it approved for ADNOC sites?

That approval belongs to ADNOC and follows the certificates rather than preceding them. Supplier registration and an ICV certificate are the commercial half of it, and we are building that channel with a UAE partner.

Which zones and gas groups does it cover?

Zone 1 and Zone 21, gas and dust, with an ATEX marking target of II 2 G Ex ib IIC T4. NEC 500 Class I Division 1 is being pursued as well, which matters on UAE projects written to the American code.

Will it hold up offshore?

It is built for it, with IP68 sealing, rugged build and a 4,000 mAh battery sized for a full shift. Even so, we would rather you tested a unit on your own platform before committing a fleet.

Does it support Arabic?

Yes. Arabic and English are both available in the Android 16 interface and the documentation will be issued in both. Your own applications keep whatever languages they already run in.

What happens when one is damaged?

A device holding an Ex certificate cannot be opened by a general repair shop without voiding it, so battery replacement and repair run through SAMS and the UAE partner it is appointing. Turnaround and spares pooling are part of what we are appointing distributors to provide.

Will it work with our MDM and push-to-talk setup?

It runs Android 16 with standard enterprise management, so it enrols into the EMM or MDM platform you already use, and push-to-talk over cellular runs as an application. Send your build requirements with the evaluation request.

How much does an explosion proof phone cost in the UAE?

No list price is published before certification completes. Pre-launch pricing is quoted in writing against quantity and configuration. Supply into ADNOC and the other operators runs through a registered partner, and ICV status affects how a bid is evaluated, so pricing is discussed alongside the channel rather than in isolation.
