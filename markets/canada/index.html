Zone and Division on one label

Section 18 classifies by Zone while Annex J18 keeps the Division system, so buyers ask for both. The marking target is II 2 G Ex ib IIC T4, with Class I Division 1 pursued on the same intrinsic safety basis.

The ambient range on the certificate

Unless a certificate says otherwise, hazardous area equipment is assessed for -20 to +40 degrees Celsius. Before a January shift at Fort McMurray that matters more than the drop rating. The marked range is published when the certificate issues.

Combustible dust, not only gas

Sulfur handling at an upgrader, coke and coal transfer, grain terminals and pellet plants are dust locations where a gas-only fleet stops. The Zone 21 design target covers both.

Long routes, cold batteries

A pad route north of Fort McMurray puts hours between charging points, and every battery gives up capacity in the cold. A 4,000 mAh cell, a Snapdragon octa-core platform and four-constellation GNSS suit a shift away from the truck.

Heavy gloves, one usable hand

Winter gloves, FR coveralls and a fixed ladder leave one hand for the work and none for a notebook. A 50 MP camera with PDAF and NFC capture an asset tag where it is found.

Both official languages, centrally managed

Android 16 runs the interface in English and French, which matters for crews in Quebec. EMM and MDM enrollment locks a device to an approved app set and wipes it at demobilization.

Regulatory route · Canada

What your HSE and procurement teams will ask for.

The code your electrical inspector works from is CSA C22.1, the Canadian Electrical Code, adopted into law province by province. Section 18 classifies explosive atmospheres by Zone, while Annex J18 keeps the older Division system for installations built and maintained that way. That is why one operator calls an area Zone 1 IIC and the plant down the road calls it Class I Division 1 Group B. Rule 18-050 decides the purchase: equipment in a Zone location must be designed, tested and certified for the atmosphere and equipment group present, by a certification body accredited by the Standards Council of Canada.

Two further approvals sit beside it. Any radio device sold or operated in Canada needs ISED certification against RSS-Gen and the applicable Radio Standards Specification, with RSS-102 covering radio frequency exposure, and the IC number has to appear on the product. The duty to use suitable equipment falls on the employer under provincial health and safety law, and the authority enforcing the code differs by province: Alberta Municipal Affairs and the Safety Codes Council issue STANDATA interpretations of Section 18, Technical Safety BC administers it in British Columbia, and the Electrical Safety Authority does so in Ontario. Work on the Grand Banks adds the Canada-Newfoundland and Labrador Offshore Energy Regulator, renamed from the C-NLOPB in 2025.

Is the IS-04Z1 a certified explosion proof phone in Canada today?

No. It is designed for Zone 1, Zone 21 and Class I Division 1 duty and certification is in progress ahead of the Q4 2026 launch. Until a certificate from an SCC-accredited body exists we will not describe it as certified.

Is this a flameproof mobile phone or an intrinsically safe one?

Buyers use flameproof, exproof and Ex proof phone for the same thing, but they are different concepts. Flameproof, Ex d, contains an ignition inside a heavy enclosure and suits fixed equipment. Handhelds take the intrinsic safety route, Ex ib, which limits available energy.

We hold a US Class I Division 1 listing on other equipment. Is that enough?

No. A Canadian inspector looks for a mark from a body accredited by the Standards Council of Canada. Many laboratories run both assessments together and issue a combined mark, but it is the Canadian side of it that counts.

Will it work at minus 40 in the oil sands?

The governing number is the ambient range on the certificate, not the drop rating. Assessment covers -20 to +40 degrees Celsius unless the certificate states otherwise, so we publish the marked range when it issues. Batteries also lose capacity in deep cold.

What does an explosion proof phone cost in Canada?

There is no published price for a device whose certificate has not issued. Pre-launch pricing is given on request once we know fleet size, configuration and supply route. Evaluation units go out under a trial agreement rather than sold.

Is French documentation available?

Android 16 runs the interface in French as well as English, and French user documentation is part of the launch package. Buyers in Quebec should ask for the French manual and label copy when the certification schedule is sent.

Can it be used offshore, and will it have ISED certification?

It is designed for Zone 1 offshore duty, with IP68 and rugged build and Wi-Fi where cellular coverage stops. ISED certification, required before any radio device is sold here, is part of the same launch program.
