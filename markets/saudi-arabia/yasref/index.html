The site · YASREF

Explosion proof mobile phone for a Red Sea refinery at Yanbu

YASREF is owned 62.5 per cent by Saudi Aramco and 37.5 per cent by Sinopec. It is a 400,000 barrel a day integrated refinery in Yanbu Industrial City, processing Arabian Heavy crude and producing gasoline, diesel and LPG, with sulphur and petroleum coke leaving as by-products for export. A refinery configured that way has the full hydroprocessing and coking sequence behind its distillation units, so the area classification runs from hydrocarbon vapour at the crude unit to hydrogen in the hydrotreaters and hydrogen sulphide in the sulphur recovery area. Coke and sulphur handling add combustible solids, and the plant sits on the coast with the sea at one boundary and the wider Yanbu industrial area at the other.

What the Red Sea coast does to a handset

Yanbu does not behave like the Gulf side of the Kingdom. The air carries salt, humidity is higher, and the neighbouring plants share a coastal setting. These are the points that follow for a handset.

Salt air and humidity

The Red Sea coast adds salt and moisture to the usual heat. IP68 sealing and rugged build are specified with that in mind, and charging contacts are a place to check during evaluation.

Hydrogen and the IIC question

Hydroprocessing means hydrogen is part of the area classification. The IS-04Z1 marking target is II 2 G Ex ib IIC T4. The certificate will state its scope once issued.

Coke and sulphur as dust

Handling solids at the export side adds Zone 21 or Zone 22 areas. The device is designed for Zone 21 dust, which suits the more demanding of the two.

Two parents, two certification habits

Sinopec's home market runs the NEPSI scheme, which shares an IEC basis with IECEx but is a separate process. The technical file is being prepared so that cross-reference is easy to see, without suggesting a Chinese certificate is already held.

Turnaround crews from several countries

Planned shutdowns bring contractors from several countries onto one site. A handset enrolled in the operator's console can be configured for a gang in minutes, and Arabic and English are both available.

Questions · YASREF

Frequently asked questions

Does YASREF need a Chinese Ex certificate?

That is a question for YASREF's own engineering team. The site is in Saudi Arabia, and Saudi operators work from IECEx. The documentation is being prepared to make the link to NEPSI clear should a reviewer ask.

How is Yanbu different from Jubail for a handset?

Salt and higher humidity on the Red Sea, compared with the Gulf coast climate at Jubail. Sealing and charging contact condition are the points to look at in evaluation.

Does the same device handle coke and sulphur dust?

Zone 21 dust is part of the design target, alongside Zone 1 gas. The dust group and the surface temperature limit on the area drawing still decide where it may be used.

Has YASREF approved the IS-04Z1?

No. It is pre-launch, certification is in progress ahead of Q4 2026, and no vendor approval can exist before the certificate does.
