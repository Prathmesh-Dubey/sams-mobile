The site · Oil and gas

Explosion proof mobile phone for the Saudi oil and gas chain

The chain starts at the fields: Ghawar, with its gas-oil separation plants, Shaybah and Khurais, and offshore production such as Safaniya in the Gulf. Crude is stabilised at Abqaiq and moves to Ras Tanura for export on the Gulf, or across the Kingdom by the East-West pipeline to Yanbu on the Red Sea. Associated and non-associated gas goes to plants such as Hawiyah, Haradh, Uthmaniyah and Fadhili, where it is compressed, treated and split into sales gas and NGL. Refineries at Ras Tanura, Jubail and Yanbu close the loop. The hazard profile changes along the way: sour gas and crude vapour upstream, hydrogen in hydroprocessing downstream, and long loading lines at the terminals. A single area drawing never tells the whole story, so the sector view is useful mainly for working out which gas groups and temperature classes recur.

One specification along the whole chain

Zone 1 gas runs from the separation plant to the jetty. The IS-04Z1 is designed for Zone 1 and Zone 21, with the marking target II 2 G Ex ib IIC T4, so an operator does not need one device for upstream and another for the refinery.

Distances between sites

Shaybah and the offshore platforms are reached by long drives, boats or helicopters. A failed handset is not a same-day problem, so IP68 sealing and rugged build are specified accordingly, with an enrolled spare ready to go.

Sour gas means the detector comes first

Hydrogen sulphide is toxic well below the flammable range, so personal gas detectors are the first line of defence. A handset is not a substitute, and the site rules on what else is carried will be set by the operator.

Turnaround surges at gas plants and refineries

A planned shutdown multiplies the contractor population and the permit traffic. Enrolled handsets can be configured centrally and collected at the end of the job.

A single console for a large estate

Enrolment in an existing MDM console lets an operator push the camera policy and application list to units spread over several sites, which suits an estate that crosses regions.

Questions · Oil and gas

Frequently asked questions

Which gas groups should we expect on a Saudi oil and gas site?

Mostly IIA and IIB hydrocarbons, with hydrogen at the refineries in group IIC. The area classification drawing is the authority, and the marking target for the IS-04Z1 is IIC, which is the broadest group.

Does the IS-04Z1 replace a personal gas detector?

No. A phone is not a gas detector. Where the operator requires detectors, they stay, and the handset sits alongside them.

Which operator pages should we read next?

Start with the pages on Saudi Aramco, SATORP and YASREF, and the site pages for Ras Tanura, Jubail and Yanbu. Each lists the units and approval points for that operator.

Is the IS-04Z1 available for oil and gas sites yet?

No. It is pre-launch, certification is in progress ahead of Q4 2026, and it will not be supplied into a classified area before the certificate exists.
