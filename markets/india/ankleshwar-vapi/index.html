India · Ankleshwar and Vapi

Explosion proof phone for Ankleshwar and Vapi, Gujarat

Ankleshwar and Vapi are two of the largest chemical industrial estates in India, both on the south Gujarat belt. Both were laid out by the state development corporation and are packed with small and mid-sized plants making dyes, pigments, pesticides, intermediates and pharmaceuticals. The hazard here is not crude oil but solvent vapour, hydrogen and fine powder inside reactor halls and dryer rooms. A phone carried into those rooms has to be approved first. The SAMS IS-04Z1 is pre-launch: certification is in progress and no certificate has been issued yet.

The site · Ankleshwar and Vapi

The sites: two GIDC chemical estates

Ankleshwar sits in Bharuch district beside the Narmada estuary, while Vapi lies in Valsad district near the Maharashtra border. Both are GIDC industrial estates with plants packed close together, often on small plots. Reported unit counts run to well over a thousand in each, so most operators are small or mid-sized. Treatment of effluent and waste is handled through common facilities, and many plants run batch processes rather than continuous ones.

These are crowded, humid, chemically aggressive estates where one small plot can hold gas, dust and corrosive atmospheres at once. Those conditions decide how a handset is sealed, cleaned, managed and documented more than any single process does.

Small plants, crowded plots

Many units sit on small plots with reactors, dryers and drum stores close together, so a single walk can cross several classified areas. A handset approved for Zone 1 gas and Zone 21 dust is easier to manage than a mixed set of devices. Classification is the plant's own decision and should be checked area by area with its competent person.

Solvents, hydrogen and gas groups

Common process solvents such as methanol, acetone and toluene sit in lower gas groups, but hydrogenation steps bring hydrogen, which is group IIC. A phone marked only for IIA or IIB would be excluded from those blocks. The marking target of II 2 G Ex ib IIC T4 is chosen so that one device can serve the whole plant, subject to the certificate.

Pigment and dye dust

Dye and pigment plants dry, mill and bag fine powders, and organic dust can ignite. That is why Zone 21 is part of the design target and not an afterthought. A dryer room fed with wet cake can also contain solvent vapour, so some rooms need both a gas and a dust assessment, which only the operator can carry out.

Monsoon humidity and chemical air

South Gujarat has a wet monsoon season and long humid spells, and the plant atmosphere adds acid and solvent exposure on top. IP68 sealing is aimed at water and dust, but the enclosure's resistance to specific chemicals has to be checked against what the plant actually uses. Treat an evaluation unit as a test, not a guarantee.

Inspections, audits and documented equipment

Chemical plants in these estates are inspected by environmental and safety authorities and audited by customers. Audits tend to reward documented equipment rather than a site exemption. A phone with a certificate and a clear marking is easier to defend than an informal rule. Which evidence an inspector asks for depends on the authority and the plant.

Mixed-language workforce

Both estates draw labour from several states, so interface language varies between operators and contract workmen. Android 16 supports English and Hindi with enterprise management, and EMM enrolment lets the plant push one approved configuration to every phone. Gujarati and other languages should be confirmed against the datasheet.

Use cases · Ankleshwar and Vapi

Where the IS-04Z1 explosion proof mobile phone goes to work.

Chemicals01

Batch reactor charging and sampling

An operator charging a reactor or taking a sample needs to record batch details, photograph a gauge and call the supervisor without leaving the room. A sealed phone avoids carrying paper and a separate camera into a solvent area. Which rooms it may enter depends on the certificate and on the plant's own classification.

Pharmaceutical02

Solvent recovery and dryer-room checks

Active ingredient plants recover solvent by distillation and dry product afterwards. A maintenance technician can photograph a gasket, log the asset tag and send a work request from the plant floor. Pairing over Bluetooth with a gas detector lets a reading be recorded against the tag, though the detector remains the safety device.

Dyes and pigments03

Dust area housekeeping and inspection

Dust control depends on regular inspection and cleaning of mills, conveyors and bagging points. A Zone 21 design target means an inspector can photograph a dust accumulation and file the report on the spot, within the limits set by the certificate and the plant's permit, rather than writing it up later from memory.

Effluent and waste04

Treatment plant and tank inspections

Common effluent and waste plants have tanks, pumps and sumps where flammable vapour can build up. Operators doing rounds can photograph readings and flag a fault by message. Entry into confined spaces follows its own permit and gas-testing rules, and a phone does not replace them.

Frequently asked questions

Can we buy an explosion proof phone in Ankleshwar or Vapi today?

Not yet. The IS-04Z1 is pre-launch and its IECEx, ATEX and NEC 500 Class I Div 1 certification is in progress at TÜV in Germany ahead of a Q4 2026 launch. No unit goes into a classified area before the certificate covering it exists. The datasheet, the schedule and safe-area evaluation units are available now.

What does an explosion proof phone cost in Ankleshwar or Vapi?

No price is published. While certification is in progress there is no list price and no committed lead time. Indicative pre-launch pricing can be issued in writing against a stated quantity and site, and a supplier quoting a firm figure for an uncertified Ex handset is promising something it cannot stand behind.

Which approvals will a chemical or pharma plant here ask for?

Hazardous-area equipment in India needs PESO approval, built on a recognised certificate such as IECEx, and the handset also needs BIS registration and WPC radio approval, as set out on the India page. Each plant then applies its own acceptance rules, so confirm the requirement with its EHS and procurement teams.

Does gas group IIC matter in a solvent plant?

Many solvents belong to lower groups, but hydrogen, used in hydrogenation, is in group IIC. A plant that has even one hydrogen block may prefer a single IIC-marked device across the site. Confirm the group for your own areas with your competent person, because the classification belongs to the operator.

Who would support and repair the phones in south Gujarat?

Support and repair would come from SAMS together with the local partner SAMS is appointing for India. No partner, stock or service point is announced yet. Ask early about turnaround time, spares and how a damaged unit is exchanged, because a sealed Ex device should only be opened by an authorised centre.

Can a small plant manage a phone fleet?

Yes, in principle. EMM enrolment lets one administrator apply a single configuration, lock features and remove a device when staff or contractors leave. Small plants often have no dedicated IT team, so ask the partner SAMS is appointing what enrolment support and remote management would be available before committing to a fleet.
