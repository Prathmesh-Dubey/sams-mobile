Where it works · India

Where the IS-04Z1 is meant to work

India’s classified areas fall into two populations. The first is the hydrocarbon chain: coastal refining, inland gas processing, LNG terminals and ONGC’s offshore platforms. The second is pharmaceutical manufacturing, where solvent handling classifies rooms inside buildings that look nothing like a process plant.

Operators and contractors such as Indian Oil Corporation, Bharat Petroleum and Hindustan Petroleum need an explosion proof mobile phone they can carry into classified areas at Jamnagar, Gujarat, Dahej and the Gujarat PCPIR and Hazira and Surat. The IS-04Z1 is designed for Zone 1 and Zone 21 duty there.

A marking your PESO file can use

The ATEX marking target is II 2 G Ex ib IIC T4, with IECEx pursued in parallel, because the IECEx certificate is the evidence PESO approval is built on. IIC is the most demanding gas group and covers hydrogen service as well as IIA and IIB duty.

Heat, humidity and monsoon

Pre-monsoon heat and post-monsoon humidity are what kill industrial handsets here. Construction is IP68 sealing and a rugged build, and the certified ambient range is whatever the certificate states.

Dust as much as gas

Zone 21 is part of the design target, not an afterthought. Coke handling, catalyst charging in petrochemicals and powder handling in formulation are dust environments a gas-only specification leaves out.

Solvent areas in pharma plants

API blocks in Hyderabad, Ahmedabad and Baddi classify solvent rooms as Zone 1 and Zone 2, and audits there reward documented equipment rather than a site exemption.

A full shift between charge points

Units, jetties and platforms rarely offer a socket where the work is. A 4,000 mAh battery on a Qualcomm Snapdragon octa-core platform, with 4G LTE, Wi-Fi and four-constellation GNSS, is specified around a shift.

English, Hindi and a managed fleet

Interface language matters when operators, contract workmen and engineers share one fleet. Android 16 supports English and Hindi with enterprise management, and NFC and Bluetooth cover asset tags and gas detectors.

Regulatory route · India

What your HSE and procurement teams will ask for.

India asks for more paperwork than any other market SAMS is launching into, and it is worth knowing why before anyone fixes a date. Three separate approvals apply, on three independent timelines. Hazardous-area approval comes from PESO, the Petroleum and Explosives Safety Organisation in Nagpur, granted against the IS/IEC 60079 series on the basis of a test report from a recognised laboratory or a recognised foreign certificate, which here means the IECEx Certificate of Conformity. That is what your safety officer and your factory inspector will ask to see.

The other two apply because this is a phone rather than a fixed instrument. BIS registration under the Compulsory Registration Scheme covers the handset as an electronic product and is mandatory for mobile phones. The WPC wing of the Department of Telecommunications issues Equipment Type Approval for the radio, which for a handset in de-licensed bands runs through self-declaration on the Saral Sanchar portal. All three are needed before commercial import, and all three are in progress.

Use cases · India

Where the IS-04Z1 explosion proof mobile phone goes to work.

Typical Zone 1 and Zone 21 duty for the IS-04Z1 explosion proof mobile phone in India includes: Shutdown work inside a refinery unit; Jetty and regasification operations; Solvent handling in an API block; Platform work in the Mumbai basin.

Refining01

Shutdown work inside a refinery unit

A crude or hydrocracker shutdown at Panipat, Koyali or Paradip puts thousands of contract workmen into classified areas on rotating permits for weeks. An approved handset lets a shift in-charge verify an isolation and reach the planning cell from inside the battery limit.

LNG and gas02

Jetty and regasification operations

At a terminal like Dahej or Hazira, unloading puts marine, terminal and vessel teams on one jetty with a narrow window and a large classified footprint. One device for voice, photographs and checklists removes a step from every hand-off.

Pharmaceutical03

Solvent handling in an API block

Charging, distillation and recovery areas are classified because of solvent vapour, and they are among the most heavily audited spaces in Indian industry. A certified handset lets a chemist record a batch observation or scan a container tag inside the block.

Offshore04

Platform work in the Mumbai basin

Offshore in the Mumbai High complex, crew changes are scheduled and spares are not, so a failed device waits for the next boat. IP68 and rugged build, platform Wi-Fi and Bluetooth pairing to gas detection keep it usable through a rotation.

Ask about India →
Is the IS-04Z1 PESO approved?

Not yet. The PESO application is part of the launch programme and is in progress. Until the approval letter is issued we will not call the device PESO approved, and you should treat any supplier who does with caution.

We accept IECEx certificates. Do we still need PESO?

Yes. An IECEx Certificate of Conformity is the evidence PESO approval is built on, but it does not replace the approval itself for equipment used in a classified area in India.

What about BIS and WPC?

Both are required and both are in progress. BIS registration covers the handset as an electronic product; WPC Equipment Type Approval covers the radio. Commercial import needs all three, PESO included.

Will it survive a Gujarat summer and the monsoon?

That is the condition it is designed around. IP68 sealing and a rugged build cover water, dust, shock and vibration. The marking target of II 2 G Ex ib IIC T4 refers to maximum surface temperature, not ambient, so the certified range is published when the certificate fixes it.

Can it be used in a pharmaceutical solvent area, and is there a Hindi interface?

It is designed for Zone 1 and Zone 21, gas and dust, at gas group IIC, which is what solvent handling and hydrogenation areas need; confirm the classification for your block with your own competent person. Android 16 supports Hindi alongside English.

Who handles warranty and repair in India?

An in-country partner is being appointed to hold stock and handle warranty and repair, so a failed unit does not travel out of India. That is not in place yet; the partner and service terms will be published before the first fleets ship.

What is the price, and when can we order?

Launch is Q4 2026 and there is no price list for an uncertified device. Pre-launch pricing is on request once we know fleet size and whether you are buying through a tender.
