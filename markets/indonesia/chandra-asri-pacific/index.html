The site · Cilegon, Banten

What is classified in the Cilegon cracker complex

Chandra Asri operates a naphtha cracker at Cilegon that turns naphtha into ethylene, propylene and other olefins, and downstream units turn those into polyethylene, polypropylene, styrene monomer and butadiene. The site is at the western end of Java, near the Sunda Strait, in an industrial zone that also holds steel and chemical plants. A cracker has furnaces, quench and compression, and cold separation, with hydrogen-rich and ethylene-rich gas in many lines. Downstream, polymer units move powder and pellets through silos and bagging, which is why dust classification sits next to gas classification. The result is a site where one operator may pass from a Zone 1 gas area to a Zone 21 powder area in a single walk.

What a cracker complex does to a handset

A petrochemical complex is as much about gas groups and dust as heat. These are the points that evaluators at Cilegon raise.

Gas groups from IIA to IIC in one walk

Naphtha is typically IIA, ethylene IIB and hydrogen IIC. A single handset marked for IIC avoids issuing different devices for different units. The IS-04Z1 marking target is II 2 G Ex ib IIC T4.

Powder as well as gas

Polyolefin powder and pellets create dust hazards in the polymer units. The Zone 21 design target is relevant here, and one handset replaces a gas device and a dust device.

Furnace heat and surface temperatures

Cracker furnace areas are hot, and temperature class is part of the classification. The certificate is the document your instrument engineer checks, not the marketing line.

Humid coastal air

Cilegon is on the Sunda Strait coast. IP68 sealing and rugged build are the design answer to humidity, rain and salt air.

Turnaround and expansion crews

A cracker shutdown or construction project brings large contractor numbers into a live complex. Enrolment through an existing MDM console lets the operator keep control of who has a handset and what it can do.

Turnarounds, contractors and approval for an explosion proof mobile phone at Chandra Asri, Indonesia

A cracker runs continuously for long periods and then stops for a planned turnaround, when inspection, mechanical and instrument crews work through the plant against a tight programme. In between, operator rounds cover furnaces, compressors, tank areas and polymer lines. Chandra Asri has its own HSE and procurement processes, which we have not seen published, so we have not named a programme. The usual starting evidence is an IECEx or ATEX certificate, with Indonesian type approval handled separately. Ask the site's procurement and HSE teams what a supplier must submit.

Operations01

Furnace and compressor rounds

Operators walk through hot, noisy areas recording readings and photographing leaks or hot spots. A handset with camera, torch and voice replaces three items carried separately.

Polymers02

Silo and bagging area inspection

Powder handling areas need a device designed for dust as well as gas, so the same handset can be used in the polymer lines as in the cracker.

Turnaround03

Shutdown inspection and punch lists

Inspectors raise findings against equipment tags, photograph them and update the planner, then pass the device through the console to the next crew.

Frequently asked questions

Why does a cracker need both Zone 1 and Zone 21?

Because the gas side and the powder side sit on one complex. The IS-04Z1 is designed for Zone 1 gas and Zone 21 dust, so a polymer operator and a cracker operator can carry the same model.

Is IIB enough for a cracker?

Ethylene is IIB, but hydrogen is IIC. A handset marked IIC covers both. The marking target of the IS-04Z1 is IIC.

Do you supply Chandra Asri today?

No. The device is pre-launch, certification is in progress, and nothing on this page implies any relationship with the operator. It describes where the device is designed to fit.

What about the nearby petrochemical and fertiliser sites?
