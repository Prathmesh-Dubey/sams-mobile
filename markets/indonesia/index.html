Tropical heat and constant humidity

Coastal plants at Dumai, Cilacap and Balikpapan run hot and humid all year, and salt air is part of the specification on most of them. IP68 sealing and rugged build address that; the certificate fixes the ambient range.

Distance is the real risk

A handset that fails at Teluk Bintuni or on the Rokan block is not replaced the same day. Full shift endurance from the 4,000 mAh battery and a sealed, drop tested body keep a unit in service between resupply runs.

Dust zones as well as gas zones

Urea and fertiliser handling, coal fired captive power at industrial estates and mineral processing put Zone 21 alongside Zone 1 in the same operation. The device is designed for both, not for gas alone.

Coverage that comes and goes

Four constellation GNSS fixes a position where a field has no landmark worth describing, and Wi-Fi, Bluetooth and NFC pick up plant systems where the cellular signal does not reach. Push to talk runs on the same handset.

Bahasa Indonesia on the handset and in the manual

Android 16 runs the interface in Bahasa Indonesia or English, and Indonesian rules require an Indonesian language manual and warranty card for products of this kind.

Regulatory route · Indonesia

What your HSE and procurement teams will ask for.

Your K3 and electrical people will ask for the certificate, and the argument they have to win is that the equipment matches the classified area it enters. Workplace electrical safety sits under the Ministry of Manpower framework, area classification is normally done to the IEC 60079-10 method by a certified expert, and Indonesian standards for explosive atmospheres follow the IEC 60079 series. That makes an IECEx Certificate of Conformity the working evidence. Upstream, the operator and the upstream regulator add their own equipment requirements on top of the national ones. None of the certificates exists yet.

The commercial route into Indonesia is heavier than in most markets and is worth planning early. A radio device needs type approval from the Directorate General under the Ministry of Communication and Digital Affairs, the body previously known as SDPPI, and the certificate number and device identifier are what customs and the networks work from. An IMEI has to be registered or the handset will not be carried on an Indonesian network. LTE handsets are separately subject to the domestic component requirement, TKDN, assessed by the Ministry of Industry, and an Indonesian language manual and warranty card is a trade requirement for electronic and telematics products. Upstream buyers add supplier qualification and their own local content rules. All of it is in progress and none of it is finished.

Ask about Indonesia →
Is the IS-04Z1 an explosion proof phone that can be used in Indonesia now?

No. It is pre-launch and every certification, IECEx included, is still in progress ahead of the Q4 2026 launch. Until then you can request the datasheet, register a requirement or join the evaluation programme.

Does Indonesia accept IECEx and ATEX certification?

Indonesian standards for explosive atmospheres follow the IEC 60079 series, so an IECEx Certificate of Conformity is the technical evidence a K3 department and an electrical engineer work from. Operator requirements can sit on top, particularly upstream.

Does an imported explosion proof phone need type approval and TKDN?

Yes. Any radio device needs type approval from Komdigi, the body previously known as SDPPI, and an IMEI registered so an Indonesian network will carry it. LTE handsets are also subject to the domestic component requirement assessed by the Ministry of Industry.

How much does an explosion proof mobile phone cost in Indonesia?

We do not publish a price and will not quote one before certification completes. Pre-launch pricing is on request against a quantity and configuration, and the landed cost of a compliant unit includes type approval, the local content route and the Indonesian documentation.

Which zones and marking is it designed for?

Zone 1 and Zone 21, gas and dust. The ATEX marking target is II 2 G Ex ib IIC T4, and NEC 500 Class I Division 1 is in the same programme for Indonesian projects written to the American code.

Will it work on remote sites in Papua Barat or Kalimantan?

That is the case it is designed for. IP68 sealing, rugged build and a 4,000 mAh battery are specified for sites where a replacement arrives by boat or aircraft rather than by van.

Is there a Bahasa Indonesia interface and manual?

Yes. Android 16 runs the interface in Bahasa Indonesia, and the manual and warranty card will be supplied in Indonesian as the rules for electronic and telematics products require.

How do repair and battery replacement work here?

An intrinsically safe handset cannot be opened by a general phone workshop without invalidating its certification, so service will run through SAMS and the Indonesian partner it is appointing. That route is being defined now.
