The sector · Petrochemical and fertiliser

Where petrochemical and fertiliser plants are classified in Indonesia

Pupuk Indonesia is the state fertiliser holding, and its operating companies include Pupuk Kaltim at Bontang, Petrokimia Gresik in East Java and Pupuk Sriwidjaja at Palembang, along with plants at Cikampek and in Aceh. Ammonia plants take natural gas, make hydrogen and nitrogen and combine them. Urea plants turn ammonia and carbon dioxide into urea, which is finished as prills or granules. Petrokimia Gresik also makes phosphate and NPK products, with sulphur and acids upstream. At Cilegon, the Chandra Asri cracker serves the petrochemical side; see the page for Chandra Asri Pacific. The mix is gas hazard in the synthesis and reforming areas and dust hazard in the finishing, bagging and bulk handling areas.

What fertiliser and petrochemical plants do to a handset

These plants run for years between major shutdowns, and their areas mix gas and dust. Five conditions matter.

Gas and dust in one operation

A fertiliser operator may pass from a reformer to a bagging hall. The Zone 1 and Zone 21 design target means one device replaces two.

Hydrogen and IIC

Reformer and synthesis units carry hydrogen. A handset marked for IIC covers it. The IS-04Z1 marking target is II 2 G Ex ib IIC T4.

Urea dust and washdown

Fertiliser dust gets into everything and plants wash down regularly. IP68 sealing is designed to resist dust and water.

Heat and humidity

Gresik, Bontang and Palembang are hot and humid. The certificate fixes the ambient range.

Large shutdown crews

Turnaround brings many contractors. The managed console lets the operator control enrolment and removal.

Methanol and other liquids at Bontang

Beyond ammonia and urea, the Bontang industrial area also handles methanol and other flammable liquids, so a handset in that area meets IIA vapour as well as hydrogen and dust.

Long walks between units

Fertiliser complexes are large, with urea towers, ammonia storage, jetties and warehouses far apart. A full-shift battery and four-constellation GNSS matter more than any single feature.

Work and approval · Petrochemical and fertiliser

Turnarounds, rounds and approval for an explosion proof mobile phone in Indonesian fertiliser plants

Fertiliser plants run continuously and stop for planned turnarounds, when inspection and maintenance crews work through the plant. Operator rounds cover reformers, compressors, urea synthesis, finishing and storage. State-owned operators follow their own procurement and HSE rules, which we have not seen published, and Indonesian state entities generally also have local content expectations that a supplier should raise early. Ask each plant's procurement and HSE teams what evidence is needed, who must approve a new device class and whether a pilot on one unit comes first. Contractors such as engineering and maintenance firms often bring their own devices into these plants, so the operator's policy on contractor handsets is as important as its own. IECEx documentation is the usual technical base, with Indonesian type approval handled separately.

Questions · Petrochemical and fertiliser

Frequently asked questions

Does ammonia change which phone I need?

Ammonia is a toxic as well as flammable gas, so personal detection and procedure matter. The phone does not replace a gas detector.

Is urea dust explosive?

Combustibility depends on the material, particle size and moisture, and sulphur and organic additives behave differently from urea itself. Area classification for dust is done by a competent person for each building, and the handset is designed for Zone 21 where that classification applies.

Which page covers Cilegon?

See the page for Chandra Asri Pacific.

Do I need a different phone for gas and dust?

No. The IS-04Z1 is designed for both Zone 1 and Zone 21.

Is the phone approved for these plants?

No. It is pre-launch and certification is in progress. Nothing here implies a relationship with Pupuk Indonesia or its companies.
