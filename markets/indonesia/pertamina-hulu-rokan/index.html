Indonesia · Pertamina Hulu Rokan

Explosion proof mobile phone for Pertamina Hulu Rokan in Indonesia

An explosion proof mobile phone for Pertamina Hulu Rokan in Indonesia has to work across the Rokan block in Riau, where Duri and Minas are the best-known fields and the working area is a spread of gathering stations, wells and pipelines rather than one fenced plant. Pertamina Hulu Rokan took over operation of the block from Chevron Pacific Indonesia in August 2021. The IS-04Z1 is designed around intrinsic safety rather than a flameproof enclosure.

The block · Rokan, Riau

What is classified across the Rokan block

The Rokan block is oilfield country rather than a single site. Minas, discovered in the 1940s, produces light crude, while Duri is a heavy oil field developed with one of the largest steamflood operations anywhere, where steam is injected to thin the oil so that it flows. Between them sit other fields and many gathering stations. Crude is moved by pipeline towards the Dumai area on the Strait of Malacca. Steam generation, cogeneration and water treatment support the heavy oil operation, and associated gas, produced water and heated crude tanks all create gas hazards at the facilities. The field is spread over a large area of Riau, much of it plantation and forest, so a technician's day involves long drives between classified areas, with the nearest safe-area charging point at the camp or district office.

What the Rokan block does to a handset

Rokan combines a hot, wet equatorial climate with distance and heavy contractor traffic. Four conditions matter most.

Heat on the heavy oil side

Steam lines and heated crude push surface temperatures up around Duri. Temperature class matters here: the IS-04Z1 marking target is II 2 G Ex ib IIC T4, and the certificate states what that means for ambient range and surface temperature.

Rain, mud and drops

Wet season operations mean mud, standing water and a steady risk of a handset dropping from a truck step. IP68 sealing and rugged build are designed for that environment, which is the main reason to carry a sealed handset rather than a phone in a pouch.

Distance between wells

A well route can take hours by road, and a coordinate is the most reliable way to report a fault in plantation country. Four-constellation GNSS records one, and the 50 MP camera attaches a photograph that is legible without the supervisor visiting.

Patchy cellular coverage

Coverage across the block changes with terrain and tower location. Wi-Fi at stations and push-to-talk on the same handset reduce the need for a radio and a phone to be carried separately.

Work and approval · Rokan

Rotations, contractors and approval for an explosion proof mobile phone at Rokan, Indonesia

Pertamina Hulu Rokan is a Pertamina subsidiary, so it works within the group's procurement and HSE frameworks, and as an upstream operator it also reports to Indonesia's upstream regulator. The usual evidence for an electrical device in a classified area is an IECEx or ATEX certificate, with Indonesian type approval and local content rules handled separately. We do not know the details of any Rokan-specific equipment list, so the right route is to ask the operator's procurement and HSE teams what a supplier must provide.

Production01

Field operator rounds

A production operator visits wells and flowstations in sequence, records readings and photographs anything abnormal. One handset replaces the radio, camera and paper sheet, and positions each record on the map.

Maintenance02

Steam and utility call-outs

A fault on a steam generator or injection line brings a technician from the district to a location that is easier to describe by coordinates than by directions.

Contractors03

Drilling and workover crews

Rig crews work to permits issued each morning and change as campaigns do. Enrolling a handset in the existing MDM console, and removing it at demobilisation, keeps control with the operator.

Questions · Pertamina Hulu Rokan

Frequently asked questions

Is the Rokan block a gas hazard or a dust hazard?

Gas, overwhelmingly. Hydrocarbon vapour and associated gas drive classification at flowstations, tanks and wells. Zone 21 matters little in the field itself, and the dual Zone 1 and Zone 21 design is relevant when the same staff also visit coal or powder handling elsewhere.

Does Pertamina Hulu Rokan buy through Pertamina?

We cannot say how its purchasing is organised. Ask the operator's procurement team which route applies and what registration is needed. For the refining side of the group, see the page on an explosion proof phone for Pertamina.

Is the phone usable on a rig with limited coverage?

The device can use Wi-Fi and Bluetooth for local systems and has push-to-talk, but any link back to a control room depends on the operator's network. Test it on the actual network before committing.

How long would a handset last on a well route?

The 4,000 mAh battery is specified for a full shift. Actual endurance depends on screen use, camera and network, so an evaluation unit on a typical route gives a better answer than a datasheet.
