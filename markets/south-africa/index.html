South African sites mix highveld weather, with hot summers, cold winter mornings and violent storms, with the dust of coal and mineral handling and the sheer scale of the Secunda complex.

For evaluators in South Africa, the IS-04Z1 explosion proof mobile phone is designed around these points: Highveld heat and winter cold; Hydrogen-rich process streams; Coal dust and Zone 21; A full shift away from a charger; Coverage across very large sites; Control over a large fleet.

Highveld heat and winter cold

Summers are hot and winter mornings on the highveld are cold. IP68 sealing keeps water and dust out, and the certificate will state the ambient range the device is approved for.

Hydrogen-rich process streams

Synthetic fuels and refining involve hydrogen-rich streams, which put some areas into gas group IIC. A marking target of Ex ib IIC covers the most demanding gas group as well as IIA and IIB.

Coal dust and Zone 21

Coal handling, conveying and preparation at mines and power stations create Zone 21 dust areas, which the design covers. Underground mining is equipment Group I and outside this device's scope.

A full shift away from a charger

Process plant and conveyor routes offer no safe area socket mid shift. The 4,000 mAh battery is sized for a working day with camera, torch and GNSS in use.

Coverage across very large sites

On sites the size of Secunda, or out on a mine's surface plant, the device falls back to plant Wi-Fi, and four constellation GNSS fixes a position between structures.

Control over a large fleet

Handsets pass between shifts and contractors. Android 16 with EMM and MDM support gives enrolment, remote lock and remote wipe, and keeps the fleet inside corporate policy.

Regulatory route · South Africa

What your HSE and procurement teams will ask for.

Under the Occupational Health and Safety Act and its electrical regulations, explosion protected apparatus used in a hazardous location in South Africa must be certified by a certification body accredited by SANAS, the South African National Accreditation System. Certification is to the SANS 60079 series, which mirrors IEC 60079, and the IECEx test report is normally the technical basis. Mines work under the Mine Health and Safety Act with equivalent requirements.

Radio equipment needs ICASA type approval before it can be sold or used. Large buyers such as Sasol and the mining houses also apply their own engineering standards and supplier vetting, including broad-based black economic empowerment scoring, so supply runs through a locally registered partner.

Ask about South Africa →
Is the IS-04Z1 approved for use in South Africa yet?

No. The device is pre-launch: IECEx certification is in progress and the South African Ex certificate follows it. Nothing should be planned into a hazardous location until the certificates exist.

Do I need a South African Ex certificate?

Yes. Explosion protected apparatus used in a hazardous location in South Africa must be certified by a SANAS-accredited certification body to the SANS 60079 series. It is in the programme, sequenced after IECEx.

Is it suitable for underground mines?

No. Underground mining is equipment Group I, a separate category from the surface industry Group II that this device targets. Surface plant classified as Zone 1 or Zone 21 is within scope.

Does the phone need ICASA approval?

Yes. It carries 4G LTE, Wi-Fi, Bluetooth and NFC radios, so ICASA type approval is required and forms part of the launch programme alongside the Ex certification.

Can it be used where hydrogen is present?

The marking target is Ex ib IIC T4, and IIC is the gas group that includes hydrogen. The final marking will be confirmed on the certificate, and operator management of change still applies.

When will units be available, and who repairs them?

Launch is Q4 2026, with supply in South Africa following the local Ex certificate. Warranty and service run through the South African partner SAMS is appointing, and repair of certified Ex equipment must be done by a recognised facility.

What is the price of an explosion proof phone in South Africa?

No list price is published before the certificates exist. Pricing is quoted in writing against quantity and configuration through the South African partner, so it is set with the channel rather than published.
