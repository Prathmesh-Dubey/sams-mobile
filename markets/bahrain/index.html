Bahrain’s summer combines high temperature with heavy coastal humidity, harder on a sealed enclosure than dry heat alone. The ATEX marking target is II 2 G Ex ib IIC T4, and the temperature class is the first line an instrumentation engineer checks.

Regulatory route · Bahrain

What your HSE and procurement teams will ask for.

Conformity in Bahrain runs through the Bahrain Standards and Metrology Directorate (BSMD) under the Ministry of Industry and Commerce, which implements the GCC-wide GSO technical regulations that Bahrain shares with Saudi Arabia, the UAE, Kuwait, Qatar and Oman. For hazardous-area equipment the technical evidence behind that conformity marking is an IECEx Certificate of Conformity, and because this product is a phone it also needs Telecommunications Regulatory Authority (TRA) type approval for the radio. None of the three is finished yet.

Getting past the plant gate is a separate exercise from national conformity. Bapco and Alba each run their own approved-vendor and engineering-evaluation process for equipment entering a classified area, and a foreign manufacturer is normally expected to supply through a Bahraini-registered partner who can carry local stock and paperwork. A device can be fully GSO-marked and still be unusable on site until the operator’s own instrumentation or telecoms group has listed it, so plan for two timelines rather than one.

Can we buy an explosion proof mobile phone from SAMS in Bahrain today?

Not yet. The IS-04Z1 is pre-launch and every certification behind it, including IECEx, ATEX and the BSMD/GSO conformity route, is still in progress ahead of the Q4 2026 launch. What exists today is the datasheet, the certification schedule and a place in the evaluation programme.

What is the price of an explosion proof phone in Bahrain?

We do not publish one. Until certification completes there is no list price and no quoted delivery, and a supplier who gives you a firm figure for an uncertified Ex device is telling you something they cannot support. Pre-launch pricing is available on request against a stated quantity and site.

Is an explosion proof phone the same thing as an intrinsically safe phone?

They are different protection concepts, although the search terms are used interchangeably. Flameproof, written Ex d, contains an explosion inside a heavy enclosure. Intrinsic safety, written Ex ib, limits circuit energy so ignition cannot occur, and that is what a Zone 1 handset uses. The marking target here is II 2 G Ex ib IIC T4.

Which zones and gas groups is it designed for?

Zone 1 and Zone 21, gas and dust. NEC 500 Class I Division 1 is being pursued alongside, which matters on projects at Bahraini sites run by contractors working to the American code rather than IEC 60079.

Will Bapco or Alba accept it on site?

That decision belongs to each operator and follows the certificates rather than preceding them. The IECEx certificate is the evidence their engineering review is built on, and the vendor file is being prepared with a Bahraini partner.

Will it cope with Bahrain’s heat and humidity?

It is designed to, with IP68 sealing and rugged build behind it. The number to evaluate is the temperature class on the certificate, checked against the maximum surface temperature your area classification permits.

Does it work in Arabic?

Yes. Android 16 runs the interface in Arabic or English on the same handset and the user manual will be issued in both. Your own site applications keep whatever languages they already use.

How do repairs and battery replacement work?

A device holding an Ex certificate cannot be opened on site or repaired by a general phone workshop without invalidating it. The service route runs through SAMS and the Bahraini partner it is appointing, with spares pooled near Sitra.
