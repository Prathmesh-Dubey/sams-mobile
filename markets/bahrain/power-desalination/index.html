Bahrain · Power & Desalination

Explosion proof mobile phone for power and desalination in Bahrain

Bahrain's power and water plants are gas-fired and clustered on the coast, so an explosion proof mobile phone in Bahrain's power sector is mostly a question about fuel-gas areas, turbine halls and the contractors who maintain them. This page covers the independent plants at Al Dur and Hidd, the state-owned stations and what the IS-04Z1 is designed for there. The IS-04Z1 is designed around intrinsic safety rather than a flameproof enclosure.

The sector · Power and desalination

Explosion proof mobile phone for Bahrain's power and desalination plants

Bahrain generates electricity from natural gas and the Electricity and Water Authority (EWA) buys power and water from independent producers as well as running its own stations. Al Dur Power and Water Company operates the Al Dur plants on the south-east coast, Al Ezzel Power Company is at the Hidd Industrial Estate, and Hidd Power Company runs the Hidd power and water station. EWA also has Sitra power and water assets, and trade press reports it prequalifying bidders for a new Sitra independent water and power project and a standalone Hidd seawater reverse osmosis plant.

Desalination is not itself a classified process, which surprises people. The classified areas are the fuel-gas supply, the turbines and the hydrogen or battery systems that a power plant carries. A desalination hall next door may be an ordinary area.

How work runs · Power

How a power plant is operated, and what it asks of a handset

Rounds are fixed and repeated

Operators walk the same route each shift: turbine hall, fuel-gas skid, water treatment, control room. A handset that logs readings and photographs at each stop turns the round into data.

Outages are planned around summer demand

Major maintenance is scheduled outside the peak cooling season, so contractor numbers spike in cooler months. Enrolment at that scale is a console job.

Coastal air and humidity

These plants draw seawater for cooling and desalination, so salt air and humidity are constant. IP68 sealing is the design answer to splash and wash-down.

Hydrogen sets the demanding gas group

Where hydrogen is present at generators or battery rooms it is group IIC. The Ex ib IIC T4 marking target is written to cover it.

Each plant is its own company

Al Dur, Al Ezzel and Hidd are separate legal entities with separate procurement, so an approval at one is not an approval at another.

Approvals · Power and desalination

What the operators will ask of a supplier

The IS-04Z1 is pre-launch (Q4 2026) and no certificate has been issued; IECEx, ATEX, NEC 500 and BSMD work is in progress. SAMS has no relationship with EWA or any plant named here.

Bahrain's Tender Board publishes public tenders and a list of prequalified vendors, and EWA publishes prequalification forms for its own purchasing. The independent plants are private companies with their own procurement, so ask each plant's procurement and HSE teams how they approve equipment for classified areas, whether they accept an IECEx certificate on its own, and whether they require a local agent. Expect questions on the gas group and temperature class, the battery and charger, the camera policy and the repair route. The national route (BSMD, GSO and TRA) is on the Bahrain country page.

Questions · Power and desalination

Frequently asked questions

Is desalination a hazardous-area risk in the same way as gas turbines?

The desalination process itself is not usually classified. Where it shares a plant with generation, the fuel-gas areas of the power side are classified, and chemical dosing or hydrogen from hypochlorite generation may need assessment.

Does the same device suit Al Dur, Al Ezzel and Hidd?

The specification target is the same, but each plant is a separate operator and approves equipment itself.

Is hydrogen from generator cooling a concern for a handset?

It is a group IIC gas, the most demanding gas group, and the marking target Ex ib IIC T4 is written for it. The plant's classification drawing says where it applies.

Who maintains the plants and do they need devices too?

Original equipment manufacturers and contractors maintain turbines and generators on service agreements. They can be issued enrolled devices for a campaign and the console removes the profile at the end.
