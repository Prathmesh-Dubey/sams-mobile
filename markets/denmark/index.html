Regulatory route · Denmark

What your HSE and procurement teams will ask for.

The product side is European and short. ATEX 2014/34/EU governs the equipment, Sikkerhedsstyrelsen is the Danish authority for the ATEX executive order and can require an economic operator to put a non-compliant product right, and no separate Danish approval sits on top of the notified body certificate and the CE marking. What surprises buyers from other EU countries is how the workplace side is split. The employer duty to assess explosion risk and hold an explosion protection document comes from the Danish Working Environment Authority, Arbejdstilsynet, under its executive order number 478. The classification of hazardous areas into zones, however, comes from Beredskabsstyrelsen, the emergency management agency, under executive order number 590 of 26 June 2003. In other words the drawing your equipment selection depends on is made under fire and rescue rules, and the selection itself is judged under working environment rules. Radio functions run under RED 2014/53/EU, with spectrum administered by the Danish Energy Agency.

Offshore adds a third layer and a commercial one. Safety and health on installations used for exploration and production of oil and gas sit under the offshore safety act, offshoresikkerhedsloven, supervised by Arbejdstilsynet, while Energistyrelsen remains the authority that grants and administers the licences themselves and takes advice from the working environment side. The commercial gate is Achilles JQS, which began in the 1990s as a joint Norwegian and Danish sector initiative to stop every operator running duplicate supplier audits, and is now the register that operators and major contractors on both shelves look up first. It is a renewable subscription rather than a certificate, which is why a stale profile can be as much of a problem in a tender as no profile at all. A supplier without a live entry rarely reaches the technical evaluation, however good the datasheet is.

Where the IS-04Z1 explosion proof mobile phone goes to work.

Typical Zone 1 and Zone 21 duty for the IS-04Z1 explosion proof mobile phone in Denmark includes: Permit work and handover on the North Sea hubs; Shutdown work in the Triangle Region; Solvent and powder rooms at Kalundborg; Rounds at an upgrading plant with one technician on site.

Offshore01

Permit work and handover on the North Sea hubs

On a producing hub the walk back to the control room is the price of every question about an isolation or a permit. A Zone 1 handset lets the technician photograph the valve, confirm the tag and close the task at the point of work, and at crew change the same device is wiped and handed to the incoming rotation rather than tracked as personal kit.

Refining and Power-to-X02

Shutdown work in the Triangle Region

A Fredericia turnaround fills the site with contractor crews for a few weeks, and the electrolysis and Power-to-X plant being built alongside it will add hydrogen areas to the same fence. Crews carrying hazardous area handsets enrolled in the site console keep the job pack, the isolation photograph and the sign-off in one system instead of a folder that leaves with the contractor.

Fermentation and pharma03

Solvent and powder rooms at Kalundborg

Insulin and enzyme production put ethanol handling, drying and powder dispensing in adjacent rooms of the same building, under gowning and cleaning regimes. One device valid in both zone types removes the ritual of leaving a phone at one airlock and collecting another at the next, and it keeps the batch photograph attached to the batch.

Biogas04

Rounds at an upgrading plant with one technician on site

Biomethane upgrading and grid injection plants are classified areas with very small crews, often a single technician working a route between sites. Equipment there has to hold a data link outdoors, keep a position fix and let one person document a fault properly the first time, because there is nobody in the next building to ask.

Is the IS-04Z1 already ATEX certified for use in Denmark?

No. It is designed to ATEX 2014/34/EU for Zone 1 and Zone 21 and the programme is in progress, with IECEx running alongside it. Launch is planned for Q4 2026, and nothing is supplied for use in a classified area before the certificate covering it exists.

Who decides our zones in Denmark, and does that affect equipment selection?

Zone classification is governed by Beredskabsstyrelsen under executive order 590 of 26 June 2003, while the duty to assess and document explosion risk comes from Arbejdstilsynet under executive order 478. Equipment selection has to match the classification drawing, so the practical answer is that you specify against the drawing you already hold rather than against a general statement about the site.

Can it be used offshore on the Danish shelf?

That is one of the design cases. Zone 1 classification, IP68 sealing and rugged build are aimed at exposed decks, and the certificate evidence is written to sit inside the documentation that Arbejdstilsynet supervises under the offshore safety act. Offshore trials belong to the pilot programme rather than being arranged afterwards.

Our tender asks for an Achilles JQS profile. Where do you stand?

Registration in Achilles JQS is part of the launch programme for this market, because operators on the Danish and Norwegian shelves both work from that register and treat it as a live subscription rather than a one-off certificate. Ahead of it, documentation and evaluation units are supplied under a direct trial agreement, which is the usual route for a product still in certification.

What is the price of an explosion proof phone in Denmark?

There is no list price before launch, and a supplier quoting one for an uncertified device deserves a second look. Pre-launch pricing is quoted against a stated quantity, configuration and support scope, in Danish kroner or euros to suit your framework. Evaluation units are issued under a trial agreement rather than sold.

Will there be Danish documentation and a Danish interface?

Yes. Android 16 carries a Danish interface, and the manual, the safe-use instructions and the declaration of conformity are produced in Danish alongside English. On a site where contractors rotate through quickly, working-language documentation is part of the safety case rather than a nicety.

Is it suitable for organic dust areas at a biogas or enzyme plant?

Zone 21 is a design target of the same programme as Zone 1. Enzyme powder, feed, grain and digestate solids all create dust atmospheres, and the certificate will state the dust marking and the maximum surface temperature so your selection can be justified against the classification drawing rather than by analogy with a gas rating.
