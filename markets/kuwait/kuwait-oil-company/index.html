Kuwait · Kuwait Oil Company

Explosion proof mobile phone for Kuwait Oil Company sites

Kuwait Oil Company runs upstream production from Greater Burgan in the south to the Jurassic gas fields near the northern border. This page is for HSE and telecoms teams looking for an explosion proof mobile phone for Kuwait Oil Company sites, with the northern sour-gas facilities in mind. The IS-04Z1 is designed around intrinsic safety rather than a flameproof enclosure.

The company · KOC

Gathering centres, sour crude and Jurassic gas

The Ahmadi page covers the headquarters town and the Greater Burgan gathering centres. This page looks at what is built further out. Gathering Centre 32 at Burgan is described as the first sour crude gathering centre in the field, built by Petrofac to handle about 120,000 barrels a day from the Arifjan, Marat, Minagish Oolite and Burgan Wara fields, which carry high hydrogen sulphide. In the north, the Jurassic production facilities at Sabriya and Umm Niqa, East Raudhatain and West Raudhatain were commissioned between 2017 and 2019, each reported at about 40,000 barrels a day of crude and more than 100 million standard cubic feet a day of sour gas. These sites are remote, newer than the southern plants and often run with small crews.

Hydrogen sulphide and a gas detector on every belt

Crews on sour service already wear a personal detector, and the handset must not compete with it for attention or a hand. Bluetooth lets the detector and the IS-04Z1 work together. Hydrogen sulphide is itself group IIB, so a group IIC design target covers it.

Distance from help

A wellpad visit in the north may be an hour from a workshop. Four-constellation GNSS puts a coordinate on an alarm or a photograph, and a 4,000 mAh battery is specified to last the shift.

Heat and shamal dust

Open sites hold summer heat all day and a northerly dust event can arrive within an hour. IP68 sealing is what keeps ports and seams clear, and the ATEX marking target II 2 G Ex ib IIC T4 is what your classification is checked against.

Photography on a permit

KOC facilities control photography, so a camera has to be manageable. The enrolled IS-04Z1 lets the console switch the 50 MP camera off or restrict it to approved applications.

What KOC teams will ask for

Registration at KOC is a separate track from national conformity

The country page notes that KOC runs its own vendor registration and prequalification. We have not verified the programme name or document list, so treat this as questions to ask.

Certificate basis
IECEx Certificate of Conformity once issued, with the marking string, gas group and temperature class.
National conformity
KUCAS Technical Evaluation and Inspection Reports and CITRA radio type approval.
Vendor route
Ask KOC procurement whether a registered Kuwaiti agent is required and which engineering review applies to telecoms equipment.
Telecoms and HSE
Ask what the telecoms group needs to see about enrolment, and what the HSE group needs for equipment selection.
Questions · Kuwait Oil Company

Frequently asked questions

Is the IS-04Z1 approved for KOC sites?

No. It is pre-launch, no certificate has been issued, and KOC's own engineering review follows the national conformity route.

Is SAMS a registered KOC vendor?

No. SAMS has no KOC registration. We are preparing documentation and intend to work through a registered Kuwaiti agent, and that is intent, not an approval.

Does it replace a personal H2S detector?

No. A handset is a communication device and never a gas detector. It is designed to sit alongside the detector crews already carry.

Does it work in Arabic?

Android 16 runs the interface in Arabic or English on the same handset, and your own applications keep their languages.
