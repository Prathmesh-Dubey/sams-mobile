Kuwait · Petrochemical & Utilities

Explosion proof mobile phone for petrochemicals and utilities in Kuwait

Kuwait's petrochemical plants and its power and water stations sit largely on the southern coast. This page is for the engineers there who need an explosion proof mobile phone for Kuwait petrochemical and utilities work, covering both the Shuaiba cluster and the power and desalination sites further south. The IS-04Z1 is designed around intrinsic safety rather than a flameproof enclosure.

The sector · Petrochemicals and utilities

Crackers, polymers and the power and water that serve them

At Shuaiba, EQUATE operates ethylene glycol and polyethylene production and Kuwait Olefins Company runs the second cracker, with Petrochemical Industries Company as a shareholder. Further south, Az-Zour North is described as Kuwait's first independent water and power project, with 1,500 MW of gas-fired combined-cycle generation and a desalination plant of up to 107 million gallons a day, commissioned in 2016 and purchased by the Ministry of Electricity and Water. A phase two and three scheme with ACWA Power was reported as signed in 2025. Sabiya is another gas-fired power and desalination site north of Kuwait Bay. The two halves of the sector are run differently. Petrochemical plants run continuously with shutdowns planned in blocks, while power and water stations work to summer peak demand, so outage windows are usually set around the hottest months. Both rely on contractor crews for maintenance, and both have operators who spend most of a shift on foot between buildings that are not classified and pockets that are. The Shuaiba page covers the industrial area and the PIC page the company, for the site-level view.

Gas group IIC for hydrogen, covering ethylene too

Hydrogen is group IIC and ethylene is group IIB, so a IIC design target covers both. The IS-04Z1 is designed to Ex ib IIC T4, and certification is still in progress.

Dust in the polymer halls

Polyethylene pellets and powders make combustible dust, so Zone 21 is a design target next to Zone 1.

Ordinary-looking power stations with classified pockets

Fuel gas skids and generator hydrogen mean a power station operator may cross classified boundaries without noticing, so one intrinsically safe handset removes the question.

Salt, humidity and heat

On the coast, IP68 sealing and a 4,000 mAh battery matter more than any other specification points.

Questions · Petrochemicals and utilities

Frequently asked questions

Is the IS-04Z1 approved for these sites?

No. It is pre-launch and no certificate has been issued.

Does a power station need Ex equipment?

Parts of it do, such as fuel gas skids and generator hydrogen systems. The classification drawings show which.

Which gas groups apply?

Hydrogen is group IIC, ethylene is group IIB and methane is group IIA. Equipment designed for IIC is suitable for all three, subject to temperature class.

Does Az-Zour North need Ex equipment?

Fuel gas and generator hydrogen systems typically do. We have not seen the plant's classification drawings, so ask the operator which areas are classified before assuming anything.
