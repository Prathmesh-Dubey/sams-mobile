Explosion proof mobile phone for PIC's Shuaiba operations

Petrochemical Industries Company is Kuwait's state petrochemical holding, with its roots in the Shuaiba fertiliser complex built in the 1960s. This page is for the HSE and telecoms teams who want an explosion proof mobile phone for Kuwait's Shuaiba petrochemical estate, where gas and dust classification meet on the same shift. The IS-04Z1 is designed around intrinsic safety rather than a flameproof enclosure.

The company · PIC

A fertiliser heritage, now olefins and aromatics

PIC started production at Shuaiba in 1966, adding further ammonia and urea plants in 1970. Its 1.1 million tonne a year urea plant closed permanently on 30 June 2018 as the company moved its focus to olefins and aromatics, according to ICIS. PIC is a shareholder in the EQUATE and Kuwait Olefins complexes at Shuaiba. An aromatics complex running KNPC naphtha to paraxylene and benzene is reported among its assets, and a larger olefins and aromatics scheme tied to Al-Zour is still described as pre-investment decision. Earlier descriptions of PIC as a current urea producer are out of date, so this page describes a site in transition rather than a fertiliser works. Some ageing plant remains next to modern units, which affects documentation more than hardware.

What is classified in the estate

Aromatics units
Naphtha, reformate, benzene and paraxylene, plus hydrogen in the reforming section.
Steam crackers
Ethylene (group IIB), hydrogen (IIC) and high temperature furnaces in the neighbouring complexes.
Polyethylene handling
Powder and pellet silos and bagging, where dust classification applies.
Storage and loading
Spheres, refrigerated tanks and jetty loading arms.
Former fertiliser areas
Mostly shut down, with legacy ammonia and storage still needing area records.
Utilities
Fuel gas, flare and power systems across the fence line.

Gas group IIC in the cracker and reformer

Hydrogen in the reformer calls for group IIC and ethylene is group IIB, so IIC covers both. The IS-04Z1 is designed to Zone 1, group IIC, temperature class T4, with certification still in progress ahead of the Q4 2026 launch.

Dust where pellets and powders move

Polyethylene pellet handling and bagging produce combustible dust, so a gas-only device is the wrong answer in those buildings. Zone 21 is a design target alongside Zone 1, and the site's classification register decides where it applies.

A shared fence line with several employers

Staff from different companies and many contractors work in the same area. Enrolment gives a clear way to issue and recover handsets per contract.

Salt, humidity and summer heat

Shuaiba sits on the coast, so IP68 sealing and rugged build are the relevant figures for a handset carried outdoors all day.

Questions · Petrochemical Industries Company

Frequently asked questions

Does PIC still make urea at Shuaiba?

Trade reports say the urea plant closed permanently on 30 June 2018. Check the current site list with PIC before specifying anything for a former fertiliser area.

Is the IS-04Z1 approved for PIC sites?

No. It is pre-launch and no certificate has been issued.

Does a phone need Zone 21 in a petrochemical plant?

Where combustible dust is present in normal operation, as in pellet and powder handling, the area may be Zone 21 or 22. Ask PIC for the classification drawings for each building.

Is a registered Kuwaiti agent required?

Ask PIC procurement. Evaluation quantities can be supplied directly while a local channel is appointed.
