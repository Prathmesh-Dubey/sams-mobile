Czech Republic · Spolana

Explosion proof mobile phone for Spolana, Neratovice

An explosion proof mobile phone for Spolana has to suit a chemical site with a long history, a small operating team and a mixed set of hazards. Spolana in Neratovice, on the Elbe, is the Czech Republic's PVC producer, part of the Orlen Unipetrol group since 2016. This page is for the HSE officers and engineers deciding what may be carried into its plants in the Czech Republic. The IS-04Z1 is designed around intrinsic safety rather than a flameproof enclosure.

The site · Spolana

Explosion proof mobile phone for Spolana, Neratovice

The Neratovice site dates from 1898, began as a soap and candle works, and grew into a chemical complex through the twentieth century. It now employs more than 700 people, and its products include PVC sold as Neralit, along with sodium hydroxide, hydrochloric acid, oleum and sulphuric acid, and in 2018 a granulated fertiliser was added. Spolana ended its mercury-cell chlorine electrolysis on 30 November 2017 to comply with EU rules, and now buys ethylene dichloride (EDC) for PVC production rather than making its own chlorine. This changes the picture on the country page: chlorine electrolysis is no longer a hazard here, but EDC is a flammable, toxic liquid, vinyl chloride monomer is a flammable gas, and PVC itself is handled as a powder, which brings dust classification into play. Natural-gas boilers replaced the old coal plant. Older descriptions of the site also list caprolactam; confirm the current status of that unit with the company before assuming it.

Toxic and flammable together

EDC is both toxic and flammable. The handset deals with the ignition side; toxicity stays with Spolana's own gas detection, alarms and emergency procedures, which the device has to work alongside.

Gas and dust in one device

The target of II 2 G Ex ib IIC T4 plus Zone 21 dust protection sits in a single programme, which matters at a site where powder and gas areas are close together.

A site with several eras of building

Plants extended repeatedly across a century leave classification drawings of mixed vintage. A broadly rated device suits a site that is hard to map exactly.

Wash-down and river air

IP68 sealing is aimed at wash-down and humidity near the Elbe. Certified ambient limits will be on the datasheet.

Czech documents

Instructions and the declaration of conformity are prepared in Czech alongside English.

Questions · Spolana

Frequently asked questions

Is the IS-04Z1 certified for use at Spolana yet?

No. It is pre-launch and ATEX certification is in progress ahead of the Q4 2026 launch.

Does Spolana still make chlorine?

Spolana ended mercury-cell chlorine electrolysis in November 2017 and says it now buys EDC for PVC production.

Is PVC powder a dust hazard?

Fine polymer powder can form an explosive dust cloud, and operators classify such areas as dust zones. The classification at each point comes from Spolana's own assessment.

Does a gas rating cover EDC toxicity?

No. Ex certification addresses ignition, while toxicity is handled by the site's detection and emergency procedures.
