Czech Republic · MERO ČR

Explosion proof mobile phone for MERO ČR pipelines

An explosion proof mobile phone for MERO ČR has to serve a company whose assets are pipe, pumps and tanks rather than process units. MERO ČR is the Czech state operator of the crude oil transport system into the Czech Republic, running the Družba and IKL pipelines and the Nelahozeves central tank farm. This page is for the engineers and HSE officers who maintain that system and decide what a technician may carry along its route and inside its stations. The IS-04Z1 is designed around intrinsic safety rather than a flameproof enclosure.

The system · MERO ČR

Explosion proof mobile phone for the Czech crude pipelines

Published figures put the Družba pipeline at 357 km within the Czech Republic, reaching Záluží near Litvínov in 1965, with an annual transport capacity of 9 million tonnes. The IKL pipeline runs from Vohburg in Germany to Nelahozeves, 168 km of it in the Czech Republic and rated at 10 million tonnes a year, and it feeds the Kralupy and Litvínov refineries. At Nelahozeves the central crude oil tank farm holds 16 tanks: four of 50,000 cubic metres, six of 100,000 and six of 125,000, which adds up to roughly 1.55 million cubic metres. Press reports say Družba deliveries to the Czech Republic stopped in March 2025 and that the refineries now run on crude arriving by the TAL pipeline from Trieste through Germany and onward by IKL. The physical assets remain, so the work is now weighted towards IKL and the tank farm. Crude oil vapour is a gas group IIA hazard, and the classified areas are pumping and metering stations, pig traps, the tank farm and the points where the pipelines meet the refinery fence.

What a pipeline operator asks of a handset

Distance is the defining condition. A route of several hundred kilometres is worked by small crews who spend much of the shift driving between stations that are often unmanned.

Unmanned stations in open country

A pumping or valve station may have a fence and a control cabinet and nobody else for kilometres. The 4,000 mAh battery is specified to carry a day of calls, a maintenance application and the camera without a safe-area charge.

Navigation to a point on a pipe

Four-constellation GNSS puts a coordinate on an inspection record, which is more useful than a chainage figure when the route passes through fields and woodland.

Two directions, one policy

With supply routes able to change, procedures change too. A managed device lets the operator push a revised procedure or form to every handset at once through Android Enterprise.

Tank farm hazards

At Nelahozeves a technician on a roof or in a bund works in a gas group IIA vapour area. The design target of II 2 G Ex ib IIC T4 is a broader envelope, to be confirmed by the certificate.

Cold and wet

Czech winters are a working condition on a tank roof. IP68 sealing and the cold-weather testing in rugged build are aimed at that; the certified ambient range is stated on the datasheet once the certificate fixes it.

Questions · MERO ČR

Frequently asked questions

Is the IS-04Z1 certified for use at MERO ČR sites?

No. It is pre-launch and ATEX certification is in progress ahead of the Q4 2026 launch.

Has the end of Družba deliveries changed the hazardous areas?

Press reports say Czech Družba deliveries stopped in March 2025, but the stations remain and still need maintenance. The classification at a station comes from its own documents, not from where the crude arrives from.

Is the Nelahozeves tank farm a dust area as well as a gas area?

Crude storage is a gas and vapour hazard. Dust classification does not normally apply to a crude tank farm.

Does MERO ČR also handle refined products?

No. Refined product is moved by ČEPRO, which runs its own pipelines and depots.
