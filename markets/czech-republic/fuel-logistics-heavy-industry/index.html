Czech Republic · Fuel logistics and heavy industry

Explosion proof mobile phone for fuel logistics and heavy industry in the Czech Republic

An explosion proof mobile phone for fuel logistics and heavy industry in the Czech Republic has to serve two quite different worlds: pipelines, tank farms and gas storage on one side, and the coke ovens and gas networks of steelmaking on the other. This page is for the operators and contractors who decide what may be carried into those sites. The IS-04Z1 is designed around intrinsic safety rather than a flameproof enclosure.

The sector · Fuel logistics and heavy industry

Explosion proof mobile phone for Czech pipelines, storage and steelworks

Crude arrives through MERO ČR's pipelines to Nelahozeves and the refineries; refined product moves through ČEPRO's network of about 1,100 km and its depots. Natural gas is stored underground in South Moravia, where Moravské naftové doly operates storage at Uhřice on ground it previously used for oil and gas production near Hodonín. Further east, in the Ostrava and Třinec area, the coke ovens carry the coke oven gas hazard. In Ostrava, press reports say the primary steelmaking of Liberty Ostrava has been idle and the plant changed hands in October 2025 under the Nová Huť name, while OKK Koksovny remains a separate coke producer; the status of any plant should be checked with the owner. Třinecké železárny operates a closed cycle from coke ovens to hot rolled steel. The sector covers crude vapour, refined fuel vapour, natural gas, hydrogen-rich coke oven gas and combustible dust from coal and coke.

What this sector asks of a handset

The sector is held together by distance on the logistics side and by gas networks on the steel side.

IP ratingIP68
Battery4,000 mAh

Distance between sites

Crews cover pipeline routes and depots by road. Four-constellation GNSS and a 4,000 mAh battery are aimed at long days away from a charging point.

Natural gas and hydrogen-rich gas

Natural gas is a lower gas group; coke oven gas is hydrogen-rich. The design target of II 2 G Ex ib IIC T4 is aimed at the more demanding group, with scope defined by the certificate.

Dust at steelworks

Coal and coke handling are dust areas, and Zone 21 is in the same programme.

One managed fleet

Android Enterprise lets one console manage devices across depots, stations and works.

Weather

IP68 sealing is aimed at Czech winters and wash-down; the certified ambient range will be on the datasheet.

Use cases · Fuel logistics and heavy industry

Where the IS-04Z1 goes to work.

Pipelines01

Station and depot rounds

Operators and technicians move between depots and pipeline stations, recording readings and defects, photographing a leaking flange and calling the control room from one device, without a safe-area stop.

Gas storage02

Surface plant at Uhřice

Crews at wellheads, compressors and metering report faults with a photograph and position, which matters on a storage field spread over vineyards and farmland with few landmarks.

Steelworks03

Coke oven gas network

Gas crews check mains, valves and condensate points in coke oven gas areas, and record a suspected leak with a photograph and position rather than a verbal description.

Questions · Fuel logistics and heavy industry

Frequently asked questions

Is the IS-04Z1 certified for these sites yet?

No. It is pre-launch and ATEX certification is in progress ahead of the Q4 2026 launch.

Is coke oven gas covered by a pipeline gas rating?

No. It is hydrogen-rich and needs a gas group IIC device; the certificate will state its scope.

Do pipelines and steelworks need different devices?

The design intent is one standard, though each site's classification should be confirmed.

Has Ostrava coking changed?

Press reports describe changes at Liberty Ostrava in 2025. Check the current status with the owner.
