Where it works in the Czech Republic

Czech classified areas are concentrated and well mapped. Refining sits at Litvínov in the north, where the refinery and the steam cracker share the Záluží complex, and at Kralupy nad Vltavou, which runs a conversion refinery with a fluid catalytic cracker. Chemistry runs from Neratovice on the Elbe through Lovosice to the tar and benzol plant at Valašské Meziříčí. Around them sits a fuel logistics network of crude and product pipelines, terminals and gas storage, and in Moravia and Silesia the coke ovens and metallurgy that still carry some of the country's largest gas hazards.

Operators and contractors such as Orlen Unipetrol, Spolana and Synthos Kralupy need an explosion proof mobile phone they can carry into classified areas at Litvínov and Záluží, Kralupy nad Vltavou and Neratovice. The IS-04Z1 is designed for Zone 1 and Zone 21 duty there.

Regulatory route · Czech Republic

What your HSE and procurement teams will ask for.

The product route is straightforward and entirely European. ATEX 2014/34/EU is transposed by nařízení vlády č. 116/2016 Sb. on conformity assessment of equipment and protective systems intended for use in an explosive atmosphere. A notified body certificate, the EU declaration of conformity and CE marking place the device on the Czech market, and no further national approval follows. The country has an unusual advantage here: FTZÚ, the Physical-Technical Testing Institute at Ostrava-Radvanice, is ATEX notified body 1026, so Czech buyers are used to reading certificates closely and often ask which body issued one before they ask anything else. The radio side runs under RED 2014/53/EU with ČTÚ as the national telecommunications authority, and Czech law expects the instructions supplied with the product to be in Czech.

The duty side belongs to the operator. Nařízení vlády č. 406/2004 Sb. sets the closer requirements for safety and health at work in an environment with a risk of explosion, and it is the Czech implementation of the workplace side of the ATEX pair. It requires the written document on protection against explosion, with the zone classification, the risk assessment and the reasoning behind equipment selection. Enforcement runs through SÚIP, the State Labour Inspection Office, and its regional labour inspectorates, which can ask to see that document and the evidence behind the equipment named in it. In practice a Czech evaluation of a handheld device is an argument about whether the certificate, the marking string and the Czech documentation are complete enough to be quoted in that file.

Ask about Czech Republic →
Is the IS-04Z1 already ATEX certified for the Czech market?

No. It is designed to ATEX 2014/34/EU for Zone 1 and Zone 21 and the certification programme is in progress. Launch is planned for Q4 2026, and nothing is supplied for use in a classified area before the certificate that covers it exists.

Which notified body will issue the certificate?

The issuing body is named on the certificate and will be published with it rather than described in advance. It is a fair question to ask here, because FTZÚ at Ostrava-Radvanice is ATEX notified body 1026 and Czech engineers are used to checking who signed a certificate before reading the rest of it.

Is a separate Czech approval needed after ATEX?

No. Nařízení vlády 116/2016 Sb. transposes ATEX 2014/34/EU, so a notified body certificate with the EU declaration of conformity and CE marking is the whole product-side requirement. What follows is the employer duty under nařízení vlády 406/2004 Sb., which SÚIP enforces.

How much does an ATEX phone cost in the Czech Republic?

There is no list price before launch, and a published price for an uncertified device would not mean much. Pre-launch pricing is quoted in writing against a stated quantity, configuration and support scope. Evaluation units are issued under a trial agreement rather than sold.

Will the instructions and the interface be in Czech?

Yes. Android 16 carries a Czech interface, and the instructions, the safe-use guidance and the declaration of conformity are produced in Czech alongside English. Czech law expects product instructions in Czech, so this is built into the launch rather than added on request.

Is it a flameproof mobile phone or an intrinsically safe one?

Intrinsically safe. The target protection concept is Ex ib, which limits the energy available in the device so that it cannot ignite the atmosphere, rather than the flameproof Ex d approach of containing an ignition inside a heavy enclosure. For a handheld device carried through a plant, ia is the concept that makes sense.

Who repairs the device and replaces the battery?

The Czech partner SAMS is appointing or a facility recognised for repairing Ex equipment. Opening the device outside that route breaks the protection concept the certificate depends on, so the repair and battery path is defined as part of the launch rather than settled after the first failure.
