Czech Republic · Orlen Unipetrol

Explosion proof mobile phone for Orlen Unipetrol, Litvínov & Kralupy

An explosion proof mobile phone for Orlen Unipetrol has to cover two refineries, the country's largest chemical complex and a contractor population that swells every time a unit comes down. Orlen Unipetrol is the Czech refining and petrochemical group owned by the Polish PKN Orlen, running refineries at Litvínov and Kralupy nad Vltavou and the petrochemical plants at Záluží. This page is for the HSE officers, operations managers and telecoms teams deciding what may be carried past the gate in the Czech Republic. The IS-04Z1 is designed around intrinsic safety rather than a flameproof enclosure.

The sites · Orlen Unipetrol

Explosion proof mobile phone for Litvínov, Záluží and Kralupy

Záluží near Litvínov is described by the company as the largest chemical production site in the Czech Republic. The steam cracker there was commissioned in 1979 and has ten cracking furnaces, and it is the starting point for the polyethylene and polypropylene units downstream. The refinery shares the site. Kralupy nad Vltavou, on the Vltava north of Prague, is the group's second refinery and makes fuels and agrochemical products. Both refineries now receive crude through the IKL pipeline fed by the TAL line from Trieste, which is covered on the MERO ČR page. The hazards are the usual refinery and olefin plant list with some particular emphasis. Ethylene is gas group IIB, hydrogen is IIC, and naphtha, propylene and gasoline vapours are IIA, so a plant-wide device standard has to be rated for IIC to be usable in the cracker's hydrogen and ethylene areas. The polyolefin side adds powder and pellet handling, where dust classification studies apply, which is the reason the Zone 21 design target matters here.

What Záluží and Kralupy ask of a handset

On a site of this size the walk between units is long, the units are interdependent and a handset has to be useful in both gas and powder areas. These are the points that usually come up.

A whole shift in the plant

There is no charging point inside a classified area and the walk to one at Záluží is not short. The 4,000 mAh battery is specified to cover a full shift with the camera and a maintenance application in use.

Hydrogen and ethylene in the same programme

The design target of II 2 G Ex ib IIC T4 is aimed at the more demanding gas groups. The certificate, once issued, states the exact marking, which your own classification drawings are checked against.

Gas and powder together

Zone 21 dust protection sits in the same certification programme, so one device can cross from the cracker area to a polymer powder area without a separate device for each.

Turnaround crews and re-issue

Planned outages at the Litvínov petrochemical plant have run to several weeks, with refinery units elsewhere on the site kept in operation. Android Enterprise enrolment, restriction and wipe handle a crew arriving and leaving.

Group and Czech language files

Czech instructions and the declaration of conformity are being prepared alongside English, and PKN Orlen's own documentation expectations should be asked for at the start.

Questions · Orlen Unipetrol

Frequently asked questions

Is the IS-04Z1 certified for use at Litvínov or Kralupy today?

No. It is pre-launch and ATEX certification is in progress ahead of the Q4 2026 launch.

Why does the cracker need a handset rated for IIC?

Hydrogen is a gas group IIC substance and ethylene is IIB. A single standard device for plant-wide use needs to cover the most demanding group present.

Does Unipetrol's ownership by PKN Orlen change the requirements?

The legal framework is the same ATEX one across the EU. Group engineering standards may add to the documentation requested, which is a question for the operator's own teams.

Do Paramo and Spolana share Unipetrol's procurement?

They belong to the same group, but each site has its own hazards and its own equipment review, so do not assume a decision at one carries to another.
