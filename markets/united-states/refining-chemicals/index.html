The sector · Refining and chemicals

The Gulf Coast corridor, plant by plant

The Houston Ship Channel runs 52 miles from the Gulf of Mexico to Houston and is home to the largest petrochemical complex in the country. Named sites along it include the ExxonMobil Baytown complex, Shell Chemicals at Deer Park, LyondellBasell at Channelview and the ITC terminal at Deer Park. East of Houston, Beaumont and Port Arthur on the Sabine-Neches add the Motiva Port Arthur refinery and the ExxonMobil Beaumont refinery, and further east are the Lake Charles and Calcasieu complexes. The Baton Rouge corridor in Louisiana runs refining and chemical plants along the Mississippi toward Plaquemine, and Marathon’s Garyville refinery is further down the river. Chevron’s Pascagoula refinery sits just beyond on the Mississippi coast. In all of them the question is the same: a very large classified footprint with a very large contractor population, and a handset standard that has to work for both.

A mix of Division and Zone documents

Older units were drawn under the Division system and newer or international-standard units may carry Zone drawings. The IS-04Z1 is being certified to NEC 500, IECEx and ATEX so the same handset can be matched to either set of drawings.

Dust areas that gas-only fleets miss

A fleet specified only for gas leaves out coke, sulphur and polymer-dust areas. The Zone 21 design target is aimed at both, subject to each plant’s classification.

Outages bring hundreds of unfamiliar crews

During a turnaround, one fence may contain dozens of firms. Android 16 with Android Enterprise lets the plant issue, lock down and wipe units in bulk, and photographs and GNSS positions attach to the permit record.

Handset as one tool instead of three

Voice, camera and torch on one device replace a phone, a camera and a radio carried separately into the classified area. Check the plant’s rules before relying on any of those functions.

Approvals · Refining and chemicals

Questions a plant safety team will raise

We have not verified any operator-specific registration programme, so these are generic.

Standards01

Division, zone or both

The National Electrical Code permits both Article 500 Divisions and Article 505 Zones, and API publishes separate recommended practices for each. Ask the plant which governs the unit your people will enter.

Listing02

NRTL mark and group

The label must cover the gas or dust group present. Hydrogen is the demanding case, and a device not listed for it should not be taken into a hydrogen area.

Fleet03

Management and replacement

Plan for charging stations in the safe area, spares for outages and a policy for devices that leave site with contractors.

Questions · Refining and chemicals

Explosion proof mobile phone questions for United States

Which gas groups should a refinery fleet cover?

Group IIA covers most hydrocarbon vapour, group IIB covers ethylene, and group IIC covers hydrogen. The IS-04Z1 marking target is Ex ib IIC, so the design intent is to cover all three, subject to the certificate once issued.

Does the plant need an Ex phone if it already uses radios?

Radios do voice, not photographs, checklists or position records. Many plants run both, with a handset handling the data and the radio kept for push-to-talk.

Is the IS-04Z1 available for turnaround season this year?

No. It is pre-launch, certification is in progress and launch is planned for Q4 2026. Any turnaround use would follow the certificates, not precede them.
