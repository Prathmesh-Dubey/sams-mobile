Explosion proof mobile phone for mining in Australia

An explosion proof mobile phone for mining in Australia depends on the mineral. Iron ore is not a combustible dust, but coal is, and underground coal mines are governed by a different regime altogether. This page separates iron ore, surface coal and coal handling, and underground coal, and says plainly where the IS-04Z1 Zone 1 and Zone 21 design target does and does not apply. The IS-04Z1 is designed around intrinsic safety rather than a flameproof enclosure.

The sector · Australia

Explosion proof mobile phone use across Australian mining

Pilbara iron ore, run by BHP, Rio Tinto and Fortescue, moves ore along private railways to ports at Port Hedland and Dampier. Queensland's Bowen Basin and New South Wales's Hunter Valley produce coal, which is exported through terminals such as Hay Point, Abbot Point, Gladstone and Newcastle. Anglo American and Glencore operate Bowen Basin coal mines. Gladstone also handles alumina. For a classified area, the useful question is what the site handles: diesel, coal dust and methane create hazards, whereas iron ore generally does not, and a site's hazardous area register, not the commodity name, is the document to read.

Dust that is a nuisance, not a hazard

Iron ore and alumina dust mainly wear on seals and ports. Coal dust is combustible and may be classified. IP68 sealing helps either way, and the Zone 21 target applies where a site has classified dust.

Underground coal is a separate case

Underground coal mines use Group I equipment and are regulated by state regulators such as Resources Safety and Health Queensland and the NSW Resources Regulator. The IS-04Z1 is designed for Group II surface areas and should not be assumed to suit underground coal.

Distance and rosters

Many sites run on fly-in fly-out rosters, and a haul-road drive can be long. The 4,000 mAh battery is specified for a full shift away from a charger.

A transmitter near explosives

Radio-frequency controls near blast loading apply to any phone, whatever its Ex marking. The IS-04Z1 should be treated as a transmitter under the site blast procedure.

Many sites, one console

Android Enterprise management lets a mining company run one profile across mines, rail and ports, with different camera and application policies by site.

Work pattern and buying

How work is organised, and what buyers ask

Mining companies run their own contractor management and supplier approval, and the process differs between a Pilbara iron ore operator and a Queensland coal mine. These are the points to raise with each company's own teams. SAMS makes no claim of any relationship with any miner named here.

Rosters
Most remote mines use fly-in fly-out workers on fixed rotations. Handsets passed between crews need a known enrolment state.
Contractors
Mining contractors work to the host company's site rules. Ask who supplies the handset, the host or the contractor.
Supplier approval
Each operator's procurement and HSE teams set what a supplier must provide. SAMS has not been through any such process; ask for the current documents.
Evidence
IECEx certificate and scope, ANZEx from the same test reports, and the ACMA RCM radio marking. All in progress ahead of Q4 2026.
Group I and Group II
Confirm with the state regulator and the mine whether the area is surface Group II or underground Group I before any handset is considered.
Questions · Mining

Frequently asked questions

Is the IS-04Z1 certified for mining use in Australia yet?

No. The device is pre-launch, with IECEx and ANZEx certification in progress ahead of the Q4 2026 launch. No certificate has been issued.

Can it be used in underground coal mines?

It is designed for Group II surface areas, not Group I underground coal. Underground use needs equipment certified to the mining group and accepted by the state regulator.

Does iron ore dust need a Zone 21 handset?

Iron ore is not normally treated as combustible dust. Coal dust is, so coal preparation and handling plants are where dust classification arises.

Is there a page for BHP specifically?

Yes. The explosion proof phone for BHP in the Pilbara page covers the Pilbara chain of mines, railway and ports in more detail.
