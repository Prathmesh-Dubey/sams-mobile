Certification is underway with TÜV in Germany, ahead of the Q4 2026 launch.

Technical Overview

SAMS IS-04Z1

Powered by Qualcomm Snapdragon technology and engineered for demanding hazardous environments.

The SAMS IS-04Z1 is an intrinsically safe Android smartphone engineered for Zone 1/21 and Zone 2/22 hazardous environments. Built around a Qualcomm Snapdragon platform running Android 16, it combines a 4,000 mAh battery, a 50 MP inspection camera and IP68 ingress protection, with IECEx, ATEX and NEC 500 certification in progress — engineered to work exactly as hard as the sites it’s built for.
